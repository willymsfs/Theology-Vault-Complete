# Canonical Norms (CIC cc. 998-1007)

| Canon | Provision |
|---|---|
| c. 1003 | Every priest, and only a priest, validly administers it |
| c. 1004 §1 | For one who, having the use of reason, begins to be in danger from sickness or old age |
| c. 1004 §2 | May be repeated on recovery and fresh illness, or if the danger becomes graver |
| c. 1005 | In doubt as to illness, death, or use of reason — administer it |
| c. 1006 | May be given on implicit request made while in control of one's faculties |
| c. 1007 | Not to be conferred on those obstinately persisting in manifest grave sin |

c. 999 permits a priest to bless the oil in a case of necessity.
