# Augustine's Three Goods

| Good | Content |
|---|---|
| Proles | Offspring, to be received and raised in the faith |
| Fides | Mutual fidelity, the exclusion of adultery |
| Sacramentum | The indissoluble bond signifying Christ's union with the Church |

For Augustine sacramentum here means the permanent bond, not "sacrament" in the later technical sense. The framework governed Western marriage theology for fifteen centuries.
