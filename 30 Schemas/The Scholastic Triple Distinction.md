# The Scholastic Triple Distinction

| Level | For Baptism | Can it be blocked? |
|---|---|---|
| sacramentum tantum | The washing with water plus the Trinitarian words | No — it either happens or it does not |
| res et sacramentum | The indelible character | No — hence unrepeatability |
| res tantum | Sanctifying grace | Yes — by the recipient's disposition; restored later by reviviscentia |

This distinction is how the tradition explains a valid but unfruitful sacrament.
