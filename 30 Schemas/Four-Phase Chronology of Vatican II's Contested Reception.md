# Four-Phase Chronology of Vatican II's Contested Reception

| Phase | Years | Character |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Acknowledged, Received, Refused | 1965-1980 | Periti-led commentary; Humanae Vitae crisis; SSPX rejection |
| 2. Celebrated and Enforced | 1980-1990 | Roman doctrinal policy under JPII/Ratzinger; 1985 Synod; Ratzinger Report; Apostolos Suos trajectory |
| 3. Historicized | 1990-2000 | Alberigo's Bologna school; five-volume History of Vatican II |
| 4. Toward a New Fight | 2005-present | Benedict's election; subsistit in clarification (2007); Summorum Pontificum (2007); SSPX excommunications lifted (2009) |

Framework via Massimo Faggioli's reception scholarship.
