# New Testament Theologies and Paul's Three Christologies

## NT theologies
"a Matthean theology, a Marcan theology, a Lucan theology and a Johannine theology."

## Paul's three Christologies
| Type | Starts from | Meaning |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Ascending | Jesus' humanity | stresses His humanity |
| Descending | His divinity | begins from His divinity |
| Cosmic | the universe | Christ linked to the whole cosmos |
