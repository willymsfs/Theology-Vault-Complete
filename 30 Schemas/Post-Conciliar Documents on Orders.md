# Post-Conciliar Documents on Orders

| Document | Date | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Sacrum Diaconatus Ordinem | 1967 | Restores the permanent diaconate, including for married men |
| Pontificalis Romani | 1968 | Revised ordination rites; episcopal prayer drawn from the Apostolic Tradition |
| Ministeria Quaedam | 1972 | Suppresses minor orders and the subdiaconate; lay ministries of lector and acolyte |
| Inter Insigniores | 1976 | CDF declaration on the admission of women to the ministerial priesthood |
| Pastores Dabo Vobis | 1992 | Four dimensions of formation: human, spiritual, intellectual, pastoral |
| Ordinatio Sacerdotalis | 1994 | No authority to confer priestly ordination on women; to be definitively held |
| Omnium in Mentem | 2009 | In persona Christi Capitis reserved to bishops and priests; deacons ordained to serve |
| Querida Amazonia | 2020 | Declines the Synod's viri probati proposal |
