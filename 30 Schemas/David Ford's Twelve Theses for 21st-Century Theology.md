# David Ford's Twelve Theses for 21st-Century Theology

Closing maxims from the Epilogue, distilled from David F. Ford editing three editions of *The Modern Theologians*.

1. **"God is the One who blesses and loves in wisdom."**
   The ultimate ground and object of all theological inquiry is a personal, loving, superabundantly wise God.

2. **"Theology is done for the sake of God and the Kingdom of God."**
   Theology's purpose is not academic self-preservation but service of God and the active realization of God's reign in history.

3. **"The cry of prayer is the beginning, accompaniment, and end of theology: Come, Holy Spirit! Hallelujah! and Maranatha!"**
   Prayer and worship are the indispensable starting point, continuous accompaniment, and final destination of any authentic theology.

4. **"Study of scripture is at the heart of theology."**
   Rigorous, postcritical study of the Bible is Christian theology's primary, normative, animating anchor.

5. **"Describing reality in the light of God is a basic theological discipline."**
   Theology must realistically describe the physical, biological, and social world through the lens of divine creation and providence.

6. **"Theology hopes in and seeks God's purposes while immersed in the contingencies, complexities, and ambiguities of creation and history."**
   Theology stays eschatologically oriented toward God's promised future while remaining fully embedded in present historical struggle.

7. **"Theological wisdom seeks to do justice to many contexts, levels, voices, moods, genres, systems, and responsibilities."**
   Theology must actively incorporate diverse regional contexts, academic methods, and marginalized voices, not one dominant standpoint.

8. **"Theology is practiced collegially, in conversation and, best of all, in friendship; and, through the communion of saints, it is simultaneously premodern, modern, and postmodern."**
   Theology is inherently relational and collaborative, spanning premodern, modern, and postmodern eras at once through the communion of saints.

9. **"Theology is a broker of the arts, humanities, sciences, and common sense for the sake of a wisdom that affirms, critiques, and transforms each of them."**
   Theology plays a crucial interdisciplinary role, correlating and transforming the sciences, arts, and humanities for the sake of a unified wisdom.

10. **"Our religious and secular world needs theology with religious studies in its schools and universities."**
   Rigorous theology belongs alongside religious studies in secular educational institutions, informing the wider public sphere.

11. **"Conversation around scriptures is at the heart of interfaith relations."**
   Shared, collaborative reading of scriptural texts (Scriptural Reasoning) is the most fruitful path for interreligious dialogue.

12. **"Theology is for all who desire to think about God and about reality in relation to God."**
   Theological reflection is not the exclusive domain of academic specialists but a calling open to all who seek to understand reality in relation to God.
