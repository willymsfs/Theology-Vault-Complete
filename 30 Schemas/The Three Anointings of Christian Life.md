# The Three Anointings of Christian Life

| Sacrament | Anoints for | Oil |
|---|---|---|
| Baptism | Incorporation into Christ as priest, prophet, king | Sacred chrism, after the washing |
| Confirmation | Mission and witness | Sacred chrism |
| Anointing of the Sick | The final struggle and passage | Oil of the sick (oleum infirmorum) |

All three oils are blessed at the Chrism Mass of Holy Thursday, together with the oil of catechumens.
