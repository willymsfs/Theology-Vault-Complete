# Synoptic Ecclesiologies

| Gospel | Core Image | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Mark | Community of disciples | Defined by service (Mk 10:42-45); slow to understand; the cross (8:34) |
| Matthew | Community fulfilling the Law | New Torah (Sermon on the Mount); mixed body (Mt 13); mission (28:19-20); only Gospel using ekklesia (16:18; 18:17) |
| Luke-Acts | The little flock | Universality (Jew and Gentile); "Gospel of the Holy Spirit"; structured (Acts 6, 15) and charismatic (Acts 10) |
