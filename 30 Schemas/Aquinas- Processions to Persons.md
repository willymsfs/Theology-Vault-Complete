# Aquinas: Processions to Persons

| Step | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Processions | Word by intellect (generation) — Love by will (spiration) |
| 2. Relations | Paternity/Filiation (from generation) — Active/Passive Spiration (from spiration) |
| 3. Persons | Each person = relatio subsistens, a subsisting relation identical with the simple divine essence |

Only three persons: active spiration, shared identically by Father and Son, generates no opposition between them, so no fourth person.
