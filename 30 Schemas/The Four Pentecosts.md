# The Four Pentecosts

Tongues mark four new beginnings — the Spirit falling on each group as the gospel crosses the next frontier. The repetition is Luke's argument: each group enters on identical terms.

| # | On whom | Passage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Jews at Jerusalem | Acts 2 |
| 2 | The Samaritans | Acts 8 |
| 3 | The Gentiles (Cornelius' household) | Acts 10 |
| 4 | The disciples of John at Ephesus | Acts 19 |
