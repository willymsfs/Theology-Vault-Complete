# Trent Session XXIV (1563)

| Canon | Teaching |
|---|---|
| 1 | Matrimony is truly and properly one of the seven sacraments, instituted by Christ |
| 2 | Polygamy is unlawful |
| 4 | The Church may establish impediments and grant dispensations |
| 5 | The bond is not dissolved by heresy, difficulty of cohabitation, or wilful desertion |
| 7 | The Church does not err in teaching that the bond is not dissolved on account of adultery |
| 10 | The state of virginity or celibacy is better and more blessed than marriage |

Canon 7's wording — that the Church does not err — was chosen deliberately so as not to condemn the Greek practice of oikonomia. The same session's Tametsi decree made canonical form a condition of validity.
