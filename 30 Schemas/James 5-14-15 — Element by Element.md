# James 5:14-15 — Element by Element

| Element | Greek | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Is any sick | asthenei | Weak, without strength — general debility, not necessarily terminal |
| Let him call for | proskalesasthō | The sick person takes the initiative |
| The presbyters of the church | presbyterous tēs ekklēsias | Plural, and an office — the point Trent defends in canon 4 |
| Pray over him | proseuxasthōsan ep' auton | The preposition suggests hands extended over the sick |
| Anointing with oil | aleipsantes elaiō | aleiphō (ordinary/medicinal), not chriō (ritual/messianic) |
| Will save | sōsei | Bears both heal and save |
| Will raise him up | egerei | Both raise from the sickbed and the resurrection verb |
| If he has committed sins | kan hamartias ē pepoiēkōs | Conditional — sin is not assumed to be the cause |

The vocabulary deliberately holds physical healing and spiritual salvation together; the history of the sacrament is largely the history of that balance tipping.
