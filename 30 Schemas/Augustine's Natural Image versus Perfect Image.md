# Augustine's Natural Image versus Perfect Image

|  | Object | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Natural image | Mind remembering/understanding/loving itself | Structurally unlosable, present in every mind as such |
| Perfect image | Mind remembering/understanding/loving God | Achieved only through grace; without it the mind remains "foolish" |
