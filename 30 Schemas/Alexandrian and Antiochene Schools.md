# Alexandrian and Antiochene Schools

The two great patristic schools, a Christological polarity that drove the councils.

| School | Emphasis | Method | Risk | Figure |
|--------|----------|--------|------|--------|
| **Alexandrian** | Christ's **divinity** | allegorical | downplay humanity | Cyril of Alexandria |
| **Antiochene** | Christ's **humanity** | literal/historical | loosen unity with divinity | Theodore of Mopsuestia |

Cappadocians (Alexandrian orbit): Gregory Nazianzen, Basil of Caesarea, Gregory of Nyssa.
