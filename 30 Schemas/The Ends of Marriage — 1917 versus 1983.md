# The Ends of Marriage — 1917 versus 1983

|  | 1917 CIC c. 1013 §1 | 1983 CIC c. 1055 §1 |
|---|---|---|
| Primary end | Procreation and education of offspring | — (no hierarchy asserted) |
| Secondary ends | Mutual help; remedy for concupiscence | — (no hierarchy asserted) |
| Formulation | Ranked, in two clauses | "Ordered to the good of the spouses and the procreation and education of offspring" — one sentence, bonum coniugum first |

The change registers Gaudium et Spes 50's deliberate refusal to repeat the hierarchy; amendments seeking to restore it were rejected at the Council.
