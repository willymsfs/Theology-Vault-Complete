# The Three Dissolutions of a Valid Bond

| Type | Canon | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Ratum non consummatum | c. 1142 | A valid but unconsummated marriage dissolved by the Roman Pontiff for a just cause |
| Pauline privilege | cc. 1143-1147 | Marriage of two unbaptised persons dissolved when one is baptised and the other departs (1 Cor 7:12-15) |
| Petrine privilege | Papal act | Marriage of a baptised with an unbaptised person dissolved in favour of the faith |

None of the three dissolves a consummated sacramental marriage, which no power on earth can dissolve (c. 1141).
