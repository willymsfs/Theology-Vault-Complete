# Old Testament Theologies

The OT holds a plurality of theological streams, reflecting Israel's changing history (agrarian → monarchy → vassal of Assyria, Greece, Rome):

- **Yahwist** · **Elohist** · **Priestly** · **Deuteronomic** · **Wisdom** theologies
