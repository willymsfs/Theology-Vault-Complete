# Contrition versus Attrition

|  | Contrition | Attrition |
|---|---|---|
| Motive | Love of God offended | Fear of hell, ugliness of sin, loss of heaven |
| Chief proponents | Abelard and the contritionists | Duns Scotus and the attritionists |
| Sufficient alone? | Yes, with the desire for the sacrament | No — but sufficient when joined to sacramental absolution |
| Trent's settlement | Praised as perfect | A true gift of God; disposes to grace in the sacrament |

The attritionist settlement is what makes the sacrament pastorally available to ordinary penitents.
