# Nine Paradigm Shifts of Vatican II

| # | Pre-Conciliar Category | Post-Conciliar Category |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Institutional Church | Mystery dimension (LG 1-8) |
| 2 | Hierarchy (pyramid, passive flock) | People of God (LG 9-17) |
| 3 | Papal Monarchy | Episcopal Collegiality (LG 18-24) |
| 4 | Universal/Roman Church over local jurisdictions | Local Church / World Church |
| 5 | Roman Catholicism (exclusive identification) | Christian Church (subsistit in, Unitatis Redintegratio) |
| 6 | Christianity as the one true religion | Many religions (Nostra Aetate) |
| 7 | Church-centred / teaching Church (ecclesia docens) | Listening / learning Church (ecclesia discens) |
| 8 | Static, "perfect society" | Dynamic, evolving pilgrim Church |
| 9 | Church-centred mission | Kingdom-centred mission |
