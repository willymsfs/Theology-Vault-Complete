# Three Conciliar Arguments for Episcopal Collegiality

| Argument | Basis |
|---|---|
| Christ's collegial commissioning | Authority given to Peter and the Twelve together as a stable body |
| Sacramentality of episcopal consecration | LG 21: bishops receive the fullness of the sacrament of Order directly, not by papal delegation |
| Communion of local churches | The universal Church as communio ecclesiarum; each bishop the visible centre of unity of his local church |
