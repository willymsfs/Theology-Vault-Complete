# Periods of Theological Development

| Period | Rough dates | Marker |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| Biblical theologies | OT era → 1st c. AD | plurality within Scripture |
| Patristic (Fathers) | Apostolic → mid-8th c. | building a vocabulary |
| Scholasticism | 800–1500 | university; faith + reason |
| Post-Scholastic | 16th–19th c. | Reformation, Trent, Neo-Scholasticism |
| Modern / Contextual | 20th–21st c. | Vatican II; contextual theologies |

## Close of the Patristic era (dates)
John Damascene †749 (East); Gregory the Great †604, Isidore of Seville †639 (West).
