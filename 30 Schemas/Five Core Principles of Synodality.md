# Five Core Principles of Synodality

1. **Sensus fidei fidelium** (LG 12) — see [[Sensus Fidei as Locus Theologicus]]. Consultation is a
theological necessity, not a democratic concession.
2. **The People of God and baptismal dignity** — every Christian shares the *tria munera* (Priest,
Prophet, King) through baptism, establishing a fundamental equality of dignity preceding any
differentiation of ministry — the primary theological antidote to clericalism.
3. **Communion, Participation, Mission** — the triad governing the 2021-2024 Synod. Communion is the
vertical relationship with God flowing horizontally into communion among believers; Participation is
its structural manifestation; Mission is the outward, evangelising drive that keeps synodality from
becoming self-referential.
4. **Omnes, Aliqui, Unus** — see [[Omnes Aliqui Unus]]. All consulted, some discern, one decides.
5. **Synodality, Collegiality, Primacy as concentric, not competing** — synodality is the whole
Church's attribute; collegiality (*cum et sub Petro*) is a restricted manifestation of it in the
College of Bishops; primacy is re-dimensioned as a listening office within synodality, not compromised
by it.
