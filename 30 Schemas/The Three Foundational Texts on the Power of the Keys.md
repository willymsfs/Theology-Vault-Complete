# The Three Foundational Texts on the Power of the Keys

| Text | Recipient | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Matthew 16:19 | Peter | The keys of the kingdom; binding and loosing |
| Matthew 18:18 | The disciples / community | Binding and loosing, set in the context of fraternal correction |
| John 20:22-23 | The apostles at Easter | Breathing of the Spirit; forgiving and retaining sins |

"Retaining" is the crucial half for later doctrine: discretionary authority presupposes knowledge of what is to be forgiven.
