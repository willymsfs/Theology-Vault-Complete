# The Two Medieval Controversies

| Controversy | Protagonists | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| First (9th c.) | Paschasius Radbertus vs Ratramnus of Corbie | Unresolved; Ratramnus republished by Reformers in the 16th c. |
| Second (11th c.) | Berengar of Tours vs Lanfranc of Bec | Berengar's oaths of 1059 (crude) and 1079 (substantialiter converti) |

Both controversies ask the same question: how can the presence be real without being crudely physical?
