# The Development of Clerical Celibacy

| Date | Council or code | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| c. 306 | Elvira, c. 33 | Married clergy to abstain from their wives — continence, not celibacy |
| 325 | Nicaea | Reportedly declined universal continence (the Paphnutius tradition) |
| 692 | Trullo (Quinisext) | Eastern settlement: married men may be ordained deacon and presbyter; bishops celibate |
| 1139 | Lateran II | Western settlement: marriages of clerics in major orders declared invalid |
| 1563 | Trent, Session XXIV | Celibacy reaffirmed against Reformation objection |
| 1983 | CIC c. 277 | Clerics obliged to perfect and perpetual continence |

Celibacy is Latin discipline, not doctrine: not required for validity, not observed in the Eastern Catholic Churches, and already excepted for permanent deacons and Ordinariate clergy.
