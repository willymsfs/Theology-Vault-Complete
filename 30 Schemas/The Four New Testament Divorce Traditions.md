# The Four New Testament Divorce Traditions

| Text | Form | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Mark 10:2-12 | Absolute; includes the wife divorcing | Appeals behind Moses to creation; sklerokardia |
| Matthew 19:3-9 (cf. 5:31-32) | With the porneia exception | The great exegetical crux |
| Luke 16:18 | Absolute | No exception clause |
| 1 Corinthians 7:10-11 | Absolute, attributed to the Lord | Paul distinguishes this from his own ruling at 7:12 |

The most widely attested ethical teaching of Jesus in the New Testament, across four independent strands.
