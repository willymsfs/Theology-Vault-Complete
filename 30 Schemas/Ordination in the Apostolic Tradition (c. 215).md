# Ordination in the Apostolic Tradition (c. 215)

| Order | Who imposes hands | Prayer asks for |
|---|---|---|
| Bishop | Other bishops, with the assent of all | The princely Spirit; high-priestly ministry; power to forgive sins |
| Presbyter | The bishop, with the presbyters also laying on hands | The Spirit of grace and counsel; to share in the presbyterate |
| Deacon | The bishop alone | The Spirit of grace and zeal — "ordained not to the priesthood but to the service of the bishop" |

The statement about the deacon is re-inscribed canonically by Omnium in Mentem (2009) and echoed at LG 29.
