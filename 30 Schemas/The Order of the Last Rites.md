# The Order of the Last Rites

| Order of celebration | Rite | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Penance or absolution | Reconciliation first |
| 2 | Anointing of the Sick | Strengthening of the sick person |
| 3 | Viaticum | The Eucharist as food for the journey |

Order of importance differs from order of celebration: for the dying, Viaticum has priority over anointing (c. 921). "Last rites" names a sequence, not a single sacrament.
