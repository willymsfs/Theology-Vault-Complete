# Reformation Positions on the Real Presence

| Position | Figure | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Transubstantiation | Catholic teaching | Substance converted; accidents remain |
| Sacramental union | Luther | "In, with, and under"; bread remains bread; supported by ubiquity |
| Memorialism | Zwingli | "Est" means significat; Christ's body locally in heaven |
| Spiritual real presence | Calvin | True feeding by the Spirit; the communicant is raised to Christ (sursum corda) |
| Receptionism / virtualism | Cranmer | Christ received by the faithful in the act of reception; elements unchanged |

Marburg (1529): agreement on 14 of 15 articles, irreparable split on the fifteenth.
