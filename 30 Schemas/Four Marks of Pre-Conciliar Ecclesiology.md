# Four Marks of Pre-Conciliar Ecclesiology

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| Clericalism | Clergy alone as active agents of Church life; laity reduced to passive recipients |
| Juridicism | Church life read primarily through canon-law categories of rights, offices, jurisdiction |
| Triumphalism | The Church pictured as a well-drilled army arrayed against a hostile, evil secular world |
| Patriarchalism | A wholly male-governed structure in which women have no institutional voice |

Collective gains: strong doctrinal/structural endorsement; powerful corporate identity continuous with patristic tradition. Collective losses: weak grounding in Scripture and earliest communal practice; power tends to control rather than serve, stifling charismatic diversity under rigid, legalistic obedience.
