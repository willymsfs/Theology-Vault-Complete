# Patristic Witnesses to the Eucharist

| Father | Date | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Didache 9-10, 14 | c. 100 | Oldest Eucharistic prayers; calls it thysia (sacrifice), cites Mal 1:11 |
| Ignatius of Antioch | c. 107 | "Medicine of immortality"; realism as corollary of anti-Docetism |
| Justin Martyr | c. 155 | First Apology 65-67: earliest full description of Sunday Eucharist |
| Irenaeus | c. 180 | Oblation of the new covenant; argument from Eucharist to resurrection of the body |
| Cyprian | c. 253 | Ep. 63: the mixed chalice; priest offering in the place of Christ |
| Cyril of Jerusalem | c. 350 | Mystagogical Catecheses 4-5: epiclesis, manner of reception |
| Ambrose | c. 390 | De Sacramentis IV: conversion of the elements by the word of Christ |
| Augustine | c. 400 | Sign/reality distinction; totus Christus; "be what you see" |
