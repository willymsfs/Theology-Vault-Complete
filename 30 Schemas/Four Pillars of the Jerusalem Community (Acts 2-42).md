# Four Pillars of the Jerusalem Community (Acts 2-42)

"And they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers" (Acts 2:42). The verse is the earliest description of Christian common life and reads as a parish audit.

1. **The apostles' teaching** — doctrine handed on, not privately derived → [[Magisterium]]
2. **Fellowship** (*koinonia*) — goods held in common, need abolished
3. **The breaking of bread** — the Eucharist
4. **The prayers** — the fixed hours of Jewish prayer, continued
