# Three Interpretations of Collegiality

| Position | Claim |
|---|---|
| Conservative | The episcopal college holds supreme power only by human/positive right, granted by the Pope |
| Extreme liberal | The college is the sole ultimate subject of supreme power; the Pope is bound in conscience to consult it |
| Moderate liberal (Paul VI's own; locked in by the Nota Praevia) | Both the Pope personally and the college in union with the Pope hold supreme power |
