# MTh Critical Debates on Synodality

Four unresolved tensions, each requiring genuine engagement rather than resolution by assertion.

1. **The dual-ecclesiology trap** (Mulackal) — Vatican II holds a monarchical-hierarchical ecclesiology
and a communio-synodal ecclesiology side by side, unreconciled; both sides can quote the Council for
opposed visions, producing systemic paralysis.
2. **The majoritarian danger** (Thonippara) — *vox populi* is not automatically *vox Dei*; see
[[Vox Populi Vox Dei - The Majoritarian Risk]].
3. **Niti and nyaya** (Ponniah) — see [[Niti and Nyaya]]. Formal declaration and lived justice answer
different questions.
4. **The economics of formation** (Susairaj) — roughly 80% of diocesan resources form the 5% who are
priests and religious, while the 95% laity receive almost none; clericalism is a budget line before it
is an attitude.
