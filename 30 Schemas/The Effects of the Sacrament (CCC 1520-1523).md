# The Effects of the Sacrament (CCC 1520-1523)

| # | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | A particular gift of the Holy Spirit — strength, peace, courage |
| 2 | Union with the passion of Christ |
| 3 | An ecclesial grace — the sick contribute to the sanctification of the Church |
| 4 | Forgiveness of sins, if the person could not obtain it through penance |
| 5 | Restoration of health, if conducive to the salvation of the soul |
| 6 | Preparation for the final journey |

Note that healing appears fifth and conditionally — but it genuinely appears, which medieval practice had all but forgotten.
