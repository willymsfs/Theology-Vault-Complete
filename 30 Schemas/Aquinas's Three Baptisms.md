# Aquinas's Three Baptisms

| Latin | English | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Baptismus fluminis | Of water | The sacrament properly so called |
| Baptismus sanguinis | Of blood | Martyrdom for Christ before sacramental baptism |
| Baptismus flaminis | Of desire (in voto) | Explicit or implicit longing for baptism |

ST III q.66 a.11. The doctrine that prevents Catholic sacramental theology from collapsing into exclusivism.
