# Types of Papal Magisterial Documents

| Type | Character |
|---|---|
| Apostolic Constitution | Most solemn form; gravest matters, e.g. promulgating a council's documents |
| Encyclical | Circular letter to the whole Church, e.g. Humanae Vitae, Evangelii Gaudium |
| Apostolic Exhortation | Post-synodal reflection, pastoral in character |
| Apostolic Letter | Less solemn than a constitution or encyclical |
| Motu Proprio | Issued on the pope's own initiative, often administrative/legislative |
