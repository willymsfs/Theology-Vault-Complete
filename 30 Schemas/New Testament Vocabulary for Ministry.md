# New Testament Vocabulary for Ministry

| Term | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|
| hiereus / sacerdos | Priest | Used of Jewish and pagan priests, of Christ, and of all the baptised — never of a Christian minister |
| episkopos | Overseer | A secular administrative term, not cultic |
| presbyteros | Elder | From synagogue eldership; interchangeable with episkopos (Acts 20:17, 28) |
| diakonos | Servant, agent | Applied to Paul, to Christ (Rom 15:8), and to Phoebe (Rom 16:1) |

The sacerdotal vocabulary for ministers is a post-biblical development, entering decisively with Cyprian in the third century.
