# The Medieval Seven Orders

| Minor orders | Major (sacred) orders |
|---|---|
| Porter | Subdeacon |
| Lector | Deacon |
| Exorcist | Priest |
| Acolyte | — (the episcopate is absent) |

The episcopate was not counted among the orders, since orders was defined by its relation to the Eucharist, which a priest can already confect. Minor orders and the subdiaconate were suppressed by Ministeria Quaedam (1972).
