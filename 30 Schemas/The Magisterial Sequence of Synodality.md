# The Magisterial Sequence of Synodality

1. **Vatican II** (*Lumen Gentium*, *Gaudium et Spes*, 1962-65) — the "Copernican revolution" from a
pyramidal *societas perfecta* to a communion ecclesiology of the People of God (LG ch. 2); restores the
common priesthood of all baptised (LG 10); grounds the Church in Trinitarian communion (LG 4);
validates the *sensus fidei* (LG 12).
2. **Pope Francis, 50th Anniversary Address** (2015) — "The path of synodality is the path which God
expects from the Church of the third millennium"; the "inverted pyramid" of ecclesial authority.
3. **International Theological Commission** (2018) — *Synodality in the Life and Mission of the
Church*: the most systematic treatment; three levels — fundamental style, structures/participatory
organs, synodal events/assemblies.
4. **Episcopalis Communio** (2018) — reforms the Synod of Bishops from a static gathering into a
continuous process in three phases: consultation of all the faithful, discernment of the bishops,
implementation under the Pope.
5. **The 2021-2024 Synod on Synodality** — "For a Synodal Church: Communion, Participation, Mission,"
the largest consultation of the People of God in history.
