# Trent Session XXIII (1563)

| Point | Teaching |
|---|---|
| Sacrifice and priesthood | A visible sacrifice requires a visible and external priesthood |
| Institution | At the Last Supper, in "Do this in remembrance of me" |
| Sacramentality | A true sacrament conferring grace by imposition of hands and words (can. 3) |
| Character | Indelible; one ordained cannot become a layman again (can. 4) |
| Hierarchy | Instituted by divine ordination: bishops, priests, ministers (can. 6) |
| Bishops | Superior to priests; power to confirm and ordain (can. 7) |

Left open: whether the episcopate is a distinct sacramental order, and whether episcopal jurisdiction comes directly from God or through the pope.
