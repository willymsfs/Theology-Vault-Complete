# Three Legs of Acts (the Acts 1-8 Itinerary)

Acts 1:8 is the table of contents of the book. Luke follows it exactly.

| Leg | Territory | Chapters | Guide | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I — Base Camp Jerusalem** | Jerusalem | 1:1–8:3 | Peter | c. 2 years |
| **II — Crossing Borders** | Judea & Samaria | 8:4–12:25 | Peter / Philip | c. 10 years |
| **III — To the Ends of the Earth** | the nations, ending at Rome | 13:1–28:31 | Paul | c. 17 years |

The three legs are also the course's teaching structure — see [[Acts MOC]].
