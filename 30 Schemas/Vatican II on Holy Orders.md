# Vatican II on Holy Orders

| Reference | Teaching |
|---|---|
| LG 21 | Episcopal consecration confers the fullness of the sacrament, with the tria munera |
| LG 10 | Common and ministerial priesthood differ essentia et non gradu tantum, yet are ordered one to another |
| LG 28 | Presbyters as cooperators of the episcopal order, forming one presbyterium with the bishop |
| LG 29 | The diaconate: non ad sacerdotium sed ad ministerium; permanent diaconate restored |
| Presbyterorum Ordinis | From an isolated cultic model to a relational servant-leader model |
| Optatam Totius | Holistic seminary formation integrating philosophy, theology, Scripture, and pastoral experience |

LG 21 settles the question the scholastics and Trent left open.
