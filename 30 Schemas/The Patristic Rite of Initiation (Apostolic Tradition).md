# The Patristic Rite of Initiation (Apostolic Tradition)

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Scrutiny of life | Examination of motives and occupation; certain trades disqualify |
| 2. Catechumenate | Three years of formation; dismissal before the Eucharist |
| 3. Election | Entry into the final Lenten stage as competentes / electi |
| 4. Lenten preparation | Exorcisms, scrutinies, traditio and redditio symboli |
| 5. The Vigil | Renunciation facing West, profession facing East, stripping, triple immersion with triple interrogation |
| 6. Presbyteral anointing | Anointing and clothing in a white robe |
| 7. Episcopal hand-laying | Second anointing on the forehead — the future Confirmation |
| 8. First Eucharist | Including a cup of milk and honey |

Attributed to Hippolytus, c. 215; authorship and dating are genuinely contested. The 1972 RCIA is an explicit attempt to recover this structure.
