# Rahner, Moltmann, Zizioulas, Volf, Emery — A Comparative Grid

| Thinker | Starting Point | Chief Critic(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Rahner | Grundaxiom — economic Trinity is the immanent Trinity | Jowers (simplicity, anointing sequence) |
| Moltmann | Social Trinity — unity via perichoresis of co-equal persons | McDougall (tritheism, covert monarchy), Kilby (projection) |
| Zizioulas | Monarchy of the Father — personhood as ekstasis/communion | Volf (asymmetry, coerced subordination) |
| Volf | Symmetrical relations of love vs. asymmetrical relations of origin | Implicit tension with Nazianzen/Zizioulas's monarchy logic |
| Emery | Thomistic relatio subsistens and undivided operations ad extra | Positions itself against Rahner and social trinitarianism generally |
