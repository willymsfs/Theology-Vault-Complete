# Structure of the Classical Anaphora

| Element | Content |
|---|---|
| Opening dialogue | Sursum corda and thanksgiving |
| Preface and Sanctus | Thanksgiving for creation and redemption |
| Consecratory epiclesis (Roman, 1969) | Invocation of the Spirit upon the gifts |
| Institution narrative | The words of Christ at the Supper |
| Anamnesis | Memorial of passion, resurrection, ascension; the offering |
| Communion epiclesis | Invocation of the Spirit upon the assembly for unity |
| Intercessions | For the Church, living and dead |
| Doxology and Amen | Through him, with him, in him; the people's assent |

The Apostolic Tradition's anaphora (c. 215) is the direct model for Eucharistic Prayer II of the 1969 Missal.
