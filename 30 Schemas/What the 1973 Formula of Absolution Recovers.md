# What the 1973 Formula of Absolution Recovers

| Phrase | Doctrine expressed |
|---|---|
| God, the Father of mercies | The Father's initiative |
| through the death and resurrection of his Son | The paschal mystery as the source |
| has reconciled the world to himself | The objective reconciliation already accomplished |
| and sent the Holy Spirit among us for the forgiveness of sins | The pneumatological mission (Jn 20:22) |
| through the ministry of the Church | The ecclesial dimension (LG 11) |
| and I absolve you from your sins | The indicative absolution, now set within all the above |

The medieval Ego te absolvo survives as the conclusion rather than the whole.
