# Additional Resources of Theology

Beyond the three primary sources — because God is active in all history (Ch 2, D).

1. **People & their experience** — "the meeting-point between God's self-gift and the human response."
2. **Cultural resources** — [[Gaudium et Spes]] 58; India's [[Cosmotheandric Vision]].
3. **Diverse religious traditions** — [[Nostra Aetate]] 2, "a ray of that truth which enlightens all."
4. **Peoples' movements & the marginalized** — Dalit, tribal, women's, peasant movements.
5. **The cry of the poor** — "epistemological privilege of the poor."
