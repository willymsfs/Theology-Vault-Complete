# The Old Testament Foundations

| Text | Contribution |
|---|---|
| Gen 1:27-28 | Male and female in the image of God; blessing of fruitfulness |
| Gen 2:18-24 | lo tov (not good to be alone); 'ezer kenegdo; leaving and cleaving; one flesh |
| Hos 1-3 | Marriage as the figure of God's covenant fidelity to an unfaithful Israel |
| Mal 2:14-16 | The wife as companion and wife by covenant (berith); God hates divorce |
| Deut 24:1-4 | Divorce presupposed on the ground of 'erwat dabar — the crux dividing Shammai and Hillel |
| Tob 8:7 | Marriage as prayerful and companionate — "not because of lust, but with sincerity" |
