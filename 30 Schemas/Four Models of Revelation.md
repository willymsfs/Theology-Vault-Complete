# Four Models of Revelation

Revelation is "a very complex reality." Four models — different emphases, all true together (Ch 2, A).

| # | Model | Emphasis | Exact phrase |
|---|-------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | Doctrine | truths to believe | "cognitive contents... as propositions or deposit of truth" |
| 2 | Presence | personal encounter | "the presence of the living and life-giving God" |
| 3 | Experience | lived, universal | "existential experience... universal and possible for all" |
| 4 | History | public events | "universal and public historical event" |

**Why four?** Each corrects the others: doctrine alone is dry, experience alone vague, history alone bare fact.
