# FCRA Penalties

| Violation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Unapproved purpose or State | 30% of amount, or Rs 1,00,000 |
| Speculative investment | 30% invested + 10% of returns, or Rs 1,00,000 |
| Accepting FC without registration | 30% of FC, or Rs 1,00,000 |
| Transfer or sub-granting | 10% of amount, or Rs 1,00,000 - compoundable once only |
| Admin expenses above 20% | 5% of the excess, or Rs 1,00,000 |
| FC into a non-designated account | 5%, or Rs 1,00,000 |
| Local funds into an FC account | 2%, or Rs 1,00,000 |
| Failure to report or maintain accounts | 5% of FC for the period, or Rs 1,00,000 |
| Utilisation account not intimated in 45 days | Rs 10,000 per account |
| Quarterly website disclosure failure | Rs 10,000 each |

Maximum penalty in any instance is capped at the value of the foreign contribution received.
