# Consent versus Consummation

| School | Position | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Gratian and the Bolognese canonists | Consent initiates; consummation completes the marriage | Not adopted as such |
| Peter Lombard and the Paris theologians | Consensus facit nuptias — consent alone makes the marriage | Adopted as to validity |
| Alexander III's synthesis | Consent creates a valid marriage (ratum); consummation makes it absolutely indissoluble | Catholic law to this day |

Hence a ratum non consummatum marriage may be dissolved by the Roman Pontiff (CIC c. 1142), while a consummated sacramental marriage may not (c. 1141).
