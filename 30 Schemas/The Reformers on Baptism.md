# The Reformers on Baptism

| Reformer | Position | Infant baptism? |
|---|---|---|
| Luther | God's promise attached to a sign; faith receives, does not create | Yes — fides infantium and the faith of the Church |
| Zwingli | Covenant sign and pledge of allegiance; does not confer grace | Yes — the counterpart of circumcision |
| Calvin | Sign and seal of engrafting into Christ; instrumental but not automatic | Yes — covenant continuity (Inst. IV.16) |
| Anabaptists | Only baptism following personal profession of faith is baptism | No — Schleitheim Confession (1527), Art. 1 |
