# Council of Florence — Key Dates

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1438 | Council opens in Ferrara; Mark of Ephesus presses the legality question |
| 1439 | Council transfers to Florence; doctrinal debate begins (Montenegro vs. Mark of Ephesus) |
| 5 July 1439 | Laetentur Caeli signed — Mark of Ephesus the sole refusal |
| April 1443 | Three Eastern Patriarchs condemn the union at Jerusalem |
| 1484 | Council of Constantinople formally annuls the Union of Florence |
