# The Four Reformation Objections and Trent's Replies

| Objection | Trent Session XIV |
|---|---|
| Enumeration is impossible and tortures consciences | Required only for mortal sins of which one is conscious after diligent examination |
| Satisfaction impugns Christ's satisfaction | Real and salutary, but derives all its power from Christ's merit |
| Attrition is servile and worthless | A true gift of God that disposes to grace within the sacrament |
| Priestly absolution usurps God's prerogative | Absolution is a judicial act exercised by Christ's authority through his minister |
