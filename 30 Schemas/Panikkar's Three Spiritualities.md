# Panikkar's Three Spiritualities

| Person | Spirituality | Religious Exemplar |
|---|---|---|
| Father | Apophaticism | Buddhism (nirvāna) |
| Son | Personalism | Judaism, Islam, Christianity, theistic Hinduism |
| Holy Spirit | Immanence | Advaita Hinduism, Śakti/Tantric traditions |

Critiqued by Gavin D'Costa as an inversion of the vestigia Trinitatis tradition.
