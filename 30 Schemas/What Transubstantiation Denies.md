# What Transubstantiation Denies

| Rejected account | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Annihilation of the bread | There would be substitution, not conversion |
| Consubstantiation | "This is my body" would be false; the bread would remain bread |
| Impanation | Confuses hypostatic union with sacramental presence |
| Local motion from heaven | Christ is present per modum substantiae, not locally |

Aquinas, ST III qq. 75-77. The positive claim is conversion of the whole substance with the species remaining.
