# Five Apologiai of Paul

Acts 22–26 gives five defences before audiences of rising rank. Paul keeps the gospel and changes the entry point each time — the course's model for contextual apologetics.

| # | Before | Passage | Entry point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Jerusalem crowd | 22:1-21 | Jewish credentials, told in Hebrew |
| 2 | The Sanhedrin | 23:1-10 | The resurrection as the real issue |
| 3 | Felix | 24:10-21 | "The Way" as continuous with the faith of the fathers |
| 4 | Festus | 25:1-12 | Innocence, then the citizen's appeal to Caesar |
| 5 | Agrippa | 26:1-29 | The hope of Israel's restoration |
