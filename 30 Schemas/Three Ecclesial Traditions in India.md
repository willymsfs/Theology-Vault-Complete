# Three Ecclesial Traditions in India

The Catholic Church in India = a communion of three Individual Churches:

| Church | Key facts |
|--------|-----------|
| **Syro-Malabar** | most ancient; always in communion with Rome; ~5 million, mainly Kerala; Major Archiepiscopal |
| **Syro-Malankara** | formed 1930 (Mar Ivanios, Mar Theophilus reunited from the Jacobite Church) |
| **Latin** | Portuguese missions; Franciscans c. 1518; Francis Xavier, Goa 1542 |

## St Thomas Christians
Tradition: St Thomas, South India, 52 AD. "Hindu in culture, Christian in faith and Syrian in worship." Persian (4th c.) connection → Syrian liturgy; 16th-c. Portuguese Latinization caused divisions.

## Protestant unions
CSI (1947); CNI (1970).
