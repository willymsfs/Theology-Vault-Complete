# Guilt, Punishment, and Indulgences

| Term | Meaning | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Culpa (guilt) | The offence against God | Absolution in the sacrament |
| Poena temporalis | Residual disorder and debt remaining after guilt is forgiven | Satisfaction in this life, or purgatory |
| Indulgence | Remission of temporal punishment, not of sin | Granted from the treasury of merits by the power of the keys |

An indulgence presupposes that the sin has already been forgiven. Popular preaching routinely obscured this, which is a large part of why the system detonated.
