# Methods of Theologizing in India

| Method | Core |
|--------|------|
| [[Inculturation]] | Gospel takes flesh in the culture; not translation |
| [[Dialogue of Life]] | from debating dogmas to solving problems together |
| [[Ashram Movement]] | integrate *nishkama karma* + *jnana* + *bhakti* |

## Two poles held together
**Inculturation** (rooting in culture/philosophy) + **Liberation** (responding to poverty, caste, injustice).

## Flashcard
Name the three methods of theologizing in India.::Inculturation, Dialogue of Life, the Ashram Movement
