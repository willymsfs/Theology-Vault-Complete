# Four Connotations of Ekklesia

| Connotation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Freedom | Called out of bondage, on the Exodus pattern |
| Unity | One assembly, not a scattering of factions |
| Self-rule | A body capable of its own internal governance and voice |
| Participation | Each member has a place, not merely a passive presence |

From EK (out of) + KALEO (to call), by way of qahal Yahweh.
