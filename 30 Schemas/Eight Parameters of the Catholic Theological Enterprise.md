# Eight Parameters of the Catholic Theological Enterprise

The criteria that authenticate a theology as Roman Catholic:

1. **Knowability of God** — rejects agnosticism
2. **Faith and Reason** — rejects Fideism and Rationalism
3. **Universality** — Jesus Christ, "the one and only Good News" for all
4. **Communitarian Context** — done in the Church; *sensus fidelium*
5. **Catholicity in Time** — continuity without relativism; growth in understanding
6. **The Sacramental Principle** — grace "mediated by created realities," supremely the Christ event
7. **Scripture and Tradition** — Scripture situated within Tradition
8. **The Magisterium** — receive and weigh its statements by their qualitative differences
