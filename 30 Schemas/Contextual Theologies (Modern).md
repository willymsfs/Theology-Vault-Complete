# Contextual Theologies (Modern)

After Vatican II's "World Church" turn, theology focuses on specific social realities.

| Theology | Origin | Defining focus |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| Theology of Secularisation | Bonhoeffer | "to Christianise the world is to secularise it" |
| Black Theology | USA | the Word for the Black community |
| Feminist / Ecofeminism | Western | gender + "oppression of women and the rest of nature" |
| Dalit Theology | India | rejects "false consciousness of pollution" |
| Minjung Theology | Korea | the "powerless people"; repressed *han*; Jesus as victim |
