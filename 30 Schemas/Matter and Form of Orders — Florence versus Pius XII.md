# Matter and Form of Orders — Florence versus Pius XII

|  | Florence (1439) | Sacramentum Ordinis (1947) |
|---|---|---|
| Matter | Traditio instrumentorum — handing over of chalice and paten | The imposition of hands alone |
| Form | The words accompanying the handing over | The words of the consecratory preface determining the effect |
| Status of the traditio | Held to be essential | Not required for validity |

A clean instance of the magisterium correcting an earlier magisterial text on historical evidence, in the direction of the more ancient and ecumenically shared practice.
