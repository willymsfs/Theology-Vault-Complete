# The Entailment Chain

| Step | Follows because |
|---|---|
| Abba experience | God encountered as unconditional love, at the baptism |
| All people are siblings | Sonship has as its "inseparable obverse" the experience of humankind as brothers and sisters |
| Discrimination becomes impossible | "Rules out all discrimination on any ground whatsoever" — caste, class, race, gender |
| Solidarity with the poor | Identification with those the discrimination excludes |
| Confrontation with the powerful | Those served by the arrangement resist its dismantling |
| The Cross | "The natural outcome of a life of solidarity with the poor and the outcast and of confrontation with the powerful who oppress them" |

Not six topics but one argument. Each step entails the next; this chain is his theology in compressed form.
