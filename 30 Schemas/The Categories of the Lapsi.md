# The Categories of the Lapsi

| Category | What they did |
|---|---|
| Sacrificati | Actually offered sacrifice — the gravest |
| Thurificati | Burned incense before the idols |
| Libellatici | Obtained a certificate falsely stating they had sacrificed, usually by bribery |
| Acta facientes | Had a false declaration entered in the public records |

Cyprian graded penance by category in De Lapsis (251), against both confessor laxity and Novatianist rigorism.
