# Six Schools of Indian Philosophy

"Nyaya, Vaiseshika, Sankya, Yoga, Mimamsa and Vedanta."

| School | Simple meaning |
|--------|----------------|
| Nyaya | logic — right reasoning as path to salvation |
| Vaiseshika | dualist — matter and soul distinct |
| Sankhya | dualist — *prakrti* (matter) + *purusha* (consciousness) |
| Yoga | discipline/meditation for union with the Absolute |
| Mimamsa | Vedic exegesis |
| [[Vedanta]] | culmination — teaching of the Upanishads |
