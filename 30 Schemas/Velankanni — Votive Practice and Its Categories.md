# Velankanni — Votive Practice and Its Categories

| Practice | Form | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Ex-voto offerings | Wax or metal limbs, organs, infants, houses, boats, tools | Offered ex voto suscepto; localised visualisation of grace |
| Nadai vows | Conditional covenant pledging an offering on healing | Non-fulfilment carries real moral weight |
| Tonsure and sea-bathing | Shaving the head; ritual purification | Embodied surrender of the self |
| Anga pradakshinam | Rolling or crawling along the sacred path | Prostrate penitential advance |
| Ratha yatra | Chariot procession of Marian and saintly images | Tolerated and incorporated; poses no threat to the institution |

Scholarship: Visvanathan (canonical vs domestic ritual), Narayanan (shakti/barakat; time-sensitive petition), Raj (organic interreligious dialogue), Bayly (the converters converted).
