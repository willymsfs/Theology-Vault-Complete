# Socio-Political Realities of India

The realities Indian theology must respond to:

1. **Poverty & inequality** — "Mixed Economy"; widening rich–poor gulf; globalization leaves the poor "unwanted and unemployed"
2. **Caste** — "constitutionally and legally abolished," yet "remains practically unchanged"; linked to class
3. **[[Hindutva]] communalism** — the "Hindu Rashtra" threat to minorities
4. **Women** — "unimaginable forms of violence and discrimination"; female foeticide, dowry deaths
5. **Dalits & Adivasis** — *panchamas*, segregation; tribals "uprooted... due to industry and deforestation"
