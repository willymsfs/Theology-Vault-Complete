# Seven Creative Polarities of Theologizing

A *polarity* = two opposite pulls held together in tension. Drop either side and theology goes wrong. (Ch 1, section C.)

| # | Polarity | Essence | Between the errors of |
|---|----------|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | Mystery ↔ Intelligibility | reason penetrates, mystery remains | Fideism / Rationalism |
| 2 | Identity ↔ Change | Christ-event fixed, forms reform | rigidity / rupture |
| 3 | Committed ↔ Critical | love the tradition AND question it | fideism / detachment |
| 4 | Community ↔ Individual | locus theologicus vs prophetic gift | collectivism / individualism |
| 5 | One ↔ Many | universal Christ-event, plural theologies | monism / relativism |
| 6 | Universal ↔ Contextual | "no theology is perennial" | abstraction / parochialism |
| 7 | Orthodoxy ↔ Heterodoxy | fidelity vs new insight | stagnation / betrayal |

## Claims grown from these rows
- [[Theology must hold mystery and intelligibility together]] (1)
- [[The community, not the individual, is the locus theologicus]] (4)
- [[All theologies are contextual; no theology is perennial]] (6)
- [[Heterodoxy may carry insights orthodoxy later needs]] (7)
