# Old Testament Types of the Eucharist

| Type | Text | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Melchizedek's offering | Gen 14:18; Ps 110:4 | Bread and wine offered by a priest-king; named in the Roman Canon |
| Passover | Exod 12 | Sacrificial lamb, saving blood, memorial meal |
| Manna | Exod 16; Ps 78 | Bread from heaven; the explicit foil of John 6 |
| Sinai covenant blood | Exod 24:8 | Quoted verbatim in the institution narrative |
| Todah sacrifice | Lev 7:11-15; Ps 116 | Unbloody thanksgiving offering with bread and cup |
| Showbread | Exod 25:30; Lev 24 | Lehem happanim, bread of the Presence |

Add Mal 1:11 (the pure offering among the nations) and Isa 25:6 (the eschatological banquet) as the two prophetic anticipations.
