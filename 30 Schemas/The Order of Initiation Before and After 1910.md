# The Order of Initiation Before and After 1910

|  | Sequence | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Patristic and Eastern | Baptism, Confirmation, Eucharist | Eucharist as the consummation of initiation (CCC 1322) |
| Modern West (post-Quam Singulari) | Baptism, Eucharist, Confirmation | First Communion lowered to age 7; Confirmation stayed tied to episcopal visitation |
| Restored Order dioceses | Baptism, Confirmation, Eucharist | Deliberate return to the ancient sequence, permitted by CIC c. 891 |
