# The Sixteen Documents of Vatican II

| Tier | Count | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutions | 4 | Lumen Gentium, Dei Verbum, Sacrosanctum Concilium, Gaudium et Spes |
| Decrees | 9 | Unitatis Redintegratio, Christus Dominus, Ad Gentes, Presbyterorum Ordinis... |
| Declarations | 3 | Dignitatis Humanae, Nostra Aetate, Gravissimum Educationis |

Descending order of solemn authority; the four Constitutions supply the interpretative key to the rest.
