# The Coherence of 'Person' — A Historical Shift

| Era | Definition | Implication for the Trinity |
|---|---|---|
| Patristic/Boethian | "An individual substance of a rational nature" — objective subsistence, no psychological content | Compatible with "three persons" and one divine will/intellect |
| Thomistic | Relatio subsistens — constituted wholly by relation of origin | Blocks independent agency or subjectivity among the persons (R. Williams) |
| Cartesian-Lockean/Modern | Autonomous center of self-consciousness, volition, agency | Applied to the Trinity, implies three wills/intellects — tritheism (Rahner's own warning) |
