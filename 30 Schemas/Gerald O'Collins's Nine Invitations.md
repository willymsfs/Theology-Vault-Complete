# Gerald O'Collins's Nine Invitations

| # | Invitation |
|---|---|
| 1 | Be liturgical |
| 2 | Be committed to the needy |
| 3 | Be biblical |
| 4 | Be priestly, prophetic, and kingly |
| 5 | Be Jesus-centred |
| 6 | Be in dialogue |
| 7 | Be reformers (ecclesia semper reformanda) |
| 8 | Be collegial |
| 9 | Be in touch with Mary and the saints |

A compact synthesis of the Council's entire spirituality, useful as a memorization/recall device.
