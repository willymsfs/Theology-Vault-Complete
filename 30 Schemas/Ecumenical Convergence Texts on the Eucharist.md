# Ecumenical Convergence Texts on the Eucharist

| Text | Date | Achievement and limit |
|---|---|---|
| ARCIC Windsor Statement + Elucidation | 1971, 1979 | "Substantial agreement" via anamnesis; limited by unresolved questions of ministry and orders |
| BEM / Lima, Eucharist section | 1982 | Five aspects: thanksgiving, anamnesis, epiclesis, communion, meal of the Kingdom; leaves the mode of presence open |
| Lutheran-Catholic dialogues | 1960s onward | Broad agreement on real presence; sacrifice and ordained ministry remain |
