# The Four Institution Narratives

| Tradition | Texts | Distinctive features |
|---|---|---|
| Markan / Matthean (Palestinian) | Mk 14:22-25; Mt 26:26-29 | "My blood of the covenant, poured out for many"; more Semitic; no command to repeat |
| Pauline / Lukan (Antiochene) | 1 Cor 11:23-26; Lk 22:14-20 | "The new covenant in my blood"; includes "Do this in remembrance of me" |

1 Cor 11:23-26 (c. AD 54) is the earliest written account, introduced with the technical rabbinic language of received and delivered tradition.
