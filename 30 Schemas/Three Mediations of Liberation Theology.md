# Three Mediations of Liberation Theology

Clodovis Boff's formal account of how the method actually operates.

1. **Socio-analytic mediation** — name the situation with the social sciences: who is poor, by what structures, in whose interest. Not illustration but analysis; this is where Marxist tools entered and where the standard criticism lands.
2. **Hermeneutic mediation** — read scripture and tradition *from* that situation. Segundo's **hermeneutic circle**: the situation raises a suspicion about the received reading, which sends you back to the text, which changes the situation, which raises a new suspicion.
3. **Practical mediation** — act, at the level of structures as well as persons, then re-enter the circle. Theology is a **second act**, following commitment.

**Pastoral form:** see–judge–act, in [[Base Ecclesial Communities]].
**Governing conviction:** the **preferential option for the poor** is first a statement about God's gratuitous love, not a strategy.
