# Eight Functional Specialties

Lonergan's division of theological work. Two phases, pivoting on [[Conversion]].

## Phase 1 — encountering the data (mediating)
| # | Specialty | Precept | Governing question |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research | Be attentive | Have we got an accurate text? |
| 2 | Interpretation | Be intelligent | What is the literary form? What did the author intend? |
| 3 | History | Be reasonable | What was moving forward — what really happened? |
| 4 | Dialectics | Be responsible | Where do I stand before these oppositions? |

## Pivot
**Conversion** — religious, moral, intellectual, psychic.

## Phase 2 — handing on the tradition (mediated)
| # | Specialty | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Foundations | Objectify one's conversion; choose framework and categories |
| 6 | Doctrines | Judgements of fact and value affirming the tradition's truths |
| 7 | Systematics | Coherent understanding of the mysteries, by analogy |
| 8 | Communications | Transpose into common-sense language for a culture |

The eighth feeds the first: what communications produces becomes the historical data of the next generation's research — the [[Hermeneutic Circle]] at the scale of a tradition.

**Standing criticism:** the split between Doctrines and Systematics is often judged artificial and unworkable.
