# Familiaris Consortio 84 and Amoris Laetitia 8

|  | Familiaris Consortio (1981) | Amoris Laetitia (2016) |
|---|---|---|
| Framing | Objective state contradicting the union of Christ and the Church | Accompanying, discerning, integrating; attention to subjective imputability |
| Sacraments | Not admitted unless living as brother and sister | AL 305 fn. 351: "in certain cases, this can include the help of the sacraments" |
| Key claim | The objective situation governs | AL 301: it cannot simply be said that all in irregular situations are in mortal sin |
| Status | Post-synodal exhortation | Post-synodal exhortation; the Buenos Aires criteria published in the AAS as authentic magisterium |

The interpretive dispute is whether AL 8 develops FC 84 by applying imputability and the internal forum, or introduces a discontinuity in Eucharistic discipline.
