# Eight Goals of Theology

Complementary goals — theology serves faith, Church, and world (Ch 1, section E).

1. **Awakening Faith** — "awaken, strengthen and communicate the faith-experience."
2. **Assimilation** — systematize for "better understanding of faith-experience," an "ongoing and continuous process."
3. **Biblical Discovery** — "sift the human words in the Bible... to discover... the real Word of God."
4. **Relevance** — an "interpretative and prophetic role" to "make the faith relevant... for today."
5. **Systematic Exposition** — show the "unity and the interconnectedness" of doctrines in Christ.
6. **Pastoral Empowerment** — "strengthening the faith and empowering Christian life."
7. **Missionary Witness** — "to witness to Christ, to proclaim the Gospel... continue Jesus' mission."
8. **World Transformation** — "the transformation of the whole world into the Kingdom of God."
