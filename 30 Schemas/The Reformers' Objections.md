# The Reformers' Objections

| Reformer | Objection |
|---|---|
| Luther | James not apostolic; no dominical institution; the promise to "raise up" manifestly fails, since the anointed die |
| Calvin | Cessationism — the healing gift was temporary and apostolic; continuing the sign is a "hypocritical stage-play" |
| Cajetan (Catholic) | Exegetical only: James 5 concerns anointing for recovery, not extreme unction |

Cajetan's position was not condemned, showing the exegetical case was live within Catholic theology before the Reformation.
