# The Five FCRA Categories

| Category | Approved purposes | Typical BCPTI relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Religious | 16 | Seminary as theological institution; chapel; formation; retreats; interfaith |
| Cultural | 18 | Not on BCPTI's certificate |
| Economic | 19 | Not on BCPTI's certificate |
| Educational | 22 | Seminary and college; hostels; library; scholarships; teacher training |
| Social | 30 | Not on BCPTI's certificate |

BCPTI is registered for Educational and Religious only. Anything else requires a Rule 17B amendment first.
