# East versus West on Confirmation

|  | East | West |
|---|---|---|
| Who anoints | The presbyter, immediately after baptism | The bishop reserves it to himself |
| Episcopal link preserved by | The myron, consecrated by the patriarch or bishop | The bishop's physical presence |
| Age | Infancy — all three sacraments together | Delayed; drifted upward from 7 |
| Result | Unity of initiation preserved | Separation into a distinct sacrament |

The divergence was administrative, not doctrinal; the Western theology of confirmation was constructed retrospectively.
