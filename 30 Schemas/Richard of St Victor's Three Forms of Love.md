# Richard of St Victor's Three Forms of Love

| Person | Form of Love | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Father | Pure gratuitous love | Unoriginate — giving without having received |
| Son | Gratuitous and owed love | Owed toward the Father (received); gratuitous toward the Spirit |
| Spirit | Owed love alone | Wholly received — terminus of Father's and Son's condilectio |
