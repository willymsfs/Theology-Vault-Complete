# The Three Rites of the 1973 Ordo Paenitentiae

| Rite | Structure | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Rite I | Reconciliation of individual penitents | The ordinary form |
| Rite II | Communal celebration with individual confession and absolution | Strongly recommended; clearest expression of the ecclesial dimension |
| Rite III | Communal celebration with general confession and absolution | Danger of death, or grave necessity judged by the diocesan bishop |

A large gathering or a great feast does not of itself constitute grave necessity (CIC cc. 961-963; Misericordia Dei, 2002).
