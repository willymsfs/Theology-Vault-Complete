# Canons Governing Synodal Structures in the Latin Church

| Canon | Provision | Consequence for synodality |
|---|---|---|
| c. 129 §1 | Power of governance (*potestas regiminis*) restricted to those in sacred orders; laity
may only "co-operate" | The primary theological bottleneck — baptismal equality stops at the
threshold of governance |
| c. 536 | Parish Pastoral Council — consultative vote only | A parish priest may legally act
contrary to unanimous council consensus |
| c. 537 | Parish Finance Council — consultative | Lay members can be denied access to actual
ledgers and property transactions |
| c. 511 | Diocesan Pastoral Council — consultative | Diocesan-level lay discernment carries no
binding force |

## Claims
- [[The consultative vote required by canons 511, 536 and 537 means a unanimous council can be legally overridden by the cleric who convened it]]
