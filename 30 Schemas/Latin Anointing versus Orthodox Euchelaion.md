# Latin Anointing versus Orthodox Euchelaion

|  | Latin | Orthodox |
|---|---|---|
| Ministers | One priest suffices | Ideally seven priests |
| Structure | Forehead and hands; one form | Seven epistles, gospels, prayers, anointings |
| Orientation | Healing emphasis recovered in 1972 | Healing emphasis never lost |
| Recipients | The seriously ill | The ill, and in some traditions the healthy in Holy Week |
| "Extreme unction" phase | Yes, c. 9th-20th century | Never developed |
