# The Sacrament Across Five Eras

| Era | Who applied it | For whom | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Testament | Presbyters (Jas 5); the Twelve (Mk 6:13) | The sick | Save, raise up, forgive |
| Patristic (to c. 800) | Anyone — priest, layperson, the sick themselves | The sick, for recovery | Healing of body and soul |
| Carolingian to medieval | Priest only | The dying | Remission of the reliquiae peccati |
| Trent (1551) | Priest | "Especially" those near death | Grace, strengthening, forgiveness, sometimes health |
| Since 1972 | Priest | Those beginning to be in danger from sickness or old age | Strengthening, union with the passion, forgiveness, healing if conducive |

The oil is blessed by the bishop throughout (a priest may bless in necessity, c. 999).
