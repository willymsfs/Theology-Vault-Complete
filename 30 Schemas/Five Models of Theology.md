# Five Models of Theology

| Model | Theologian | Key phrase | Strength | Risk alone |
|-------|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Syriac-Semitic | [[Ephraem]] | "poetry"; "paradox and symbol" | wonder, mystery | vagueness |
| Scholastic | [[Aquinas]] | "sacred science"; *via doctrinae* | order, clarity | dry rationalism |
| Theological Anthropology | [[Rahner]] | "God and humanity are correlative terms" | connects to experience | blurs nature/grace |
| Liturgical | [[Schmemann]] | *Lex orandi lex credendi* | rooted in worship | inward-looking |
| Praxis (Liberation) | [[Gutierrez]] | "from the standpoint of the poor" | justice, impact | Gospel → politics |
