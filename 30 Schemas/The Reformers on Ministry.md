# The Reformers on Ministry

| Figure | Ordination | Ministry |
|---|---|---|
| Luther | Not a sacrament; public calling to an office (Amt) | Word and sacrament; no character; no sacrificing priesthood |
| Calvin | Laying on of hands retained but not a sacrament | Four offices: pastors, teachers, elders, deacons (Inst. IV.3) |
| Anglican | Threefold ministry retained in the 1550 Ordinal | Sacrificial language and traditio removed — the ground of Apostolicae Curae |
| Radical Reformation | Rejected as an order | Congregational election; lay preaching |

The underlying question in every case: is ministry constitutive (an ontological change) or functional (an authorisation)?
