# Four Gradations of Magisterial Teaching and Assent

| Level | What it is | Assent required |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1. Definitive **Dogma** | truths revealed by God, defined *ex cathedra*/council/universal Magisterium | **Assent of Faith** |
| 2. Definitive **Doctrine** | irreversible teachings logically connected to revealed truth | **Firm Acceptance** |
| 3. Non-definitive **Authoritative Doctrine** | authoritative but not infallible | **Religious Submission of Intellect and Will** |
| 4. **Prudential Admonitions** | pastoral judgments, discipline | obey "the spirit" of the law |

Higher level → stronger assent. Weigh statements "according to their qualitative differences."
