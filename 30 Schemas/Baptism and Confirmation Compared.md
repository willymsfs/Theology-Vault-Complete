# Baptism and Confirmation Compared

|  | Baptism | Confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| Scriptural anchor | Mt 28:19; Jn 3:5; Rom 6:3-4 | Acts 8:14-17; 2 Cor 1:21-22 |
| Matter | Natural water | Chrism (olive oil and balsam) |
| Form | "I baptise you in the name of..." | "Be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit" |
| Ordinary minister | Bishop, priest, deacon; anyone in necessity | Bishop (priest with faculty) |
| Aquinas's analogy | Birth (generatio spiritualis) | Coming of age (augmentum, robur) |
| Necessary for salvation? | Yes (with baptism of blood/desire) | No |
| CCC / CIC | 1213-1284 / cc. 849-878 | 1285-1321 / cc. 879-896 |
