# The Post-Nicene Factions (mid-4th century)

| Faction | Position on the Son |
|---|---|
| Nicene (homoousios) | Same essence as the Father |
| Homoiousian (homoiousios) | Like in essence to the Father |
| Homoian (homoios) | "Like" the Father — ousia-language banned altogether |
| Heterousian/Neo-Arian (anhomoios) | Radically unlike the Father — the Son's essence fully knowable and distinct |

The four-way split filling the vacuum between Nicaea (325) and Constantinople (381).
