# Development of the Sacrament at a Glance

| Era | Form | Key marker |
|---|---|---|
| NT | Authority to bind and loose; communal discipline | Mt 18; Jn 20:22-23 |
| 2nd-6th c. | Canonical penance | Hermas: one repentance only; ordo paenitentium |
| 6th-11th c. | Tariffed penance | Irish penitentials; private and repeatable |
| 12th-15th c. | Scholastic synthesis | Lateran IV (1215): annual confession and the seal |
| 16th c. | Division and definition | Ninety-Five Theses (1517); Trent Session XIV (1551) |
| 17th-19th c. | Manuals and moral systems | Jansenist rigorism; Liguori's equiprobabilism |
| 20th c.- | Reform and crisis | LG 11; Ordo Paenitentiae (1973); collapse in practice |
