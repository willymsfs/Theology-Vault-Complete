# Grounds of Nullity

| Category | Canons | Principal grounds |
|---|---|---|
| Defect of consent | cc. 1095-1103 | Grave lack of discretion of judgement; incapacity to assume essential obligations for psychic causes; simulation; force or grave fear |
| Impediments | cc. 1083-1094 | Age; impotence; prior bond; disparity of cult; orders; public perpetual vow; abduction; crime; consanguinity; affinity; public propriety; adoption |
| Defect of form | c. 1108 | Not celebrated before the competent minister and two witnesses |

c. 1095 is by far the most commonly invoked ground in modern tribunals. A declaration of nullity is not a dissolution: it states no marriage ever existed.
