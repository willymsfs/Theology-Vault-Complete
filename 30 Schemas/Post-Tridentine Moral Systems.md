# Post-Tridentine Moral Systems

| System | Principle | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Rigorism / Tutiorism | Always follow the safer opinion favouring the law | Condemned (Alexander VIII, 1690) |
| Probabiliorism | Follow liberty only if that opinion is more probable | Permitted; Dominican tendency |
| Equiprobabilism | Where opinions are equally probable, liberty may be followed | Alphonsus Liguori's middle course |
| Probabilism | Any solidly probable opinion for liberty may be followed | Permitted; Jesuit tendency |
| Laxism | A slenderly probable opinion suffices | Condemned (Innocent XI, 1679) |
