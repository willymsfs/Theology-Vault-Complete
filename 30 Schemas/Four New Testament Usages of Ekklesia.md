# Four New Testament Usages of Ekklesia

| Usage | Example |
|---|---|
| Local assembly | "the church in Corinth" (1 Cor 1:2) |
| The whole Church | "I persecuted the church of God" (Gal 1:13) |
| The cosmic Church | Christ's Body filling the universe (Eph 1:22-23) |
| The heavenly Church | "the assembly of the firstborn enrolled in heaven" (Heb 12:23) |
