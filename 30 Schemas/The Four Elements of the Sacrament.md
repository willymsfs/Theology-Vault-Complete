# The Four Elements of the Sacrament

| Element | Content | Whose act |
|---|---|---|
| Contritio | Sorrow for sin with purpose of amendment | The penitent |
| Confessio | Disclosure of sins to the priest | The penitent |
| Satisfactio | The penance performed | The penitent |
| Absolutio | Sacramental absolution | The priest |

Aquinas treats the three acts of the penitent as the quasi-materia and the absolution as the form. Penance imprints no character, which is why it is repeatable.
