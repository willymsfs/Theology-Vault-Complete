# Old Testament Types of Baptism

| Type | Text | Prefigures |
|---|---|---|
| Spirit over the waters | Gen 1:2 | Water as womb of creation; baptism as new creation |
| The Flood | Gen 7-8; 1 Pet 3:20-21 | Judgement on sin; salvation "through water" |
| Red Sea crossing | Exod 14; 1 Cor 10:1-2 | Liberation from slavery; "baptised into Moses" |
| Crossing the Jordan | Josh 3 | Entry into the promised inheritance |
| Circumcision | Gen 17; Col 2:11-12 | Covenant incorporation, "not made with hands" |
| Naaman's washing | 2 Kgs 5:10-14 | Cleansing of the Gentile through obedient washing |

All six appear in the Easter Vigil's Blessing of Water. Ezekiel 36:25-27 supplies the decisive water-and-Spirit structure echoed in John 3:5.
