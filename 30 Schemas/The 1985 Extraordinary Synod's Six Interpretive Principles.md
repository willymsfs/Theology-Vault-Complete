# The 1985 Extraordinary Synod's Six Interpretive Principles

| # | Principle |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read each passage in the context of the whole conciliar corpus |
| 2 | The four Constitutions are the hermeneutical key to the decrees and declarations |
| 3 | Pastoral import must never be separated from doctrinal content |
| 4 | No opposition between the "spirit" and "letter" of the Council |
| 5 | Read in continuity with the great tradition and prior councils |
| 6 | Accept the Council as illuminating the problems of our own day |

John O'Malley later proposed a seventh norm: also account for genuine discontinuity.
