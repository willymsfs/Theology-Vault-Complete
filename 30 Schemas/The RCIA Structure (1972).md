# The RCIA Structure (1972)

| Period | Threshold rite that ends it |
|---|---|
| Precatechumenate (inquiry, evangelisation) | Rite of Acceptance into the Order of Catechumens |
| Catechumenate (formation, often a year or more) | Rite of Election / Enrolment of Names (Lent I) |
| Purification and Enlightenment (Lent; scrutinies) | Sacraments of Initiation at the Easter Vigil |
| Mystagogy (Easter to Pentecost) | Integration into ordinary parish life |

For adults, Baptism, Confirmation, and Eucharist are celebrated together in that order by the same minister — restoring the ancient unity.
