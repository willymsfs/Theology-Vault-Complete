# The Anglican Orders Question

| Document | Date | Argument |
|---|---|---|
| Apostolicae Curae | 1896 | Anglican orders "absolutely null and utterly void": defect of form and defect of intention |
| Saepius Officio | 1897 | The same standard would invalidate the ancient Roman rite, which also lacks explicit sacrificial specification |
| Sacramentum Ordinis | 1947 | Removes the traditio from the essential rite, weakening one line of the 1896 argument |
| Bonn Agreement onward | 1931- | Old Catholic co-consecrations introduce lines Rome regards as valid |
| Women's ordination | 1970s- | From Rome's perspective a new obstacle, now the practical centre of the question |
