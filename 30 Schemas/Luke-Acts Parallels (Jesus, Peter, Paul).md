# Luke-Acts Parallels (Jesus, Peter, Paul)

Luke's intertextual key: what Jesus *began* to do (Acts 1:1) he continues to do through the Church.

**Jesus // the Church**

| Jesus (Luke) | The Church (Acts) |
|---|---|
| Prays at his baptism (Lk 3:21) | The disciples pray awaiting Spirit-baptism (1:14) |
| Anointed by the Spirit (Lk 3:21-22) | The Church anointed at Pentecost (2:1-4) |
| Heals a paralytic (Lk 5:17-26) | Peter at the Beautiful Gate (3:1-10); Paul at Lystra (14:8-13); Eutychus (20:9-12) |
| Four trials (Lk 22–23) | Paul's four trials (23–26) |
| Dying words (Lk 23:34, 46) | Stephen's dying words (7:59-60) |
| Pilate: nothing deserving death (Lk 23:16, 22) | Agrippa: "could have been set free" (26:32) |

**Peter // Paul**

| Peter | Paul |
|---|---|
| Heals a man lame from birth (3:1-10) | Heals a man lame from birth (14:8-13) |
| Pentecost sermon (2) | Pisidian Antioch sermon (13:16-41) |
| Rebukes Simon the magician (8:20-24) | Blinds Elymas (13:11) |
| Freed from prison by an angel (12:6-11) | Freed by earthquake at Philippi (16:25-26) |
| Raises Tabitha (9:36-42) | Raises Eutychus (20:9-12) |
