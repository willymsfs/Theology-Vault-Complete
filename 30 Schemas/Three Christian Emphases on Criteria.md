# Three Christian Emphases on Criteria

| Tradition | Primary criterion | Key term |
|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| Orthodox | authority within the communion | [[Sobornost]] / synodality |
| Protestant | Scripture alone | [[Sola Scriptura]] / *Solus Deus* |
| Roman Catholic | Scripture **and** Tradition | Trent; [[Avery Dulles]] |

## Dulles's three objections to Sola Scriptura
1. "the Bible did not collect itself" (the Church gathered it)
2. "the Bible is not self-interpreting"
3. the Bible itself refers to others "speaking by the Spirit"
