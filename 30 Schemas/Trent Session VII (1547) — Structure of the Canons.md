# Trent Session VII (1547) — Structure of the Canons

| Set | Number of canons | Key content |
|---|---|---|
| Sacraments in general | 13 | Canon 8 defines ex opere operato; canon 9 defines character |
| Baptism | 14 | Canon 4 upholds heretical baptism; canons 13-14 defend infant baptism |
| Confirmation | 3 | A true and proper sacrament; chrism; the bishop as ordinary minister |

Memorise as "13-14-3." Session V (1546) on original sin supplies the anthropological premise.
