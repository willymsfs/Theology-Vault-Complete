# Aquinas on Matrimony

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Matter and form | The words or signs expressing consent |
| Minister | The spouses themselves; the priest is the Church's official witness |
| Effect | The bond (vinculum) and grace to fulfil the duties of the state |
| Character | None — hence a widow or widower may remarry |
| Primary end | Procreation and education of offspring |
| Secondary ends | Mutual help; remedy for concupiscence |

That the spouses are the ministers is unique among the seven sacraments and is the root of the entire law of nullity.
