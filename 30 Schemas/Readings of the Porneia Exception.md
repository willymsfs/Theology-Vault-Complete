# Readings of the Porneia Exception

| Reading | Content | Tradition |
|---|---|---|
| Unlawful union | Marriage within forbidden degrees — never valid, hence a nullity clause | Catholic exegesis |
| Adultery | Marital infidelity permitting divorce and remarriage | Orthodox and Protestant |
| Separation only | Permits separation from bed and board but not remarriage | Western canonical position |
| Betrothal infidelity | Unfaithfulness during the Jewish betrothal period (cf. Mt 1:19) | A minority scholarly view |
