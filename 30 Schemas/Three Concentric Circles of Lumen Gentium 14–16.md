# Three Concentric Circles of Lumen Gentium 14–16

| Circle | Who | Bond |
|---|---|---|
| LG 14 — inner | The Catholic faithful | Profession of faith, sacraments, communion with pope and bishops |
| LG 15 — middle | Other baptized Christians | Baptism; real elements of truth and sanctification; "imperfect communion" |
| LG 16 — outer | Non-Christians | Related/associated in different ways: Jews, Muslims, sincere seekers, upright atheists |
