# Basil's Ousia / Hypostasis Distinction

| Term | Refers To | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Ousia | The common, general divine nature | mia ousia — "one essence" |
| Hypostasis | The particular, concrete individual person | treis hypostaseis — "three hypostases" |

Against Eunomius: "unbegotten"/"begotten" name relational properties (idiomata), not the incomprehensible divine essence itself.
