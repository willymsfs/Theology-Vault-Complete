# Trent Session XIV on Extreme Unction

| Point | Teaching |
|---|---|
| Institution | Insinuated by Christ in Mk 6:13, promulgated by James 5:14-15 |
| Matter and form | Oil blessed by a bishop; the prayer of the priest |
| Effects | Grace of the Spirit; wiping away sins and the remnants of sin; alleviating and strengthening the soul; sometimes restoring bodily health when expedient for salvation |
| Minister | Priests — canon 4 rejects "presbyters" as meaning elders by age |
| Recipients | "Especially" (praesertim) those so dangerously ill as to seem near death |

The adverb praesertim, not defining an exclusive restriction, is what left the door open for SC 73.
