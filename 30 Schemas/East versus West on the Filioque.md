# East versus West on the Filioque

|  | East (Monopatrism) | West (Double Procession) |
|---|---|---|
| Principle/Cause | Father alone (archē, aitia) | Father and Son together (tamquam ex uno principio) |
| "Through the Son" | Economic/temporal mission only | Equivalent in meaning to "and the Son" |
| Chief concern | Guards the Father's monarchy; avoids "diarchy" | Distinguishes the Son's and Spirit's processions from each other |
