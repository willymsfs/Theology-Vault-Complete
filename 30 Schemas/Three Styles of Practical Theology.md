# Three Styles of Practical Theology

| Style | Root | Figures | Characteristic move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Liberal-rational** | Tillich's correlation | **Hiltner** — the *shepherding perspective*, practical theology as an operation-centred discipline. **Browning** — a *fundamental practical theology*: descriptive, historical, systematic and strategic stages yielding **practical wisdom (*phronesis*)** defensible in public | Correlate faith with the best available human science |
| **Neo-traditional confessional** | Reaction against reliance on secular psychology | **Nouwen** — contemplative, fragmentary reflection on vocation and the Desert Fathers. **Oden** — retrieval of classical patristic pastoral wisdom against humanist therapy | Recover the tradition's own pastoral resources |
| **Liberationist / radical** | Liberation, feminist, black, disability theologies | Marginalized voices; systemic oppression | Judge practice by its efficacy in promoting holistic well-being and dismantling abusive power; revise metaphors that hinder liberation |

**Shared method** — the **experiential-inductive** three-way conversation between lived experience, theological norms and the human sciences, replacing Schleiermacher's **applicationist** model (theory formulated elsewhere, then applied).
