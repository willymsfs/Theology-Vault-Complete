# Three Principles of Unitatis Redintegratio

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Subsistit in | The Church of Christ subsists in the Catholic Church; real ecclesial elements exist outside her visible boundaries |
| Imperfect communion | Other baptized Christians are joined to the Catholic Church by real, if incomplete, bonds |
| Hierarchy of truths | Revealed truths differ in their proximity to the foundation of the faith (the Trinity, the Incarnation) |
