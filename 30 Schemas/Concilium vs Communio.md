# Concilium vs Communio

|  | Concilium (1964) | Communio (1972) |
|---|---|---|
| Founders | Rahner, Küng, Congar, Schillebeeckx | de Lubac, Ratzinger, von Balthasar (defectors from Concilium) |
| Theological lean | Neo-Thomist; confident engagement with modernity | Neo-Augustinian; wary of theological optimism |
| Reading of the Council | Emphasizes novelty, ongoing reform, the "spirit" | Emphasizes continuity with the whole tradition |

The split originated in tensions during the drafting of Gaudium et Spes.
