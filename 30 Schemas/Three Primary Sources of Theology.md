# Three Primary Sources of Theology

The three bound together by [[Dei Verbum]] 10 — "one cannot stand without the others."

| Source | Its job | Alone it... |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| [[Scripture]] | the written Word | opens to endless private readings |
| [[Tradition]] | the living handing-on | loses its written anchor |
| [[Magisterium]] | the authentic interpreter | has nothing to guard |

Image: a **tripod** — remove any leg and it falls.

## Claims
- [[Scripture, Tradition and Magisterium cannot stand without one another]]
- [[The Magisterium serves the Word, it does not master it]]
