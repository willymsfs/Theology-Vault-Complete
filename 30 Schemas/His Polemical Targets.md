# His Polemical Targets

| Target | Charge |
|---|---|
| Western academic exegesis | "The pretended neutrality... is an illusion"; ideologically loaded; "academic barrenness"; exegetes who "fiddle while Rome burns" |
| Latin American liberation theology | Right on structures, blind to Asian religiosity; must add liberation "from personal bondage (moksha)" |
| The institutional Indian Church | "A wholly owned and controlled subsidiary of a Rome-based company"; caste makes the Eucharist "a sacrilege" and "parody" |
| Ashram spirituality | "A brahmanical spirituality, the spirituality of an affluent, privileged class"; "a mockery for the five hundred millions of our people" |

In writing, deploy at most one per piece, earned by the exegesis preceding it. Four reads as grievance; one reads as prophecy.
