# Dulles's Five Models of the Church

| Model | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Institution | Strong magisterial endorsement; strong corporate identity | Weak scriptural/patristic basis; prone to clericalism, juridicism, triumphalism |
| Mystical Communion | Strong biblical foundation (koinonia); ecumenically fruitful | Unclear why loyalty to visible structures is required |
| Sacrament | Synthesizes institution and communion; open to reform | Can appear complacent, insufficiently prophetic |
| Herald | Strong biblical (prophetic) foundation; clear missionary identity; rich theology of the Word | Weak on incarnation/sacrament; can reduce to bare proclamation |
| Servant | Highly relevant to contemporary struggles; bridges Church and world | Risks secularizing into a mere social agency |
| Community of Disciples (integrating) | Broadly inclusive; bridges all five other models | Still only one more perspective, not a supermodel |

Criteria for any model: (1) basis in Scripture, (2) basis in Christian tradition, (3) capacity to give members a sense of corporate identity and mission.
