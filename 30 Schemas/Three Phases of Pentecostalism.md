# Three Phases of Pentecostalism

| Phase | Dates | Form |
|---|---|---|
| **Classical Pentecostalism** | from the **Azusa Street Revival, 1906–08** | Distinct Pentecostal denominations |
| **Charismatic movement** | from the 1960s | Renewal *within* historic Protestant and Catholic churches |
| **Neopentecostal / "Third Wave"** | from the mid-1970s | Independent churches and megachurches |

**Theological emphases** — an enacted **pneumatology**: the Spirit as active leader of worship, empowering prophecy, tongues, healing and exorcism; the **"full gospel"** (Jesus as Saviour, Healer, Baptizer in the Spirit, Soon Coming King); biblical literalism; **spiritual warfare** and deliverance. The classical **doctrine of consequence** — tongues always follow Spirit baptism — is increasingly challenged from within.

**Why it grows** — a **holistic worldview** uniting physical and spiritual, addressing felt needs for healing and power that rationalistic Western theology does not. Fastest-growing segment of world Christianity, and the reason the Latin American poor left the base communities.
