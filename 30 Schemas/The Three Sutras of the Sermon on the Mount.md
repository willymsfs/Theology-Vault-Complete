# The Three Sutras of the Sermon on the Mount

| Sutra | Claim | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Not law but love | Fulfils the Law rather than enforcing it | Interiorization (acts to intentions) and simplification (many rules to one undivided love); teleios = wholehearted, not flawless |
| 2. Not law but gospel | Consequence of being loved, not condition for favour | "We love because he first loved us" (1 Jn 4:19) |
| 3. A goal-directed norm | Direction of travel, not static casuistic code | Demands fruits (spontaneous output) not works (anxious performance) |

Read as juridical law the Sermon is "thoroughly utopian" — his reductio, not his verdict. Read as dharma it is the Christian svadharma.
