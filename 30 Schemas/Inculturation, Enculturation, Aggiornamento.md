# Inculturation, Enculturation, Aggiornamento

| Term | Meaning | Who it applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Inculturation | Creative, decolonizing "double movement"; transition across underlying cultures; a mental conversion | Culturally alienated groups, victims of cultural imperialism |
| Enculturation | Gradual growth or consolidation after a religion is established in a culture | Established churches |
| Aggiornamento | "Bringing up to date" — reinterpreting doctrine for modern conditions | The West, whether it admits it or not |

His charge: what Westerners call inculturation is really only aggiornamento — updating without crossing any cultural boundary.
