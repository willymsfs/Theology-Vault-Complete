# The Reformation Objection to the Sacrifice of the Mass

| Objection | Basis | Catholic reply (Trent XXII) |
|---|---|---|
| Christ's sacrifice was once for all | Heb 7:27, 9:12, 10:10 (ephapax) | The Mass re-presents rather than repeats; anamnesis in the Hebraic sense |
| It makes the Mass a human work | Justification by faith alone | Christ is the principal offerer; priests act by his ministry |
| It underwrote stipends and chantries | Observable abuse | Trent's own decree on abuses orders bishops to suppress avarice and superstition |
