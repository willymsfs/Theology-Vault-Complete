# Eight Themes of the Johannine Ideal Community

| # | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1 | The saving significance of Jesus's life, death, and resurrection |
| 2 | Foot-washing (Jn 13) as the paradigm of leadership as humble service |
| 3 | Faith in Jesus as the sole foundation of belonging (Jn 20:31) |
| 4 | A movement sovereignly guided by the Spirit / Paraclete |
| 5 | Organic relationship with Christ: the vine and the branches (Jn 15) |
| 6 | Disciples as people of hope |
| 7 | A community charged with mission to the world |
| 8 | Unity: the supreme sign of discipleship (Jn 17:21; 13:35) |
