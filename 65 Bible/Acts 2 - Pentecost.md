# Acts 2 - Pentecost

Wind, fire, and Galileans understood by pilgrims from fifteen nations; Peter preaches, three thousand are baptized.

- **Sinai staging** (Ex 19): fire, theophany, covenant, new law of the Spirit (Rom 8:2). Wind and fire in the OT: Ex 3:1-3; 13:21-22; 19:16-21; Ps 104:3-4.
- **Babel reversed** (Gen 11:1-9) — *Ad Gentes* 4.
- **Peter's sermon**: Joel 2:28-32 fulfilled; "the last days" = the end of the Old Covenant; the messianic proofs of 2:22-36.
- **Response**: repent and be baptized — the new circumcision of the heart (1 Pet 3:21).
- **Result**: the four pillars of 2:42.
