# Acts 10 - Cornelius and the Sheet

A millennium of separation ends on a rooftop in Joppa — the port Jonah used to flee his own mission to Gentiles.

Cornelius, a centurion and God-fearer, prays at the ninth hour and is told his prayers have risen like incense. Peter, at noon, sees a sheet let down by its four corners (= the whole earth) and hears "kill and eat" three times. Gentile messengers arrive before he has finished puzzling. In Cornelius' house the Spirit falls on the uncircumcised while Peter is still speaking — the third Pentecost, and the end of the wall of the table.
