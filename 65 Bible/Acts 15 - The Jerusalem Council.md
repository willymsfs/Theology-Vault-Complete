# Acts 15 - The Jerusalem Council

The Church's first doctrinal crisis and the prototype of every later council.

**Question:** must Gentiles be circumcised — that is, submit to the whole Law (Gen 17; Lev 12:3) — to be saved?
**Process:** debate; Peter's speech ("we believe that we shall be saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus, just as they will," 15:11); James' pastoral application with OT warrant (Am 9:11-12; Jer 12:15; Is 45:21).
**Decree:** no circumcision; abstain from idol-food, unchastity, and blood (Lev 17–18) — temporary provisions for table unity, not new law.
**Formula:** "It has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us" (15:28).
