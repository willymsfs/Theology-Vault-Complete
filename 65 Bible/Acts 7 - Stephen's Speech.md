# Acts 7 - Stephen's Speech

The longest speech in Acts, and the one that gets its speaker killed.

Stephen retells salvation history from Abraham with two themes running: Israel's recurring rejection of the deliverers God sends, and God's habit of appearing *outside* the land — to Abraham in Mesopotamia, to Moses in Midian and at a bush in Sinai. The cumulative argument dismantles the fixation on land and Temple: God's two concessions were a king (1 Sam 8) and a house, and "heaven is my throne" (Is 66:1-2). Then the vision of the Son of Man standing (Dn 7:13-14), the stoning, and the dying prayer for his killers — with Saul holding the cloaks.
