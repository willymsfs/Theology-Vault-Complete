# Acts 3 - The Beautiful Gate

The first recorded miracle, at 3 PM — the hour of the evening sacrifice (Ex 29:38-42; Nm 28:1-8; Dn 9:21), the foundation of the Liturgy of the Hours.

A man lame from birth begs at the gate; Peter has no silver or gold, only the Name, and the man leaps into the Temple — the messianic sign of Is 35:6 and Mal 4:2. Luke's double-witness pattern follows: the sign (3:1-11), then the apostolic testimony (3:12–4:23). Peter's sermon in Solomon's Portico presents Jesus as the Servant of Is 42 and 52–53.
