# Acts 1-8 - The Map Verse

"You shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria and to the end of the earth."

Jesus' answer to the wrong-shaped question. The disciples ask about restoring the kingdom to Israel — thrones, armies, territory — and receive a mission instead of a map: *dunamis* instead of dominion, *martus* instead of magistrate. The verse then functions as the book's structure, and its third clause is left grammatically open at 28:31.

**Teaching move.** Each student writes their own Acts 1:8 — naming their Jerusalem (those closest), their Judea and Samaria (those outside the circle), their ends of the earth.
