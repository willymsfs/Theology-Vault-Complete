# Acts 28-31 - The Unhindered Gospel

"...preaching the kingdom of God and teaching about the Lord Jesus Christ quite openly and unhindered."

*Akolytos* — "unhindered" — is the last word of Acts, and it is an adverb describing a prisoner. Luke knows how Paul dies and does not tell us; the third clause of Acts 1:8 is left open on purpose. The ending is a commissioning: the reader supplies the next chapter.
