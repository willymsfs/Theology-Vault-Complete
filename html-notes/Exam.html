Theological Method — Question Bank

Source unit: Theological Methods - MOC (12 course notes, 4 notebooks). Concepts and claims live in this vault; the long treatment lives in the course folder.

Define / explain (each: term, whose, one-line definition, one example)

  1. Theological method — Lonergan — normative pattern of recurrent and related operations yielding cumulative and progressive results → Theological Method
  2. Functional specialty — Lonergan — division of theological work by task, not by field → Eight Functional Specialties
  3. Conversion — Lonergan/Doran — religious, moral, intellectual, psychic shift of horizon → Conversion
  4. Horizon — the boundary within which meaning and choice occur → Horizon
  5. Method of correlation — Tillich — existential question ↔ symbolic answer → Method of Correlation
  6. Epoché + eidetic vision — Bleeker/Kristensen — suspend judgement, then seek the essence → Epoche
  7. Anubhava — Indian theology — direct spiritual experience as starting point → Anubhava
  8. Crucified mind — Koyama — kenotic posture against the crusading mind → Crucified Mind
  9. Theology of the cross — Luther — God known in weakness, not in glory → Theology of the Cross
  10. Ressourcement — return to biblical, patristic, liturgical sources → Ressourcement
  11. Historical consciousness — sources become data, not premises → Historical Consciousness
  12. Supernatural existential — Rahner — nature always already graced → Supernatural Existential

Short answer (5 marks)

  1. Distinguish method, technique and system. → Method is a pattern of operations, not a set of rules
  2. Name the four transcendental precepts and the operation each governs. → Eight Functional Specialties
  3. Why is discord between methods worse than opposition between doctrines? → Discord between methods is graver than opposition between doctrines
  4. What are the three distortions produced by imbalance among the sources? → Over-emphasis on experience, history or reason distorts theology in three predictable ways
  5. State the two possible starting points and one consequence of each. → The choice of starting point decides the theology
  6. What is the "short-circuit" fallacy in exegesis? → The short-circuit from what a text meant to what it means is the commonest exegetical error
  7. Give the four criteria for choosing a method. → Four Criteria for Choosing a Theological Method
  8. What two criteria guard inculturation from relativism? → Contextual method is not anti-Western but anti-absolutised-Western

Essay (15–20 marks)

  1. "Every major theological reversal is first a reversal of method." Test on Luther→Aquinas and Barth→Schleiermacher. → Every major theological reversal is first a reversal of method
  2. "Objectivity is the fruit of authentic subjectivity." Expound and assess as a foundation for method. → Objectivity is the fruit of authentic subjectivity
  3. How did historical consciousness change theological method, from the Enlightenment to Vatican II? → Historical consciousness turned the sources of theology into data
  4. Compare Aquinas's gratia perficit naturam with Rahner's supernatural existential. → Rahner dissolves the two-tier separation of nature and grace
  5. Is the historical-critical method adequate for an Indian reading of Scripture? → The historical-critical method is necessary but not sufficient for an Indian reading
