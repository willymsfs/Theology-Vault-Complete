# Ad Gentes

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Documents/Ad Gentes (Vatican II course)|Ad Gentes — Vatican II course treatment]].


Trinitarian foundation: the Church is "by its very nature missionary," continuing the Father's sending of Son and Spirit. Advances an incarnational, non-imposing model of inculturation.

Linked from: [[Vatican II MOC]]


### Initiation and Mission (AG 14)
AG 14 treats the catechumenate as integral to missionary practice, describing it as a progressive formation of the whole Christian life rather than a mere transmission of doctrine — a conciliar anticipation of the RCIA's restored structure. See [[Sacraments MOC]].
