# Collected Writings of George M. Soares-Prabhu

Published by Jnana-Deepa Vidyapeeth, Pune, in the JDV Theology Series. **Vol 1** *Biblical Themes for a Contextual Theology Today* (1999, ed. Isaac Padinjarekuttu, 15 chapters, 260+ pp) — mission; inculturation, liberation, dialogue; contextual interpretation. **Vol 2** *A Biblical Theology for India* (332 pp, 16 chapters) — biblical methods, OT, non-Christian scriptures; portraits of Jesus; Indian readings. **Vol 3** *Biblical Spirituality of Liberative Action* — the Abba experience, the dharma of Jesus, miracles, religious life. **Vol 4** *Theology of Liberation: An Indian Biblical Perspective* — the Indian mind, the prophet as theologian, the Kingdom as new society, a Dalit reading of the Decalogue. The title essay "The Dharma of Jesus" is Vol 4 ch. 10, pp. 153-172.

These are collected *articles*: most first appeared in Indian theological journals — *Vidyajyoti*, *Jeevadhara*, *Jnanadeepa*, *Bible Bhashyam*. The essay, not the monograph, was his native form.
