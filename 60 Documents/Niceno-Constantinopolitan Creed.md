# Niceno-Constantinopolitan Creed

The expanded 381 Creed adds extensive material on the Holy Spirit in liturgical co-glorification language ("who with the Father and the Son together is worshipped and glorified") rather than an explicit homoousios, following Basil of Caesarea's pastoral reserve. The Western filioque addition to its article on the Spirit, absent from the original text, became a central catalyst of the Great Schism of 1054.
