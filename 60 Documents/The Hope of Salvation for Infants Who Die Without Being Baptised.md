# The Hope of Salvation for Infants Who Die Without Being Baptised

Concludes that there are "serious theological and liturgical grounds for hope" that infants dying without baptism are saved, and clarifies that limbus infantium was always a theological hypothesis and never defined doctrine. Effectively closes the pastoral wound left by Augustine's anti-Pelagian argument.
