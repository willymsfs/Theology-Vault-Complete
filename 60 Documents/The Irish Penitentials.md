# The Irish Penitentials

The handbooks of Finnian, Columbanus, Cummean, and Theodore of Canterbury, listing sins with assigned penances and governed by the medicinal principle contraria contrariis sanantur. They embody the private, repeatable, tariffed system that displaced canonical penance — a practice condemned at Toledo III (589) before being recognised at Chalon-sur-Saône (813) and becoming universal in the West.
