# Transiturus de hoc mundo

Urban IV's bull extending the feast of Corpus Christi to the universal Church, with an office traditionally ascribed to Thomas Aquinas — the source of Pange Lingua, Lauda Sion, Verbum Supernum, and Sacris Solemniis.
