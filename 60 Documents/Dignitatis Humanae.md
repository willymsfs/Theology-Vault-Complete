# Dignitatis Humanae

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Documents/Dignitatis Humanae (Vatican II course)|Dignitatis Humanae — Vatican II course treatment]].


The most bitterly contested conciliar document. Moves from "error has no rights" to religious freedom grounded in personal human dignity and civil immunity from coercion, chiefly through John Courtney Murray's drafting. Ratzinger later called it a "counter-syllabus."
