# Quam Singulari

Issued 8 August 1910 under Pius X, lowering the age of First Communion to the age of discretion (about seven) and ending the practice of withholding the Eucharist from children until twelve or fourteen. A magnificent pastoral reform with a large unintended consequence: because Confirmation remained tied to episcopal visitation schedules, the ancient order of initiation was inverted in the West from Baptism-Confirmation-Eucharist to Baptism-Eucharist-Confirmation.
