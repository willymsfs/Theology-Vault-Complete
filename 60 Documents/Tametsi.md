# Tametsi

The decree of Session XXIV requiring, for validity, that marriage be contracted before the parish priest or a delegate and two or three witnesses — the single most consequential practical change in the sacrament's history, since for fifteen centuries a marriage had been valid by consent however expressed. It finally solved the chronic problem of clandestine marriages, which the Church had always held illicit but valid. Its flaw was that it bound only in parishes where it had been promulgated, producing legal chaos in mission territories until Ne Temere (1907) imposed canonical form universally.
