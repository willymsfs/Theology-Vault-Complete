# Pastor Aeternus

## ch. 3
"Defines the Pope's "full, supreme, and universal power of jurisdiction" — ordinary and immediate over every particular church and every pastor and faithful."

## ch. 4
"Defines papal infallibility under four conditions: speaking ex cathedra, as supreme pastor, on faith or morals, intending to bind the whole Church."
