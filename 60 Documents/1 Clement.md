# 1 Clement

Written from Rome to Corinth to settle a revolt in which presbyters had been deposed. Chapters 40-44 argue from order, drawing an analogy with the Old Testament cult of high priest, priests, Levites, and laypeople — introducing the word laikos into Christian literature — and from succession, stating that the apostles appointed bishops and deacons and provided that approved men should succeed to their ministry. The earliest post-apostolic text on ministry and the seed of the doctrine of apostolic succession.
