# Divinae Consortium Naturae

Promulgated by Paul VI on 15 August 1971, the definitive modern document on Confirmation. Locates the sacrament firmly within Christian initiation rather than as a free-standing coming-of-age rite, and defines the essential rite as anointing with chrism on the forehead done by the laying on of the hand, with the words "Accipe signaculum Doni Spiritus Sancti" — "Be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit." This form was deliberately borrowed from the Byzantine rite, replacing the medieval Latin formula: a genuine act of ecumenical ressourcement.
