# Unigenitus Dei Filius

Clement VI's bull formalising the doctrine of the treasury of merits: the infinite merits of Christ, together with those of Mary and the saints, constitute a thesaurus which the Church may apply to individual members through the power of the keys. The theological basis of the indulgence system.
