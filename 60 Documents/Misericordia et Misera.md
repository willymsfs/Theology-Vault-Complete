# Misericordia et Misera

Francis's letter concluding the Jubilee of Mercy, continuing the Missionaries of Mercy beyond the Jubilee and extending to all priests permanently the faculty to absolve the sin of abortion — framing the sacrament principally in terms of divine mercy and pastoral accessibility.
