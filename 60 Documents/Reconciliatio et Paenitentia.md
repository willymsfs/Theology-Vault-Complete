# Reconciliatio et Paenitentia

John Paul II's exhortation following the 1983 Synod on reconciliation. Notable for §16's careful analogical treatment of social sin, which accepts the usage — sin has genuine structural and communal dimensions — while insisting that sin remains ultimately a personal act, so that talk of sinful structures must not dissolve individual responsibility.
