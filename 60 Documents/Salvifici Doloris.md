# Salvifici Doloris (SD)

John Paul II's letter on suffering. The doctrinal backing for the "suffering as seed" thread of Acts.

## SD 27
Suffering, united to Christ's, shares in the treasure of the Redemption — the reading behind Col 1:24 and behind Paul's commission "how much he must suffer for my name" (Acts 9:16).
