# Veritatis Splendor

John Paul II's encyclical on the foundations of moral theology. Sections 65-70 address fundamental option theory: the encyclical accepts that freedom has a fundamental orientation, but rejects separating that orientation from concrete choices, holding that a gravely disordered act freely chosen with full knowledge can itself constitute the rejection of God — precisely the inference the theory was constructed to block.
