# Sacramentum Ordinis

Pius XII's constitution of 30 November 1947 definitively settling the matter and form of Holy Orders. Declares by apostolic authority that the matter of the ordination of deacons, priests, and bishops is the imposition of hands alone, and that the form consists of the words of the consecratory preface determining the effect, specifying which words in each rite. The traditio instrumentorum, which Florence (1439) had taught to be the matter, is declared not required for validity — a correction of an earlier magisterial text in light of historical evidence, running toward the more ancient and ecumenically shared practice, and one which incidentally weakened a pillar of Apostolicae Curae.
