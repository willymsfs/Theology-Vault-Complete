# Laetentur Caeli

Signed 5 July 1439, declaring the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son "as from one principle and by a single spiration," and Greek "through the Son" equivalent to Latin "and the Son." Mark of Ephesus alone among the Greek bishops refused to sign; the union was repudiated within years and formally annulled by the Council of Constantinople in 1484.
