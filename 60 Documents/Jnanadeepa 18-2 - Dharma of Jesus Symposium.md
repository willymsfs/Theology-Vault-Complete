# Jnanadeepa 18-2 - Dharma of Jesus Symposium

*Jnanadeepa: Pune Journal of Religious Studies*, Vol. 18 No. 2 (July 2015), ed. **George Karuvelil SJ**, Jnana Deepa Publications, Pune. Reprints Soares-Prabhu's own essay (p. 9) and adds **eight scholarly responses** — the record of how his own tradition received and extended him: Joyce Kaithakottil on the philosophy/theology crossroads (25) · George Pattery on its use for Indian theology today (37) · Scaria Kuthirakkattel on experience and context (57) and on prophetic theologizing (77) · **Helen Dantis on a feminist perspective (97)** · Denis Lemos on pastoral application (119) · Stephen Jayard on science (137) · George Karuvelil on philosophical foundations (155).
