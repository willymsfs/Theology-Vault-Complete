# Unitatis Redintegratio

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Documents/Unitatis Redintegratio (Vatican II course)|Unitatis Redintegratio — Vatican II course treatment]].


States the three principles of ecumenism: subsistit in (recalling LG 8), imperfect communion, and the hierarchy of truths.

Linked from: [[Subsistit In]] · [[Hierarchy of Truths]] · [[Imperfect Communion]]


### Black Week Note
Paul VI inserted nineteen last-minute amendments after the fathers had already voted on the final text (20 November 1964) — accepted without further floor debate, temporarily clouding the ecumenical breakthrough this decree represents.

### Baptism as the Sacramental Bond of Unity (UR 3, 22)
UR 22 teaches that baptism "constitutes a sacramental bond of unity linking all who have been reborn by it," and UR 3 that those baptised outside full communion are "truly incorporated into Christ" and rightly called Christians. Read alongside Trent Session VII canon 4 — which upheld the validity of baptism conferred by heretics — this is the doctrinal foundation of modern Catholic ecumenism. See [[Sacraments MOC]].
