# Inter Insigniores

The Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith's declaration on the question of the admission of women to the ministerial priesthood, arguing from the constant tradition of East and West, from Christ's choice of the Twelve, and from the priest's acting in persona Christi. The immediate precursor of Ordinatio Sacerdotalis (1994).
