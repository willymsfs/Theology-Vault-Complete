# The Dharma of Jesus (Anthology)

Nineteen essays by George M. Soares-Prabhu, edited by **Francis X. D'Sa, SJ** (Maryknoll: Orbis, 2003), in four parts: **I Portraits of Jesus and His Community** (Jesus and the Spirit · Jesus the Teacher · Jesus the Prophet · The Kingdom of God: Jesus' Vision of a New Society · The Jesus of Faith · Radical Beginnings) · **II The Praxis of Jesus** (Table Fellowship for Dalit Christians · Miracles · a Dhvani Reading of the Stilling of the Storm · The Unprejudiced Jesus and the Prejudiced Church · Jesus and Conflict) · **III The Teaching of Jesus** (ch. 12, the title essay, on the Sermon on the Mount · the Synoptic Love Commandment · Speaking to Abba · As We Forgive · Good News to the Poor) · **IV The Mission of Jesus** (Expanding the Horizon of Christian Mission · The Church as Mission · Following Jesus in Mission).

Several chapters overlap the *Collected Writings*. D'Sa's editorial achievement was to lift the Christological essays out of the volume-by-volume arrangement and organise them around a single question: who is Jesus and what does following him require?
