# Rite of Marriage

Promulgated in 1969 and revised in 1991 in fulfilment of Sacrosanctum Concilium 77-78. Restores a full Liturgy of the Word, recovers a fuller nuptial blessing with ancient Trinitarian and epicletic language, provides for celebration within or outside Mass, and permits adaptation to local custom — a provision of real significance for the Indian context, where the reception of the couple, the tying of the thali, and family rituals may be integrated into the rite.
