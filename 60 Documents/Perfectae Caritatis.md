# Perfectae Caritatis

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Documents/Perfectae Caritatis (Vatican II course)|Perfectae Caritatis — Vatican II course treatment]].


Combines ressourcement (return to each order's founding charism) with aggiornamento. Received over 14,000 amendments; women's religious communities implemented it far beyond what the hierarchy anticipated.
