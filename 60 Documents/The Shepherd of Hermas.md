# The Shepherd of Hermas

Reports a revelation permitting one — and only one — post-baptismal repentance, framed explicitly as a reluctant concession of mercy to a Church discovering its members in grave sin, and strictly non-repeatable. The origin of the whole penitential system and of the "second plank after shipwreck" tradition; widely read and in some places treated as near-canonical in the second century.
