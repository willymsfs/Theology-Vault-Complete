# Dei Verbum (DV)

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Documents/Dei Verbum (Vatican II course)|Dei Verbum — Vatican II course treatment]].


Vatican II's Dogmatic Constitution on Divine Revelation. Central to Ch 2, referenced in Ch 1 on revelation and Scripture.

## DV 10
Scripture, Tradition, Magisterium "so linked and joined together that one cannot stand without the others."

## DV 11
Inerrancy scoped: Scripture teaches "without error that truth which God wanted to put into the sacred writings **for the sake of our salvation**."

## DV 12
Scripture "needs to be interpreted"; final authority = "the judgment of the Church."

Linked from: [[Revelation]] · [[Theology]] (fuller use in Ch 2 notes).


## Ecclesiology (BTh course)

### DV 10
"This Magisterium is not superior to the Word of God, but is its servant." — grounds Unit 12's claim that the teaching Church is also a learning Church. See [[Lit - Ecclesiology Unit 12]].

### Origins in De Fontibus Revelationis
Rebuilt from the rejected De Fontibus Revelationis (halted 20-21 November 1962) by periti including Daniélou, Rahner, and Ratzinger. Witherup's analysis reads it as combining continuity (reaffirming Trent and Vatican I on inspiration/canon/inerrancy) with novelty (personalist language, the Incarnation analogy for Scripture, and official acceptance of three historical layers of Gospel tradition).
