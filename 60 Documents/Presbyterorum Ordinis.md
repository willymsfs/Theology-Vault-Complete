# Presbyterorum Ordinis

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Documents/Presbyterorum Ordinis (Vatican II course)|Presbyterorum Ordinis — Vatican II course treatment]].


Shifts from an isolated "cultic model" of priesthood to a relational "servant leader" model embedded in a web of relationships with bishop, fellow priests, and laity.

Linked from: [[Vatican II MOC]]


### Theology of the Presbyterate
The decree's shift from an isolated cultic model of priesthood to a relational servant-leader model — the priest embedded in a web of relationships with his bishop, his brother priests in the presbyterium, and the laity — is the practical working-out of LG 28. PO 4 gives primacy to the ministry of the word, which stands in unresolved tension with centuries of practice giving primacy to the altar: a live question in the contemporary theology of the presbyterate. See [[Presbyterium]] and [[Holy Orders MOC]].
