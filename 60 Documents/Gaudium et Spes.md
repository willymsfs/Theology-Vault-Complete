## GS 58
God "speaks to humans according to their culture of different ages" — grounds culture as a resource of theology.

### Drafting History (Schema XIII/XVII)
Had no pre-conciliar model; underwent four complete rewrites (the "Roman text" through Suenens's "Malines text"). Resolved the Neo-Augustinian/Neo-Thomist tension via a Christological anthropology (GS 22). Passed 2,309-75 on 7 December 1965, the Council's final working session.

### Marriage and the Family (GS 47-52)
GS 48 describes marriage as "the intimate partnership of married life and love" (intima communitas vitae et amoris coniugalis) established by a "conjugal covenant of irrevocable personal consent" — substituting foedus (covenant) for contractus (contract) as the governing category. GS 49 offers the first substantial magisterial theology of conjugal love as such. Most significantly, GS 50 deliberately declines to repeat the classical hierarchy of ends: while affirming that marriage is by its nature ordained toward the begetting and educating of children, it states that marriage is "not instituted solely for procreation" and that its character persists "even when offspring are lacking" — and amendments seeking to restore the primary/secondary language were rejected. The change is registered in law at 1983 CIC c. 1055 §1, which names the bonum coniugum alongside procreation with no hierarchy asserted. See [[Hierarchy of Ends]] and [[Matrimony MOC]].
