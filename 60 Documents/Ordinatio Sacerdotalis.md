# Ordinatio Sacerdotalis

John Paul II's letter of 22 May 1994 declaring that "the Church has no authority whatsoever to confer priestly ordination on women and that this judgement is to be definitively held by all the Church's faithful." A 1995 CDF responsum stated that the teaching belongs to the deposit of faith. The letter addresses priestly ordination specifically and does not treat the diaconate, which is why the diaconal question has remained open and subject to papal study commissions in 2016 and 2020.
