# De Lapsis

Cyprian of Carthage's treatise on the crisis of the lapsed after the Decian persecution. Against the confessors issuing libelli pacis on their own merits, it insists reconciliation belongs to the bishop's ordinary ministry; against Novatianist rigorism, it holds the lapsed can be forgiven after penance graded by the gravity of the lapse. The settlement it brokered underlies the Church's claim that no sin is beyond its power to forgive.
