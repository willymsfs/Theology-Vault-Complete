# Saepius Officio

The reply of the Archbishops of Canterbury and York to Apostolicae Curae, arguing that if the standard applied to the Edwardine Ordinal were applied consistently, the ancient Roman rite would also fail, since its consecratory prayer likewise lacks explicit specification of the priesthood as an order for offering sacrifice. In effect, that Rome was judging Anglican orders by a criterion its own tradition does not meet.
