# Traditionis Custodes

Francis's motu proprio substantially restricting the permissions granted by Benedict XVI's Summorum Pontificum (2007), and affirming that the liturgical books promulgated by Paul VI and John Paul II are "the unique expression of the lex orandi of the Roman Rite." The document and the controversy surrounding it are the current focus of debate over liturgical reception of Vatican II.
