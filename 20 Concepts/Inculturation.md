# Inculturation

Letting the Gospel take genuine root in a culture — "taking flesh" in its forms — not translating a Western package. "Inculturation has been always a watchword in Indian theology"; a "natural process" involving the Church in the "struggles, languages, and 'smaller cultures'" of the marginalized.

### Soares-Prabhu's three-term distinction
He uses three terms precisely, and conflating them is the commonest error in writing about him. **Inculturation** is a creative, decolonizing "double movement" proper to "culturally alienated groups... victims of cultural imperialism," requiring transition across underlying cultures — a mental *conversion*. **Enculturation** is the later consolidation once a religion is established. **Aggiornamento** is merely "bringing up to date." His charge: **what Westerners call inculturation is really only aggiornamento** — reinterpreting doctrine for modernity without crossing any cultural boundary at all.
