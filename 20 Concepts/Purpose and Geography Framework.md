# Purpose and Geography Framework

The Amendment Rules 2026 (S.O. 3272(E), 22 June 2026) changed registration from organisation-centric to **purpose-based and geography-specific**. The certificate now names approved purposes and States/UTs; narrative self-described aims were abolished; purposes must be chosen from the prescribed Schedule. Migration via **Form FC-6F by 22 June 2027**.
