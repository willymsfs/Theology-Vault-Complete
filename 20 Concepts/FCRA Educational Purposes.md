# FCRA Educational Purposes

The **Educational** category of the FCRA Schedule (Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)) — 22 approved purposes.

| ID | Approved purpose |
|---|---|
| **EDU-01** | Establishment and management of educational institutions and facilities, including early childhood education centres, schools, colleges, universities, hostels, libraries, reading rooms, and community learning centres |
| **EDU-02** | Scholarships, fellowships, stipends, and financial aid for disadvantaged students |
| **EDU-03** | Adult literacy, continuing education, and night schools for workers |
| **EDU-04** | Inclusive education for marginalized and differently-abled children with Braille, sign language, assistive technologies |
| **EDU-05** | Teacher training institutes, refresher courses, and academic fellowships |
| **EDU-06** | Establishment and running of technical, industrial, and vocational education institutions leading to recognized certification, diploma, degree, or accreditation |
| **EDU-07** | Research institutions, think-tanks, innovation labs, and academic chairs |
| **EDU-08** | Digital education platforms, smart classrooms, e-libraries, and distance learning |
| **EDU-09** | Girls' education initiatives: residential schools, hostels, scholarships |
| **EDU-10** | Education for children of migrant workers, refugees, and nomadic tribes |
| **EDU-11** | Bridge courses, remedial coaching, and dropout reintegration programmes |
| **EDU-12** | Environmental education programmes in schools and colleges |
| **EDU-13** | STEM education programmes, science clubs, and innovation fairs |
| **EDU-14** | Structured sports education and training academies attached to schools/colleges |
| **EDU-15** | Language teaching, translation studies, and linguistic training |
| **EDU-16** | Career counselling centres, mentorship programmes, and educational placement cells |
| **EDU-17** | Higher academic fellowships abroad in government-recognised institutions for meritorious students from disadvantaged backgrounds |
| **EDU-18** | Value-based and ethics education in schools |
| **EDU-19** | Awareness programmes on constitutional rights, fundamental duties, and civic responsibilities, strictly non-political |
| **EDU-20** | Disaster preparedness education: mock drills, safety training, community insurance literacy |
| **EDU-21** | Digital inclusion initiatives: assistive technologies for differently-abled students, rural e-learning hubs |
| **EDU-22** | Youth leadership and civic engagement programmes |
