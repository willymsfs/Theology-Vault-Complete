# Orthodoxy

Right/accepted belief — "fidelity to the original and authentic Christian faith," a "perennial concern." Held in tension with heterodoxy (Polarity 7). The criteria of Ch 5 exist to discern where legitimate pluralism ends and heterodoxy begins.
