# Agape as Effective Love

Not affection but "effective love," a "love of concern": "to do good to someone." "To love God means, concretely, to love neighbour" — Jesus's fusion of the two commandments makes the neighbor the locus of encountering God. And in context: "In an unjust society agapē inevitably becomes a struggle for justice: it strives 'to set free the oppressed'." Love without structural consequence is not yet biblical love.
