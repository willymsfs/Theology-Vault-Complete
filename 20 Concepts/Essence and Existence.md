# Essence and Existence (Esse)

**Essence** (or quiddity) is *what* a thing is, as captured by its definition. **Existence** (*esse*)
is the actual act of being in reality. [[Avicenna]] and, following him, [[Aquinas]] hold these are
*really* distinct in created things: one can fully understand what a "phoenix" or a "man" is without
knowing whether it actually exists. This real distinction underwrites Aquinas's account of how God can
sustain the accidents of bread in existence apart from their usual substance — see
[[Accidens Sine Subjecto]].
