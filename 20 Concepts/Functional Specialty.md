# Functional Specialty

Lonergan's eightfold division of theological work, each specialty corresponding to a distinct operation of human consciousness. Two phases pivoting on [[Conversion]]:

- **Positive (mediating)** — Research · Interpretation · History · Dialectics: retrieving the tradition.
- **Normative (mediated)** — Foundations · Doctrines · Systematics · Communications: handing it on to a culture.

Specialisation is *functional*, not by field or by period: the division is by the **task performed**, which is what makes theology cumulative rather than endlessly restarted.
