# Trinitarian Appropriations

The medieval (Hugh and Richard of St Victor, later Aquinas and Emery) practice of "appropriating" common, undivided divine attributes to specific persons by relational analogy to their origin — Power/Father, Wisdom/Son, Goodness/Spirit.
