# Tria Munera

The threefold office of teaching (docendi), sanctifying (sanctificandi), and governing (regendi), conferred by episcopal consecration itself according to LG 21 — mapping onto Christ's prophetic, priestly, and kingly office and Israel's threefold anointing.
