# Administrative Expenses Cap

FCRA caps administrative expenses at **20% of foreign contribution received** in a financial year (Sec 8(1)(b); Rule 5 FCRR 2011). Rule 5 lists eight categories. Two exclusions rescue an expense: salaries of training or field-data personnel where the body is primarily engaged in research or training, and expenses **directly in furtherance of stated objectives** — the Rule's own examples being salaries to hospital doctors and school teachers. The test is **function, not the label on the invoice**: the same repair is administrative for the office and programme for the classroom.
