# Communalism

The "turning in of religious communities upon themselves to form tightly knit defensive groups maintaining a hostile attitude" toward rivals — life perceived as religious communities "competing with other religious groups for survival and growth," fueled by insecurity. Soares-Prabhu's counter: the "anticommunal Jesus," who abolished the purity boundaries separating sacred from profane (Mk 7:1–23) and practiced open commensality.
