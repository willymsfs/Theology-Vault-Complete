# The Three Sutras

Soares-Prabhu's three aphorisms interpreting the Sermon on the Mount: (1) **not law but love** — it fulfils the Law by interiorization and simplification, and teleios means "wholehearted," not "flawless"; (2) **not law but gospel** — the spontaneous consequence of being loved, not a condition for earning favour; (3) **a goal-directed norm** — outlining "the direction in which Christian life is moving," demanding fruits rather than works.
