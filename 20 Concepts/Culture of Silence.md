# Culture of Silence

Via Freire: an "alienated society" silenced and treated as the "mere object" of a dominant or colonizing power. Soares-Prabhu pairs it with the **tinsel facade** — "the glittering but tinsel facade of Westernization" imposed by a privileged elite that hides the overwhelming poverty of the "real India." The pairing names his double diagnosis: the poor rendered voiceless, the elite rendered blind.
