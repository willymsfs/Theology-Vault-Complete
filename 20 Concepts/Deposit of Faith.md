# Deposit of Faith

The whole treasure of revealed truth entrusted to the Church. Not frozen: guided by the Spirit, its implicit truths become explicit over time — but only as "explicitations... never entirely new additions."

## Claim
- [[Doctrine develops by making the implicit explicit, never by adding anew]]
