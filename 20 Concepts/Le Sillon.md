# Le Sillon

**Marc Sangnier**'s movement (from 1899), founded by an ex-army officer: an eclectic body aiming at **reconciliation between Catholicism and the French Republic**, with a paramilitary wing that grew to 10,000 members and contacts with **freethinkers and Protestants**.

**Condemned by Pius X in 1910** — especially for the proposition that **Catholics need obey pope and bishops only in spiritual matters**. That is the nerve: not democracy as such but the claim that ecclesiastical authority has a bounded jurisdiction.

**Place it in the sequence**: [[Felicite de Lamennais]] (1834) → French [[Christian Democracy]] fenced by *Graves de communi re* (1901) → Le Sillon (1910). Three condemnations of the same instinct across eighty years — and all three questions reopened at Vatican II.
