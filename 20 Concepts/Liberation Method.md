# Liberation Method

Theology as **critical reflection on praxis in the light of the Word**, done from the standpoint of the oppressed — not an academic discourse applied afterwards to social problems.

Operative structure (Clodovis Boff's three mediations): **socio-analytic** (name the structures, with the social sciences), **hermeneutic** (read scripture from that standpoint — Segundo's *hermeneutic circle*), **practical** (act, then re-read). Pastoral form: **see–judge–act**, in base ecclesial communities.

Core terms: **preferential option for the poor** — first a statement about God's gratuitous love; **structural sin**; the *non-person* as theology's interlocutor, replacing the bourgeois unbeliever.

**Criticisms:** temporal messianism; thin social theory; and the postcolonial charge that it romanticized the Bible and homogenized "the poor."
