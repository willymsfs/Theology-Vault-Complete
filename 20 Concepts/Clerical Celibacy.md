# Clerical Celibacy

A discipline of the Latin Church, not a doctrine: continence for married clergy legislated at Elvira (c. 306), the Eastern settlement fixed at Trullo (692), and celibacy made a condition of the clerical state in the West by Lateran II (1139). Not required for validity, and not observed in the Eastern Catholic Churches.
