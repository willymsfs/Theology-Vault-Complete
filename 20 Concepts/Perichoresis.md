# Perichoresis (Greek) — "mutual indwelling"

The dynamic, reciprocal interpenetration of the three trinitarian persons — for Moltmann, a social and political, not just metaphysical, model.

## Connected
[[MT - Ch08 Jürgen Moltmann|Jürgen Moltmann]] · [[Modern Theologians MOC]]


### Trinity (BTh course)
In the wider Trinitarian debate, perichoresis is the technical mechanism by which social trinitarians (Moltmann, Zizioulas) ground divine unity in the mutual indwelling of co-equal persons rather than in a prior shared substance — the very move Karen Kilby's "vicious hermeneutical circle" critique targets, since perichoresis is the term onto which preferred human political ideals are most often projected before being read back out as divine norms.
