# Inspiration of Non-Christian Scriptures

Soares-Prabhu's paradigm argument (Vol 2): the OT is inspired "not in itself but inasmuch as it is illumined by the New." By the same logic, since Christ is the Word who enlightens everyone, salvation history cannot be restricted to Israel — "the inspiration of non-Christian scriptures is a distinct possibility." Grounds his intertextual reading of the Bible alongside Indian scriptures.
