# Theology of Religions

The theological account of **other faiths**: their status, their salvific value, and their relation to Christ.

Standard threefold typology — **exclusivism** (explicit confession of Christ required), **inclusivism** (Christ normative, salvation possible outside the visible Church; Rahner's *anonymous Christian*), **pluralism** (all religions valid paths; Hick's Kantian Real).

Two serious challenges to the map itself: **D'Costa** — pluralism is a disguised *exclusivist secular agnosticism*, and trinitarian engagement should replace the typology; **South Asian theology** (Wilfred, Panikkar, Pieris) — the paradigm has already moved past exclusivism and inclusivism alike: *Jesus is the Christ; Christ is not Jesus only.*
