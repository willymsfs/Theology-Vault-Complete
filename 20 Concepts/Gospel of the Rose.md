# Gospel of the Rose

Gandhi's image, taken up by Soares-Prabhu for mission in India: as a rose does not speak but spreads its fragrance, the Church's mission is the quiet, infectious witness of a holy and sharing community rather than aggressive verbal crusade. Fits India's preference for darshan (visual encounter) over verbal proclamation.
