# New Exodus in Acts

The signs and wonders of Acts (2:19; 4:30; 6:8; 14:3; 15:12) renew the signs of Egypt: the Church is leading an exodus out of the bondage of sin, the end of the exile that Daniel's seventy weeks were counting toward. Luke stages it explicitly at Acts 12 — Passover night, "move quickly," sandals on (Ex 12:11), Peter "brought out" of Herod's hand with the verb of Ex 3:8.
