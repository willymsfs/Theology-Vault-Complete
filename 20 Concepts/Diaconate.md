# Diaconate

*Diakonia*, service. Acts 6 institutes it under pressure: Greek-speaking widows are being overlooked, and the apostles refuse the false choice between the Word and the widows, ordaining seven Spirit-filled men by prayer and the laying on of hands (cf. Nm 8:10; 1 Tim 3:8-13; Phil 1:1). All seven bear Greek names — the marginalized wing is not merely served but entrusted. Vatican II restored the permanent diaconate on this warrant.
