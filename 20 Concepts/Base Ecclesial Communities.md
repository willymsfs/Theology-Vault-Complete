# Base Ecclesial Communities

*Comunidades eclesiais de base*. Small grassroots groups — mostly poor, mostly lay-led — reading scripture together and acting on it. The **social location** where Latin American liberation theology was actually produced; theology's move from the faculty to the neighbourhood.

**Leonardo Boff** gave them an ecclesiology (*ecclesiogenesis*: the Church born from the base), which is what drew Roman censure. Method inside them: **see–judge–act**.

**What happened:** since the 1990s the poor have moved in large numbers from base communities into **Pentecostal** churches — the single hardest fact for liberation theology, and the reason the movement is often described as in crisis.
