# Antigreed and Antipride

Indigenous Indian tribal values Soares-Prabhu lifts up as mirrors of Jesus's ethic. **Antigreed**: freedom from consumerism and accumulation (Mk 10:17–27 — sell, give to the poor). **Antipride**: "not self-abasement but fearless and active service" (Mk 10:35–45 — greatness as servanthood). Remedies to both consumer culture and caste hierarchy; evidence that tribal India is closer to the Gospel than the "tinsel facade" of its cities.
