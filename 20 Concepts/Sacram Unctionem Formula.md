# Sacram Unctionem Formula

The 1972 form: "Through this holy anointing may the Lord in his love and mercy help you with the grace of the Holy Spirit. May the Lord who frees you from sin save you and raise you up" — the second sentence closely tracking James 5:15's sōsei and egerei.
