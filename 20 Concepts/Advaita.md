# Advaita

"Not two" — non-dualism, [[Sankara]] (9th c.). Only [[Brahman]] is truly real; the individual soul (*atman*) is identical with it; realizing this identity is [[Moksha]]. Image: a wave realizing "I am the ocean." Christian use: a "Christian Advaita" of inward union ([[Swami Abhishiktananda]]).
