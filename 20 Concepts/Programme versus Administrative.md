# Programme versus Administrative

The single most consequential distinction in FCRA accounting, because it decides what counts against the 20% cap. Decide it **before** classifying under a Schedule category. For mixed-use buildings and staff, apportion on a documented and consistently applied basis (floor area or usage) — auditors accept a documented basis and reject an undocumented assertion.
