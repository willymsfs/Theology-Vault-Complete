# Saccidananda

*Sat* (Being) + *Chit* (Consciousness) + *Ananda* (Bliss) — a three-fold Vedantic name for ultimate Reality. [[A. J. Appasamy]] mapped it onto the **Trinity**: Sat→Father, Chit→Son/Logos, Ananda→Spirit.
