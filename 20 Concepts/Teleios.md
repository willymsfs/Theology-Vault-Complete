# Teleios

Greek, "perfect" (Mt 5:48). Soares-Prabhu insists it does not mean "without flaws" but **wholehearted, undivided** — matching "the undivided, wholeheartedness of the love of God himself" who sends rain on just and unjust alike. Mistranslating it as moral flawlessness turns the Sermon into an impossible law.
