# Parrhesia

Boldness of speech — the keynote of Acts 4 (vv. 13, 29, 31). The Sanhedrin's astonishment is diagnostic: they see "uneducated, common men" and recognize the source, "that they had been with Jesus." *Parrhesia* is therefore not temperament but derived presence. When the released apostles pray, they do not ask for safety but for boldness, and the place is shaken — a second Pentecost in miniature.
