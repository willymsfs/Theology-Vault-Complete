# Temple as Domestication of God

Soares-Prabhu on Solomon's Temple (Vol 2): built for political legitimation, it domesticated Yahweh and transformed the tribal federation into a static, purity-obsessed society. The project "effectively reverses the movement of the Mosaic revolution" — economics of equality replaced by affluence, politics of justice by forced labor and imperial oppression. Paradigm of institutionalization as the standing threat to liberative faith; background to Jesus's Temple conflict and his own critique of absolutized institutions.
