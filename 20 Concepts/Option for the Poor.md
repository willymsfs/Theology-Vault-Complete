# Option for the Poor

The defining element of biblical spirituality for Soares-Prabhu: the poor are "those on whose side God stands, and from whom God's salvation comes." The interpreter's "radical commitment to the poor" is the indispensable first step of any genuine Third World theology — before exegesis, not after.
