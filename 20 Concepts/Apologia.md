# Apologia

Reasoned defence, as in Plato's *Apology* of Socrates — not "apology" in the modern apologetic-for-wrongdoing sense, and the root of "apologetics." Acts 22–26 gives five of them before audiences of rising rank: the crowd, the Sanhedrin, Felix, Festus, Agrippa. The pedagogical point is that Paul adapts the argument to each hearer without changing the gospel — Jewish credentials to the crowd, the resurrection to the council, continuity with the fathers to Felix, citizenship to Festus.
