# Sola Scriptura

The Protestant principle — "Scripture Alone" as sole final authority, restoring "God Alone" (*Solus Deus*) over Church offices. Scripture held to have **material sufficiency** (contains all saving truth) and **formal sufficiency** (clear enough to interpret itself). Rejected by Trent: truth is "contained in the written books and unwritten traditions."
