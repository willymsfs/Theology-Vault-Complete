# Power of Jurisdiction

The canonical authority to exercise that capacity over a determinate territory or group.

## Connected
[[Ecclesiology MOC]]


### Holy Orders course (BTh)
The authority to exercise sacramental power in a given community, deriving from office rather than from ordination and therefore losable. The distinction was sharpened by the Investiture Controversy's separation of spiritual conferral from temporal investiture, and it governs every modern case from schismatic episcopal consecrations (valid orders, no jurisdiction) to laicisation. See [[Power of Orders and Jurisdiction]] and [[Holy Orders MOC]].
