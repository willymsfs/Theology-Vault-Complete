# Mortara Affair (1858)

**Edgardo Mortara**, a Jewish boy in Bologna, had been secretly baptized by a maid. On that basis papal authorities removed him from his family and he was raised in [[Pius IX]]'s own household.

**Why it belongs in a course on diplomacy**: it **outraged international opinion** and did more damage to the papacy's standing than any encyclical — a concrete case in which the logic of sacramental validity overrode the family, and in which the pope's exercise of civil jurisdiction in the [[Papal States]] became an argument against the [[Temporal Power]] itself.

**The line to hold**: the affair is where the abstract claim that the Church must be sovereign in her own territory meets a named child.
