# Ekklesia in Acts

"The called-out ones." Rare in the Gospels (Mt 16:18; 18:17) but appearing twenty-odd times in Acts, *ekklesia* is the Septuagint's word for the congregation of Israel — so Luke's usage is a claim, not a label: this assembly is Israel, reconstituted. The claim is enforced narratively in Acts 5, where lying to the community's Spirit kills, exactly as *herem* violation killed Achan (Josh 7).
