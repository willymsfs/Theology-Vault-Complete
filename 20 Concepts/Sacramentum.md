# Sacramentum

"Sacrament": sign and instrument, applied to the Church itself in LG 1.

## Connected
[[Ecclesiology MOC]]


### Sacraments course (BTh)
For a millennium sacramentum (Latin for the Greek mystērion) was used loosely — Hugh of St Victor could number around thirty sacraments. The septenary was fixed only by Peter Lombard's Sentences IV (c. 1150s) and made magisterial at Lyons II (1274) and Florence (1439). See [[Septenary]] and [[Sacraments MOC]].
