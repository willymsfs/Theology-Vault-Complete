# Emergence

The view that higher-level complex realities (life, mind, culture) have genuinely new, irreducible explanatory principles not fully captured by their lower-level physical components.

## Connected
[[MT - Ch20 Theology and the Physical Sciences|Theology and the Physical Sciences]] · [[Modern Theologians MOC]]

## Eucharistic application (Klima volume)
Contemporary philosophers (e.g. Ross McCullough) apply **transformational emergence** to transubstantiation: as hydrogen and oxygen bond into water, losing their separate identity while the resulting molecule gains genuinely new properties, so the bread's underlying matter is "taken up" into Christ's sacramental presence and reorganised under a new principle — the accidents are not floating in a vacuum but inhere, in a transformed way, in Christ. A realist alternative to Ockham's "unexplained miracle" and to nominalist deflation alike.
