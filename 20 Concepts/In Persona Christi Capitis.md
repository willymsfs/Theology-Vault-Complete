# In Persona Christi Capitis

"In the person of Christ the Head" — the concept explaining both how the ordained minister's sacramental action is Christ's own action and how the ministerial priesthood participates in Christ's unique priesthood without adding to it; restricted by Omnium in Mentem (2009) to bishops and priests.
