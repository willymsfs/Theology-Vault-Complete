# Infallibility

The Spirit-guaranteed preservation from error in matters of faith. Exercised by: the **whole body of the faithful** ("cannot be mistaken in belief," [[Lumen Gentium]] 12); the **college of bishops** (council, or dispersed but agreeing); and the **Pope** speaking *ex cathedra* ("from the chair" of Peter).
