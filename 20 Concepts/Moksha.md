# Moksha

Liberation — release (*muc*, "to release") from the cycle of birth-death (*samsara*), from [[Karma]], ignorance, and suffering. Not escape from the world but freedom from attachment while in it. Schools differ: [[Advaita]] = merging atman into [[Brahman]]; [[Bhakti]] = eternal loving communion with a personal God.
