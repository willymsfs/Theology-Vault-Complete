# Karma

"Action" and its consequences — the natural moral law by which past actions shape the present and present actions the future. In [[Advaita]], the soul is "the result of one's own karma," veiled by conditioning and ignorance until [[Jnana]] dawns. Image: planting apple seeds yields apple trees.
