# Integral Indian Reading

Soares-Prabhu's signature hermeneutic: the convergence of two complementary readings of scripture. **Religious reading** — traditional Indian exegesis (dhvani, intertext with Indian scriptures): first "assimilate my own cultural and religious past by a reading of the Indian scriptures," then re-read the Gospel. **Social reading** — the text "in the light of a liberating praxis among the socially oppressed." Converging, they disclose "the liberation announced by Jesus in its totality" — freedom from social oppression *and* from personal bondage (moksha). Historical criticism retained beneath both as "control" against eisegesis.
