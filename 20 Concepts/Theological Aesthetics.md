# Theological Aesthetics

Theology done through **beauty and form**: the claim that perception of the self-disclosing, enrapturing *form* of God's revelation comes **before** critical analysis, and that beauty is a transcendental alongside the true and the good.

**Balthasar** is the architect (*The Glory of the Lord*): the *Gestalt Christi* is the form in which every other worldly form finds its centre and judgment, always under the rule of *maior dissimilitudo*. Downstream: Begbie on music as theology (polyphony as a non-competitive model of the hypostatic union), de Gruchy and Dillenberger on the visual arts, Marion on icon versus idol.

**Criticism:** an aesthetic that intuits harmony too readily can side-step the real contradictions of history.
