# Hiereus and Presbyteros

The New Testament's decisive vocabulary distinction: hiereus (priest, sacerdos) is used of Jewish and pagan priests, of Christ, and of all the baptised — but never of a Christian minister, who is called presbyteros, episkopos, or diakonos. The sacerdotal vocabulary for ministers is a post-biblical development.
