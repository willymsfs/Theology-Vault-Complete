# Horizon

The structured boundary or perspective within which meaning, knowing and choosing occur. What lies beyond a horizon is not merely unknown but *unaskable* from within it.

Two consequences for method: (1) there is no view from nowhere — the "illusion of neutrality" is itself a horizon; (2) shifting a horizon radically is not argument but [[Conversion]]. Dialectics, the fourth functional specialty, exists precisely to expose conflicting horizons rather than to settle surface disagreements.
