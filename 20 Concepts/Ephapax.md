# Ephapax

"Once for all" (Heb 7:27; 9:12; 10:10) — the assertion of the uniqueness and non-repeatability of Christ's sacrifice, the basis of the Reformation objection to a sacrificing priesthood and the problem any theology of ministerial priesthood must answer.
