# Apostolic Succession

The continuity of the apostolic mission, faith, and communion, of which the succession of bishops is the sacramental sign; seeded in 1 Clement 44 and reframed ecumenically by BEM as succession in the apostolic tradition rather than a merely mechanical chain.
