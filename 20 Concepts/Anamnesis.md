# Anamnesis (Greek: ἀνάμνησις) — "memorial"

The biblical concept used in BEM to resolve eucharistic disputes: the Eucharist is not a repetition of Christ's sacrifice but a dynamic, present realization of its benefits.

## Connected
[[MT - Ch32 Ecumenical Theology|Ecumenical Theology]] · [[Modern Theologians MOC]]


### Eucharist course (BTh)
In Eucharistic theology anamnesis translates the Hebrew zikkaron and denotes an active memorial that makes a past saving event present and effective for the current generation — not mere recollection. This is the pivot of the Catholic theology of Eucharistic sacrifice (the Mass re-presents rather than repeats Calvary) and, through Max Thurian and the ARCIC and Lima texts, the single concept that unlocked twentieth-century Eucharistic ecumenism. See [[Zikkaron]] and [[Eucharist MOC]].
