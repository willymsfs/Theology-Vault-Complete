# Traditio Instrumentorum

"The handing over of the instruments" — the medieval Western rite of presenting chalice and paten to the ordinand, declared by Florence (1439) to be the matter of the sacrament and definitively removed from the essential rite by Pius XII's Sacramentum Ordinis (1947).
