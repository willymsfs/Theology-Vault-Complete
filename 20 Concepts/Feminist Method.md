# Feminist Method

Theology as a **political and ethical act**, run in three moves: a **hermeneutic of suspicion** turned on exclusively male God-language as idolatry; **retrieval** of suppressed female voices in scripture and tradition; **gender analysis** exposing the binaries (man/spirit vs woman/body/nature) that license domination.

Key terms: **patriarchy**; **kyriarchy** (Schüssler Fiorenza — intersecting systems of domination, gender never the sole axis); ***ekklesia* of wo/men**; revaluation of the body.

**Internal debate:** do female images of God and female identification with the body break the binaries or reinforce them (Irigaray's problem)?
