# Conversion

In Lonergan, a **radical vertical shift from one horizon to another** that transforms experiencing, understanding, judging and deciding. Four modalities: **religious, moral, intellectual** and (with Doran) **psychic**.

Methodologically decisive: conversion is the pivot between the positive and normative phases of the [[Functional Specialty|functional specialties]]. Control of theology lies not in logic alone but in the converted subject — which is what blocks arbitrary construction and safeguards fidelity.

## Claim
- [[Conversion, not logic, is what makes theology faithful]]
