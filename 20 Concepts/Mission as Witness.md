# Mission as Witness

Soares-Prabhu dismantles conquest-mission: "There is no uniform, unchanging, universal understanding of mission." Mission = "making God's love present in whatever way it can" — "concerned not with the power, security or growth of the missionizing Churches, but with the welfare of the missionized world." In India: "not so much doing as being; not so much noisy proclamation as infectious witness." The Church is "the symbol and the servant of God's Reign," not its own end. Against "colonial Christianity" — "aggressive mission carried out under the patronage of the avaricious and ruthless colonial powers."
