# Transignification

The 1960s proposal (Schillebeeckx, Schoonenberg) that Christ's giving of a radically new meaning to the bread constitutes a real change in its deepest reality for persons; judged by Mysterium Fidei to be inadequate as a substitute for ontological conversion.
