# Prophet as Theologian

Soares-Prabhu's model for doing theology: like the biblical prophets, rooted in a "concrete historical situation," not abstract academic exegesis. A two-fold protest: **religious critique** — condemning idolatry, "the absolutization of things like money, kingship and temple," God turned into "the legitimizing principle of absolutized values... or of absolutized institutions like the Temple or the state"; **social critique** — attacking "concrete expressions of exploitation and oppression." Theology begins with social analysis and must end in social change.
