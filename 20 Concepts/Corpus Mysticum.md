# Corpus Mysticum (Latin) — "mystical body"

De Lubac's historical retrieval showing the phrase originally named the Eucharist, only later coming to name the Church — evidence of their original, organic sacramental unity.

## Connected
[[MT - Ch04 Henri de Lubac|Henri de Lubac]] · [[Modern Theologians MOC]]


### Eucharist course (BTh)
Henri de Lubac's Corpus Mysticum (1944) shows that before the twelfth century corpus verum named the Church and corpus mysticum named the Eucharist, and that the Berengarian controversy caused the terms to exchange referents. The cost, on his reading, was that the Eucharist ceased to be primarily the sacrament of the Church's unity and became an object of individual devotion, while the Church became an abstract "mystical" reality — a drift his formula "the Eucharist makes the Church" was designed to reverse.
