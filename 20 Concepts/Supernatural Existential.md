# Supernatural Existential

Rahner's term bridging **nature and grace**. Grace is not "an alien force dropped from above" but is *already present* in human questioning, exploring, and the natural desire for the Infinite. So the Incarnation is "the ultimate historical answer to the innate human search for an 'absolute saviour'."

- [[Grace is not dropped from above but already present in human questioning]]
