# Analogical Knowledge

The middle path by which human words truly apply to God — neither *univocally* (same meaning as for creatures) nor *equivocally* (wholly different), but *analogically* (similar-but-different). Words like "good" or "father" point to God truly, yet always fall short.

| Way | Meaning | Problem |
|-----|---------|---------|
| Univocal | same as for creatures | God = a bigger creature |
| Equivocal | totally different | we say nothing real |
| **Analogical** | similar-but-different | the correct path |

## Key quotes
> "Our human words always fall short of the mystery of God."
> We must "continually purify our language."
> — Pathil & Veliath, ch. 1
