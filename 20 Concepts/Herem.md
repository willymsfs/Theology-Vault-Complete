# Herem

The ban — total devotion of spoil to God, as at Jericho. Achan's theft from the *herem* brought death on the community (Josh 7), and Luke writes Ananias and Sapphira over that template: the gift is voluntary, the lie is not survivable, and the lesson is that in a body there is no private sin. Peter names the offence precisely — lying not to men but to the Holy Spirit, i.e. to a divine Person (so Constantinople, AD 381).
