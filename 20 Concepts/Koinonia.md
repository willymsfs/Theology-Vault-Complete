# Koinonia (Greek) — "communion / fellowship"

Vatican II's central ecclesiological paradigm (elaborated by Yves Congar), shifting the Church's self-understanding from monarchical hierarchy to sacramental communion of local churches.

## Connected
[[MT - Ch16 Roman Catholic Theology after Vatican II|Roman Catholic Theology after Vatican II]] · [[Modern Theologians MOC]]


### Eucharist course (BTh)
1 Cor 10:16-17 makes koinonia the specifically Eucharistic term: "The cup of blessing... is it not a koinonia in the blood of Christ?... Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body." Communion with Christ and communion with one another are a single reality, which is why Paul's rebuke about "discerning the body" (11:29) is simultaneously sacramental and social. See [[Discerning the Body]] and [[Eucharist MOC]].
