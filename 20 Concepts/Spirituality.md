# Spirituality

The **transforming encounter with God** — for most of Christian history indissoluble from theology. Evagrius: *the theologian is the one who prays*.

**The divorce, in two stages:** university scholasticism moved theology from contemplative biblical encounter to abstract propositions; the modern turn to the subject reduced spirituality to interior psychological states cut off from trinitarian and historical realities. Result: doctrine without prayer, prayer without doctrine.

**The reunion**, urged by the *nouvelle théologie*: **Stolz** — mysticism is sacramental and trinitarian, grounded in the missions of Son and Spirit, not an elite psychological state; **Weil** — attention as prayer; **Balthasar** — the life of the saints is theology in practice. The epistemological price: theological truth is **participation, not mastery of facts**.
