# Revelation

God's free self-disclosure, reaching out to humans as invitation and promise to share God's own life, mediated through persons, nature, history, and — ultimately — through the Word, Jesus Christ.

## Key quote
> "God's gracious self-disclosure reaching out to humans as an invitation, as well as promise, to participate in God's own life of unfathomable love... in and through God's very Word, Jesus Christ."
> — Pathil & Veliath, ch. 2
