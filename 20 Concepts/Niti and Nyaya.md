# Niti and Nyaya

James Ponniah's distinction, naming why formal synodal reform and unchanged lived practice are not
felt as contradictory by the institutions producing both. **Niti** is the formal, institutional
declaration of synodality — councils established, documents issued, consultations run. **Nyaya** is
the actualised, lived experience of justice on the ground — who is actually heard, who actually
decides. A diocese can accumulate an exemplary *niti* record while *nyaya* remains untouched, because
the two are answering different questions; producing the first does not obligate the second.
