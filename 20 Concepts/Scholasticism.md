# Scholasticism

University theology (c. 800–1500) driven by "lectures and disputations," integrating Aristotelian philosophy with revelation. Four sub-periods: Pre (800–1050), Early (1050–1200), High (1200–1350), Late (1350–1500).

## Key figures
[[Anselm]] · [[Aquinas]] · [[Bonaventure]] · [[Duns Scotus]] · [[Ockham]]

## Claims
- [[The Aristotelian turn moved theology from the plane of action to the plane of being]]
- [[Ockham's nominalism dissolved the synthesis of faith and reason]]
