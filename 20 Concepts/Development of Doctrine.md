# Development of Doctrine

The organic unfolding of the deposit of faith — the implicit made explicit, like a seed becoming a tree: same life, fuller form. Nothing brand-new is invented.

## Example
The full doctrine of the Trinity took centuries to state clearly, yet was already implicit in the New Testament.

## Claim
- [[Doctrine develops by making the implicit explicit, never by adding anew]]
