# Threefold Ministry

Bishop, priest (presbyter), deacon — the office-structure fixed by the early second century.

## Connected
[[Ecclesiology MOC]]


### Holy Orders course (BTh)
The crystallisation of bishop, presbyter, and deacon as three distinct offices is a genuine post-apostolic development: Acts 20:17 and 20:28 and Titus 1:5-7 use presbyteros and episkopos of the same men, and the first unambiguous evidence of a single bishop over presbyters and deacons is Ignatius of Antioch (c. 107), who urges rather than merely describes the pattern. The Apostolic Tradition (c. 215) preserves the earliest distinct ordination prayers for all three. See [[Monepiscopate]] and [[Holy Orders MOC]].
