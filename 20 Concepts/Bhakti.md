# Bhakti

The path of loving personal devotion to a **personal** God — "a personal deity worthy of human devotion, praise and love" ([[Ramanuja]]). Warm, experiential, inclusive. Christian use: [[A. J. Appasamy]] read the Trinity through Bhakti, keeping "our separate personalities in our experience of union with God."

## Contrast
[[Advaita]] (impersonal Brahman) vs Bhakti (personal God).
