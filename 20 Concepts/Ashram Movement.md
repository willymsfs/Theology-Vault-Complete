# Ashram Movement

Christian ashrams — simple community life centred on prayer and discipline (from the Hindu tradition), integrating [[Nishkama Karma]], [[Jnana]], and [[Bhakti]] into a "spiritual-contemplative approach." Vital meeting points for [[Dialogue of Life]]. Pioneers include [[Swami Abhishiktananda]], [[Vandana Mataji]], [[Sara Grant]].

## Soares-Prabhu
His sharpest critique: ashram spirituality is "a brahmanical spirituality, the spirituality of an affluent, privileged class... a mockery for the five hundred millions of our people who subsist below, on, or just above the poverty line." One pole of his [[Double-Distancing]]. See [[Lit - Soares-Prabhu Theological Glossary]].
