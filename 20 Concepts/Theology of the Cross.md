# Theology of the Cross

Luther's *theologia crucis*, set against the *theologia gloriae*: **God is known only in the crucified Christ**, and therefore precisely where human reason least expects him — in weakness, suffering, hiddenness. Natural theology and autonomous reason ("the Devil's whore") breed pride and idolatry.

Methodologically it is a rule about the **source** of the knowledge of God, not only a doctrine of atonement: it forbids reasoning from creation upward to God's glory.

**Asian resonance:** Kitamori's theology of the pain of God makes a neighbouring claim in another idiom — a God incapable of suffering cannot bear the sins of the world.
