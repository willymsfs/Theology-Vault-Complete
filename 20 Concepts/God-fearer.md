# God-fearer

A Gentile adherent of the synagogue — attracted to Israel's God, instructed, praying, almsgiving, but not a covenant member (Acts 13:16 distinguishes them from Israelites). Cornelius is the type. The category matters theologically because it exposes the limit of sincerity: Cornelius is already devout, and still needs Jesus — his prayers are heard precisely so that Peter may be sent.
