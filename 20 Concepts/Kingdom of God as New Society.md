# Kingdom of God as New Society

For Soares-Prabhu the basileia is "God's definitive liberative intervention in human and cosmic history" — not heaven-after-death, not the Church. It is realized "whenever humans become free internally and externally, when there is fellowship of concern among humans and when they strive for a just society." Three dimensions — **personal freedom, communitarian fellowship, structural justice** — are the "parameters by which the arrival or the delay of God's reign is to be gauged."
