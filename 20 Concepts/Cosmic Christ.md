# Cosmic Christ

Christ as the principle, coherence and end of **the whole created order**, not only of human salvation history. Scriptural base: Col 1:15–20, Eph 1:10, John 1.

Modern carriers: **Teilhard de Chardin** — evolution converging on the **Omega Point**, the "Christification of the universe"; **K. H. Ting** — a cosmic Christ whose love embraces even atheistic communists, in a Chinese socialist setting; **ecological theology** — the ground for treating creation as internal to redemption rather than its backdrop; **Panikkar** — the Christ who is not a Christian monopoly.

**Risk:** a cosmic Christ detached from the crucified Jew of Nazareth becomes a principle rather than a person.
