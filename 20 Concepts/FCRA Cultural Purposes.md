# FCRA Cultural Purposes

The **Cultural** category of the FCRA Schedule (Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)) — 18 approved purposes.

| ID | Approved purpose |
|---|---|
| **CUL-01** | Archaeological exploration, excavation, conservation, and restoration of monuments, heritage sites, forts, and palaces |
| **CUL-02** | Establishment and maintenance of museums, archives, libraries, cultural interpretation centres, and heritage walks |
| **CUL-03** | Documentation, preservation, and promotion of folk songs, dances, oral epics, storytelling, and puppetry |
| **CUL-04** | Support for classical performing arts (Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Carnatic, Hindustani, Yakshagana) |
| **CUL-05** | Promotion of contemporary arts inspired by Indian traditions (excluding political/ideological content) |
| **CUL-06** | Training and capacity-building for artisans, craftspersons, handloom weavers, potters, woodworkers, metal workers |
| **CUL-07** | Preservation of non-religious manuscripts, palm-leaf texts, paintings, frescoes, and rare cultural artefacts |
| **CUL-08** | Promotion and preservation of regional languages, dialects, and scripts, including translation projects |
| **CUL-09** | Establishment of literary academies, support to writers, poets, translators, book fairs |
| **CUL-10** | Support to tribal and indigenous cultural heritage — festivals, crafts, oral traditions, languages, customs (excluding religious rituals and worship) |
| **CUL-11** | Organisation of national and international cultural festivals, fairs, and exhibitions |
| **CUL-12** | Development of craft villages, heritage circuits, art hubs, and cultural tourism facilities |
| **CUL-13** | Promotion of traditional culinary practices, traditional sports, food festivals, and culinary heritage preservation |
| **CUL-14** | Research in cultural anthropology, ethnography, and folk studies |
| **CUL-15** | Archiving and digitisation of films, radio programmes, theatre scripts, and photographs |
| **CUL-16** | Documentation and preservation of traditional knowledge systems as intangible cultural heritage |
| **CUL-17** | Preservation of scientific and knowledge heritage (astronomy, mathematics, metallurgy manuscripts, traditional technologies) |
| **CUL-18** | Documentation and archiving of modern historical movements and oral histories of national importance |
