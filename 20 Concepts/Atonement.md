# Atonement

How the cross saves. The classic families: **recapitulation** (Irenaeus), **satisfaction** (Anselm), **penal substitution** (Reformation, and normative for evangelical theology), **moral influence** (Abelard), ***Christus Victor*** (Aulén's recovery of the patristic pattern).

The twentieth century put the question under new pressure. **Feminist and womanist theology** — Delores Williams's surrogacy critique — asks whether a substitutionary logic sacralizes coerced suffering. **Liberation theology** shifts from the transaction to the *crucified people*. **Moltmann and Jüngel** relocate the cross inside the doctrine of God rather than treating it as a transaction outside it.
