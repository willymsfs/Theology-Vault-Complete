# Dharma of Jesus

Soares-Prabhu's redefinition of dharma for Christology: "the complex of religious insight and ethical concern (of experience, world-view and value) which determines the life-style that Jesus proclaimed and practised" — a "dharma of unconditional and therefore of absolutely universal love." The Sermon on the Mount read as dharma, not law: as juridical code it is "thoroughly utopian"; as dharma it "proclaims freedom from the burden of the law," grounded in the Abba experience.

### The three sutras (added from *The Dharma of Jesus*, ed. D'Sa)
Soares-Prabhu interprets the Sermon through three aphorisms: **not law but love** (fulfilled by interiorization and simplification; *teleios* = wholehearted, not flawless) · **not law but gospel** (consequence of being loved, not condition for favour) · **a goal-directed norm** (the direction Christian life is moving; fruits, not works). He also distinguishes the dharma Jesus *practised* from the dharma he *preached*. See [[The Three Sutras]] · [[Svadharma]] · [[Teleios]] · [[Soares-Prabhu MOC]].
