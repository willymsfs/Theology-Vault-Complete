# Porneia Exception

The Matthean clause "except for porneia" (Mt 5:32; 19:9), variously read as referring to unlawful unions within forbidden degrees (the Catholic reading), to adultery permitting remarriage (Orthodox and Protestant), to separation without remarriage, or to betrothal infidelity.
