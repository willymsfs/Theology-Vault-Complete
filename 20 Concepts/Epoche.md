# Epoché

Phenomenological **suspension of judgement**: accept religious phenomena as they present themselves to the believer, without pronouncing on their absolute truth. Followed by **eidetic vision** — the search for the underlying essence or structure.

The entry move of comparative and interreligious method: listen first, judge later, and build an impartial terminology that does not beg the question (Burtt's "value" in place of "good/bad").
