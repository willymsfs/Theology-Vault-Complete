# Iconoclasm as Dark Side of Monotheism

Soares-Prabhu's Indian-mind reading of Isa 44:9–20: the anti-idol satire is a "damaging and therefore sinful misrepresentation of a people's religion and culture." Idolaters do not worship wood but the divine that comes to "dwell" in the image — as "bread and wine in the Christian Eucharist mediate the 'real presence' of Jesus." The Bible's "fanatical iconoclasm" is the "dark side of its monotheism," historically issuing in "devastating destruction of peoples and cultures." Rare case of Soares-Prabhu critiquing the biblical text itself from Indian sensibility.
