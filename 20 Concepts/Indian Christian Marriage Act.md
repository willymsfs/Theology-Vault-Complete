# Indian Christian Marriage Act

The 1872 Act governing the solemnisation of Christian marriages in India; with the Indian Divorce Act 1869 (substantially amended 2001) it forms the civil framework within which Indian Catholic marriage operates — a civil decree having no effect on the canonical bond.
