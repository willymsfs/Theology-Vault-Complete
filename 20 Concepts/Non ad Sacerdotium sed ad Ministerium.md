# Non ad Sacerdotium sed ad Ministerium

"Not unto the priesthood but unto the ministry" — LG 29's formula for the diaconate, echoing the Apostolic Tradition's statement that the deacon is ordained to the bishop's service, and re-inscribed canonically by Omnium in Mentem.
