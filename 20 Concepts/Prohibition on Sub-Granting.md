# Prohibition on Sub-Granting

Section 7, as amended in 2020, **absolutely prohibits transferring foreign contribution to any other person or organisation**, whether or not the recipient holds FCRA registration. There is no exception for partners, dioceses, congregations, or bodies within the same family. Penalty: 10% of the transferred amount or ₹1,00,000, whichever higher — compoundable **only once**.
