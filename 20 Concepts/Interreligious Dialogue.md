# Interreligious Dialogue

The practice, as distinct from the theory in [[Theology of Religions]].

Four recognized forms: the **dialogue of life** (shared neighbourhood existence — the Asian starting point), of **action** (common struggle for justice), of **theological exchange**, and of **religious experience**. **Pieris** adds the decisive qualification: **core-to-core** dialogue — Buddhist *gnosis* meeting Christian *agape* at the centre of each tradition, not doctrine traded at the edges. **Knitter** adds the political one: conceptual dialogue is elitist unless rooted in a preferential option for the poor. **Ford's** eleventh thesis makes conversation around scriptures — Scriptural Reasoning — the heart of the whole enterprise.
