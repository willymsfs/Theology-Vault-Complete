# Contrast Community

Soares-Prabhu's ecclesiology-in-miniature: a community showing "it is possible to live fruitful and fulfilled lives based on values which are the opposite of those proposed by the power and possession hungry consumer society in which we live" — where "all alienation is at an end and men and women live together as children of the one Father in heaven in freedom, fellowship and justice." The original Jesus movement (radically free, egalitarian, serving) is its archetype.
