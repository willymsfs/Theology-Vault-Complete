# God

The Ultimate Reality, Ultimate Ground of Being, and Ultimate Horizon of Meaning — conceived personally (Christianity, Judaism, Islam) or impersonally (Absolute, Pure Act) across traditions. The "God-Question" is the root of all theology: the human being is by nature a questioner reaching beyond the visible.

## Can God be known by reason?
Yes, in principle — [[Dei Filius]] (Vatican I): God "can be known with certainty from the created world by the natural light of human reason." But:
- [[Aquinas's proofs convince believers, not sceptics]]
- [[Our God-language is analogical — it points but never captures]]
