# Redemptive Suffering

Persecution in Acts does not slow the gospel; it distributes it — the scattering after Stephen becomes the sowing of Acts 8:4. Saul is told at his conversion how much he must suffer for the Name (9:16), and beaten apostles leave the council rejoicing to be counted worthy of dishonour. John Paul II's *Salvifici Doloris* 27 gives the doctrine: suffering shares in the treasure of the Redemption; Col 1:24 makes room for our participation.
