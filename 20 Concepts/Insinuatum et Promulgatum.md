# Insinuatum et Promulgatum

Trent's careful pair of verbs: the sacrament was "insinuated" (hinted at, prepared) by Christ in Mark 6:13 and "promulgated" by James 5:14-15 — a formulation that stops short of claiming Mark 6:13 as the institution.
