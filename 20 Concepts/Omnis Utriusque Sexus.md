# Omnis Utriusque Sexus

Lateran IV's canon 21 (1215) requiring annual confession and Easter communion of all the faithful — legislated precisely because actual reception had become so rare.

[[Eucharist MOC]]


### Penance course (BTh)
The same canon 21 of Lateran IV (1215) that requires Easter communion also imposes the obligation of **annual confession** to one's own priest, and legislates the **seal** in the strongest terms: the priest is to be "discreet and cautious, so that in the manner of a skilled physician he may pour wine and oil on the wounds of the injured," and any priest who betrays a sin revealed in confession is to be deposed and confined to a monastery for perpetual penance. The seal is thus as old as the universal obligation itself. See [[Sigillum Confessionis]] and [[Penance MOC]].
