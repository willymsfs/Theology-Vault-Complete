# Character Dominicus

Augustine's term for the indelible "brand" or military tattoo conferred by baptism, which explains why baptism remains unrepeatable even when unfruitful; formalized by scholasticism as the res et sacramentum.

## Connected

### Holy Orders course (BTh)
Augustine's soldier's-brand analogy applies to orders as directly as to baptism: a deserter still bears the mark, which is precisely why he can be punished as a deserter rather than as a civilian. Applied to ordination this yields three permanent consequences — re-ordination is impossible rather than merely forbidden, an unworthy minister ordains validly, and orders can be valid but illicit.
