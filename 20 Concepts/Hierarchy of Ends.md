# Hierarchy of Ends

The classical scheme, codified at 1917 CIC c. 1013 §1, ranking the procreation and education of offspring as the primary end of marriage and mutual help and the remedy for concupiscence as secondary; deliberately not repeated by Gaudium et Spes.
