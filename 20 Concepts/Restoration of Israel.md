# Restoration of Israel

Paul tells Agrippa he stands trial for "the hope of the restoration of Israel" (Acts 26) — the reunion of the twelve tribes, ten of which have been mixed among the nations since 722 BC. This reframes the Gentile mission: going to the nations *is* the ingathering of Israel (Jer 31:1, 9-10, 31-33; Ez 36:22-25; 37:16-22), and Rom 11 adds that it stirs Israel to jealousy. Restoration is possible only through the Resurrection — hence the resurrection is the real issue in every trial.
