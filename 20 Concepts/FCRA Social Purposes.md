# FCRA Social Purposes

The **Social** category of the FCRA Schedule (Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)) — 30 approved purposes.

| ID | Approved purpose |
|---|---|
| **SOC-01** | Establishment and operation of hospitals, community health centres, dispensaries, mobile health clinics, ambulance services, patient transport, emergency medical response |
| **SOC-02** | Preventive healthcare: vaccination drives, health awareness camps, vector-borne disease control |
| **SOC-03** | Maternal and child healthcare, maternity homes, neonatal care, nutrition supplementation, community-based management of malnutrition |
| **SOC-04** | Nutrition and food security programmes: midday meals, community kitchens, feeding programmes, grain banks, ration support, hunger relief |
| **SOC-05** | Drinking water supply projects, community RO plants, piped water schemes |
| **SOC-06** | Sanitation: construction of toilets, hygiene awareness, menstrual health initiatives |
| **SOC-07** | Orphanages, foster care, adoption support, and child protection homes |
| **SOC-08** | Old-age homes, day-care centres, geriatric clinics, pensions facilitation |
| **SOC-09** | Disability care: assistive devices, rehabilitation, therapy, vocational support, caregiver support, community inclusion |
| **SOC-10** | Rehabilitation centres for drug addicts, alcoholics, and substance abusers |
| **SOC-11** | Mental health counselling centres, suicide prevention helplines, community psychiatry |
| **SOC-12** | Women's safety and empowerment: shelters, legal aid, self-defence training |
| **SOC-13** | Anti-trafficking programmes: rescue, rehabilitation, vocational reintegration |
| **SOC-14** | Homeless shelters, night shelters, low-cost housing, rehabilitation facilities for homeless, destitute, abandoned, and vulnerable persons |
| **SOC-15** | Legal aid clinics, paralegal training, access-to-justice programmes |
| **SOC-16** | Disaster relief, emergency response, and post-disaster rehabilitation (floods, earthquakes, cyclones, pandemics) |
| **SOC-17** | Provision of basic civic and community infrastructure — electrification, rural roads, solar lighting, community halls — strictly non-commercial |
| **SOC-18** | Community-based environmental protection and restoration (afforestation, biodiversity parks, mangrove projects) |
| **SOC-19** | Water harvesting and watershed management for community welfare and drinking water security |
| **SOC-20** | Waste management at community level (solid waste, e-waste, plastic-free campaigns, recycling units) |
| **SOC-21** | Road safety: awareness, trauma-care centres, first-aid training |
| **SOC-22** | Operation of community radio stations, helplines, and awareness platforms, subject to broadcasting/telecom/IT regulations |
| **SOC-23** | Rehabilitation and reintegration of released prisoners |
| **SOC-24** | Support to victims of domestic violence, dowry harassment, and acid attacks |
| **SOC-25** | Community-level sports and recreation facilities for underprivileged children and youth (non-institutional) |
| **SOC-26** | Animal protection, rescue and rehabilitation |
| **SOC-27** | Public health research: disease mapping, epidemiology, health data systems |
| **SOC-28** | Palliative care and hospice services for terminally ill patients |
| **SOC-29** | Community blood banks, eye banks, and organ donation awareness |
| **SOC-30** | Pre-disaster risk reduction, mitigation, preparedness, and resilience — flood control works, cyclone shelters, early-warning, community drills, emergency response teams |
