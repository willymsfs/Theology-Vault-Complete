# The Consultative-Majoritarian Double Bind

Synodality's structural problem stated as a single bind rather than two separate complaints. On one
side, [[The Consultative Vote (Canon Law)|canon law]] keeps the laity's voice non-binding, which
produces disillusionment and withdrawal. On the other, [[Vox Populi Vox Dei - The Majoritarian Risk|unconverted majoritarianism]] risks replacing clerical capture with faction capture. Neither
"give the laity a vote" nor "keep the cleric's veto" solves the problem alone — the [[Omnes Aliqui Unus]] schema and [[Conversation in the Spirit|Conversation in the Spirit's]] emphasis on discernment
over counting are the tradition's attempt to escape the bind rather than pick a side of it.
