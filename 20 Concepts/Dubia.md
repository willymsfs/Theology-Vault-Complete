# Dubia

The five questions submitted in 2016 by Cardinals Brandmüller, Burke, Caffarra, and Meisner seeking a yes-or-no clarification of Amoris Laetitia, principally on access to the sacraments and on intrinsically evil acts; no formal reply was given.
