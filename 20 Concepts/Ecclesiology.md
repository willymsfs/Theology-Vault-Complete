# Ecclesiology

The doctrine of the **Church**: what it is, who constitutes it, how it is ordered, and what it is for.

The modern field runs along five lines: **Bonhoeffer** — Christ existing as community, analysed with sociological tools; **Vatican II / de Lubac** — the Church as mystery and People of God, recovered from patristic sources; **Zizioulas** — being as communion, the Church as eucharistic event; **Jenson** — the sacramental church as "the risen Christ's Ego"; **Leonardo Boff** — ecclesiogenesis, the Church born from the base, with the institution under judgment. Ecumenical work adds *koinonia* as the shared framework and BEM as its test case.
