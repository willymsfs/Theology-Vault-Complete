# Historical-Critical Method

Interpreting the biblical text **free of imposed theological categories**, attending to its historical particularity — author, date, sources, forms, redaction, original setting. Pioneered by Semler and Michaelis out of the Enlightenment.

Its gains are permanent; its limits are the modern argument. **Barth** subordinated it to the Bible as witness to God's grace; **Bultmann** ran it into existential interpretation; **Childs** answered with **canonical criticism** (the final form is the authoritative object); **Frei** exposed what it cost the narrative; **postcolonial critics** unmasked the Eurocentric ideology behind its claim to neutrality; Indian exegesis (Soares-Prabhu) calls it *necessary but not sufficient*.
