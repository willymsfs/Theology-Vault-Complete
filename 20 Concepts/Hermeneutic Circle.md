# Hermeneutic Circle

Via Segundo: interpretation "always presupposes a profound human commitment," proceeding from the interpreter's life-experience to the text and back. **Pre-understanding**: the sociological and ideological commitment every reader brings, consciously or not — "no such thing as an autonomous, impartial, academic theology floating free above the realm of human options and biases." **Ideological suspicion**: the circle's "negative pole," questioning accepted views of reality and forcing fresh readings. Historical criticism stays inside the circle as "control": recovering "what the original author intended to say" and preventing eisegesis — the "fanciful flights" of reading one's biases into the text.
