# Vestigia Trinitatis (Trinity)

"Traces of the Trinity" found in creation — e.g. Hugh of St Victor's Immensity/Beauty/Utility triad — read as reflections of the triune Creator; Panikkar's cosmotheandric model is criticized by Gavin D'Costa for inverting this tradition.
