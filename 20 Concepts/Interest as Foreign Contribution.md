# Interest as Foreign Contribution

Explanation 2 to Section 2(1)(h): interest accrued on foreign contribution, and any income derived from FC or from that interest, **is itself foreign contribution**. It must be credited to the designated FC account and utilised only for approved purposes. A common and expensive oversight.
