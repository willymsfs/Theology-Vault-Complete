# Agraphon

A saying of Jesus preserved outside the Gospels. The classic instance is Paul's at Miletus: "It is more blessed to give than to receive" (Acts 20:35), quoted as dominical though no Gospel records it — a small, hard piece of evidence that the apostolic churches carried a living oral tradition alongside the written texts.
