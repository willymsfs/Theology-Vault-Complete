# Salvation as Integral Liberation

Salvation is "eschatological but not other-worldly": it comes from God but "is actualized in and through the struggles of the poor." Integral = both poles at once — liberation from social, economic, structural oppression *and* freedom from personal bondage (greed, illusion, anxiety — moksha). Soares-Prabhu's correction to both Latin American liberation theology (blind to religiosity) and traditional Indian spirituality (blind to structures).
