# Vox Populi Vox Dei: The Majoritarian Risk

The standing caution against collapsing synodality into secular democracy. Francis Thonippara: "the
voice of the people" does not automatically equal "the voice of God," because parish and diocesan
elections are frequently marred by groupism, ethnic lobbying and power struggles — evidence that
vote-counting alone does not guarantee the Spirit is being heard. This is the reciprocal danger to
[[The Consultative Vote (Canon Law)|the consultative trap]]: where the consultative trap under-empowers
the laity, majoritarianism over-trusts an unconverted electorate. Genuine spiritual conversion, not
procedure alone, is what synodal consensus requires — see [[Conversation in the Spirit]].
