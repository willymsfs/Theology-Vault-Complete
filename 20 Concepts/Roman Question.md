# Roman Question

The long dispute over the **[[Papal States]] and the pope's temporal sovereignty**: whether the pope must rule a territory in order to be independent of every other ruler.

**Sequence** — restored at Vienna (1815) → eroded by the Carbonari, 1848 and the Risorgimento → by **1861 the Kingdom of Italy leaves the pope only Rome and the Patrimony of Peter** → **1870 Rome falls**, the pope becomes "the prisoner of the Vatican" → **1929 Lateran settlement**, prepared by Italy's 1906 payment of 9 million lire.

It is not only an Italian matter: it shaped papal reading of **every** foreign crisis. Alvarez shows Pius IX and Antonelli assessing the American Civil War through it — a strong United States would check Britain, an antagonist of the Papal States.
