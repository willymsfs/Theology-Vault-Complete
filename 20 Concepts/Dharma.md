# Dharma

From *dhr*, "to hold/support" — cosmic order, moral law, and sacred duty. Each person has *svadharma* (own duty by role/stage). The beautiful concept was corrupted into rigid caste hierarchy; a Christian task is to redeem it: every person has inherent dignity, not determined by birth.
