# Speculative Activity Prohibition

Rule 4 FCRR 2011: FC cannot be invested in anything with risk of appreciation or depreciation linked to market forces — **including mutual funds and shares** — nor in schemes promising high returns such as chits or land not directly linked to declared objects. Penalty: 30% of the amount invested plus 10% of returns, or ₹1,00,000.
