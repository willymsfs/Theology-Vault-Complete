# Sensus Fidei as Locus Theologicus

The epistemological claim underneath synodality: consulting the laity is a theological necessity, not
a democratic concession. Within Melchior Cano's classical framework, the *sensus fidelium* is a genuine
*locus theologicus* — a proper source of theological argument alongside Scripture and the councils —
because by the anointing of the Spirit received in baptism, the whole body of the faithful cannot err
in believing (*infallibilitas in credendo*). This dismantles the post-Tridentine binary of *Ecclesia
docens* (the teaching Church, restricted to the hierarchy) and *Ecclesia discens* (the learning Church,
reduced to passive obedience); Augustine's *concordissima fidei conspiratio* — the most harmonious
consensus of faith — names the same reality centuries earlier.
