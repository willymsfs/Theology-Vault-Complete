# Postmodern Theology

Theology after the collapse of **foundationalism** — scepticism toward universal rational principles, objective truth, progress and **metanarratives**; **deconstruction** as the standard tool.

Two wings, agreeing on the diagnosis and opposed on the cure:
- **Liberal postmoderns** — Mark C. Taylor's deconstructive *a/theology*, Altizer's death of God as a historical epoch, Cupitt's non-realism: give up transcendence.
- **Conservative postmoderns** — **Marion** (icon against idol, God without being), **de Certeau** (mysticism as rupture of the written word), **Milbank / Radical Orthodoxy** (theology's own metadiscourse, ontology of peace): theology never needed those foundations, and is now free of the secular referee.

**The open question:** is that freedom recovery or retreat?
