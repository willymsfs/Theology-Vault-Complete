# The Way (Hē Hodos)

The movement's earliest self-designation (Acts 9:2) — also an Essene term, which places the young Church inside a live Jewish argument about who is walking the Lord's road. The phrase carries the Exodus: the way of the Lord out of Egypt (Ex 13:21), the way prepared in the wilderness (Is 40:3), and finally a person — "I am the way" (Jn 14:6). Jesus leads the return from the exile of sin.
