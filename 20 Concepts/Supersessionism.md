# Supersessionism

The traditional belief that **the Church replaced Israel** in God's covenant — that the new covenant supersedes and annuls the old.

**The post-Holocaust reversal.** From the 1950s–60s **Roy Eckardt** and **John Pawlikowski** demanded that Christians renounce supersessionism and anti-Judaism as roots of the Shoah. **Barth** holds the paradox: Christocentric formulations that appear to denigrate the synagogue, joined to a strong affirmation of **Israel's irrevocable election** — the foundation his students Marquardt and von der Osten-Sacken built non-supersessionist Christologies upon.

**R. Kendall Soulen**, *The God of Israel and Christian Theology*: the deeper fault is a **"semi-gnostic"** habit of subordinating creation and consummation to redemption-from-sin, which detaches God's identity from Israel's election. Christ is the **climactic recapitulation** of God's work for the **mutual blessing of Jew and Gentile**.

**Where it touches this vault's Vatican material**: the [[Mortara Affair]] and the recurrent restrictions on Jews in the [[Papal States]] are supersessionism as administrative practice, not only as doctrine. *Nostra Aetate* is the formal turn.
