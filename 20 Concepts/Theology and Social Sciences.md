# Theology and the Social Sciences

Sociology arose as a **rival grand theory**, casting itself as the emancipatory successor to theology and religion as a premodern residue. Five theological strategies in reply:

| Strategy | Figure | Move |
|---|---|---|
| Fundamentalism | — | Repel modernity; subcultural isolation |
| Sociology overcomes theology | **Troeltsch** | Historicism; church–sect typology; all truth relative |
| Theology recruits sociology | **Bonhoeffer** | *Sanctorum Communio* — borrowed tools under theological control |
| Mutual assimilation | **Farley** | Phenomenology plus sociology; "ecclesial being" |
| Refusal | **Milbank** | Secular sociology is a Christian heresy resting on an ontology of violence |

The choice is not innocent: it decides whether theology can be publicly argued or only confessed.
