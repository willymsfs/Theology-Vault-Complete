# Footnote 351

The note in Amoris Laetitia 305 stating that the help of the Church in certain cases "can include the help of the sacraments," and that the Eucharist "is not a prize for the perfect, but a powerful medicine and nourishment for the weak" — the focus of the subsequent interpretive dispute.
