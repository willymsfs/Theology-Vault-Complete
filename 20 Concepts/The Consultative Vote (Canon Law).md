# The Consultative Vote (Canon Law)

The principal canonical obstacle to synodality in the Latin Church. The 1983 Code establishes genuine
participatory structures — Parish Pastoral Councils (c. 536), Parish Finance Councils (c. 537),
Diocesan Pastoral Councils (c. 511) — but restricts every one of them to a **consultative**, not
deliberative, vote. Behind this stands **canon 129 §1**, which reserves the power of governance
(*potestas regiminis*) to those in sacred orders, permitting the laity only to "co-operate" in its
exercise. Practical effect: a council may reach unanimous consensus, and the bishop or parish priest
may legally decide the opposite. The canonist Sabine Demel's diagnosis: this reduces lay
co-responsibility to a concession from clerical authority rather than a right rooted in baptism.
