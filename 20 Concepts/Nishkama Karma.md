# Nishkama Karma

Selfless action — action without attachment to its fruits. Purifies the doer and prepares for [[Moksha]]. Examples: mother feeding a child, doctor treating without thought of fee. Christian echo: service without attachment to reward; "whether eating or drinking, do it for God's glory."
