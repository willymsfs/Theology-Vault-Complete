# Dunamis

The keyword of Acts. *Dunamis* — power, the root behind "dynamite" — is what Jesus promises in Acts 1:8 in place of the political kingdom the disciples ask about: not a restored throne but a force carried by ordinary people to the ends of the earth. The whole book is the working-out of that one word. Pentecost is where it falls (Acts 2), and it is distributed sacramentally in confirmation (CCC 1302–1305), so the promise is not merely narrated but handed on.
