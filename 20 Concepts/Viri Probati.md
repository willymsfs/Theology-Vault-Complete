# Viri Probati

"Men of proven virtue" — the proposal to ordain suitable married men in regions of acute priest shortage, voted for by the Amazon Synod (2019) and not adopted in Querida Amazonia (2020); a prudential rather than dogmatic question, since celibacy is discipline.
