# Pentecost

The Feast of Weeks, fifty days after Passover (Lev 23; Dt 16:16) — a harvest feast that had become the feast of the giving of the Law at Sinai. Acts 2 is therefore staged as Sinai: fire, theophany, covenant, and a new law — the law of the Spirit (Rom 8:2). It is simultaneously the reversal of Babel (Gen 11): the division of tongues undone in a Church "which speaks all tongues" (*Ad Gentes* 4). Luke then repeats the event at each frontier.
