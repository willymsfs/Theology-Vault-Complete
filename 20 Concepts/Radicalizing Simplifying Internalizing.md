# Radicalizing Simplifying Internalizing

The three verbs by which, on Soares-Prabhu's account, Jesus "set us free from the burden of the Law." Radicalizing is the one usually dropped, and it is what prevents the Sermon being read as merely a gentler law. Summary formula: "law and cult are radically subordinated to love."
