# Scriptural Reasoning

An inter-Abrahamic practice in which **Jewish, Christian and Muslim scholars read their scriptures together** — not to agree, but to discover shared rules for cooperative reasoning across traditions.

Grew out of postliberal, non-supersessionist work; the Society for Scriptural Reasoning was co-founded by **Daniel Hardy** and **David Ford** with Peter Ochs. Its wager: the texts, read in each other's presence, generate a discipline of argument that no prior theory of religions can supply. **Ford's thesis 11**: conversation around scriptures is at the heart of interfaith relations.

**Indian extension:** the same practice with the Gita, the Qur'an and the Dhammapada is the textual form of the *dialogue of life*.
