# Political Theology

Theology that takes the **public, structural and political** dimension of faith as internal to the doctrine itself, not as an applied afterthought.

European strand: **Metz** and **Moltmann** — the "new political theology" after Auschwitz, with the memory of the suffering (*memoria passionis*) as its engine, against a privatized bourgeois Christianity. American strand: **Reinhold Niebuhr's** Christian realism — relative justice under permanent sin. Radical strand: **Milbank** — refuse the secular's terms entirely. Global South strand: liberation, black, Dalit and minjung theologies, where the political is not a topic but the condition of survival.

**The permanent question:** does the Church have a politics of its own (Hauerwas, Yoder) or a responsibility inside the world's politics (Niebuhr)?
