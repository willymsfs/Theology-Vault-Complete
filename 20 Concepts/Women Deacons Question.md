# Women Deacons Question

The open historical and theological question of whether the deaconesses attested in antiquity — Phoebe, and the Byzantine ordination rites closely paralleling those for male deacons — were sacramentally ordained to the same order or held a parallel non-sacramental office; studied by papal commissions in 2016 and 2020 without unanimity.
