# Double-Distancing

Indian theology must distance itself from two seductions at once: "the academic theology of the West" (irrelevant, ideologically loaded, an "inner-club affair") and "traditional Indian forms which may blunt its liberative thrust" — the elitist, other-worldly ashram spiritualities, "a brahmanical spirituality, the spirituality of an affluent, privileged class." Neither Tübingen nor the ashram: theology from the field and the slum.
