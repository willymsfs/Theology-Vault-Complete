# Historical Consciousness

The modern awareness that every text, doctrine and institution is conditioned by its time and place. Its effect on theology was structural, not decorative: **the sources ceased to function as absolute premises and became data requiring critical investigation.**

Every modern method is an answer to the question this opened: *how can criticised history still be normative?* Schleiermacher relocates the norm in consciousness; Barth in the Word; Tillich in correlation; Lonergan in the converted subject working through functional specialties.

- [[Historical consciousness turned the sources of theology into data]]
