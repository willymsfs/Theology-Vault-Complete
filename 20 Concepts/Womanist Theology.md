# Womanist Theology

Theology from **African American women's religious experience** — named from Alice Walker's "womanist," and born as a double critique: of black theology's patriarchal blindness and of white feminist theology's race-blindness.

**Delores Williams** is the paradigm: the **Hagar** narrative rather than the Exodus, God's provision as **survival and quality of life** rather than straightforward liberation, and the **surrogacy critique** of an atonement that makes coerced substitution salvific. M. Shawn Copeland extends it to the body and to Catholic systematics.

**Indian parallel worth working:** Dalit women's theology stands in the same double position — critiqued out of Dalit theology's male voice and out of Indian feminist theology's upper-caste voice.
