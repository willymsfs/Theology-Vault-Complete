# Table Fellowship

Jesus's habit of eating with outcasts (Mk 2:15ff): an "acted parable, through which he brings home to listeners his experience of God as Abba the loving parent." A shared meal in first-century Palestine was a religious affirmation of belonging; Jesus's open table subverted purity laws and enacted the Kingdom as community of grace. Soares-Prabhu's direct weapon against caste: where caste divides the Lord's table, the Eucharist becomes "sacrilege" and "parody."
