# Indian Mind

Soares-Prabhu's epistemological category (via A.K. Ramanujan): "context-sensitive rather than context-free," experiencing reality as an interrelated, interdependent whole; "inclusive, cosmocentric, symbolic, and practical"; preferring the "logic of complementarity (both/and) rather than the Aristotelian way of exclusion (either/or)." "Because of its passion for wholeness the Indian mind is prepared to risk the chance of error rather than the loss of any part of truth." The interpreter must *convert* to this worldview — the second conversion alongside commitment to the poor.
