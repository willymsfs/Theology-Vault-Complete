# Postcolonial Criticism

Reading theology and scripture with **colonialism at the centre** — of the texts themselves (produced under Egypt, Assyria, Babylon, Rome) and of their interpretation (Eurocentric historical criticism presenting itself as neutral).

Key terms: **orientalism** (homogenizing misrepresentation of the Other), **hybridity**, **subaltern**, **contrapuntal reading** (Sugirtharajah — read simultaneously from margin and centre), **hermeneutics of diaspora** (Segovia).

**Its quarrel with liberation hermeneutics:** the Bible is a tainted text of emancipation *and* enslavement; the Exodus paradigm ignores the displaced Canaanites; "the poor" homogenized economically erases caste, race, gender and ethnicity.
