# Viaticum

"Food for the journey" — communion given to the dying, which the tradition regards as the proper and priority sacrament of the dying rather than a merely optional devotion.

## Connected
[[Eucharist MOC]]


### Anointing course (BTh)
Viaticum's priority is the point modern pastoral practice most often inverts. "Last rites" names a **sequence** — penance, anointing, viaticum — not a single sacrament, and while that is the order of celebration, the order of *importance* for one who is actually dying places **Viaticum first**: it is Christ himself and the pledge of the glory the dying person is entering. CIC c. 921 obliges the dying to receive it and permits it even to those who have already communicated that day. See [[Order of the Last Rites]] and [[Anointing MOC]].
