# Vows as Communitarian Self-Liberation

Soares-Prabhu reinterprets the three vows as communitarian "acts of self-liberation," not individual asceticism. **Poverty** "frees us from the tyranny of possessions" — "the sharing that creates a community of sharing." **Chastity** frees "from the pull of inordinate affections" — "the unrestricted love which fashions a community capable of loving." **Obedience** frees "from the addictive need for achievement and the craving for power" — "commitment to God's will that enables a community to make common cause for the Kingdom." Religious life itself is prophetic: "mystical" and "political" at once, a productive model for the whole Church. "We are freed from all things, in order that we might make ourselves slaves of all through love."
