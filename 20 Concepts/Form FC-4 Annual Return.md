# Form FC-4 Annual Return

The FCRA annual return, due **within nine months of the close of the financial year — 31 December**. A **NIL return is mandatory** even in a year with no foreign receipts. Since 2026 it must carry project-wise and location-wise utilisation, asset creation, publications, website and social media accounts, and ultimate-donor disclosure for funds arriving via Donor Advised Funds. *Note: oral training material in circulation gives 31 July, confusing this with the income-tax audit date.*
