# Jesus of Faith

Soares-Prabhu's Third World christological starting point — "the Jesus (not the Christ) of faith (not of history)": "the real Jesus who lived in Palestine... as he was encountered and experienced by his first followers." Against both academic historical reconstruction and imported Hellenistic dogmatic formulas. "To be a disciple of Jesus means to experience God the way that Jesus experienced God" — discipleship as shared Abba experience, leading to solidarity with the poor, conflict with the powerful, and the cross as "natural outcome."
