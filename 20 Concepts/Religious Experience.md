# Religious Experience

*"That intimate and immediate awareness of being touched or grasped by the Divine."* Because humans are embodied, this experience is mediated through **symbols** and expressed in symbolic language.

## Why symbols?
A symbol is a concrete thing carrying an invisible reality (water in Baptism = cleansing/new life). Embodied beings meet God through the concrete.
