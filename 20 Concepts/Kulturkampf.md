# Kulturkampf

Bismarck's campaign against the Catholic Church in the new German Reich (from 1871): state control of education and clerical appointments, expulsion of religious orders, imprisonment of bishops.

**Its root is the dual-allegiance suspicion**: **Bismarck saw Catholics as enemies of the Reich** because loyalty to the pope competed with loyalty to the nation ([[Ultramontanism]] read as treason). The nation-state as "secular deity" (Hegel) could not tolerate a rival claim on conscience.

**Its cost inside theology**: it absorbed the German bishops from 1873, which is why **[[Wilhelm von Ketteler]]'s social programme was ignored by his own colleagues** and had to wait for [[Leo XIII]] and [[Rerum Novarum]].
