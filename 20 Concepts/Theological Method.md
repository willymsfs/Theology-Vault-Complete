# Theological Method

The examination of **the bases, the proper ordering, and the norms of theology** — a procedure that can be worked out and critically appraised so as to reach systematic understanding in religion. Lonergan's definition is the standard: *a normative pattern of recurrent and related operations yielding cumulative and progressive results.*

Not a technique (mechanical rule) and not a system (the dated end-product). Method is the open, self-correcting process that **produces** systems and lets theologians collaborate across generations.

## Claim
- [[Method is a pattern of operations, not a set of rules]]
- [[Discord between methods is graver than opposition between doctrines]]
- [[The choice of starting point decides the theology]]
