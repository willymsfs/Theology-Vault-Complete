# Sacramental Theology

The doctrine of the sacraments — and, more broadly, of how **material, historical realities mediate God**.

The modern recovery ran through three moves: **Schillebeeckx**, sacrament as *encounter with Christ* rather than as an efficacious mechanism; **de Lubac**, *Corpus mysticum* — reuniting the eucharistic body and the ecclesial body that medieval usage had separated; **Rahner**, the Church as *Ursakrament*, the primordial sacrament of which the seven are the actualizations. Orthodoxy (Schmemann) reframes the whole world as sacramental. Ecumenically, BEM is the test case.
