# Collegiality

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Concepts/Collegiality (Vatican II course)|Collegiality — Vatican II course treatment]].


A specific, restricted manifestation of [[Synodality]]: the co-responsibility of the College of Bishops,
acting *cum et sub Petro* (with and under the Bishop of Rome), in governing the universal Church.
Synodality is the wider reality — an attribute of the whole Church as the People of God, including
laity, religious and ordained — of which collegiality is one expression, rooted in the synodal life of
the local Churches rather than opposed to it.
