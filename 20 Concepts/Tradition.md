# Tradition

"The living transmission accomplished in the Holy Spirit is called Tradition." Not just old documents but the living handing-on of the whole Gospel through liturgy, creeds, and the Fathers.

## Distinction
- **Tradition** (capital T) = the living apostolic faith
- **traditions** (small t) = the customs that carry it

## Claims
- [[Doctrine develops by making the implicit explicit, never by adding anew]]
