# Cheirotonia and Cheirothesia

Greek terms for the laying on of hands in ordination; the New Testament root of ordination as a sacramental gesture conferring a charism (1 Tim 4:14; 2 Tim 1:6), identified by Pius XII in 1947 as the sole essential matter.
