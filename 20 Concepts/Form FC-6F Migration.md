# Form FC-6F Migration

The one-off compulsory filing by which an association registered before 22 June 2026 declares its approved purposes and States to migrate into the new framework. **Due 22 June 2027.** Also the vehicle for later scope changes under Rule 17B, at ₹300 per additional purpose or State.
