# FCRA Religious Purposes

The **Religious** category of the FCRA Schedule (Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)) — 16 approved purposes.

| ID | Approved purpose |
|---|---|
| **REL-01** | Construction, renovation, and maintenance of temples, mosques, churches, gurudwaras, monasteries, synagogues, and other places of worship |
| **REL-02** | Preservation, printing, translation, and digitisation of sacred scriptures and commentaries used in liturgy |
| **REL-03** | Preservation and support of institutions engaged in the study, documentation, and preservation of religious philosophy, theology and religious history (excluding proselytisation) |
| **REL-04** | Provision of amenities for pilgrims at heritage religious sites (drinking water, sanitation, shelter) |
| **REL-05** | Setting up of dharamshalas, langars, annadans, and community kitchens under religious auspices |
| **REL-06** | Conduct of religious education, moral instruction, satsangs, discourses, and meditation retreats (excluding proselytisation) |
| **REL-07** | Promotion of devotional music, chants, theatre, and liturgical arts |
| **REL-08** | Documentation, preservation, and revival of indigenous and tribal faith practices, rituals and systems of worship (excluding proselytisation) |
| **REL-09** | Protection of sacred relics, shrines, places of worship, and archaeological religious heritage primarily associated with religious practice |
| **REL-10** | Archiving, digitization, and oral-history recording of religious rituals, festivals, and traditions of faith communities |
| **REL-11** | Inter-faith dialogue, harmony-building programmes, and peace initiatives through religious institutions |
| **REL-12** | Religious publications, journals, and faith-based research |
| **REL-13** | Establishment of religious libraries, museums, and archives |
| **REL-14** | Faith-based counselling centres, de-addiction centres, and social care programmes operated through religious organisations |
| **REL-15** | Training in traditional sacred crafts: idol-making, church art, calligraphy of scriptures, iconography |
| **REL-16** | Burial/cremation ground development and maintenance |
