# Semiotic Method

Reading religion as a **system of signs**: asking not what a doctrine asserts but how religious signs are produced, deployed and made authoritative within a community.

Proponents in the method catalogue: **Nancey Murphy**, **J. Z. Smith**. Kin to [[Cultural-Linguistic Method]] — both treat religion as a grammar rather than as propositions or as expressed feeling — but semiotics keeps the analyst outside the community's own framework, where Lindbeck's intratextuality deliberately stands inside it.
