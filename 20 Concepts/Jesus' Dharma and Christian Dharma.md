# Jesus' Dharma and Christian Dharma

Soares-Prabhu's distinction between the dharma Jesus *practised* (visible in the baptism, the table fellowship, the Cross) and the dharma he *preached* (the Sermon on the Mount). Useful analytically: the first is Christology, the second is ethics, and they must be held together.
