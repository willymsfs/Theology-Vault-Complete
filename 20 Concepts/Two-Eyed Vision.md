# Two-Eyed Vision

Truth is "elliptical," made of "contradictory facets which cannot be apprehended adequately from any single point of view." Requires complementary perspectives held together — Asian and Semitic religious experience, religious and social readings, mysticism and politics. The epistemic ground of the whole Integral Indian Reading and of the Indian mind's both/and logic.
