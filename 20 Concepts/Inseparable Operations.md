# Inseparable Operations

Gregory of Nyssa's doctrine that every act of the Trinity ad extra proceeds as a single, undivided action "from the Father, through the Son, perfected in the Holy Spirit" — later formalized by Aquinas and Emery as opera trinitatis ad extra indivisa sunt.
