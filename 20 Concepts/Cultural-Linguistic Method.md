# Cultural-Linguistic Method

**Lindbeck**, *The Nature of Doctrine* (1984). Religion works like a **language and a culture**: it is a framework that shapes experience, not an expression of a prior inner experience and not primarily a set of propositions.

Consequences: doctrines are **communally authoritative rules of discourse, attitude and action** (the **rule theory**), which explains how doctrine can be constant while its formulations change; and theology proceeds by **intratextuality** — redescribing reality inside the scriptural framework rather than translating scripture into alien categories. Slogan: *the text absorbs the world*.

**Criticisms:** obscures the metaphysical work of systematics; makes mutual correction by other cultures hard; charged with sectarianism and quietism.
