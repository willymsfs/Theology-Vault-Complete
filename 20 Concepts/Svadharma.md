# Svadharma

From Bhagavadgita XVIII.47; svabhavaniyata-karma — action flowing spontaneously from the intrinsic tendencies of one's own being. Soares-Prabhu calls the Sermon on the Mount "the Christian svadharma": not a code imposed from outside but a description of the "natural lifestyle of a Christian," lived spontaneously to the extent one has experienced God's unconditional love.
