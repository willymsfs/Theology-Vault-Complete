# Han-puri (Korean: 한풀이) — "purging of han (unresolved grief/resentment)"

A Korean shamanistic ritual of telling histories of unjust, unavenged suffering, retrieved by Chung Hyun Kyung to describe the Spirit's liberating, healing work among oppressed Asian women.
