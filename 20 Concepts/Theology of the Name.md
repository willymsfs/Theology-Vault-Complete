# Theology of the Name

*Onoma* occurs some sixty times in Acts. In Hebrew thought a name expresses essence, so to invoke the Name is to invoke presence and authority — YHWH unpronounceable, spoken once a year by the high priest; the LORD's name a "strong tower" (Prov 18:10); the divine name formulas mapping God's action (Yahweh-jireh, -rapha, -nissi, -shalom, -ra'ah, -tsidkenu, -shammah). Peter has "no silver and gold" at the Beautiful Gate — only the Name, and the Name is enough. *Yeshua* itself means "God saves."
