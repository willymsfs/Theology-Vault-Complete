# Semantic Autonomy

Drawing on Paul Ricoeur: the biblical text's language "embodies an originary experience of the Absolute" and must be respected as such. This is the warrant that prevents Soares-Prabhu's contextual reading collapsing into eisegesis — the text resists absorption into the interpreter's "self-enclosed world."
