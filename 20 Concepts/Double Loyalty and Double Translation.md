# Double Loyalty and Double Translation

The Indian theologian holds "a double loyalty to the Christian message and to the Indian context," requiring a "double translation" — out of the original context and into the new one — while keeping dialectical distance from **both** Western academic models **and** traditional Hindu forms, lest the gospel's liberative thrust be blunted.
