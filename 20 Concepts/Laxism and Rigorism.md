# Laxism and Rigorism

The two condemned extremes of post-Tridentine moral theology — laxism (a slenderly probable opinion suffices, condemned by Innocent XI in 1679) and tutiorism/rigorism (one must always follow the safer opinion, condemned by Alexander VIII in 1690).
