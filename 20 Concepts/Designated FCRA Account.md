# Designated FCRA Account

Section 17: all foreign contribution must be received into one designated account at **State Bank of India, Sansad Marg / New Delhi Main Branch**. No other bank may credit a foreign inward remittance. Secondary FCRA and utilisation accounts are permitted in scheduled PFMS-integrated banks, but **no non-FC money may ever enter any of them**. New utilisation accounts: FC-6D within 45 days.
