# Personalist Turn

The movement associated with Dietrich von Hildebrand (1929) and Herbert Doms (1935) arguing that the mutual self-giving union of the spouses is the meaning of marriage rather than a means to procreation; condemned by the Holy Office in 1944 and substantially received at Vatican II.
