# Martus

Witness — and the word that becomes "martyr," because in Acts the two collapse into one. Jesus commissions witnesses (1:8); the office of apostle is defined as witness to the Resurrection (1:21-22); Stephen witnesses and dies (Acts 7); Paul is told at his call how much he must suffer for the Name (9:16). The course's closing move is to read *martus* domestically: giving ourselves for others daily.
