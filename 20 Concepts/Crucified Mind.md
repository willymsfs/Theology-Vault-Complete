# Crucified Mind

Koyama's name for the posture required of the contextual theologian: **crucified, not crusading**. Theology done kenotically — self-emptying, abandoning epistemic arrogance and the assumption of cultural superiority, moving toward the margins to meet the poor and to converse with other traditions.

Koyama's target is Western "pure theology," which he calls an ideological danger and a theological imperialism that pushes the living God aside. His remedy is not rejection but **mutual correction** between Western and non-Western theologies.
