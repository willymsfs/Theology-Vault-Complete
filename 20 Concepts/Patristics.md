# Patristics

The study of the **Church Fathers** — and, in the twentieth century, a live theological programme rather than an antiquarian one.

Two great retrievals: Catholic ***ressourcement*** (de Lubac, Daniélou, Balthasar) — go behind the neoscholastic manuals to the sources before 1300, which is what broke the nature/grace dualism and fed Vatican II; and Orthodox **neo-patristic synthesis** (Florovsky, Lossky) — "Christian Hellenism," a return to the baptized Hellenism of the Fathers against Russian religious philosophy.

Both are cases of the same rule: **a return to the sources is a methodological revolt before it is a doctrinal one.**
