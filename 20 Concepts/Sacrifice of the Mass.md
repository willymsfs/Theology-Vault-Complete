# Sacrifice of the Mass

The doctrine, defined at Trent Session XXII, that the Mass is the unbloody re-presentation of the one sacrifice of Calvary — "the same victim, the same now offering by the ministry of priests who then offered himself on the cross, the manner of offering alone being different."
