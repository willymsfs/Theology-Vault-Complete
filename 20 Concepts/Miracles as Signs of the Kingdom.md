# Miracles as Signs of the Kingdom

Jesus's miracles are not "compelling 'proofs'" of divinity but "'signs', indications perceptible to those disposed to receive them" — "a proclamation in action of the 'good news'," signifying "the subversion of a whole oppressive power structure." They announce and actualize God's saving action provisionally, pointing to total liberation, not magic or apologetics.
