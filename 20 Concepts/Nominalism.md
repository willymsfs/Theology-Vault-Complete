# Nominalism

The view (Ockham) that only individual things are real; "universals" are mere names. With Ockham's Razor ("*Entia non sunt multiplicanda sine necessitate*"), it separated faith from reason and dissolved the Scholastic synthesis — theology becomes "not a science" since its principles rely on revelation.

- [[Ockham's nominalism dissolved the synthesis of faith and reason]]
