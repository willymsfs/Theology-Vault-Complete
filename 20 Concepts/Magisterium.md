# Magisterium

The living teaching office of the Church — Pope and bishops in communion — charged with authentically interpreting the Word of God. It is "not above the word of God, but serves it."

## Structure
- **Collegiality of bishops** — the bishops as one *collegium* with supreme teaching authority
- **Papal magisterium** — Pope teaching, infallibly when *ex cathedra*
- **Sensus fidei / fidelium** → [[Sensus Fidelium]]

## Claims
- [[The Magisterium serves the Word, it does not master it]]
- [[Scripture, Tradition and Magisterium cannot stand without one another]]
