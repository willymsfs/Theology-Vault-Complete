# Liturgical Theology

Schmemann's model: theology is the articulation of the Church's "lived experience of wholeness," centred on the liturgy. "The norm of prayer is the norm of belief" (*Lex orandi lex credendi*). The Christian is "the priest, who stands in the centre of the cosmos and unifies it," receiving the world and offering it back to God.
