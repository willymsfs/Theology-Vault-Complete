# Power of Orders

The sacramentally-received capacity to teach, sanctify, and govern.

## Connected
[[Ecclesiology MOC]]


### Holy Orders course (BTh)
The sacramental power conferred by ordination, which is unlosable because it rests on the indelible character — grounded in Augustine's answer to the Donatists and defined at Trent Session XXIII canon 4. Its unlosability is why laicisation removes the exercise and the obligations but not the ordination, and why a laicised priest still validly absolves in danger of death (CIC c. 976). See [[Character (Orders)]] and [[Holy Orders MOC]].
