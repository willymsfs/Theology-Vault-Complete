# Mysterion and Sacramentum

The Vulgate's rendering of to mystērion touto mega estin (Eph 5:32) as sacramentum hoc magnum est — the linguistic bridge on which the Western sacramental theology of marriage was built, and which Calvin attacked as a philological error.
