# Narrative Method

Taking **the story as the bearer of theological meaning**, rather than treating it as illustration of a doctrine or as evidence for a reconstructed history.

**Frei**: modern historical consciousness produced the **eclipse of biblical narrative** — the stories were fitted into external frameworks (timeless truths or verified facts) and so lost their reality-constituting power. His rule: **identity before presence** — learn Jesus's specific identity from the narratives before asking what his presence means now. **H. R. Niebuhr** supplies the ethical version (the responding self within a story), **C. S. Song** the Asian version (story theology), **Suh Nam-dong** the political one (the social biography of the minjung).
