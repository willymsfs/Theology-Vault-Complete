# Biblical Inspiration

The Holy Spirit's guidance of the human authors so that their fully human writing carries God's message. Tied to **inerrancy**: Scripture teaches "without error that truth which God wanted to put into the sacred writings for the sake of our salvation" ([[Dei Verbum]] 11).

## Claim
- [[Scripture is inerrant only for the sake of salvation]]
