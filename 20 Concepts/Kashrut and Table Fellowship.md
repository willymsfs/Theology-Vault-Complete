# Kashrut and Table Fellowship

The dietary laws functioned in the exile as the wall of the table: 229 of 341 Mishnaic texts concern table fellowship. Clean and unclean animals stood in for Jew and Gentile, so lifting the ban (Mk 7:19; Acts 10:15) lifts the wall itself — which is why the vision is given three times to a man who does not want it. The Council of Acts 15 then keeps a few food restrictions not as law but as temporary courtesy, so that the two groups can eat together at all.
