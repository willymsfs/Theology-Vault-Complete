# Trinity

The Christian doctrine of one God in three persons — Father, Son, Holy Spirit. A classic case of [[Development of Doctrine]] (implicit in NT, stated over centuries) and of [[Analogical Knowledge]]. Indian theology maps it onto Saccidananda (Ch 6). (Stub.)

### Trinity (BTh course)
A full BTh-level treatment of the doctrine's development — biblical/pre-Nicene foundations, the Nicene-Constantinopolitan resolution, Augustine's psychological analogy, medieval scholastic synthesis, the filioque and Florence, the 20th-century revival (Rahner, Moltmann, Zizioulas), Volf's and Emery's contemporary responses, interfaith dimensions (Panikkar, PCID), and the current systematic debates over simplicity, analogy, personhood, and political application.
