# Religious Assent

The response owed to Church teaching — graded to the authority of the teaching. From strongest to lightest: **Assent of Faith** (dogma) → **Firm Acceptance** (definitive doctrine) → **Religious Submission of Intellect and Will** (non-definitive authoritative doctrine) → obedience to the spirit of prudential admonitions.
