# Hindutva

The ideology of Hindu-nation nationalism. The book: a "very dangerous ideology" seeking a "Hindu Rashtra" (Hindu nation) where minorities are "caricatured as 'foreigners'" and "reduced to second class citizens." Threatens India's secular character.
