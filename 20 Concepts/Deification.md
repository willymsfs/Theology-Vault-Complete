# Deification

*Theosis*. Salvation understood as **participation in the divine life** rather than only as forgiveness of guilt. Athanasius's formula: God became human so that humans might become divine.

Central to Eastern Orthodoxy, where **Palamas's essence/energies distinction** secures it — we share God's energies, never his essence. **de Lubac's** *surnaturel* is the Western equivalent move: deification by grace is human nature's true destiny, not an extrinsic addition to a self-sufficient nature.
