# Patristic Period

The era of the Fathers of the Church — apostolic period to mid-8th century (close marked by John Damascene †749 in the East; Gregory the Great †604 and Isidore of Seville †639 in the West). Its driving task: build a theological vocabulary to protect the faith.

## Groups
- Apostolic Fathers — "pastoral, didactic and exhortatory"
- Apologists — defense to win "recognition of the rights of Christians"
- Schools → [[Alexandrian and Antiochene Schools]]
- Latin Fathers → [[Augustine]]
