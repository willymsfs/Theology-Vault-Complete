# Omnes, Aliqui, Unus

The tradition's schema for how a synodal community actually decides, holding together consultation,
discernment and authority without collapsing into either parliamentary democracy or monarchy.
**Omnes** (all) — the whole body, consulted; the [[Sensus Fidei]] of everyone listened to.
**Aliqui** (some) — representative bodies (councils, chapters, synodal assemblies) that discern
and synthesise what has been heard. **Unus** (one) — the one holding the ministry of unity (parish
priest, bishop, Pope), who decides, but only in, with and after hearing the Omnes and the Aliqui.
Remove the Unus and the result is a parliament; remove the Omnes and Aliqui and the result is a
monarchy.
