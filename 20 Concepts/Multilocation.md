# Multilocation

The puzzle of how the numerically identical, physical body of Christ can exist in heaven and simultaneously on thousands of separate altars worldwide. Resolved not by supposing Christ's body is divided or multiplied, but by the doctrine of [[Circumscriptive and Non-Circumscriptive Presence]]: the sacramental presence is non-circumscriptive, so ordinary spatial reasoning about one body being in only one place does not apply to it in the way it applies to bodies present circumscriptively.
