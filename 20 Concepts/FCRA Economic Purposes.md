# FCRA Economic Purposes

The **Economic** category of the FCRA Schedule (Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)) — 19 approved purposes.

| ID | Approved purpose |
|---|---|
| **ECO-01** | Agricultural development programmes: farmer training, non-commercial input support, organic farming promotion |
| **ECO-02** | Allied activities: dairy farming, animal husbandry, poultry, fisheries, sericulture, apiculture (non-commercial) |
| **ECO-03** | Formation and strengthening of farmer-producer organisations, cooperatives, and self-help groups |
| **ECO-04** | Microfinance, microcredit, and community savings institutions for poor households |
| **ECO-05** | Vocational training and livelihood-linked short-term skilling (non-formal, not leading to recognised qualifications) |
| **ECO-06** | Promotion of rural and cottage industries (weaving, pottery, handicrafts, agro-processing) with non-profit orientation |
| **ECO-07** | Support for socially-oriented micro, small, and medium enterprises on non-profit or community-benefit basis |
| **ECO-08** | Women's entrepreneurship promotion: tailoring, food processing, handicrafts, small-scale manufacturing |
| **ECO-09** | Skill incubation centres and employability-oriented digital skilling for youth (IT, coding, electronics) |
| **ECO-10** | Infrastructure creation for livelihoods: rural haats, cold storages, community processing units |
| **ECO-11** | Promotion of fair-trade, ethical supply chains, and eco-friendly businesses |
| **ECO-12** | Sustainable community livelihood models in eco-tourism and homestays |
| **ECO-13** | Fisheries and aquaculture livelihood training for coastal and inland communities |
| **ECO-14** | Green economy livelihoods: solar technicians, e-waste handlers, recycling units |
| **ECO-15** | Job placement services, employment exchanges, and market linkages for trained youth |
| **ECO-16** | Food processing and value addition units |
| **ECO-17** | Digital literacy, financial literacy, technology access, and digital inclusion for livelihood enhancement and economic inclusion |
| **ECO-18** | Livelihood support for migrant workers and vulnerable groups, including temporary shelter-linked employment |
| **ECO-19** | Community renewable energy enterprises (solar, biogas, micro-grids) generating local jobs |
