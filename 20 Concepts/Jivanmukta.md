# Jivanmukta

In Hindu thought, one liberated while still living, freed from the tyranny of empirical selfhood. Soares-Prabhu compares Christian spiritual poverty to this state, arguing that such inner freedom does not withdraw from the world but *drives* one outward to seek others' material and social liberation.
