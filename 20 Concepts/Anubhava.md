# Anubhava

**Direct spiritual experience**, taken by much Indian theology as the starting point in preference to abstract doctrine. Fostered institutionally by the [[Ashram Movement]], which integrates *jnana* (wisdom), *bhakti* (devotion) and *nishkama karma* (selfless action) into Christian spirituality.

Distinguish from Schleiermacher's experiential turn: *anubhava* is contemplative and non-dual, not the analysis of religious feeling mounted against an Enlightenment challenge.

**Counter-voice:** Soares-Prabhu judges modern Christian ashrams often elitist and cut off from the struggles of the poor — experience without the poor is not yet Indian.
