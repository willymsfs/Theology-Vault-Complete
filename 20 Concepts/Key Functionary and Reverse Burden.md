# Key Functionary and Reverse Burden

Rule 2(1)(ca), inserted 2026: a **key functionary** is anyone exercising control over or responsible for management, whatever their title. Scrutiny shifted from every committee member to those actually in control — **concentrating personal liability on them**, with a reverse burden of proof under which they are presumed liable for diversion unless they show due diligence and good faith.
