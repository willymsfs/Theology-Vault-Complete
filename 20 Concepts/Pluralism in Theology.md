# Pluralism in Theology

The legitimate multiplicity of theological expressions around the single focus of Christ. Present from the start: the Bible itself holds many theologies. "One and the many, the universal and the particular."

## Claims
- [[The Bible is a library of theologies, not a single treatise]]
- [[Legitimate pluralism runs from the Bible to today's contextual theologies]]
