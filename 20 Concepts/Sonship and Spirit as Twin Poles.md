# Sonship and Spirit as Twin Poles

Soares-Prabhu's synoptic pneumatology: "Sonship (the experience of God as abba, Father) and Spirit (the experience of the eschatological power of God working in and through him) were in fact the twin poles of Jesus' experience of God." Spirit phases: grounds sonship (infancy), messianic installation (baptism), empowers exorcism and evangelization (ministry) — marked not by ecstatic enthusiasm but by "the authority of his teaching, and the power of his healings and exorcisms."
