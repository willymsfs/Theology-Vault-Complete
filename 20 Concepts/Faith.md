# Faith

The human response to God's self-revelation — the "cup" that receives the water of revelation. Faith and revelation are *"distinct and distinguishable"* yet *"inseparably united"*: revelation is God's self-communication, faith is the response on the human side.

## Characteristics
- gift (grace) — not a human achievement
- free personal response
- ecclesial — lived in and with the community
- the starting engine of theology (*credo ut intelligam*)

## Claims
- [[Theology is faith seeking understanding, not proof seeking certainty]]
