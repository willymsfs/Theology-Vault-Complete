# Cry for Life

Soares-Prabhu's name for what any Third World theology must answer, in three parts: a **cry for survival** (deadly economic poverty), a **cry for dignity** (racist, sexist and caste discrimination), and a **cry for meaning** (the religious pluralism of Asia). A theology that does not address all three is not yet Indian.
