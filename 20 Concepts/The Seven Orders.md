# The Seven Orders

The medieval scheme of porter, lector, exorcist, acolyte, subdeacon, deacon, and priest — notably omitting the episcopate — suppressed in the Latin Church by Ministeria Quaedam (1972), which replaced the minor orders with the lay ministries of lector and acolyte.
