# Focal Instances

Soares-Prabhu's category for the six antitheses of Matthew 5:21-47: not exhaustive statutory laws but shocking, graphic examples meant to awaken the imagination to radically non-aggressive behaviour. Reading them as statutes produces casuistry; reading them as focal instances produces conversion.
