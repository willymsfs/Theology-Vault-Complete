# Liberative Pedagogy

Jesus's teaching style per Soares-Prabhu: a "pedagogy of the oppressed with a strong commitment to societal liberation" — "nonélitist and dialogical, based on the equality of all before God." Unlike the authoritarian scribes, Jesus taught through dialogue and parable, effecting **conscientization**: liberating people "from the manipulative myths which legitimized their oppressive and alienating society" and "making them conscious of their worth." Freirean pedagogy read back into the Gospels.
