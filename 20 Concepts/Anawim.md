# Anawim

Hebrew root: "bent, bowed down, afflicted." Soares-Prabhu fiercely rejects spiritualizing the poor: the anawim/ptochoi are the "sociologically poor" — "all those who are in any way, and not just economically, deprived of the means or the dignity they need to lead a fully human existence" (Lk 6:20). Three propositions: a *sociological* group (deprived situation), a *dialectical* group (oppressed by antagonists), a *dynamic* group (through whom God shapes history). Neither Marxist class nor pious metaphor.

### The three propositions
Soares-Prabhu summarises the biblical understanding of the poor in three principles: they are (1) a **sociological** group "whose identity is defined not by their religious attitude but by their social situation"; (2) a **dialectical** group "whose situation is determined by antagonistic groups standing over and against them"; (3) a **dynamic** group "who are not the passive victims of history but those through whom God shapes his history." And the crucial qualification: the poor are blessed "**not because their dehumanizing poverty is a spiritual value but precisely because it is an evil which he is about to bring to an end**."
