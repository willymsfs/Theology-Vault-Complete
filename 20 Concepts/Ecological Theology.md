# Ecological Theology

Theology that treats the **non-human creation** as a theological subject, not a stage set.

The standing tension is **anthropocentrism vs biocentrism**. Main lines: **ecofeminism** (Ruether, McFague) — the domination of women, of the poor and of the earth is one structure, sometimes with the Gaia hypothesis as its image; **panentheism and kenosis** (Peacocke, Moltmann) — a God who acts by self-limitation through natural processes; **cosmic Christology** (Teilhard, Boff) — creation internal to redemption; **Indian and tribal theology** — land, water and forest as the actual site of the option for the poor, which is where *Laudato si'* and adivasi struggle meet.
