# Bologna School

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Concepts/Bologna School (Vatican II course)|Bologna School — Vatican II course treatment]].


The historiographical school led by Giuseppe Alberigo, reading Vatican II as a historical "event" whose "spirit" is the primary interpretive key, emphasizing real discontinuity with the pre-conciliar Church.
