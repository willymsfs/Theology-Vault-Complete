# Abba Experience

Soares-Prabhu's foundational category: Jesus's experience of God as unconditionally loving Parent — "radically liberating" because it fulfills the basic human need to be loved, freeing from possession and attachment. God as parent → all people siblings → solidarity with the poor + confrontation with the powerful. Destroys religious legalism and purity boundaries. "To experience God as Abba is therefore to experience the 'wholly other' in the give and take of human history."

### Located, and given an interfaith parallel
The *Abba* experience occurred **at the baptism by John**, where Jesus "identified himself with sinful Israel" — and Soares-Prabhu calls it "**homologous to the 'enlightenment' of the Buddha under the boddhi tree, or the call-experience of the Old Testament prophets**." He insists it is not a "soothing anodyne" but "**profoundly unsettling**," demanding both personal conversion and the dismantling of oppressive structures. Sonship has as its "inseparable obverse" the experience of humankind as siblings, which "rules out all discrimination on any ground whatsoever." See [[Soares-Prabhu MOC]].
