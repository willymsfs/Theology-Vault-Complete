# Vedanta

"The end/culmination of the Vedas" — the most important of the [[Six Schools of Indian Philosophy]], the systematic teaching of the Upanishads. Its supreme reality is [[Brahman]]; the individual self (*atman*) is ultimately identical with it. [[Sankara]]'s [[Advaita]] is its classic form; [[Ramanuja]] modified it toward [[Bhakti]].

## Theological use
[[Brahmabandhab Upadhyaya]]: use Vedanta to translate the faith, as the Fathers used Greek philosophy.
