# Saccidananda ↔ Trinity

## The mapping ([[A. J. Appasamy]])
*Sat* (Being) → Father · *Chit* (Consciousness) → Son/Logos · *Ananda* (Bliss) → Holy Spirit

## Where it illuminates
Trinity as **experienced** reality (Being–Consciousness–Bliss), not abstract formula; speaks natively to the Indian religious imagination.

## Where it strains
[[Advaita]]'s impersonal [[Brahman]] vs the Trinity's **personal communion**. Appasamy's fix: keep "our separate personalities in our experience of union with God" — [[Bhakti]], not absorption.

## Verdict (mine, revisable)
Legitimate inculturation **provided personhood is kept**. Test against [[Eight Parameters of the Catholic Theological Enterprise]] and [[Evangelii Nuntiandi]] 65.
