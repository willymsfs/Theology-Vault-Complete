# Cave of the Heart ↔ Contemplative Prayer

## The mapping ([[Swami Abhishiktananda]])
The inward journey to "the cave of the heart" ↔ the Christian contemplative descent to the innermost self where one meets God.

## Where it illuminates
Corrects a purely external, object-like search for God; recovers the mystical, apophatic depth of Christian prayer via [[Advaita]].

## Where it strains
Non-dual absorption risks dissolving the I–Thou relationship that Christian prayer preserves. Abhishiktananda held the tension as "Christian Advaita."

## Verdict
Rich for a contemplative Indian Christianity if the personal encounter with God is kept.
