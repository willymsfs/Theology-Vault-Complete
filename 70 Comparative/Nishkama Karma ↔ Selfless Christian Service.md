# Nishkama Karma ↔ Selfless Christian Service

## The mapping
[[Nishkama Karma]] (action without attachment to fruits) ↔ Christian service "for God's glory," free of craving for reward or recognition.

## Where it illuminates
Purifies motive; a spirituality of work and duty; integrates action into the path to God (used in the [[Ashram Movement]]).

## Where it strains
Indian framing aims at *moksha*/detachment; Christian service is animated by **love of neighbour and the coming Kingdom**, not only inner purification.

## Verdict
Excellent ascetical bridge; complete it with the Christian motive of love.
