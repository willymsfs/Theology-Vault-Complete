# Moksha ↔ Salvation

## The mapping
[[Moksha]] (liberation from samsara, karma, ignorance) ↔ Christian salvation (freedom from sin and death).

## Where it illuminates
Both are **liberation and fulfilment**, not mere reward; both reject bondage. Neither is "escape from the world" at its best — Kingdom of God is justice/peace here, moksha is freedom-while-in-the-world.

## Where it strains
Advaitic moksha = merging into impersonal [[Brahman]]; Christian salvation = **communion of persons** with a personal God, and **resurrection of the body**, not soul escaping matter.

## Verdict
Strong analogy of *liberation*; diverges sharply on *personhood* and *embodiment*.
