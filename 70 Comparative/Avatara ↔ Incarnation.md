# Avatara ↔ Incarnation

## The mapping ([[Vengal Chakkarai]])
Christ experienced as *avatara* — God taking visible bodily form.

## Where it illuminates
Indian hearts already grasp God-made-visible through the avatara tradition; a natural doorway to the Incarnation.

## Where it strains
*Avatara* = one of **many** repeated descents; the Incarnation = **once-for-all**, full and permanent union of God and a real human history. Avatara can seem docetic (God only *appearing* human).

## Verdict
A useful bridge if the uniqueness and full humanity of Christ are safeguarded.
