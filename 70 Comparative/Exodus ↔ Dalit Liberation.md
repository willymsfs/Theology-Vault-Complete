# Exodus ↔ Dalit Liberation

## The mapping
The Exodus ("key paradigmatic text" of [[Liberation Theology]]) ↔ [[Dalit Theology]]'s hope of liberation from caste bondage.

## Where it illuminates
A God who "actively liberates people from unjust... systems"; the Crucified-and-Risen Jesus as power to revolt and ground of hope.

## Where it strains
Class-based Exodus reading must be widened to caste and religious pluralism → [[Indian liberation theology must add caste and pluralism to class analysis]].

## Verdict
The central paradigm for Indian liberation, needing the caste dimension the Latin American model lacks.
