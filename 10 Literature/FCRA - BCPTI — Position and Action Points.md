# Part 7 — BCPTI — Position and Action Points

**Bengal Catholic Priests' Training Institute**, Reg. ## ⚠ The live barrier — Object (g)

The charter authorises **donations and aid to any other charity**. Section 7, as amended in 2020, **absolutely prohibits transferring foreign contribution to any other person or organisation** — including one that itself holds FCRA registration.

**Object (g) is exercisable with local funds only.** Any proposal to give FC as a grant, subvention, or assistance to another society, parish, congregation, or NGO — including within the same diocese or religious family — must be refused. Penalty: **10% of the transferred amount or ₹1,00,000, whichever is higher, compoundable only once.**

## The two mapped purposes

1. **EDUCATIONAL** — theological training of Roman Catholic priests and ministers for the Ecclesiastical Region of Bengal
2. **RELIGIOUS** — religious education and formation **without conversion**

**Geography: West Bengal** — the Ecclesiastical Region of Bengal, comprising the Archdiocese of Calcutta with Krishnagar, Jalpaiguri, Baruipur and Raiganj. FC may not be utilised outside West Bengal without a Rule 17B amendment.

## What this settles

Because the approved purposes are Educational and Religious, **seminary and college infrastructure is squarely in scope** — classroom smart boards, theological library archives, and seminary building repairs are all expressly justified. For any such item the only remaining question is **programme or administrative**.

Conversely, anything falling under **Social, Cultural, or Economic** — a destitute home, a livelihood project, a cultural festival — is **outside BCPTI's registered purposes**, however worthy, unless the certificate is first widened under Rule 17B.
