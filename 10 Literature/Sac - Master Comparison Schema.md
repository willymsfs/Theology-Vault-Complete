# Unit 13 — Master Comparison Schema

| | **Baptism** | **Confirmation** |
|---|---|---|
| **Scriptural anchor** | Mt 28:19; Jn 3:5; Rom 6:3-4 | Acts 8:14-17; 2 Cor 1:21-22 |
| **Greek/Latin name** | *phōtismos*, *loutron palingenesias* | *chrisma / myron* (East); *confirmatio* (West) |
| **Matter** | Natural water | Sacred **chrism** (olive oil plus balsam) |
| **Form (current)** | "N., I baptise you in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit" | "N., **be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit**" |
| **Ordinary minister** | Bishop, priest, deacon (**anyone** in necessity) | **Bishop** (priest with faculty; always for adult initiation) |
| **Effects** | Remission of original and actual sin and all punishment; divine filiation; incorporation into Christ and Church; character | *Robur* — strengthening for witness; deeper rooting in divine filiation; firmer bond with the Church; character |
| **Character?** | Yes — unrepeatable | Yes — unrepeatable |
| **Necessary for salvation?** | Yes (with baptism of blood/desire) | No |
| **Analogy (Aquinas)** | Birth | Coming of age |
| **East vs West** | Same | East: **presbyter chrismates infants immediately** — unity preserved. West: **delayed** |
| **Trent canons** | Sess. VII, 14 canons | Sess. VII, 3 canons |
| **CCC** | 1213-1284 | 1285-1321 |
| **CIC** | cc. 849-878 | cc. 879-896 |
