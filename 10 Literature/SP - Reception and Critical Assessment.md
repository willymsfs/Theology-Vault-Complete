# Reception and Critical Assessment

Two bodies of literature stand between Soares-Prabhu and any uncritical use of him.

## The Jnanadeepa symposium (2015)

*Jnanadeepa: Pune Journal of Religious Studies* 18/2, ed. **George Karuvelil SJ**, reprints his essay and adds **eight responses** — the record of how his own tradition received him. Kaithakottil on the philosophy/theology crossroads · **Pattery** on its use for Indian theology today · **Kuthirakkattel** twice, on experience and context and on prophetic theologizing · **Helen Dantis on a feminist perspective** · Lemos on pastoral application · Jayard on science · Karuvelil on philosophical foundations.

Dantis's essay matters most, because it addresses precisely the gap Schweitzer names.

## Schweitzer's critical study

**Don Schweitzer** (St Andrew's College, Saskatoon) reads him as a distinctively Indian **postcolonial "hybrid"** — synthesising Latin American liberation theology, Western historical criticism, and a Hindu-inflected culture, preserving the gospel's transcendent liberating power while grounding it in India's struggles.

**Strengths:** exemplary exegesis, defensible to the international guild while remaining "objective, relevant and Indian"; a "Barth-like respect for the gospel's autonomy," refusing "an eternal covenant" with any culture so that Indian culture too must be confronted with Kingdom values; and treating the poor as a social group with its own insight.

**Growing edges:** neglect of feminist theology; a tendency to hold the Bible "finally has one story to tell," flattening canonical diversity and missing ecological traditions; and rejection of Chalcedon and Nicaea as culturally conditioned without building alternatives, leaving "an important Christian moral source untapped."

**His summary image:** the gospel's transcendence appears when it sets the church **"working, half in and half out"** of a culture — "half in by taking up issues of concern in a culture, half out by listening to and addressing these in light of the Biblical witness." Any framework is a hybrid whose critical edge stays "precarious and provisional."

## Why this matters for use

Writing *in his method* is not the same as writing hagiography. He was relentlessly critical of his own tradition; presenting him as beyond criticism is not in his spirit. In academic work, omitting these limitations is a defect that will be caught.
