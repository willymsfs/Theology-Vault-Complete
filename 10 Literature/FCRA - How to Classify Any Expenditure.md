# Part 1 — How to Classify Any Expenditure

This handbook exists to answer one question reliably: **given a proposed item of spending, may foreign contribution pay for it, and under what heading?**

Work through five steps in order. Step 0 is the one most often skipped, and it is the one with financial consequences.

## Step 0 — Programme or administrative?

This decides whether the spend counts against the **20% cap** on administrative expenses (Section 8(1)(b); Rule 5, FCRR 2011). Exceeding the cap is compoundable at 5% of the excess or ₹1,00,000, whichever is higher.

| Question | If yes |
|---|---|
| Is this directly in furtherance of a stated objective? | **Programme** — classify under a Schedule item; outside the cap |
| Is it running the organisation rather than delivering the work? | **Administrative** — inside the cap |

**Two statutory exclusions rescue an expense from the cap:**

- Salaries of personnel engaged in **training**, or in **collection and analysis of field data**, where the association is primarily engaged in research or training
- Expenses incurred **directly in furtherance of the stated objectives** — the Rule's own examples are *salaries to doctors of a hospital* and *salaries to teachers of a school*

**The test is FUNCTION, not the label on the invoice.** The same "repairs" line is administrative for the office block and programme for the classroom.

## Step 1 — Which of the five statutory categories?

**Religious · Cultural · Economic · Educational · Social.** There are no others (Section 11(1); Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)).

## Step 2 — Which numbered Schedule item?

Match to the Schedule reproduced in Part 3. Cite the ID, not merely the category.

## Step 3 — Three gating checks

A correct classification is not by itself authority to spend. All three must pass:

1. **Is that purpose on your registration certificate?** Since 22 June 2026 the certificate names specific approved purposes. Unapproved purpose: **30% of the amount or ₹1,00,000, whichever is higher.**
2. **Is the State on your certificate?** Registration is now geography-specific. Same penalty.
3. **Is it within your own Memorandum and Bye-laws?** FCRA approval does not override your objects clause. Both must permit it.

## Step 4 — Prohibition screen

Speculative investment · transfer or sub-granting · activity outside India · personal gain · assets in personal names · proselytisation (for the three Religious items that exclude it) · cash above ₹2,000 per day.

## The answer format

> **Classification:** Category — ID "Schedule item text"
> **Type:** Programme / Administrative — counts against 20% cap: yes/no
> **Conditions:** any provisos
> **Gating checks:** purpose on certificate? State on certificate? within objects?
> **Verdict:** Permissible / Permissible with conditions / Not permissible, and why
