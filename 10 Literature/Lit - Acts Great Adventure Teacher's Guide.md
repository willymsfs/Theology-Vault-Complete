# Lit — Acts Great Adventure Teacher's Guide

Twenty-session BTh course on Acts, framed as one expedition in three legs, built from the Cavins *Great Adventure* workbook. The full teaching notes live outside the Zettelkasten in the course folder — this note is the bridge.

**Course folder:** `Willy Dec 2025/Acts - Great Adventure/` — start at [[Acts - Great Adventure MOC]].
**Zettelkasten entry point:** [[Acts MOC]].

## What the guide contains
Each of the twenty sessions carries six blocks: Trailhead, Story Script, Deeper Look (exegesis, OT connections, Luke–Acts parallels), Greek/Hebrew terms, Guideposts (CCC and magisterial documents), and Campfire discussion with a written prayer. Three appendix tables give the full Greek word bank, the CCC list by session, and the four Pentecosts.

## Flagged inferences
The guide's own source note flags two places where narrative detail was filled from the text of Acts rather than the workbook: the trial details in [[S05 - First Resistance]], and parts of the courtroom sequence in [[S18 - The Prisoner Speaks]] (the forty conspirators, Felix's hope of a bribe).
