# Part 5 — Compliance Essentials

## What counts as foreign contribution

Section 2(1)(h): donation, delivery or transfer by a **foreign source** of any article (personal gift exempt up to **₹1,00,000**, Rule 6A), any currency, or any security.

- **Explanation 2** — interest on FC, and any income derived from FC, **is itself FC**
- **Explanation 3** — fees for goods or services in the ordinary course of business are **excluded**, including fees charged by an Indian educational institution to a foreign student
- **NRI vs OCI** — donations from an Indian citizen abroad from personal savings through banking channels are **not** FC. Donations from persons of Indian origin holding **foreign citizenship, including PIO and OCI, ARE** FC
- **Relative remittance** — not FC, but above **₹10 lakh** in a financial year must be intimated in **Form FC-1 within 3 months**
- **Delegate fees** from foreign participants at a conference are not FC if used to meet the event's costs

## The designated account — Section 17

All foreign contribution must be received into a single designated account at **State Bank of India, Sansad Marg / New Delhi Main Branch**. No other bank may credit a foreign inward remittance.

You may additionally hold one secondary FCRA account and one or more **utilisation accounts** in scheduled, PFMS-integrated banks. **No non-FC money may ever enter any of them.** A new utilisation account must be reported in **FC-6D within 45 days**.

## Registration versus prior permission

| | Registration | Prior permission |
|---|---|---|
| For | Established bodies | Formative stage |
| Existence required | 3 years | None |
| Spend threshold | **₹15 lakh** over 3 years on objects, excluding admin | None — donor commitment letter instead |
| Scope | Multiple donors; approved purposes and States | One donor, one amount, one project |
| Validity | **5 years**; renew via FC-3C within 6 months before expiry | Until that contribution is fully utilised |
| Renewal threshold | **₹10 lakh** FC utilised over preceding 2 FYs (Rule 14A) | — |
| Instalments | — | Next tranche only after **75%** utilised **plus MHA field inquiry** (Rule 9A) |

## Forms and deadlines

| Form | Purpose | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| FC-1 | Relative remittance above ₹10 lakh | 3 months |
| FC-2 | Foreign hospitality | About 2 weeks before travel |
| FC-3A / FC-3B | Registration / prior permission | — |
| FC-3BB | Next instalment under prior permission | After 75% utilised and field inquiry |
| FC-3C | Renewal | Within **6 months before expiry** |
| **FC-4** | **Annual return — NIL return mandatory** | **Within 9 months of FY close: 31 December** |
| FC-6A | Change of name or address | 45 days |
| FC-6B | Change of aims and objects | 45 days |
| FC-6D | Change of utilisation account | 45 days |
| FC-6E | Change of governing body | 45 days |
| **FC-6F** | **Migration to purpose-and-State framework; later scope changes** | **By 22 June 2027** |
| FC-7 | Surrender of registration | Any time |

**A correction worth recording.** Oral training material in circulation states that FC-4 is due **31 July**. That is wrong — it confuses FC-4 with the income-tax audit date. The Rules require filing **within nine months of the close of the financial year, i.e. 31 December**.

## Suspension, cancellation, refusal

- **Suspension (Section 13)** — up to 180 days, extendable to 360. During suspension at most **25%** of unutilised FC may be spent, with prior MHA approval; **75% is frozen**
- **Cancellation (Section 14)** — barred from applying afresh for **3 years**
- **Refused renewal** — reapply after **180 days**

## Penalties — S.O. 3287(E) compounding schedule

| Violation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Unapproved purpose or State | **30%** of amount, or ₹1,00,000 |
| Speculative investment | **30%** invested + 10% of returns, or ₹1,00,000 |
| Accepting FC without registration | **30%** of FC, or ₹1,00,000 |
| Transfer or sub-granting | **10%** of amount, or ₹1,00,000 — compoundable **once only** |
| Admin expenses above 20% | **5%** of the excess, or ₹1,00,000 |
| FC into a non-designated account | **5%**, or ₹1,00,000 |
| Local funds into an FC account | **2%**, or ₹1,00,000 |
| Failure to report or maintain accounts | **5%** of FC for the period, or ₹1,00,000 |
| Utilisation account not intimated in 45 days | **₹10,000** per account |
| Quarterly website disclosure failure | **₹10,000** each |

Maximum penalty in any instance is capped at the value of the foreign contribution received.
