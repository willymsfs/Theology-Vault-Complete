# Chapter V — The Criteria of Christian Theological Reflection

## A. Need for criteria
Faith's ultimates are "not negotiable under popular pressure." [[Evangelii Nuntiandi]] 65: cultural expression yet "content of faith neither impaired nor mutilated." Eckhart: "look at what its practitioners do."

## B. Three emphases
- Orthodox — synodality / *Sobornost*
- Protestant — *Sola Scriptura* / *Solus Deus*
- Catholic — Scripture **and** Tradition (Trent; [[Avery Dulles]])

## C. Authority
Infallibility (whole faithful [[Lumen Gentium]] 12; college of bishops; Pope *ex cathedra*) + four gradations of teaching/assent → [[Four Gradations of Magisterial Teaching and Assent]].
