# Part 2 — Worked Examples

## The instructive case: "maintenance of the seminary building"

Genuinely ambiguous, and the answer turns on **function**.

**If the space is where formation and teaching happen** — classrooms, chapel, library, student residence:

- **Educational EDU-01** — "Establishment and management of educational institutions and facilities, including… hostels, libraries…"
- **Religious REL-03** — "Preservation and support of institutions engaged in the study, documentation, and preservation of religious philosophy, theology and religious history." A seminary is squarely this
- For the chapel specifically, **Religious REL-01** — "Construction, renovation, and maintenance of… churches… and other places of worship"
- **Programme.** Outside the 20% cap

**If the portion maintained is the administrative office block:**

- **ADM-iii** — "repairs to premise(s) from where the organisation or Association is functioning"
- **Administrative.** Inside the cap

**The practical answer is apportionment.** Adopt a floor-area or usage basis, document it, apply it consistently. If 80% of the building is formation space and 20% administration, book the repair 80:20. Auditors accept a documented, consistent basis. They do not accept an undocumented assertion.

## A classification table for common items

| Description | Classification | Cap? |
|---|---|---|
| Salary of a school teacher | EDU-01, programme — Rule 5 names teachers expressly | No |
| Salary of the office accountant | ADM-iv, cost of administering funds | **Yes** |
| Salary of a field trainer (research/training body) | Programme — Rule 5 training exclusion | No |
| Honorarium to a board member for attending meetings | ADM-i — and disallowed outright if merely for holding office | **Yes** |

## The two that catch well-meaning organisations

**Sub-granting.** Section 7 bars transferring foreign contribution to **any** other person or organisation, whether or not they hold FCRA registration. There is no exception for partners, dioceses, congregations, or bodies in the same family.

**Interest.** Explanation 2 to Section 2(1)(h) makes interest on FC — and any income derived from FC — **itself foreign contribution**. It must be credited to the FC account and spent only on approved purposes.
