# Unit 14 — Examination Preparation

## Likely Essay Questions

1. "The history of penance is the history of a single sacrament housed in three incompatible structures." Discuss with reference to canonical, tariffed, and modern practice.
2. Why did canonical penance collapse, and what does its collapse reveal about the relation between doctrine and pastoral viability?
3. Assess the significance of the Novatian schism for the Church's understanding of its own power to forgive, comparing it with the Donatist controversy.
4. "Thesis 1 of the Ninety-Five Theses, not the abuses of Tetzel, is the real origin of the Reformation dispute over penance." Evaluate.
5. Explain and assess Trent's teaching that absolution is a **judicial act**, and show how the requirement of integral confession follows from it.
6. Trace the contrition/attrition debate from Abelard to Trent, and assess its pastoral consequences.
7. "The 1973 Rite recovered an ecclesial dimension that scholastic theology had always taught but practice had obscured." Discuss with reference to *LG* 11 and the *res et sacramentum*.
8. How should the Church respond to legislation requiring the breach of the seal of confession?
9. Account for the collapse in the practice of confession since the 1960s, and assess the proposed remedies.

## Ten-Second Recall Anchors

- ***Teshuvah* / *metanoia* = turning, not feeling.**
- **"Hermas: one plank, once."** — the single post-baptismal repentance.
- **"Sacrificati, thurificati, libellatici"** — the categories of the lapsed.
- **"Novatian says no, the Church says yes"** — no sin beyond forgiveness (*CCC* 982).
- **"Irish monks changed everything"** — private, repeatable, tariffed.
- ***Contraria contrariis sanantur*** — the penitentials' medicinal principle.
- **"1215: once a year, and the seal"** — *Omnis utriusque sexus*.
- **"Guilt versus temporal punishment"** — the distinction indulgences presuppose.
- **"Attrition suffices *with* the sacrament"** — Trent's pastoral settlement.
- **"Judicial act, therefore integral confession"** — Trent Session XIV.
- **"Borromeo built the box in the 1560s"** — younger than the Reformation.
- **"Reconciled with God *and with the Church*"** — *LG* 11.

## Comparative Analysis Exercises

- Tabulate canonical penance against tariffed penance across minister, repeatability, publicity, and sequence.
- Compare Tertullian's *De Paenitentia* with his *De Pudicitia* and account for the change.
- Set the four Reformation objections beside Trent Session XIV's canons and assess how directly each is met.
- Compare the medieval *Ego te absolvo* with the 1973 formula and list what the revision recovers.
- Compare Novatianism and Donatism as the same instinct applied to different sacraments.
