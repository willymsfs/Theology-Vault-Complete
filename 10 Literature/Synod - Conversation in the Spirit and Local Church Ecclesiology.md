# Synodality — Conversation in the Spirit and Local Church Ecclesiology (MTh)

Source: *Synodality* (Indian Edition, 2025), via NotebookLM.

## Conversation in the Spirit
See [[Conversation in the Spirit]] for the full method. [[Jude Nirmal Doss]] (pp. 424-429) supplies
the pneumatological grounding via Congar: the Spirit sources plurality as well as unity, guarding the
method against groupthink. [[John Alexander]] (pp. 379-383) analyses the epistemology: authority
(*potestas*) is subordinately bound to discernment (*discretio*), and *parrhesia* must precede silence
for the sequence to do its work. [[Lawrence Culas]] (pp. 168-169, 246-247) locates the safeguard in the
silence itself (Acts 15:12) and separately warns that *vox populi* is not automatically *vox Dei* —
see [[Vox Populi Vox Dei - The Majoritarian Risk]].

## Local Church and the communion of Churches
Grounded in *Lumen Gentium* and *Ad Gentes*'s restored ecclesiology of the local Church: the universal
Church as *communio Ecclesiarum*, not a multinational corporation with local franchises. See the
existing vault treatment at [[Eccl 25 - 25. universal-local churches- Ratzinger-Kasper]] for the
Ratzinger-Kasper debate this connects to. [[Varghese Koluthara]] frames the CCEO and the St Thomas
Christian *Yogams* as evidence this ecclesiology was locally realised, not merely theorised — see
[[CCEO and the St Thomas Christian Yogams]].
