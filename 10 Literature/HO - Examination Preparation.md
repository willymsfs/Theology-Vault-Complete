# Unit 14 — Examination Preparation

## Likely Essay Questions

1. "The New Testament never calls a Christian minister a priest." Explain the significance of this fact and assess how a Catholic theology of Holy Orders can accommodate it.
2. Trace the sacerdotalisation of Christian ministry from Clement of Rome to Cyprian, and explain its relation to the developing theology of the Eucharist.
3. "Vatican II settled a question that had been genuinely open for eight centuries." Discuss with reference to *LG* 21 and the scholastic treatment of the episcopate.
4. Assess *Sacramentum Ordinis* (1947) as a case study in the correction of a magisterial formulation.
5. Explain and evaluate the formula *essentia et non gradu tantum* (*LG* 10). Is it a genuine clarification or a restatement of the problem?
6. "Celibacy is discipline, not doctrine." Establish this historically and assess its implications for the *viri probati* debate.
7. Examine the argument of *Apostolicae Curae* and assess how far *Sacramentum Ordinis* and the Bonn Agreement have altered its force.
8. Is clericalism a distortion of the ministerial priesthood or a consequence of how it has been framed?
9. Assess the historical evidence for women deacons and its bearing on the contemporary question.
10. "Caste within the Church is a contradiction of Holy Orders more than of any other sacrament." Discuss.

## Ten-Second Recall Anchors

- **"Never *hiereus*"** — the NT vocabulary point.
- **"Acts 20:17 and 20:28"** — presbyters and bishops the same men.
- **"Clement gives us *laikos*; Ignatius gives us the monepiscopate."**
- **"Cyprian makes the bishop *sacerdos*"** — sacerdotalisation.
- **"Nicaea c. 4: three bishops. Chalcedon c. 6: no absolute ordinations."**
- **"Elvira continence, Trullo the Eastern settlement, Lateran II invalidity."**
- **"Seven orders, and no episcopate among them."**
- **"Florence said instruments; Pius XII said hands"** — *Sacramentum Ordinis*, 1947.
- **"*LG* 21: the fullness of the sacrament."**
- **"*Essentia et non gradu tantum* — but *ordered one to another*."**
- **"*Non ad sacerdotium sed ad ministerium*"** — the deacon, *LG* 29.
- **"Absolutely null and utterly void"** — *Apostolicae Curae*, 1896.

## Comparative Analysis Exercises

- Tabulate the ordination prayers of the *Apostolic Tradition* against the 1968 *Pontificalis Romani* rites and identify what was recovered.
- Compare Florence's *traditio instrumentorum* with *Sacramentum Ordinis* and state precisely what was corrected and on what grounds.
- Set Trent Session XXIII beside *LG* 21 and identify what Trent left open and how the Council closed it.
- Compare Luther's *Amt* with *LG* 10's *essentia et non gradu tantum* — locate the exact point of disagreement.
- Compare *Apostolicae Curae* with *Saepius Officio* and assess the Anglican counter-argument on its merits.
