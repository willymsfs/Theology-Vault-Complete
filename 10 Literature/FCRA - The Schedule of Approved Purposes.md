# Part 3 — The Schedule of Approved Purposes

The Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Rules 2026 abolished narrative, self-described aims. An applicant must now select purposes **strictly from this Schedule**, and the registration certificate names the approved purposes and States.

## Citing the authority correctly

Two distinct provisions are involved, and conflating them is a common error:

- **Rule 11** of the Amendment Rules 2026 **inserted** this Schedule into the FCRR 2011
- **Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)** is the **operative provision obliging** an applicant to select activities exclusively from it

For a formal compliance document — the FC-6F filing above all — cite both:

> *Schedule inserted by Rule 11, FCRR (Amendment) Rules 2026 [S.O. 3272(E), 22 June 2026]; selection from the Schedule mandated by Rule 9(1B)(b)(i).*

**105 approved purposes across five categories.**

**Note the proselytisation exclusion** attached to Religious items **REL-03, REL-06 and REL-08**. For a seminary this is a drafting discipline as much as an activity one: formation and education must be described, in records and in FC-4, as institutional and academic rather than evangelistic.
