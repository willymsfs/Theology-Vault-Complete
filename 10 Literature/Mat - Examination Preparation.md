# Unit 14 — Examination Preparation

## Likely Essay Questions

1. "Jesus's argument on divorce is not a ruling between Shammai and Hillel but an appeal behind Moses to creation." Discuss, and assess its force as a foundation for indissolubility.
2. Examine the Matthean exception clause and evaluate the principal interpretations.
3. Trace Augustine's three goods of marriage and assess how far his account of concupiscence shaped the Western tradition's unease about sexuality.
4. Explain the twelfth-century dispute between the consent and consummation schools, and show how Alexander III's synthesis governs Catholic law to this day.
5. "The most consequential act of Trent on marriage was disciplinary, not doctrinal." Discuss with reference to *Tametsi*.
6. Account for the condemnation of Doms and von Hildebrand in 1944 and the substantial reception of their position at Vatican II.
7. What exactly did *Gaudium et Spes* change about the ends of marriage, and how is the change registered in the 1983 Code?
8. Assess the argument of *Humanae Vitae* 12 and the reasons for its contested reception.
9. "Amoris Laetitia 8 either develops *Familiaris Consortio* 84 or contradicts it." Argue for one reading.
10. Is arranged marriage compatible with the Church's doctrine of matrimonial consent? Answer with reference to cc. 1057 and 1103.
11. "Marriage is where caste is reproduced in the Indian Church." Discuss the theological implications.

## Ten-Second Recall Anchors

- ***'ezer kenegdo*** — a counterpart standing face to face, not a subordinate.
- **"From the beginning it was not so"** — Jesus goes behind Moses to creation.
- ***Mystērion* → *sacramentum*** — the Vulgate bridge at Eph 5:32.
- **"Proles, fides, sacramentum"** — Augustine's three goods.
- ***Consensus facit nuptias*** — consent makes the marriage; consummation makes it absolutely indissoluble.
- **"The spouses are the ministers"** — unique among the seven; the East differs.
- **"Tametsi 1563: form for validity"** — and *Ne Temere* 1907 made it universal.
- **"1944 condemned it; 1965 received it"** — the personalist turn.
- **"GS 50 drops the hierarchy of ends"** — and c. 1055 names *bonum coniugum* first.
- **"HV 12: the inseparable connection."**
- **"Footnote 351"** — and the Buenos Aires letter in the *AAS*.
- **"Nullity is not divorce."**

## Comparative Analysis Exercises

- Compare 1917 CIC c. 1013 §1 with 1983 CIC c. 1055 §1 and state precisely what changed.
- Set Trent Session XXIV canon 7 beside the Orthodox practice of *oikonomia* and account for the canon's careful wording.
- Compare *Familiaris Consortio* 84 with *Amoris Laetitia* 301-305 and footnote 351.
- Compare the Latin and Byzantine forms of the sacrament (consent versus crowning) and trace the consequences for the treatment of second marriages.
- Compare the majority and minority reports of the Birth Control Commission with *Humanae Vitae* 12.
