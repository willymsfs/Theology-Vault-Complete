# Unit 14 — Examination Preparation

## Likely Essay Questions

1. *"Confirmation is a rite in search of a theology."* Discuss with reference to its historical separation from baptism in the West.
2. Trace the development of the doctrine of *ex opere operato* from Augustine's controversy with the Donatists to Trent Session VII.
3. Assess the theological coherence of the current Western sequence Baptism, Eucharist, Confirmation in light of *CCC* 1322 and the Restored Order movement.
4. "The RCIA is Vatican II's *ressourcement* method applied to sacramental practice." Evaluate.
5. How far does *Unitatis Redintegratio* 22, read together with Trent Session VII canon 4, commit the Catholic Church to recognising Protestant baptism?
6. Examine Galatians 3:27-28 as a resource for a theology of baptism in a caste society.
7. Why did the Fathers hold that the sinless Christ submitted to a baptism of repentance, and what does each answer imply for our theology of baptism?
8. "Confirmation became a separate sacrament in the West by administrative accident." Assess this claim and its dogmatic implications.

## Ten-Second Recall Anchors

- **"Water, Word, Wind"** — matter, form, Spirit.
- **"Riez and Orange, 439 and 441"** — where *confirmatio* got its name.
- **"1910 broke the order"** — *Quam Singulari*.
- **"1971 borrowed from Byzantium"** — *Accipe signaculum Doni Spiritus Sancti*.
- **"Session VII: 13-14-3"** — Trent's canons on sacraments in general, baptism, confirmation.
- **"Judas baptises, Christ baptises"** — Augustine on validity vs. fruitfulness.
- **"Baptizatus sum"** — Luther's present tense.

## Comparative Analysis Exercises

- Compare the *Apostolic Tradition* §21 with the 1972 RCIA structure, stage by stage.
- Compare Trent Session VII canon 4 with *Unitatis Redintegratio* 22 — is the second a development of the first, or a reversal in tone?
- Compare Aquinas's *ST* III q.72 with *CCC* 1308 on the "maturity" reading of Confirmation.
- Compare the Eastern chrismation of infants with the Western Restored Order proposals.
