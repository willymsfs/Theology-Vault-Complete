# Synodality — Five Core Principles and Trinitarian Grounding (BTh/MTh)

Source: *Synodality* (Indian Edition, 2025), via NotebookLM.

## The five principles
See [[Five Core Principles of Synodality]] for the full schema: *sensus fidei fidelium*; the People of
God and baptismal dignity (the *tria munera*); communion-participation-mission; *Omnes, Aliqui, Unus*
(see [[Omnes Aliqui Unus]]); synodality-collegiality-primacy as concentric circles.

## Trinitarian grounding (MTh)
[[Bishop Lawrence Pius]] (pp. 322-334) grounds synodality in *perichoresis* — the eternal exchange of
equals in Trinitarian communion, with no hierarchy of domination — and in Eucharistic *koinonia*, the
Eucharist as the primary synodal event where status, class and gender are relativised before the table
(pp. 330-331). [[Jacob Parappally]] (pp. 391-397) extends this into theological anthropology: the human
person as *imago Trinitatis*, with three relational dimensions (transcendence/openness, transparency/
vulnerability, interiority/discernment) mirroring Father, Son and Spirit — so that community failure is
always traceable to a failure of one of the three.

## Sensus fidei as locus theologicus (MTh)
See [[Sensus Fidei as Locus Theologicus]]. Under Melchior Cano's framework, consultation of the laity
is a theological necessity, not a democratic concession, breaking the post-Tridentine *Ecclesia
docens*/*Ecclesia discens* binary.
