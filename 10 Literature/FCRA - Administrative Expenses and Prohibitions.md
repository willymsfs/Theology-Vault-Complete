# Part 4 — Administrative Expenses and Prohibitions

## Administrative expenses — Rule 5, FCRR 2011

Capped at **20% of foreign contribution received during the financial year** (Section 8(1)(b)).

| ID | Item |
|---|---|
| **ADM-i** | Salaries, wages, travel expenses or any remuneration realised by Members of the Executive Committee or Governing Council |
| **ADM-ii** | All expenses towards hiring of personnel for management of activities, and salaries/wages/remuneration including travel paid to such personnel |
| **ADM-iii** | Consumables: electricity and water charges, telephone charges, postal charges, repairs to premises from where the organisation functions, stationery and printing, transport and travel by Committee members, expenditure on office equipment |
| **ADM-iv** | Cost of accounting for and administering funds |
| **ADM-v** | Expenses towards running and maintenance of vehicles |
| **ADM-vi** | Cost of writing and filing reports |
| **ADM-vii** | Legal and professional charges |
| **ADM-viii** | Rent of premises, repairs to premises and expenses on other utilities |

### Excluded from the cap

- Salaries/remuneration of personnel engaged in TRAINING or in COLLECTION/ANALYSIS OF FIELD DATA, where the association is primarily engaged in research or training — NOT administrative.
- Expenses incurred DIRECTLY in furtherance of stated objectives — e.g. salaries to doctors of a hospital, salaries to teachers of a school — NOT administrative.

## Prohibitions

| ID | Prohibition | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **PRO-01** | Speculative activities — any investment with risk of appreciation/depreciation linked to market forces, including mutual funds and shares; schemes promising high returns such as chits or land not directly linked to declared objects | Rule 4 FCRR 2011; Sec 8(1) |
| **PRO-02** | Transfer or sub-granting of foreign contribution to ANY other person or organisation, whether or not that body holds FCRA registration | Sec 7 (as amended 2020) |
| **PRO-03** | Any purpose, activity, or State/UT not explicitly specified and approved on the Registration Certificate | Rule 9(1B); Sec 8(1)(a) |
| **PRO-04** | Activities carried out outside India | Rule 9(5) Explanation 2 (2026) |
| **PRO-05** | Administrative expenses exceeding 20% of FC received in the financial year | Sec 8(1)(b); Rule 5 |
| **PRO-06** | Personal gain; diversion to personal accounts or relatives of trustees/office bearers | Sec 12(4)(a) |
| **PRO-07** | Propagation of sedition or advocacy of violent methods; activity detrimental to national interest | Sec 12(4)(a)-(b) |
| **PRO-08** | Acquisition of assets in the personal name of office bearers rather than the association | Rule 4 / asset ownership mandate |
| **PRO-09** | Proselytisation — expressly excluded from Religious Schedule items 3, 6 and 8 | Schedule, Religious category |
| **PRO-10** | Cash payments exceeding Rs 2,000 per day from FC funds (advisory practice limit; IT Act permits Rs 10,000) | FCRA advisory practice |
