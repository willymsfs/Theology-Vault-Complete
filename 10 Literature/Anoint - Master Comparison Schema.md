# Unit 12 — Master Comparison Schema

## The Sacrament Across the Eras

| Era | Who blessed the oil | Who applied it | For whom | Name | Expected effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NT** | — | Presbyters (Jas 5); the Twelve (Mk 6:13) | The sick (*asthenei*) | — | Save, raise up, forgive |
| **Patristic (to c. 800)** | **Bishop** | **Anyone** — priest, layperson, the sick themselves | The sick, for recovery | *Unctio infirmorum* | **Healing**, of body and soul |
| **Carolingian to medieval** | Bishop | **Priest only** | **The dying** | ***Unctio extrema*** | Remission of the *reliquiae peccati*; preparation for death |
| **Trent (1551)** | Bishop | Priest | "**Especially**" those near death | Extreme Unction | Grace, strengthening, forgiveness, **sometimes bodily health** |
| **Since 1972** | Bishop (priest in necessity, c. 999) | Priest | Those **beginning to be in danger** from sickness or old age; repeatable | **Anointing of the Sick** | Strengthening, union with the passion, forgiveness, **healing if conducive to salvation** |

## The Three Anointings of Christian Life

| Sacrament | Anoints for | Oil |
|---|---|---|
| **Baptism** | Incorporation into Christ, priest/prophet/king | Chrism (after the washing) |
| **Confirmation** | Mission and witness | **Chrism** |
| **Anointing of the Sick** | The final struggle and passage | **Oil of the Sick** (*oleum infirmorum*) |

*(The three oils — chrism, oil of catechumens, oil of the sick — are blessed at the Chrism Mass of Holy Thursday.)*

## Comparison with the Eastern Euchelaion

| | **Latin Anointing** | **Orthodox Euchelaion** |
|---|---|---|
| Ministers | One priest suffices | Ideally **seven priests** |
| Structure | Forehead and hands; one form | **Seven** epistles, gospels, prayers, anointings |
| Orientation | Recovered healing emphasis (1972) | **Never lost** the healing emphasis |
| Recipients | The seriously ill | The ill, and in some traditions the healthy in Holy Week |
| "Extreme unction" phase | Yes, c. 9th-20th c. | **Never developed** |
