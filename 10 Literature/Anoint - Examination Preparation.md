# Unit 13 — Examination Preparation

## Likely Essay Questions

1. "The history of this sacrament is a case study in how liturgical accident generates theology." Discuss with reference to the Carolingian reform and the scholastic doctrine of the *reliquiae peccati*.
2. Give a detailed exegesis of James 5:14-15 and assess how far it supports the Tridentine doctrine of Extreme Unction.
3. "Vatican II did not contradict Trent on this sacrament; it exploited a deliberate reticence in Trent's wording." Evaluate, with attention to the word *praesertim*.
4. Assess the significance of Innocent I's Letter to Decentius for the contemporary debate over who may administer the sacrament.
5. Why did Cardinal Cajetan's exegesis of James 5 not lead to a Catholic reform in the sixteenth century, and what changed by 1972?
6. "The claim that the sick person contributes to the good of the Church is either the sacrament's deepest insight or its cruellest evasion." Discuss with reference to *LG* 11 and Colossians 1:24.
7. Compare the Latin sacrament with the Orthodox *euchelaion* and account for their divergence.
8. Assess the pastoral relationship between the sacrament of anointing and popular healing devotion in the Indian context.

## Ten-Second Recall Anchors

- **"Mark 6:13 insinuates, James 5 promulgates"** — Trent's own verbs.
- **"Innocent I, 416: *omnibus christianis*"** — all Christians may use the blessed oil.
- **"Carolingians clericalised it; the Lombard renamed it"** — *unctio extrema*.
- ***Reliquiae peccati*** — Aquinas's proper effect: the remnants of sin.
- **"*Praesertim* — 'especially,' not 'only'"** — the word that left Trent's door open.
- **"SC 73: *aptius* — 'more fittingly' called Anointing of the Sick."**
- **"Forehead and hands"** — the essential rite since 1972.
- **"Save and raise up"** — the 1972 form tracking James 5:15.
- **"Viaticum outranks anointing for the dying."**
- **"Seven priests, seven gospels"** — the Orthodox *euchelaion*.

## Comparative Analysis Exercises

- Tabulate the sacrament across the five eras (Unit 12's master schema) and identify at each stage what changed and what caused the change.
- Compare Florence's *"nisi infirmo, de cuius morte timetur"* (1439) with *SC* 73 and state precisely what was reversed.
- Compare the medieval anointing of the five senses with the 1972 anointing of forehead and hands, and account for the reduction.
- Set Innocent I's letter beside CIC c. 1003 and construct the strongest historical case for and against extending the ministry.
