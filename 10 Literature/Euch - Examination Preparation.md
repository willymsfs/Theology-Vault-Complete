# Unit 14 — Examination Preparation

## Likely Essay Questions

1. "Transubstantiation is a defence of a mystery, not an explanation of it." Discuss with reference to Lateran IV, Aquinas, and Trent Session XIII.
2. Compare the two oaths imposed on Berengar (1059, 1079) and assess what they show about the development of doctrine.
3. Account for the Reformation division over the Eucharist, distinguishing carefully the question of presence from the question of sacrifice.
4. "The recovery of *anamnesis* has done more for Eucharistic ecumenism than any other single factor." Evaluate.
5. Assess *Mysterium Fidei*'s response to transignification. Was Paul VI right that meaning cannot replace being?
6. Examine de Lubac's thesis in *Corpus Mysticum* and its consequences for Catholic ecclesiology.
7. "If the one bread makes the many one body, caste within the Church is not an imperfection but a contradiction." Discuss.
8. How far does John 6 support Eucharistic realism, given that the discourse never mentions the Supper?
9. Compare the Eastern and Western accounts of the consecratory moment, and assess the significance of the 1969 Missal's restored epiclesis.

## Ten-Second Recall Anchors

- **"XIII, XXI, XXII"** — Trent's three Eucharistic sessions: presence, both kinds, sacrifice.
- **"1215 uses the word, Aquinas builds the metaphysics"** — Lateran IV and *ST* III qq. 73-83.
- **"1059 crude, 1079 precise"** — Berengar's two oaths.
- **"HOC EST CORPUS MEUM on the table"** — Marburg, 1529.
- **"Same victim, same priest, different manner"** — Trent Session XXII on sacrifice.
- **"Per modum substantiae"** — Aquinas: not local presence, hence whole Christ in every fragment.
- **"The terms swapped places"** — de Lubac on *corpus verum* / *corpus mysticum*.
- **"Be what you see, receive what you are"** — Augustine, *Sermon* 272.

## Comparative Analysis Exercises

- Set Justin's *First Apology* 65-67 beside the Order of Mass of the 1969 Missal and map the correspondences.
- Compare the 1549 and 1552 Prayer Book words of administration and state the doctrinal shift.
- Compare Trent Session XIII with the BEM Eucharist section — what is genuinely agreed, and what is left open?
- Compare Aquinas *ST* III q.75 with Chauvet's critique of "productionist" causality.
