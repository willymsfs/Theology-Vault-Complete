# Part 6 — The 2026 Framework Shift

The **Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Rules 2026**, notified as **S.O. 3272(E) on 22 June 2026** with compounding notification **S.O. 3287(E)**, changed registration from an organisation-centric licence to a **purpose-based and geography-specific** one.

## What changed

1. **The certificate names purposes and States** (Rule 9(1B)(a)). Utilising FC for an unlisted purpose, or in an unlisted State or UT, is a compoundable offence.
2. **Narrative aims abolished** (Rule 9(1B)(b)(i)). Purposes must come from the prescribed Schedule.
3. **Form FC-6F migration** (Rule 9(1B)(c)) — every association registered before 22 June 2026 must declare its purposes and States **within one year: by 22 June 2027**.
4. **"Key functionary" defined** (Rule 2(1)(ca)) — anyone exercising control or responsible for management. Scrutiny moves from every committee member to those actually in control, **concentrating personal liability on them**. A **reverse burden of proof** applies: key functionaries are presumed liable for diversion unless they show due diligence and good faith.
5. **Fees** (Rule 9(4)) — the base fee covers one purpose and one State; **₹300** for each additional purpose or State.
6. **Foreign citizens** (Rule 9(5) Expl. 1) — associations with foreign citizens as key functionaries are ordinarily ineligible absent a specific order.
7. **Activities outside India excluded** (Rule 9(5) Expl. 2).
8. **Prior permission instalments** (Rule 9A) — released only after **75% utilisation** and MHA field verification.
9. **Renewal threshold** (Rule 14A) — **₹10 lakh** FC utilised over the preceding two financial years.
10. **Scope change** (Rule 17B) — adding or deleting a purpose or State requires FC-6F, a governing body resolution, and ₹300 per item.
11. **Reporting deepened** — FC-3C renewal needs a three-year activity report; FC-4 needs project-wise and location-wise utilisation, asset creation, publications, website and social media accounts, and **ultimate donor disclosure** where funds arrive through Donor Advised Funds.

## ⚠ Notified Rules versus the pending Bill

Everything above is **notified and in force** — S.O. 3272(E) and S.O. 3287(E), both dated 22 June 2026. Plan compliance against it.

What follows sits in the **FCRA Amendment Bill 2026**, which is a **Bill and not yet law**. It should **not** be relied on for planning:

- **Appeal to the District Judge** against MHA orders
- **Imprisonment capped at 1 year**, reduced from 5
- **Provisional asset vesting** — assets created with FC vest in a government custodian if registration ceases, with **full restoration on re-registration**; places of worship retain their religious character throughout
- **State agencies may not investigate** FCRA matters without central coordination

The asset-restoration and places-of-worship provisions are the ones that would matter most to a seminary. **Until enactment, assume the harsher position:** if registration lapses, FC-created assets — the seminary buildings among them — are exposed without a guaranteed restoration route.

That is an argument for treating the FC-6F deadline and the Rule 14A ₹10 lakh threshold as hard operational priorities rather than paperwork.
