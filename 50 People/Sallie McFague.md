# Sallie McFague

American theologian; made metaphor the working unit of theology.

## Signature ideas
**Metaphorical theology** — all God-language is metaphor, and the question is which metaphors are life-giving now. *Models of God*: God as mother, lover, friend; **the world as God's body** — a model designed to make ecological indifference theologically impossible.
