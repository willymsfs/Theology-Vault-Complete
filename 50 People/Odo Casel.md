# Odo Casel

Developed Mysteriengegenwart — the doctrine that the saving acts of Christ themselves, not merely their fruits, are made really present in the liturgical mystery. The single most influential theological idea behind the twentieth-century liturgical reform, though criticised for the obscurity of its metaphysics.
