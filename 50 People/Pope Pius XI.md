# Pope Pius XI

Issued Casti Connubii (1930) in direct response to the Anglican Lambeth Conference's cautious permission of contraception — reaffirming the three Augustinian goods, declaring contraception intrinsically vicious, and upholding indissolubility, while also containing warm language about conjugal love that the personalists would later cite.
