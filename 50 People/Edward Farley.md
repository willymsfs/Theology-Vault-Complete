# Edward Farley

Vanderbilt theologian; Husserlian phenomenology applied to the Church's own mode of existence.

## Signature ideas
**Ecclesial being** and **ecclesial universals** — the structures of the community's "social duration," described phenomenologically rather than deduced from authority. *Theologia*: theological education has lost its unity as a habit of wisdom and become a cluster of specialisms.

## Criticism
Avoids authoritarianism at the risk of losing prophetic critique; Milbank treats this mutual assimilation of theology and sociology as a surrender.
