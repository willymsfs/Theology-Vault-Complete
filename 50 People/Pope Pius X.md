# Pope Pius X

His decree Quam Singulari (1910) lowered the age of First Communion to the age of discretion — a major pastoral reform whose unintended consequence was to invert the ancient order of Christian initiation in the West, since Confirmation remained tied to episcopal visitation.

## Eucharist (BTh course)
Beyond Quam Singulari (1910), his Tra le Sollecitudini (1903) coined "active participation" as the primary source of the Christian spirit, and Sacra Tridentina (1905) encouraged frequent and even daily communion — reversing centuries of infrequent reception and preparing the ground for the Liturgical Movement.
