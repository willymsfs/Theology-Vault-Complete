# Kwame Bediako

Ghanaian theologian; with Lamin Sanneh the architect of **translation theology**.

## Signature idea
African Christianity grew explosively because the gospel is **translatable** into African vernaculars — which makes Christianity a genuinely African religion by right, not a Western import received on sufferance.

## Consequence
The story of African Christianity shifts from missionary agency to **African agency**; the vernacular Bible, not the mission station, is the engine.
