# C. S. Song

Taiwanese Presbyterian theologian; the leading advocate of Asian **story theology**.

## Signature idea
The **"leap from Israel to Asia"** — Asian creation stories, folk tales and histories are granted revelatory status rather than being treated as raw material awaiting a biblical key.

## Key terms
Story theology · **homeland theology** (Taiwan) · the third-eye of Asian imagination.

## Method companion
Archie Lee's **cross-textual hermeneutics**: read Text A (Asian realities and stories) alongside Text B (the Bible) and let meaning arise between them.
