# Cornelius

A centurion of the Italian Cohort at Caesarea; devout, almsgiving, praying at the hour of the evening sacrifice — a God-fearer, not a covenant member. His household is where the Spirit falls on the uncircumcised. The pastoral point of the episode is that his sincerity is real and insufficient: his prayers are heard precisely so that Peter would be sent to him.
