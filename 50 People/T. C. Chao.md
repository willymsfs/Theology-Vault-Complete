# T. C. Chao

Zhao Zichen: the outstanding Chinese theologian of the Republican era, a WCC president, later imprisoned by the Japanese and marginalized after 1949.

## Signature idea
Christianity restated in **Chinese cultural form** — the "Spiritual Christ," a Jesus intelligible within Confucian moral personality — moving after his imprisonment toward a more christocentric and less liberal account.
