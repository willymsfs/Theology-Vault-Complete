# C. H. Dodd

The dominant British New Testament scholar of his generation; director of the New English Bible.

## Signature idea
**Realized eschatology** — in Jesus's ministry the kingdom has *already come*, not merely drawn near; the parables are read accordingly. Also the recovery of the **kerygma** as the common apostolic preaching behind the New Testament writings.
