# Clodovis Boff

Brazilian theologian, brother of [[Leonardo Boff]]; supplied liberation theology its epistemology.

## Signature contribution
*Theology and Praxis*: the **three mediations** — socio-analytic (name the situation with the social sciences), hermeneutic (read the tradition from there), practical (act, then re-read). This is what protects liberation theology from being sociology with proof-texts.

## Later turn
Publicly argued that the movement had made the poor rather than Christ its first principle — a break with his brother's position.
