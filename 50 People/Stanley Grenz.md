# Stanley Grenz

Canadian Baptist theologian; recast evangelical theology for a post-foundationalist setting.

## Signature ideas
*Beyond Foundationalism* — theology after the collapse of the evidentialist project: scripture, tradition and culture as a **triad of sources** rather than a single foundation. *Theology for the Community of God* — the community, not the individual believer, as theology's context; the *imago Dei* as relational.
