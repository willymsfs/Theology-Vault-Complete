# Vincent Ekka

Documents tribal communitarian culture as a natural synodal soil, prior to and independent of the
Church's use of the word.

## Signature idea
Tribal life is anchored in **commensality** — communal eating after every ritual ceremony — which
functions as a levelling mechanism, smoothing personal discord and restoring solidarity, and in an
**egalitarian kinship** structure that culturally rejects hierarchical class division. Ekka reads this
alongside the sacred grove (*Sarna*, *Jaherthan*) and the tribal monotheistic vision (*Singbonga*,
*Dharmes*, *Thakurjiv*) as making the tribal Church "an inspiring lived model of synodality" rather
than a late convert to it.
