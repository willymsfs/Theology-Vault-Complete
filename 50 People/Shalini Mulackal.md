# Shalini Mulackal

Indian theologian who names the structural reason synodal renewal keeps stalling.

## Signature idea
Vatican II left two ecclesiologies side by side, never reconciled: a traditional monarchical-
hierarchical model and a communio-synodal model. Because both stand in the same conciliar texts,
progressives and conservatives each find textual support for incompatible visions — producing
systemic paralysis, with synodal structures continually muzzled by Roman and episcopal centralisation.
