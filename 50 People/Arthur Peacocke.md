# Arthur Peacocke

British biochemist and Anglican priest; with Polkinghorne and Barbour one of the founders of the modern science-and-religion field.

## Signature ideas
**Emergence** (higher levels of organization are real, not reducible) · **top-down divine action** — God influences the state of the whole system, which shapes its parts, as mind influences body · **panentheism** · humanity as **"co-creator, co-worker and co-explorer with God."**

## On suffering
The suffering built into evolutionary process pushes him, with [[Jurgen Moltmann]], toward a **"pathetic, self-emptying God"** who shares intimately in the suffering of every creature.
