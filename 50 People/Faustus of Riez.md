# Faustus of Riez

A Pentecost homily attributed to him supplies the classic Western rationale for Confirmation as distinct from Baptism: baptism gives rebirth for innocence, confirmation gives augmentum ad gratiam and strengthening for battle — the origin of the "soldier of Christ" imagery. Passages later entered the Pseudo-Isidorian Decretals under a pope's name, lending the argument spurious authority the scholastics inherited in good faith.
