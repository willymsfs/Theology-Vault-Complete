# Walter Kasper

His February 2014 address to the consistory proposed a limited penitential path by which some divorced and civilly remarried Catholics might be admitted to the sacraments, appealing to Orthodox oikonomia and patristic precedent — the proposal that framed the two Synods on the family and the debate over Amoris Laetitia.
