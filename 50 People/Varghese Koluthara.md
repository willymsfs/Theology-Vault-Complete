# Varghese Koluthara

Specialist in the CCEO, presenting the Syro-Malabar and Syro-Malankara Synods as a working canonical
model the Latin Church's synodal reform has yet to reach.

## Signature idea
Unlike the Latin Synod of Bishops (purely advisory), the Synods of the Patriarchal and Major
Archiepiscopal Eastern Catholic Churches hold actual legislative, executive and judicial power —
electing their own Patriarchs and Major Archbishops, erecting eparchies, enacting particular law
without prior Roman approval. Frames Catholic dialogue with Orthodox *sobornost* as a resource for
rebalancing papal primacy and episcopal collegiality, since Orthodox theology holds that no bishop
acts outside the college or the consent of his flock.
