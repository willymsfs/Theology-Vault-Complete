# Pope Paul VI

Elected 21 June 1963; guided Sessions II-IV to completion; personally intervened repeatedly during "Black Week" (Nota Praevia, Dignitatis Humanae postponement, Mother of the Church proclamation); later issued Humanae Vitae (1968), bypassing conciliar collegial structures.

## Sacraments (BTh course)
Promulgated the Apostolic Constitution Divinae Consortium Naturae (15 Aug 1971), locating Confirmation firmly within Christian initiation and defining its essential rite as anointing with chrism on the forehead with the laying on of the hand, using the form "Be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit" — deliberately borrowed from the Byzantine rite, an act of ecumenical ressourcement.

See: [[Sacraments MOC]]


## Eucharist (BTh course)
Issued Mysterium Fidei (1965) during the Council to check speculative drift, insisting that transubstantiation names an ontological change and that transignification and transfinalization are inadequate if offered as substitutes rather than complements. Promulgated the reformed Roman Missal (1969/1970) with four Eucharistic Prayers, a restored epiclesis, and an expanded lectionary.

See: [[Eucharist MOC]]


## Penance (BTh course)
Indulgentiarum Doctrina (1967) substantially reformed the system of indulgences, abolishing the misleading numerical attachments of days and years and re-grounding the practice in the communion of saints.

See: [[Penance MOC]]


## Anointing of the Sick (BTh course)
Promulgated the Apostolic Constitution Sacram Unctionem Infirmorum (30 November 1972), reducing the essential rite to the anointing of forehead and hands, permitting another plant oil where olive oil is unavailable, and giving the new form whose second sentence — "may the Lord who frees you from sin save you and raise you up" — closely tracks James 5:15.

See: [[Anointing MOC]]


## Holy Orders (BTh course)
Restored the permanent diaconate in the Latin Church by Sacrum Diaconatus Ordinem (1967), promulgated the revised ordination rites in Pontificalis Romani (1968) — whose prayer for episcopal ordination is drawn substantially from the Apostolic Tradition — and suppressed the minor orders and subdiaconate by Ministeria Quaedam (1972).

See: [[Holy Orders MOC]]


## Matrimony (BTh course)
Issued Humanae Vitae (1968) against the majority report of his own birth control commission, resting the teaching on the "inseparable connection" between the unitive and procreative significances of the marriage act (HV 12). The reception crisis that followed was the gravest of the post-conciliar period and had consequences for the practice of confession as well as for marriage.

See: [[Matrimony MOC]]
