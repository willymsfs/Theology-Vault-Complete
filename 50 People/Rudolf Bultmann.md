# Rudolf Bultmann

German New Testament scholar and theologian; with [[Barth]] a founder of the theology of the Word, then its most controversial dissenter.

## Signature idea
**Demythologization** — interpret mythological texts existentially to expose their continuing challenge to human **decision**, rather than discarding them or defending their worldview.

## Key terms
*Kerygma* (the proclamation, not the reconstructed Jesus) · existential interpretation · form criticism.

## Method
Ford's Type 4 in its purest form: Christianity reinterpreted consistently through a modern philosophy (Heidegger's existential analytic). What matters is not *what happened* but *that I am addressed*.

## Criticism
Evacuates the historical particularity of the incarnation; reduces salvation history to individual self-understanding; Thielicke's charge — theology collapsed into anthropology.
