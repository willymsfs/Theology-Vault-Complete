# Jeremy Begbie

Anglican theologian and professional musician; Duke and Cambridge.

## Signature idea
Music is not an illustration of theology but a way of **doing** it. Musical **tension and delayed resolution** school believers in **biblical patience**; **polyphony** — simultaneous notes that occupy the same space without crowding each other out — supplies a **non-competitive spatial model** for the hypostatic union and for the mutual indwelling of the Trinity.

## Lineage
Blackwell's Pythagorean (cosmic order) and Incarnational (sensed materiality) traditions; Barth on Mozart as theological witness; Bonhoeffer's polyphony of life in prison.
