# Susan Bayly

Author of Saints, Goddesses and Kings, arguing that south Indian Christianity is a fully indigenised religious system rather than a colonial implant, and observing that at healing shrines "the would-be converters were themselves 'converted'; they were made over into tutelaries and cult saints so as to fit in with south India's existing forms of worship."
