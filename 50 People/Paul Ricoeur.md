# Paul Ricoeur

French philosopher, Protestant layman; supplied twentieth-century theology with its standard hermeneutical vocabulary.

## Signature ideas
The **double hermeneutic** — a *hermeneutic of suspicion* (Marx, Nietzsche, Freud, the masters of suspicion) held together with a *hermeneutic of retrieval*, so that critique serves a "second naïveté." **Time and narrative** — human time becomes intelligible only when narrated. "The symbol gives rise to thought."

## Use
Underwrites both liberationist ideological critique and postliberal narrative reading — the rare figure both sides cite.
