# Leo XIII

The pope who converted a papacy stripped of territory into a papacy with a **social voice**.

## Signature acts
***[[Rerum Novarum]]*** (1891) — just wages, savings, wider ownership of property, the living wage; the first consistent papal message on the social question, and the reason every pope since has had to address it.
***[[Aeterni Patris]]*** (1879) — the neo-scholastic restoration of Aquinas.
***Graves de communi re*** (1901) — fenced in [[Christian Democracy]]: it must not promote democracy over other regimes, belittle religion, attack authority, promote socialism, or neglect the upper classes.

## How he worked
He **ratified and universalized what laymen and one bishop had already built** — [[Wilhelm von Ketteler]], [[Leon Harmel]], Albert de Mun, Émile Keller. It was Leo who suggested Harmel bring **workers**, not only employers, on pilgrimage to Rome; by 1889 ten thousand came.
