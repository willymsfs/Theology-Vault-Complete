# Priscilla and Aquila

Tentmakers expelled from Rome by Claudius, who take Paul in at Corinth and give him both trade and household. They host a house church (1 Cor 16:19) and appear with Paul in Corinth, Ephesus (18:18-19) and Rome (Rom 16:3-5) — the clearest NT case of a married couple as a working missionary unit, and the standing biblical warrant for the domestic church.
