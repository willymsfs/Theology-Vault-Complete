# Gregory of Nazianzus

Grounds trinitarian unity in the person of the Father as archē and aitia rather than shared ousia; his Five Theological Orations (380) establish partitive exegesis and finally proclaim the Spirit homoousios via the logic of baptismal theosis. Presided briefly at Constantinople (381) before resigning in disgust.
