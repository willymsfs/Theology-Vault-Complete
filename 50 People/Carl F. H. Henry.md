# Carl F. H. Henry

Founding editor of *Christianity Today*; the intellectual architect of post-war American evangelicalism.

## Signature idea
**Propositional revelation** — against Enlightenment scepticism and neo-orthodox dialectic alike, God's revelation is objective, cognitive and accessible truth, and Scripture states it. *God, Revelation and Authority* (6 vols).

## Also
*The Uneasy Conscience of Modern Fundamentalism* — the demand that evangelicals recover social responsibility instead of withdrawal.
