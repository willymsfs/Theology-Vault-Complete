# Chinliankhup Stanislaus

Extends the tribal worldview into ecclesiology of the local Church, and names its internal limit.

## Signature idea
Argues tribal communitarian values (Munda, Oraon, Tangkhul Naga, Kuki, Khasi) function as "natural
synodal soil," expressing the local Church restored by *Lumen Gentium* and *Ad Gentes* as fully Church
in a specific culture, not an administrative subdivision awaiting Roman completion. Also states the
necessary caution: tribal governance assemblies remain structurally restricted to male clan heads, so
indigenous models of synodality require their own internal conversion, not uncritical celebration.
