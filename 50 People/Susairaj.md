# Susairaj

Names the economic structure underwriting clericalism, and proposes a concrete institutional remedy.

## Signature idea
Indian dioceses spend approximately 80% of total financial resources on seminary formation and the
upkeep of priests and religious — a group constituting roughly 5% of the Catholic population — while
the remaining 95% (the laity) receive virtually no theological, canonical or leadership formation.
Proposes a legally established diocesan "Christ's Faithful Formation Fund" to correct the imbalance
directly, so laity can act as co-believers rather than collaborators.
