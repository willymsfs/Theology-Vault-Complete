# Edward Schillebeeckx

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Theologians/Edward Schillebeeckx (Vatican II course)|Edward Schillebeeckx — Vatican II course treatment]].


Peritus at Vatican II and, with Piet Schoonenberg, a principal exponent of transignification — a personalist re-expression of the Eucharistic presence in terms of radically new meaning, which Paul VI's Mysterium Fidei judged inadequate as a substitute for ontological conversion.
