# Clark Pinnock

Canadian Baptist theologian who moved from strict inerrantist conservatism to the left edge of evangelicalism.

## Signature idea
**Evangelical inclusivism** — salvation is universally accessible; general revelation carries elements of saving grace, and God credits Christ's merits to the trusting faith of those without explicit knowledge of him. *A Wideness in God's Mercy*.

## Note
Also a leading voice of open theism, for which he faced attempted expulsion from the Evangelical Theological Society.
