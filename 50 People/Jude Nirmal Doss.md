# Jude Nirmal Doss

Supplies the pneumatological grounding of "Conversation in the Spirit," the Synod's working method.

## Signature idea
Following Yves Congar, argues a sound pneumatological ecclesiology must be balanced by a
pneumatological Christology: the same Spirit who anointed Christ at his baptism now animates the whole
Body. The Spirit is the source of both plurality and unity — demanding harmony from diverse charisms,
not uniformity — which is the theological guard against groupthink in synodal process.
