# Hans Frei

Yale theologian; with [[George Lindbeck]] the founder of postliberal theology.

## Signature idea
**The eclipse of biblical narrative** — modern historical consciousness forced the biblical stories into external explanatory frameworks (transcendent truths or historical facts), robbing scripture of its reality-constituting power.

## Key terms
Realistic narrative · **identity before presence** (read the gospel narratives to learn Jesus's specific identity *before* asking what his presence means for us).

## Method
Literary and narrative reading of the gospels; refusal to translate the story into a general theory of religion or a reconstructed history.

## Criticism
Charged with obscuring the cognitive and metaphysical work of systematic discourse, and with sectarian withdrawal from public argument.
