# George Lindbeck

*The Nature of Doctrine* (1984). Lutheran observer at Vatican II; founder-figure of postliberal theology.

## Signature idea
The **cultural-linguistic model**: religion is a comprehensive cultural framework, and doctrine functions as its **grammar**.

## Method
Analyse doctrines neither as objective propositions nor as expressive symbols of inner feeling, but as rules shaping the language, life and practice of a community. Consequence for ecumenism: traditions can agree on grammar without collapsing into one another.

## Criticism
Risks relativism and incommensurability — if each tradition is its own language game, what makes any of them true?
