# Elsa Tamez

Mexican-Costa Rican biblical scholar; Latin American liberation theology's leading woman exegete.

## Signature idea
**Justification by faith rereading** — justification is God's **affirmation of life** against the condemnation imposed by poverty and marginalization, not primarily the relief of an anxious conscience. Luther's question was the guilty individual; the Latin American question is the excluded one.

## Also
*The Amnesty of Grace*; *Bible of the Oppressed*; work on James against a spiritualized reading.
