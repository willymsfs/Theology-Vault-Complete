# Basil of Caesarea

Distinguished ousia from hypostasis (mia ousia, treis hypostaseis), defining the divine persons' distinguishing properties without claiming to define the incomprehensible divine essence; maintained pastoral reserve about explicitly naming the Spirit homoousios.
