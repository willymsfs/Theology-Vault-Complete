# Peter the Apostle

The guide of the first half of Acts. Luke gives him the steward's role — *al ha-bayit*, "over the house" (cf. Is 22; Mt 16) — and the narrative bears it out: he interprets Judas' replacement, preaches at Pentecost, works the first sign, answers the Sanhedrin, judges Ananias, receives the rooftop vision, and speaks decisively at the Council. The man who denied Jesus to a servant girl becomes the definition of *parrhesia*.
