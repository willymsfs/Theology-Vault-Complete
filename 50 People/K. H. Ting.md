# K. H. Ting

Chinese Anglican bishop; long-time leader of the Three-Self Patriotic Movement and the China Christian Council.

## Signature idea
The **Cosmic Christ** — Christ's love is at work in the whole created and social order, which allows a theology that **embraces atheistic communists** as co-workers rather than opponents. He deliberately **de-emphasized justification by faith**, holding that a doctrine dividing believers from unbelievers by status could not serve the Chinese Church.

## The argument it raises
Contextual accommodation or theological capitulation? The most contested case of a church theologizing inside a hostile state — and the sharpest test of how far context may reshape doctrine.
