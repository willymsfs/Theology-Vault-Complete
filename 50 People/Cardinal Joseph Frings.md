# Cardinal Joseph Frings

Archbishop of Cologne; leader of the Rhine/European Alliance; initiated the 13 October 1962 procedural revolt; publicly rebuked the Holy Office's secretive methods (8 November 1963), with Ratzinger as his theologian.
