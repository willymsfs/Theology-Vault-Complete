# Francis Thonippara

Historian of the pre-Latinisation St Thomas Christian *Yogam* system, and the sharpest cautionary voice
against collapsing synodality into secular democracy.

## Signature idea
Documents the three-tier *Yogam* assemblies (*Palliyogam*, *Desiyayogam*, *Mahayogam*) and the native
lay *Jathikkukarthavian* (Archdeacon) who held real executive power before the Synod of Diamper (1599)
imposed Latin forms — evidence India possessed a functioning synodal polity independent of the word's
recent currency. Also warns that identifying the synodal Church too closely with majoritarian voting
endangers its spiritual character: "*vox populi* does not automatically equal *vox Dei*" unless
accompanied by genuine spiritual conversion, since parish and diocesan elections are frequently marred
by groupism and ethnic lobbying.
