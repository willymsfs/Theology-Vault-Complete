# Hippolytus

Traditionally credited with the Apostolic Tradition (c. 215), which preserves the fullest surviving account of the patristic catechumenate and initiation rite — including the triple immersion with triple interrogation and the episcopal hand-laying that became Confirmation. Authorship and dating are genuinely contested in modern scholarship.

## Anointing of the Sick (BTh course)
The Apostolic Tradition §5 provides a blessing of oil immediately after the Eucharistic Prayer, asking that it give strength to all who taste it and health to all who use it — evidence that the earliest liturgical focus was the blessing of the oil rather than any rite of anointing.
