# Félix Dupanloup

Bishop of Orléans; the man who made the [[Syllabus of Errors]] survivable.

## The 1865 pamphlet
The pope was speaking of **absolute and eternal principles about the perfect society**, not about any existing society. In condemning Rationalism he condemned **the absolute supremacy of Reason without Faith**. The Church cannot hold up as her *ultimate ideal* a society tolerating false beliefs.

Hence the distinction: **thesis** (the ideal) versus **antithesis** (what is possible in the existing state of society).

**Some 630 bishops thanked him**, as did [[Pius IX]] — though guardedly.

## Why it is worth keeping
It is a reusable hermeneutical tool for any magisterial text that reads as a denunciation of the world: ask whether the document states the ideal order or prescribes present policy. It also shows the cost of *not* saying so in the document itself.
