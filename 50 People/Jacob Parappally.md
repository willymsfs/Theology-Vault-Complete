# Jacob Parappally

Grounds synodality in Trinitarian anthropology rather than only in ecclesiology.

## Signature idea
The human person is *imago Trinitatis* — made in the image not of a solitary God but of a communion —
with three relational dimensions mirroring the Trinity: a Father-dimension (transcendence, radical
openness to the other), a Son-dimension (transparency, vulnerability, the sharing of authority), and a
Spirit-dimension (interiority, discernment). Each failure of community life is a failure of one of the
three. The synodal Church mirrors the Trinity's own *perichoresis* — an eternal exchange of equals with
no hierarchy of domination.
