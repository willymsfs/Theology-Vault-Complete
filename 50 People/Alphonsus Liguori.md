# Alphonsus Liguori

Patron of confessors and moral theologians. His Theologia Moralis charted equiprobabilism between laxism and rigorism, and his pastoral counsel insisted that the confessor is father and physician before he is judge, that absolution should not be needlessly deferred, and that scrupulous penitents require gentleness. The pivot between Tridentine juridicism and modern pastoral practice.
