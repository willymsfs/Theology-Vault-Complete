# Avicenna (Ibn Sina)

Islamic philosopher whose account of essence and accident supplied Aquinas the tool to make Eucharistic accidents without a subject non-contradictory.

## Signature move
Defined an accident not as "that which actually exists in a subject" but as "that to whose *nature* it is due or fitting to exist in a subject." This distinction — nature versus current condition — is what Aquinas borrows wholesale for the Eucharist. Also foundational for [[Essence and Existence|the real distinction between essence and existence]] that underwrites Thomist metaphysics generally.
