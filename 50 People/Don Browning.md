# Don Browning

Chicago practical theologian; gave the discipline its most complete method.

## Signature idea
**Fundamental practical theology** — theology is practical all the way down. Four movements: **descriptive** (thick description of the situation), **historical** (what do the normative texts say), **systematic** (fusion of horizons, general claims), **strategic** (what should we do here, now).

## Aim
**Practical wisdom (*phronesis*)** defensible in the public sphere, not only inside the community — a revised correlational method against Schleiermacher's applicationist model.
