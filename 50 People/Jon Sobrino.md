# Jon Sobrino

Basque-Salvadoran Jesuit; survived the 1989 murder of his UCA community because he was abroad.

## Signature idea
The **crucified people** — the oppressed as the continuing historical presence of the crucified Christ.

## Method
A Christology of **following Jesus** into the struggle against oppression: discipleship is the epistemological condition for knowing who Christ is, not its consequence.
