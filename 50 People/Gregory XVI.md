# Gregory XVI

The most reactionary pope of the century — and the one who closed the Catholic liberal option.

## Programme, set before election
***The Triumph of the Holy See against the Assaults of the Innovators*** (1799): **papal infallibility and the necessity of the [[Temporal Power]]**; revolution incompatible with the law of God.

## As pope
Forbade **gas lighting, vaccination and railroads** in the [[Papal States]]; rejected the five powers' 1832 reform programme.
- ***Cum Primum*** (1832) — rebuked the Polish bishops and people for the 1831 revolt against Tsar Nicholas I; liberal Europe outraged.
- ***Sollicitudo Ecclesiarum*** (1831) — the pragmatic side; behind the cautious acceptance of Belgium.
- ***Mirari vos*** (1832) — condemned **[[Felicite de Lamennais]]**, religious liberty, scientific progress and separation of Church and State.
- ***Singulari nos*** (1834) — the personal condemnation of Lamennais.

## Not a complete reactionary
**Condemned the slave trade in 1839**; accepted the new South American states while condemning the revolutions that made them.
