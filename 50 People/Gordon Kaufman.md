# Gordon D. Kaufman

*An Essay on Theological Method*; *Theology as Imaginative Construction*.

## Signature idea
Theology as **imaginative construction** — a human work building a worldview oriented around God as the ultimate point of reference.

## Method
Three moves: collect models and metaphors from ordinary experience to build a concept of "world"; construct a concept of God that **relativises** that world; reformulate the worldview to fit intelligibly with that concept. The construct is judged **pragmatically** — does it lead to a fruitful, humanising life?

## Criticism
If theology is construction all the way down, what remains of revelation? Critics press the question of any norm beyond usefulness.
