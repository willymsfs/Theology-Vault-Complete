# Paul Knitter

American Catholic theologian; *No Other Name?*, *One Earth Many Religions*, *Without Buddha I Could Not Be a Christian*.

## Signature idea
Conceptual interreligious dialogue is **elitist unless rooted in a preferential option for the poor**. The common ground between traditions is found in shared struggle against oppression and ecological ruin — a **soteriocentric** rather than christocentric or theocentric criterion.
