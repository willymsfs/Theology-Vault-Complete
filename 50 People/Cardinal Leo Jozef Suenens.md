# Cardinal Leo Jozef Suenens

Archbishop of Mechelen; a master strategist and one of Paul VI's four Cardinal Moderators; proposed the ad intra/ad extra structure and the placement of "People of God" before hierarchy in Lumen Gentium; argued the Spirit gives charisms to all Christians.
