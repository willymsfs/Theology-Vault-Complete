# T. F. Torrance

Torrance built a "scientific theology" treating the self-revealed, triune God as the objective reality theology investigates, arguing theology must conform its concepts to that reality exactly as physics conforms its theories to the actual, contingent order of the physical universe.

## Key Terms
[[Scientific Theology|Scientific Theology]] · [[Cataphatic - Apophatic Spiral|Cataphatic / Apophatic Spiral]] · [[Heuristic Leap|Heuristic Leap]] · [[Contingent Order|Contingent Order]]

## Connections
A devoted student of Barth and the principal English translator of the Church Dogmatics, Torrance stayed within Barth's basic dogmatic frame but extended it, as Barth himself never did, into sustained dialogue with the natural sciences. Close colleague of Colin Gunton (Part II.D), though Gunton eventually departed from Torrance's residual Augustinianism.
