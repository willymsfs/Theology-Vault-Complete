# John Hick

British philosopher of religion; moved from an evangelical background to the best-known pluralist position in the theology of religions.

## Signature idea
A **Copernican revolution** in the theology of religions: from Christ at the centre to God — later the **Real / the Eternal One** — at the centre, with all traditions as culturally conditioned responses.

## Key move
A **Kantian** distinction between the noumenal Real and its phenomenal manifestations; Christological language becomes **metaphor rather than metaphysics** (*The Myth of God Incarnate*).

## Criticism
[[Gavin D'Costa]]: pluralism is a disguised **exclusivist secular agnosticism**, ruling out in advance any truth-claim that fails its own liberal modern criteria.
