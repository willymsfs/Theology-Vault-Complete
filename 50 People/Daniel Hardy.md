# Daniel W. Hardy

American-born Anglican theologian, Regius Professor at Durham, later Director of the Center of Theological Inquiry, Princeton; wrote the *Modern Theologians* chapters on [[Barth]] and [[T. F. Torrance]].

## Signature ideas
**Sociality** — humanity's fundamental interrelatedness as a theological, not merely sociological, datum; the Church as the form in which sociality is redeemed (*Finding the Church*). With **Ford**, *Jubilate*: praise as the basic Christian logic.

## Practice
Co-founder of the Society for **Scriptural Reasoning**.
