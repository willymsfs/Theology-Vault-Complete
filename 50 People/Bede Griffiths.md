# Bede Griffiths

English Benedictine (Swami Dayananda); led **Shantivanam** after Abhishiktananda, and made it internationally known.

## Signature idea
The **marriage of East and West** — the Western Church needs the Eastern contemplative and non-dual traditions to recover its own lost intuitive, feminine and cosmic dimensions. *The Golden String*; *Return to the Centre*; *A New Vision of Reality*.

## Caution
The standard Dalit objection applies here too: an ashram theology drawn from Sanskritic sources speaks from the tradition that excluded the outcastes.
