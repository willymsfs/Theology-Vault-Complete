# Fernando Segovia

Cuban-American biblical scholar at Vanderbilt; supplied postcolonial criticism its methodological frame.

## Signature idea
The **hermeneutics of diaspora** — read from the **periphery rather than the centre**, and treat *both* the text and the reader as culturally conditioned. There is no neutral exegete, only located ones.

## Consequence
"Real readers" enter biblical studies as a proper object of study: who is reading, from where, with what interest, is part of the exegetical data rather than noise to be eliminated.
