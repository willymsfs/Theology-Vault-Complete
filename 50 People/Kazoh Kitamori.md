# Kazoh Kitamori

Japanese Lutheran theologian; *Theology of the Pain of God* (1946).

## Signature idea
The **pain of God** — a God who cannot suffer cannot bear the sins of the world; divine impassibility is the flaw in the Western inheritance.

## Method
Works "from above to below": the universal Gospel must be mediated through particular cultural realities. Chooses paradox and aesthetic form over Western propositional logic as more faithful to Eastern wisdom traditions.
