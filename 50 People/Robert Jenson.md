# Robert Jenson

American Lutheran systematician who radicalized Barth to free theology from Greek metaphysics.

## Signature idea
**God's identity is located in his dramatic, eschatological movement with creatures toward the future** — not in a timeless pretemporal eternity.

## Key terms
Triune identity · the church as **"the risen Christ's Ego"** (the Lutheran communication of properties extended to ecclesiology).
