# Schubert Ogden

American Methodist theologian; Bultmann and Hartshorne joined in one programme.

## Signature idea
The **dual criteria** of theological adequacy: **appropriateness** to the apostolic witness and **credibility** to common human experience. A claim failing either fails.

## Also
A "revisionary Christology" — the point of Christology is the meaning of God *for us*, not metaphysical assertions about a divine nature; linguistic analysis and process philosophy as working tools.
