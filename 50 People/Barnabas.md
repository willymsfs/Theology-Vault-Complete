# Barnabas

"Son of encouragement." He lays a field's price at the apostles' feet as the positive foil to Ananias (Acts 4:36-37); he vouches for the newly converted Saul when Jerusalem is afraid of him; he is sent as Jerusalem's delegate to Antioch and fetches Saul to teach there; and he takes John Mark when Paul will not. Luke uses him consistently as the man who takes a risk on a person.
