# Simone Weil

French philosopher, factory worker by choice, never baptized; a theology written in fragments about affliction and God's absence.

## Signature idea
The universe exists through God's **loving renunciation** — creation as divine withdrawal, so that what looks like God's absence is the form of God's love.

## Key terms
***Malheur*** (affliction) · **attention** — by ascetical apprenticeship the intellect renounces egocentrism and offers compassionate attention, which is itself prayer, and which discovers the trinitarian pattern in creation.

## Use
The strongest modern resource for a theology of hidden presence in suffering, and for formation that treats attention rather than technique as the pastoral skill.
