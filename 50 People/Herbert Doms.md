# Herbert Doms

Author of The Meaning of Marriage (1935), arguing that the traditional hierarchy of ends reduced the spouses to instruments of the species and that the personal union is marriage's meaning. His position was explicitly targeted by the Holy Office decree of 1944 and substantially vindicated by Gaudium et Spes.
