# Anselm Stolz

German Benedictine of Maria Laach, taught at Sant'Anselmo, Rome; author of *Theologie der Mystik*.

## Signature idea
Mysticism is **sacramental and Trinitarian**, grounded in the historical missions of the Son and the Spirit — not an exceptional private psychological state reserved for an elite.

## Diagnosis
He traced the divorce of theology from spirituality to the **extrinsic separation of nature and grace** — the same *natura pura* that [[Henri de Lubac]] attacked. Split nature from grace and you get a doctrine without prayer and a piety without doctrine.
