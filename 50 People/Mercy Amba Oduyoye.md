# Mercy Amba Oduyoye

Ghanaian Methodist theologian; founder of the **Circle of Concerned African Women Theologians**.

## Signature idea
African traditional wisdom — proverbs, storytelling, communal practice — **critically appropriated against both colonial and patriarchal oppression**. Culture is neither to be denigrated nor exempted from critique.

## Why it matters here
The exact African parallel to the Indian problem: inculturation that never asks who inside the culture pays for it. Set beside the Dalit critique in [[09 - Indian Theological Method]].
