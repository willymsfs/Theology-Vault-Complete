# Charles Villa-Vicencio

South African theologian; national research director of the Truth and Reconciliation Commission.

## Signature idea
*A Theology of Reconstruction* — a theology of **resistance** is not yet a theology of **nation-building**. After apartheid, the Church's task shifts to law, human rights, constitution-making and the discipline of saying yes to something.
