# Irenaeus of Lyons

Adversus Haereses IV.17-18 develops the oblation of the new covenant — the Church offering the firstfruits of creation — and argues from Eucharist to resurrection against the Gnostics: "our bodies, receiving the Eucharist, are no longer corruptible, having the hope of the resurrection."
