# Tillich

Faith as "ultimate concern."

## Signature phrase
Whatever a person takes with total seriousness as the ground of life functions as their god; authentic faith is when that is truly God.

## Modern Theologians (BTh course)
Tillich's "method of correlation" pairs the existential questions raised by modern secular culture with theological answers drawn from Christian symbols, and redefines God not as a being among beings but as "Being-Itself," the ground of being.

Key terms: [[Method of Correlation]] · [[Being-Itself]] · [[New Being]] · [[Ultimate Concern]]

Connections: Tillich is Ford's paradigm "type three" theologian (theology of correlation with culture), directly contested by Barth's "type two" refusal of any such correlation. His "ground of being" language was popularised by John Robinson's Honest to God, and his correlation method deeply shaped the American "revisionists and liberals" (Tracy, Kaufman, Farley) treated in Part II.C.
