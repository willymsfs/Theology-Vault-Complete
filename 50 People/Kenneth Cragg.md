# Kenneth Cragg

Anglican bishop and Arabist; the touchstone for a fairer Christian assessment of Islam.

## Signature work
***The Call of the Minaret*** — broke with the adversarial missiology of Zwemer by taking seriously the **religious and political achievement of Muhammad** and the summons of the *adhān*, while still preaching the cross as what Islam most needs to hear.

## Position
Neither polemic nor pluralism: a Christian theology that listens from inside the Qur'anic world and still confesses.
