# Wolfhart Pannenberg

Pannenberg argues revelation is not a direct, vertical "Word" but God's indirect self-revelation through acts in universal history, fully understood only from history's eschatological end, proleptically anticipated in Jesus's resurrection.

## Key Terms
[[Offenbarung als Geschichte|Offenbarung als Geschichte]] · [[Prolepsis|Prolepsis]] · [[Christology from Below|Christology from Below]] · [[Vorgriff|Vorgriff]]

## Connections
Rejects Barth's model of direct verbal revelation and criticises Barth's Trinity for collapsing the three persons into "modes" of one divine subject. Colleague of Moltmann at Wuppertal, though Pannenberg's historical-rational method contrasts with Moltmann's more dialectical, praxis-oriented path to eschatology. Shares the term Vorgriff with Rahner (Part I) but roots it in universal history rather than transcendental anthropology.
