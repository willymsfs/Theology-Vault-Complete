# Schmemann

Orthodox theologian of the liturgy; East separated from Rome in 1054.

## Signature phrases
Theology articulates a "lived experience of wholeness"; "Lex orandi lex credendi"; the Christian as "the priest... of the cosmos."

## Sacraments (BTh course)
Author of Of Water and the Spirit, the most influential modern Orthodox study of baptism and chrismation, arguing from the East's preservation of the unity of initiation against the Western fragmentation of the rites.

See: [[Sacraments MOC]]


## Eucharist (BTh course)
Author of The Eucharist: Sacrament of the Kingdom, reading the liturgy as the Church's ascent into the Kingdom rather than as a series of discrete consecratory moments, and criticising both Western scholastic "moment" theology and the reduction of liturgy to individual devotion.

See: [[Eucharist MOC]]
