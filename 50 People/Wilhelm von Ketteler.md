# Wilhelm von Ketteler

Bishop of Mainz; the bishop whose programme became [[Rerum Novarum]].

## Signature work
***The Labour Problem and Christianity*** (1864). By 1867 he argued that **only the Church had an answer to the social question**. Strongly anti-liberal: the **State should intervene** to limit working hours and preserve Sunday as a day of rest, and there should be a **factory inspectorate**.

## Why he was ignored
His fellow German bishops were absorbed from 1873 in the **[[Kulturkampf]]**. His ideas were taken up instead by **[[Leo XIII]]** a generation later.
