# Gamaliel

The Pharisee and teacher of the Law — and Saul's teacher — who counsels the Sanhedrin to leave the apostles alone: if the movement is of men it will collapse; if it is of God, "you will not be able to overthrow them; you might even be found opposing God" (5:38-39). The advice is prudential, not sympathetic, but Luke lets it stand as the book's thesis in the mouth of an outsider.
