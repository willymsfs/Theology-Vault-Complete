# John Milbank

English Anglican theologian; founder of **Radical Orthodoxy** and author of *Theology and Social Theory*.

## Signature idea
Secular social theory is not a neutral science but a **Christian heresy** — an anti-theology resting on an **ontology of violence**. Theology must not accommodate secular reason but proclaim its own **cultural-linguistic metadiscourse**, grounded in an **ontology of peace**.

## Also
Wrote the *Modern Theologians* chapter on [[Henri de Lubac]]: contemporary Catholic theology avoids secular liberalism and closed conservatism only by recovering the natural desire for the supernatural.

## Criticism
Refuses the very mediation that made public theology possible; classified in the book as "postmodern quasi-fundamentalism."
