# Philip the Deacon

One of the seven, and the first to cross a frontier. He preaches Christ in Samaria — the region observant Jews walked around — and "there was much joy in that city" (8:8), reversing Hosea's "end of mirth." Then an angel sends him to an empty desert road to meet the Ethiopian eunuch reading Isaiah 53 aloud and understanding nothing. He is carried off afterwards like Elijah (cf. 1 Kgs 18; 2 Kgs 2). Not to be confused with the apostle Philip.
