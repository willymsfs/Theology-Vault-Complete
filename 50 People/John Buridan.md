# John Buridan

Parisian arts master who used the Eucharist as a semantic and metaphysical datum, not only a theological one.

## Signature move
Argued that because separated Eucharistic accidents are visibly, tangibly real, "being" cannot mean "being in a substance" as its primary sense — it must be a **univocal** term applying equally to substances and to free-standing accidents.
