# Cardinal Augustin Bea

President of the Secretariat for Promoting Christian Unity; co-presided the joint commission that rewrote De Fontibus Revelationis into Dei Verbum; presented Nostra Aetate, framing it as strictly religious and demanding the eradication of antisemitism.
