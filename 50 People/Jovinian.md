# Jovinian

Argued that the baptised who persevere are of equal merit and therefore that virginity is not superior to marriage — provoking Jerome's Adversus Jovinianum (393), whose disparagement of marriage was so extreme that Jerome's own friends were embarrassed and Augustine wrote De bono coniugali as a more balanced treatment.
