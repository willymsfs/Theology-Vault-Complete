# Michel de Certeau

French Jesuit, historian, psychoanalytic theorist; classified in *The Modern Theologians* among the "conservative postmoderns."

## Signature idea
*The Mystic Fable* — **mysticism as a rupture of the logic of the written word**, inaugurating new spaces of knowledge driven by spiritual desire rather than doctrinal control.

## Key terms
A **"poetics of the body"** · the experience of the divine Other · (from his wider work) tactics of the everyday against institutional strategy.
