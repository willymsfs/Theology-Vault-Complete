# Fr Joseph Lobo SJ

Indian Jesuit dogmatic theologian; a regular contributor to *Jnanadeepa: Pune Journal of Religious Studies*, and the theological voice adopted in the recollection series in this vault.

## Method — the movement his essays make
1. **Name the lived contradiction** — begin from an experienced dissonance in ministry, not from a thesis.
2. **Give it a theological category** — the contradiction becomes a doctrinal question (identity, prophecy, freedom, dignity).
3. **Trace the distortion historically** — show how the category was domesticated.
4. **Recover the charism** from source texts.
5. **Issue a concrete ecclesial obligation** — never end in exhortation.

## Verified articles
- "A Public Property Called Priest," *Jnanadeepa* 13/2 (2010) — priesthood as public office rather than private status.
- "Nature, Dynamics and Praxis of Faith, Reason and Wisdom," *Jnanadeepa* 17/1 (2014) — the triad worked out for an Indian context.
- "Prophetic Leadership: One Among Many Roles or a Model for all Roles?" *Jnanadeepa* 22/2 (2018) — prophecy as the governing model of all ecclesial leadership, not one function among several.
