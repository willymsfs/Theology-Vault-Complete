# Stephen

One of the seven, and the first Christian martyr. Luke writes his passion over Jesus': irresistible wisdom, false witnesses on the Temple, the blasphemy charge (Lev 24:16), mob violence outside the city, a vision of the Son of Man, and a dying prayer for his killers. His speech is the theological hinge of Leg I — it relativizes land and Temple, and his death scatters the Church into Judea and Samaria, executing Acts 1:8.
