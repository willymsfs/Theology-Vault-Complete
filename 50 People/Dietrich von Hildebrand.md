# Dietrich von Hildebrand

His Marriage (1929) opened the personalist challenge to the classical hierarchy of ends, arguing that the mutual self-giving union of the spouses is the meaning of marriage rather than a means to procreation — a position corrected by the Holy Office in 1944 and substantially received at Vatican II.
