# Richard Swinburne

Oxford philosopher of religion (later received into Orthodoxy); the leading systematic natural theologian in the analytic tradition.

## Signature idea
**Cumulative probabilistic argument** — theism is the simplest hypothesis explaining the universe, its order, consciousness and religious experience; assessed by Bayesian confirmation rather than deductive proof.

## Position
Ford's "dogmatic philosophical theology": a decontextualized rational account of God, independent of confessional faith.
