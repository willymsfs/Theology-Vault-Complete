# Merlin Rengith Ambrose

Carries the German canonist Sabine Demel's critique of consultative structures into the Indian
discussion, and supplies the "co-believer not collaborator" formula.

## Signature idea
Demel's diagnosis: restricting lay co-responsibility to a consultative vote reduces it to a concession
granted by clerical authority rather than a canonically protected right rooted in baptism (c. 208).
Proposes formation should form the laity as **co-believers**, not mere **collaborators** — a
distinction of standing, not only of function.
