# Hugh of St Victor

Appropriated Power/Wisdom/Goodness to Father/Son/Spirit and read creation's vestigia Trinitatis through a corresponding Immensity/Beauty/Utility triad.

## Sacraments (BTh course)
Before Peter Lombard fixed the septenary, Hugh's expansive use of sacramentum could number around thirty sacraments — evidence that for a millennium the Church celebrated sacraments without agreeing how many there were.
