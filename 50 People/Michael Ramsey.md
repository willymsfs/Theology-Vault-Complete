# Michael Ramsey

Anglican theologian, 100th Archbishop of Canterbury (1961–74).

## Signature work
*The Gospel and the Catholic Church* — the Church's order and sacramental life are not additions to the gospel but the form the gospel takes; the cross shapes ecclesiology.

## Contested legacy
His celebration of Anglican **comprehensiveness** was attacked by [[Stephen Sykes]] as amounting to theological **incoherence and a failure of self-definition** — the sharpest internal quarrel in modern Anglican theology.
