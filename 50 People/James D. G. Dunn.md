# James D. G. Dunn

Durham New Testament scholar; named the **New Perspective on Paul**.

## Signature ideas
"Works of the law" as **boundary markers** of Jewish identity rather than works-righteousness — so Paul's target is exclusion, not merit. *Jesus Remembered*: the Gospels transmit the *impact* Jesus made on those who remembered him, so the oral tradition is evidence rather than distortion.
