# Ahn Byung Mu

Korean New Testament scholar; with Suh Nam-dong the founder of **minjung theology**.

## Signature idea
Jesus identified with the ***ochlos*** — the crowd, the marginalized multitude of Mark's Gospel — read as the biblical counterpart of the Korean **minjung**.

## Method
Blend the **biblical text** with the **social text** of the minjung; Suh Nam-dong's complement is reading the "social biography" of the people. Key affect: ***han***, unresolved indignation and inner anger.

## Why it matters for India
The nearest Asian analogue to Dalit theology's move — an oppressed collective, not an individual soul, as the subject of salvation history.
