# Brevard Childs

Yale Old Testament scholar; the most influential attempt to make historical criticism serve the Church again.

## Signature idea
**Canonical criticism** — the authoritative object of interpretation is the **final canonical form** of the text as received by the believing community, not the hypothetical sources reconstructed behind it.

## Why it matters
It concedes everything historical criticism established and then asks a different question: what is this text, *as canon*, for the community that reads it? A biblical-theological counterpart to Frei's narrative turn and Lindbeck's intratextuality.
