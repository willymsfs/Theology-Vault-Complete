# Pierre Teilhard de Chardin

French Jesuit palaeontologist; forbidden to publish his theology in his lifetime.

## Signature idea
The **Omega Point** — evolution's final attractor, identified with the **cosmic Christ**: the "Christification of the universe" (**Christogenesis**).

## Key terms
**Law of complexity–consciousness** (as material organization grows more complex, consciousness correspondingly rises) · critical thresholds: matter → life → thought (noosphere) → planetary super-consciousness.

## Significance and risk
The boldest attempt to make evolution internal to Christology rather than a problem for it; criticized for optimism about progress that the twentieth century did not support.
