# Luce Irigaray

Belgian-French philosopher, linguist and psychoanalyst; heavily appropriated by feminist theologians.

## Signature ideas
Women as **"the other of the same"** — without divine images in the feminine, women can be thought only as the negation or unacknowledged ground of male subjectivity. The **"sensible transcendental"** — a divine horizon that revalues female embodiment instead of transcending it.

## Debate it opens
Do female God-images break the spirit/matter binary or reinscribe it? The standing question inside feminist theology.
