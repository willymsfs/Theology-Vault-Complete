# Jules Monchanin

French diocesan priest (Swami Parama Arubi Anandam) who came to India in 1939; with Henri Le Saux founded **Shantivanam** ashram on the Kavery in 1950.

## Signature idea
A Christian **sannyasa** and a Trinitarian reading of **Saccidānanda** — Being, Consciousness, Bliss as the Indian approach to the triune mystery. He held that India's contribution would come only through a genuinely contemplative, not merely conceptual, encounter.

## Note
More cautious than Abhishiktananda about *advaita*: he doubted a non-dual metaphysic could carry the Christian doctrine of creation and person without remainder.
