# Pius IX

The longest-reigning pope; began as the liberal hope of Italy and ended as the author of the *Syllabus* and the prisoner of the Vatican.

## The two halves
**Before 1848** — freed political prisoners, welcomed exiles (Metternich horrified), permitted gas lighting and railroads, eased restrictions on Jews; praised by Mazzini and Garibaldi; U.S. diplomatic relations from 1848.
**After 1848** — Rossi assassinated, the pope fled to Gaeta, the Roman Republic declared; **"Pius returned to Rome in 1850 a changed man."**

## Acts that mattered internationally
The **[[Mortara Affair]]** (1858) · excommunication of Mazzini and others (1860) · **[[Syllabus of Errors]]** (1864) · the letters to Archbishops Hughes and Odin (1862) and to **Jefferson Davis** (1863) · blessing on Maximilian in Mexico · the loss of the Papal States (1861, 1870) and Vatican I.

## The judgement to hold
He is the case study in **how a papacy without power acquires authority**: everything he did to defend the temporal power failed, and the moral prestige of the office grew anyway.
