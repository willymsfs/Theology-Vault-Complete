# Yves Congar

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Theologians/Yves Congar (Vatican II course)|Yves Congar — Vatican II course treatment]].


Le Saulchoir Dominican; author of Vraie et fausse réforme dans l'Église (1950) and Jalons pour une théologie du laïcat (1953, the first systematic theology of the laity); silenced after Humani Generis, vindicated as a conciliar peritus, made cardinal in 1994.

## Holy Orders (BTh course)
His Jalons pour une théologie du laïcat (1953), the first systematic theology of the laity, necessarily required a corresponding theology of the ordained defined by service rather than by status over against the laity — preparing the ground for LG 10's insistence that the two priesthoods are ordered one to another and for Presbyterorum Ordinis's servant-leader model.
