# Aloysius Pieris, SJ

Sri Lankan Jesuit; founder of Tulana Research Centre (Kelaniya); first Catholic priest to earn a doctorate in Buddhist studies from a Sri Lankan university. Architect of Asian liberation theology (*An Asian Theology of Liberation*).

## Signature move
The Asian context is defined by two intertwined poles: **overwhelming poverty** and **multifaceted religiosity**. Any Asian theology must pass through both — baptism in the "Jordan of Asian religion" and the "Calvary of Asian poverty."

## In Soares-Prabhu
Adopted: the double definition of the Asian context — it structures Soares-Prabhu's whole program (liberation + inculturation + dialogue). Diverged: Pieris's proposal of the "Asian monastic ideal of voluntary poverty" as answer to enforced poverty — Soares-Prabhu suspects it achieves only a "change of heart," not the "change of structure" systemic oppression demands.
