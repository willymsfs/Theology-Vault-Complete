# Jürgen Moltmann

German Reformed theologian; prisoner of war, then the theologian who put the future back at the centre of Christian doctrine.

## Signature idea
The **dialectic of cross and resurrection** — continuity in radical discontinuity; and a God who suffers in solidarity with the oppressed.

## Key terms
**Divine passibility** · **social trinitarianism** (three subjects in mutual love, against monarchical monotheism) · **trinitarian panentheism** · eschatology as the medium of all Christian doctrine.

## Method
Dialectical, integrating **praxis and doxology**; public theology in dialogue with Marxism, critical theory and Jewish thought.

## Criticism
An early one-sided futurism; social trinitarianism accused of tritheism; panentheism suspected of nearing pantheism; criticized by Anglo-Saxon readers for lack of logical rigour.
