# Don Cupitt

Cambridge philosopher of religion; *Taking Leave of God*, the "Sea of Faith" movement.

## Signature idea
**Theological non-realism** — God is not a being who exists independently of us but a unifying spiritual **ideal** governing a way of life. French poststructuralism enlisted for a naturalistic philosophy of religion.

## Why he matters here
The clearest available case of Ford's **Type 5** — a secular worldview given complete priority, Christianity retained only so far as it fits.
