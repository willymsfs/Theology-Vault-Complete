# Simon Magus

The celebrity magician of Samaria who believes, is baptized, and then offers money for the power to confer the Spirit. Peter's rebuke gives the Church the word *simony*. Justin Martyr made him the "father of heretics" and later tradition read his secret-knowledge religion as proto-Gnosticism — the permanent counterfeit in which power is technique and salvation is information.
