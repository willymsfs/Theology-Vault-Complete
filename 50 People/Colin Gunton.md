# Colin Gunton

English Reformed theologian; the sharpest post-Barthian critic of Western trinitarianism.

## Signature idea
**The Trinity as a communion of persons**, against an Augustinian unity that subordinates the persons to a monistic essence and yields a restricted pneumatology.

## Key terms
**Mediation** (the triune God acts through creaturely means by the Spirit) · **particularity** — *The One, the Three and the Many* offers a trinitarian metaphysic of culture against modern homogenization.
