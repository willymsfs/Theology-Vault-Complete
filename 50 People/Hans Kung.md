# Hans Küng

Swiss Catholic theologian, *peritus* at Vatican II at 34; the symbol of the post-conciliar revisionist spirit.

## Signature moves
***Infallible? An Enquiry*** (1970) — a frontal challenge to papal infallibility, which cost him his *missio canonica* in 1979 though he remained a priest. Then the turn outward: **interfaith dialogue** and the **Global Ethic** — no peace among nations without peace among religions, and no peace among religions without dialogue between them.

## On Islam
Christians and Muslims believe in the **same Creator God**; ecumenical theology must face Islam rather than treat it as foreign threat.
