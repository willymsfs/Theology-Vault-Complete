# Philip Clayton

American philosopher-theologian; wrote the physical-sciences chapter in *The Modern Theologians*.

## Signature contributions
The **eight-position spectrum of divine action**, from naturalism to robust miracle; **emergentist panentheism** — mind emerges from matter without reduction, and the mind–body relation models God's action on the world without violating natural law.
