# Friedrich Schleiermacher

*On Religion: Speeches to Its Cultured Despisers*; *The Christian Faith*; *Brief Outline of the Study of Theology*.

## Signature phrase
Religion is "the consciousness of being absolutely dependent."

## Method
A Copernican revolution: relocate theology's source from external revelation to **inner religious experience**, thereby insulating faith from scientific and historical attack. Doctrines are secondary — "accounts of the Christian religious affections set forth in speech". God is known only in relation to the believer; divine attributes are modes of divine causality experienced in life. Avoids speculative metaphysics.

## Criticism
Reduces God to an impersonal, non-relational force (charge of pantheism); evacuates Christianity of objective historical truth by dissolving doctrine into feeling. Barth's verdict: "subjectivism trying to be Christianity."
