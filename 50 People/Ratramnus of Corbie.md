# Ratramnus of Corbie

Responding to his fellow monk Paschasius at the emperor's request, distinguished in figura from in veritate and argued for a presence real but spiritual and figurative in mode. His treatise was republished by sixteenth-century Reformers as evidence that the early medieval Church had not taught transubstantiation.
