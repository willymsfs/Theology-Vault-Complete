# Ambrose of Milan

Author of De Mysteriis and De Sacramentis, the West's finest mystagogical catecheses. Famously still a catechumen when acclaimed bishop — a vivid illustration of how far the practice of deferred baptism had spread after Constantine.

## Eucharist (BTh course)
De Sacramentis IV and De Mysteriis IX supply the West's decisive causal account: the elements are changed by the word of Christ. His a fortiori argument runs that if God's word made the world from nothing, Christ's word can surely change what already exists — "before the consecration it was not the body of Christ, but after the consecration, I tell you, it is now the body of Christ."
