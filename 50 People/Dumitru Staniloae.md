# Dumitru Stăniloae

Romanian Orthodox priest and dogmatician; imprisoned by the communist regime 1958–63; translator of the Romanian *Philokalia*.

## Signature idea
Creation as **gift and word**: drawing on **Maximus the Confessor**, the world is a vehicle of divine agency and of dialogue between God and humanity, brought to completion in Christ and the Church. Doctrine and spirituality are one movement, never two disciplines.
