# Léon Harmel

French industrialist; **Social Catholicism as a working factory** rather than a theory.

## Val-des-Bois
Education for workers' children, boys **and** girls; a co-operative scheme; housing; medical care; pensions; **supplementary wages according to number of children**; light work for the old; wages paid bi-monthly on a Monday. When the French government restored Sunday working in 1880, **Harmel gave the whole weekend off**.

## The innovation that reached Rome
**Organized pilgrimages to Rome** — first employers, then, at **[[Leo XIII]]**'s own suggestion, employees. By 1889 **10,000 workers** went to see the pope. After [[Rerum Novarum]] he organized workers' study groups and the **Christian Worker Congress (1894–1900)**, renamed the Congress of [[Christian Democracy]] in 1896.
