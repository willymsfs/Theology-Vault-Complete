# Ernst Troeltsch

German theologian and historian; the last great figure of liberal Protestantism and the one who drew its most radical conclusion.

## Signature ideas
**Historicism** — every theological claim is historically conditioned and relative, including the claim to Christianity's absoluteness. The **church–sect typology** (later mysticism as a third type), taken up by H. Richard Niebuhr and by the whole sociology of religion.

## Position in the argument
In Ford and Muers's scheme he is the case of **sociology overcoming theology**: theological questions absorbed into historical-social explanation. Barth's revolt is aimed squarely here.
