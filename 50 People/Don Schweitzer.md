# Don Schweitzer

McDougald Professor of Theology, St Andrew's College, Saskatoon. Author of the fullest critical study of Soares-Prabhu's approach to inculturation, reading him as a **postcolonial "hybrid"** synthesising Latin American liberation theology, Western historical criticism, and a Hindu-inflected culture. Names three "growing edges": neglect of feminist theology, reduction of Scripture to "one story," and avoidance of doctrinal re-interpretation.
