# Huldrych Zwingli

Held baptism to be a covenant sign and pledge of allegiance — the Christian counterpart of circumcision — but denied that it confers grace, establishing the memorialist line in Reformed sacramental theology.

## Eucharist (BTh course)
Held that "est" in the words of institution means significat, making the Supper a memorial and a public pledge of allegiance; since Christ's body is locally in heaven it cannot be on earth. Split irreparably from Luther at the Marburg Colloquy (1529) over precisely this point.
