# Kosuke Koyama

Japanese theologian, missionary in Thailand, later Singapore and New York. Known for *Waterbuffalo Theology* and *Mount Fuji and Mount Sinai*.

## Signature idea
The **crucified mind**, not the crusading mind.

## Method
Authentic contextualization driven by a theology of the Incarnation: the Gospel must be spoken in the local idiom, kenotically, from the margins. Rejects Western "pure theology" as ideological danger and theological imperialism. Prefers parable and paradox to propositional logic. Asks not for the rejection of Western theology but for **mutual correction**.
