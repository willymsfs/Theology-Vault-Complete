# Novatian

An able theologian (his De Trinitate is substantial) who took the rigorist line that the Church has no power to reconcile the lapsed, and was set up as rival to Cornelius in 251 — the first significant antipope. The rejection of his position established that no sin is beyond the Church's power to forgive.
