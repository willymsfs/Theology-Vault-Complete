# R. S. Sugirtharajah

Sri Lankan-British biblical scholar who brought postcolonial criticism into biblical studies.

## Signature idea
**Contrapuntal reading** — view a text simultaneously from the experience of the marginalized and of the mainstream, so that imperial imbalance becomes visible.

## Key terms
**Orientalism** · **hybridity** · **subaltern** · (with Fernando Segovia) the **hermeneutics of diaspora**.

## Two targets at once
The **biblical text itself** (imperial impulses, land appropriation, hegemonic intent) and the **Eurocentric ideology hidden inside "neutral" historical criticism**.

## Quarrel with liberation hermeneutics
Liberation theology romanticizes the Bible; privileges Exodus while ignoring the displaced Canaanites; homogenizes "the poor" in purely economic terms, ignoring race, caste, gender and ethnicity.
