# N. T. Wright

Anglican bishop and New Testament historian; the most widely read biblical scholar writing in English.

## Signature ideas
Second-Temple Judaism read as a story of **exile and return** still awaiting resolution, which Jesus claims to bring; the resurrection argued as the best **historical** explanation of Christian origins; the New Perspective's covenantal reading of justification.

## Position
Historical rigour joined to explicit theological commitment — the British line from Westcott through Dodd and Caird.
