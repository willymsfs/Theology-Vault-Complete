# Jesse Mugambi

Kenyan Anglican theologian, Nairobi; proposed the paradigm after liberation.

## Signature idea
**Theology of reconstruction** — with apartheid ended and the Cold War over, Africa must move from the reactive paradigms of inculturation and liberation to a proactive agenda: democracy, human rights, institution-building. The governing biblical image shifts from **Exodus to Ezra–Nehemiah** — rebuilding, not escaping.

## Criticism
Maluleke: reconstruction **"jumps too quickly from Egypt to Canaan,"** skipping the wilderness that post-colonial Africa is actually in.
