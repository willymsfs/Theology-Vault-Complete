# Thomas J. J. Altizer

The most radical of the 1960s "death of God" theologians.

## Signature idea
The death of God is not an atheist slogan but a **historical, kenotic event**: in the incarnation God empties himself totally into the world, so the transcendent God genuinely ceases and Christ is now wholly present in secular history.
