# Pope Leo XIII

Issued Apostolicae Curae (1896), declaring Anglican ordinations "absolutely null and utterly void" on the grounds of defect of form and defect of intention in the Edwardine Ordinal — a judgement complicated but not reversed by Sacramentum Ordinis (1947) and by Old Catholic co-consecrations since 1931.
