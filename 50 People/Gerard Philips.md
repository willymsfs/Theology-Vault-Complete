# Gerard Philips

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Theologians/Gerard Philips (Vatican II course)|Gerard Philips — Vatican II course treatment]].


Louvain theologian who drafted the moderate compromise text (Concilium Duce Spiritu) underlying the final Lumen Gentium, after the original 1962 De Ecclesia draft was set aside.
