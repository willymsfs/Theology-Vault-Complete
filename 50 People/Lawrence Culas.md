# Lawrence Culas

Reads Acts 15 as the literal method-template for Conversation in the Spirit, and separately warns
against secular parliamentarism.

## Signature idea
The Council of Jerusalem's silence after speech (Acts 15:12) is not the absence of debate but its
precondition: it prevents conversation degenerating into argument, forcing participants to hold space
for the other and listen for the Spirit. Distinguishes this from an "elected autocracy," where
majoritarian voting and lobbying can crush minority voices — precisely what synodal consensus-seeking
is designed to avoid.
