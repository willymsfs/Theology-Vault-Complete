# Leonardo Boff

Brazilian theologian, silenced by the CDF in 1985 and later left the Franciscan priesthood.

## Signature idea
A **pneumatic ecclesiology** — the Church born from the base ("ecclesiogenesis"), with a sharp critique of the institutional church's own structures of power.

## Later turn
Integral ecology: the cry of the earth and the cry of the poor as a single cry — the line that runs into *Laudato si'*.
