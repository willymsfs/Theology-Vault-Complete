# Justin Martyr

First to insist the Logos is a "Second God" (deuteros theos), numerically distinct from the Father, against modalism; used the images of kindling fire, speech, and sunlight to explain the Son's generation without division.

## Sacraments (BTh course)
First Apology 61 gives the earliest theological interpretation of baptism and supplies the term that dominates Greek baptismal vocabulary: phōtismos, "illumination." The newly baptised are photizomenoi, "the enlightened" — a metaphor of dawn set alongside Paul's metaphor of the tomb.

## Eucharist (BTh course)
First Apology 65-67 gives the earliest complete description of a Sunday Eucharist — readings, homily, common prayers, kiss of peace, bread and mixed cup, the presider's extended thanksgiving, the people's Amen, distribution by deacons, and a collection for the needy — together with the plain statement that the food over which thanks have been given "is the flesh and blood of that incarnate Jesus." The shape has not changed in nineteen centuries.
