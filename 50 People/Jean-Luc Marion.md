# Jean-Luc Marion

French Catholic philosopher; the leading "conservative postmodern" theologian.

## Signature idea
***God Without Being*** — a critique of the **onto-theology** that treats God as the highest being inside a system of being.

## Key terms
**Icon vs idol** — the idol reflects the viewer's gaze back on itself; the **icon** lets the invisible cross into the visible · God given as **pure gift of love**, revealed supremely in the *kenosis* of the cross · the saturated phenomenon.
