# Thomas Oden

American Methodist; a public turn from liberal humanist therapy to the Fathers.

## Signature move
**Paleo-orthodoxy** — pastoral care recovered from **classical patristic wisdom** (Augustine, Gregory the Great's *Pastoral Rule*) rather than from Rogerian psychology. He vowed to write nothing original: the task is to hand on the consensus of the first millennium.

## Use
The corrective when pastoral counselling has quietly become secular therapy in a Roman collar.
