# Thomas Cajetan

Luther's opponent at Augsburg (1518) and a major Thomistic commentator, who nonetheless argued on purely exegetical grounds that James 5 refers to anointing for recovery rather than to extreme unction. He was not condemned, and his position shows the exegetical case for reform was live within Catholic theology before the Reformation.
