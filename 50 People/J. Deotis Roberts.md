# J. Deotis Roberts

First-generation black theologian; Cone's principal internal interlocutor.

## Signature idea
**Liberation *and* reconciliation** — liberation without reconciliation leaves the black church with no gospel for white people, and reconciliation without liberation is capitulation. Both, in that order.
