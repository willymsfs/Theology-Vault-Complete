# R. Kendall Soulen

American Methodist theologian; *The God of Israel and Christian Theology*.

## Signature idea
Christian theology has run on a **"semi-gnostic"** standard model that makes creation and consummation subservient to redemption-from-sin, and so writes Israel out of God's identity. Against it: God's identity is **inseparable from Israel's election**, and Christ is the climactic recapitulation of God's work for the **mutual blessing of Jew and Gentile**.
