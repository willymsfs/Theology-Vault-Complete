# Elisabeth Schüssler Fiorenza

Harvard biblical scholar; the most rigorous methodologist of feminist theology.

## Signature terms
**Kyriarchy** — not simply patriarchy but *intersecting systems of domination* (lord/master rule), so gender is never the sole axis; the ***ekklesia* of wo/men** (women-church) as the interpretive community; *In Memory of Her* — reconstructing the discipleship of equals that the texts suppressed.

## Method
A fourfold hermeneutic — suspicion, remembrance, proclamation, creative actualization — with suspicion applied to the text itself, not only to its interpreters.
