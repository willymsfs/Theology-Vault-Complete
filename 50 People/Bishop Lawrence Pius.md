# Bishop Lawrence Pius

Gives the fullest Trinitarian and Eucharistic grounding of synodality as a *constitutive*, not
optional, dimension of the Church.

## Signature idea
Following the ITC 2018 document, argues the Church does not merely *hold* synods; she *is* a synod —
her *modus vivendi et operandi*. Grounds this Trinitarianly (the Church as a people gathered into the
unity of Father, Son and Spirit) and Eucharistically: the Eucharist is the primary synodal event, where
distinctions of status, class and gender are relativised before the table of the Lord. Also gives the
clearest statement of the *sensus fidei* as a genuine *locus theologicus*, breaking the post-Tridentine
*Ecclesia docens*/*Ecclesia discens* divide.
