# Ercole Consalvi

Secretary of State to Pius VII (1800–06, 1814–22); the architect of the papacy's post-Napoleonic recovery and of its Austrian alliance.

## Programme
Rapprochement with France after 1800; at Vienna, the **restoration of the [[Papal States]]**; then a **close alliance with Austria** as the only serious Catholic power (the others being Protestant or Orthodox) — which angered liberals and nationalists in Germany and Italy.

## The 1816 reform plan
Papal administration opened to laymen **at lower levels**, with advisory councils envisaged. Blocked by the ***zelanti***, and never more than administrative: **constitutionalism was incompatible with papal government**, since the absolutism of Catholicism had to be reflected in the pope's kingdom.

## Ally
**Metternich**, equally frustrated by the *zelanti*, whose only platform was restoration of the *ancien régime*.
