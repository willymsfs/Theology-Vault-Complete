# Chung Hyun Kyung

Korean feminist theologian; her 1991 WCC Canberra address — invoking the spirits of the oppressed dead and the goddess **Kwan Yin** as an image of the Holy Spirit — became the flashpoint of the syncretism debate.

## Signature idea
Theology grounded in the **lived experience and popular religiosity of Asian women** — shamanic, Buddhist and folk resources included, not filtered out — aimed at purging **han**.

## The argument it forces
Where does inculturation end and syncretism begin, and who gets to draw the line? Note the parallel with the Dalit objection: the resources a theology draws on are never politically neutral.
