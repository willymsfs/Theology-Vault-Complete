# Bonhoeffer

Theology of Secularisation.

## Signature phrase
"To genuinely Christianise the world is to secularise it" — let the world come of age.

## Connected
[[Contextual Theologies (Modern)]]


## Modern Theologians (BTh course)
Bonhoeffer centred theology on the radical sociality of human existence — the church as "Christ existing as community" — and on costly, "this-worldly" discipleship against "cheap grace."

Key terms: [[Stellvertretung]] · [[Analogia Relationis]] · [[Cor Curvum in Se]] · [[Billige Gnade]]

Connections: Shaped early by Barth's Christocentrism, Bonhoeffer later criticised Barth's "positivism of revelation" from prison. His "worldly holiness" and "religionless Christianity" directly inspired John A. T. Robinson's Honest to God (Part II.B), and he borrowed Tillich's "dimension of depth" image even while, as Eberhard Bethge put it, "Tillich discovered the world; Bonhoeffer discovered the church."
