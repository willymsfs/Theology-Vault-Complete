# Kwok Pui-lan

Hong Kong-born Anglican theologian in the USA; the leading postcolonial feminist theologian in Asian Christianity.

## Signature ideas
**Postcolonial imagination** — decolonize the theological mind, not only the political map; **multi-faith hermeneutics** — Asian women read the Bible alongside their own religious and folk traditions; *Discovering the Bible in the Non-Biblical World*.

## Double critique
Against Western feminism's universalized "women's experience" **and** against Asian patriarchy dressed as cultural authenticity.
