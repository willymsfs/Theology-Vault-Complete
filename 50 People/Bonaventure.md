# Bonaventure

Emphasized will and mystical theology over intellect.

## Signature phrase
Theology as "an itinerary of the soul toward God"; use philosophy "as the children of Israel carried away the treasures of Egypt."

## Connected
[[Scholasticism]] · [[Aquinas]]


## Trinity (BTh course)
Disputed Questions on the Trinity integrates the Dionysian axiom bonum est sui diffusivum, reframing the Father's primacy as fontalis plenitudo ("fontal fullness") rather than Aquinas's pure negation. The Son proceeds per modum naturae, the Spirit per modum voluntatis.
