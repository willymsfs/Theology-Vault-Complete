# Simon Pinto

Maps the ecumenical dimension of synodality — where Catholic practice still lags what other traditions
have already institutionalised.

## Signature idea
Sets Orthodox *sobornost* (conciliarity, spiritual harmony, no bishop acting outside the college) beside
the Anglican and Reformed three-house system (Bishops, Clergy, Laity), where major doctrinal, moral and
financial decisions require the concurrent *deliberative* approval of all three. The Anglican model is
a live demonstration that laity can hold a deliberative vote in a governing synod without destroying
apostolic ministry — a direct challenge to Catholic canon law's consultative-only structures.
