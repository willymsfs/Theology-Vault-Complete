# Mark C. Taylor

American philosopher of religion; *Erring: A Postmodern A/theology*.

## Signature idea
**A/theology** — with the death of God goes the death of the self, the end of history and the closure of the book. Meaning is endlessly deferred; theology becomes an errant, non-foundational writing rather than a system.
