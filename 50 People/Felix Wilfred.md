# Felix Wilfred

Indian Catholic theologian, Madras; wrote the South Asia chapter in *The Modern Theologians* and edits *Concilium*.

## Signature ideas
**Spatiality** — Asian theology attends to *context and space* where Western theology is obsessed with *history and time*. The **dialogue of everyday life**, not the seminar, is the starting point for a theology of religions. Theology must move from abstract faith-and-reason questions to **primordial life issues: food, water, shelter**.

## Position
Names the two internal pressures honestly: the **Dalit critique** of Brahminical inculturation, and the tension between **incarnation** (rooting in culture) and **prophecy** (denouncing it).
