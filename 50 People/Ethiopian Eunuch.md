# Ethiopian Eunuch

Treasurer of the Candace, returning from worship in Jerusalem, reading Isaiah's Suffering Servant aloud in his chariot. As a eunuch he is excluded by Dt 23:1 and promised a place by Is 56 — the whole ambiguity of the God-fearer's position, resolved by baptism on a desert road. His question — "how can I, unless someone guides me?" — is the course's standing argument for the interpreting Church against unaided perspicuity. Tradition has him evangelizing Ethiopia.
