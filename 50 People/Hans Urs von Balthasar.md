# Hans Urs von Balthasar

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Theologians/Hans Urs von Balthasar (Vatican II course)|Hans Urs von Balthasar — Vatican II course treatment]].


Balthasar re-founded theology on the transcendental of beauty rather than on modern human subjectivity: theology must begin from the "form" (Gestalt) of Christ's self-revelation, perceived in contemplation, and unfolded as a cosmic drama (Theodrama) of divine and human freedom.

## Key Terms
[[Gestalt|Gestalt]] · [[Sendung|Sendung]] · [[Theodrama|Theodrama]] · [[Triduum Mortis|Triduum Mortis]]

## Connections
Mentored by de Lubac (previous chapter), whose ressourcement method he adopted; engaged for decades with Barth, taking from him the sovereignty of divine initiative while filtering it through a Catholic, analogical, and ecclesial lens; a fierce critic of Rahner's "anonymous Christianity" (in his polemical book Cordula); and his theology of the suffering Son connects directly to Moltmann's theology of the cross.

## Vatican II (BTh course)
One of the Communio camp's founders (1972), alongside de Lubac and Ratzinger, reacting against Concilium's reading of the Council; his "hopeful universalism" was later a target of traditionalist critique in the ongoing Concilium/Communio-adjacent debate over the Council's reception.
