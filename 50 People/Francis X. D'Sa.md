# Francis X. D'Sa

Editor of *The Dharma of Jesus* (Orbis, 2003), the posthumous anthology of nineteen Soares-Prabhu essays arranged around the person and praxis of Jesus. His introduction, "George M. Soares-Prabhu — A Theologian for Our Times," supplies the **two eyes** image: one eye sees "the world of 'real' but problematic relations," the other "the world of 'ideal' but healthy and healing relationships," and depth of vision comes only from synchronising both.
