# Henri Nouwen

Dutch priest and psychologist; Notre Dame, Yale and Harvard, then L'Arche Daybreak with people with disabilities.

## Signature idea
The **wounded healer** — the minister's own woundedness, faced and offered rather than hidden, is the place of pastoral service. Ministry from vulnerability, not competence.

## Style
Refused system: fragmentary, personal, contemplative writing on solitude, vocation, and the Desert Fathers — the neo-traditional confessional style of practical theology, correcting an over-reliance on secular psychology.
