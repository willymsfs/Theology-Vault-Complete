# A. N. Whitehead

Mathematician turned metaphysician; *Process and Reality* supplied the ontology that process theology runs on.

## Signature ideas
Reality as **actual occasions** in process rather than substances; **mutual immanence** of God and world; the **dipolar God** — a *primordial* nature of possibility and a *consequent* nature that receives and is affected by the world. God as "the great companion, the fellow-sufferer who understands."

## Method
**Imaginative generalization** — build a scheme from a limited field and test its reach across all experience.
