# David Tracy

American Catholic theologian, Chicago; the leading revisionist voice after Tillich and Lonergan.

## Signature ideas
The religious **"classic"** — a work whose excess of meaning keeps generating interpretation and so makes a public claim on any reader. The **analogical imagination** (Catholic) set against the dialectical imagination (Protestant). Theology addressed to **three publics** — academy, church, society.

## Method
Hermeneutics plus a revised **method of correlation**: mutually critical correlation between the interpreted tradition and the interpreted situation.

## Criticism
Postliberals (Lindbeck, Thiemann) charge revisionism with surrendering Christian distinctiveness to the modern situation's terms.
