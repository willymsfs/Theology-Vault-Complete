# Clement of Rome

Author of 1 Clement (c. 96), written to settle a revolt in Corinth in which presbyters had been deposed. Introduces the word laikos (layman) into Christian literature by analogy with the Old Testament cult, and gives the earliest statement of apostolic succession: the apostles appointed bishops and deacons and provided that approved men should succeed to their ministry.
