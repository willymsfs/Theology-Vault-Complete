# Luke the Evangelist

Physician (Col 4:14), sometime companion of Paul, and the only Gentile author in the canon. He writes two volumes to Theophilus — a Gospel and Acts — designed as one work, with deliberate parallels binding them. The "we"-passages of Acts 16, 20–21 and 27–28 mark the stretches he travels with Paul, including the shipwreck. His interests are consistent: the Spirit, prayer, the poor, women, Gentiles, and meals.
