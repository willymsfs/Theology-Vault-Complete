# Ockham

Broke the Scholastic synthesis through [[Nominalism]].

## Signature moves
Ockham's Razor — "*Entia non sunt multiplicanda sine necessitate*"; theology is "not a science."

## Claim in my vault
- [[Ockham's nominalism dissolved the synthesis of faith and reason]]

## Connected
[[Nominalism]] · [[Scholasticism]] · [[Aquinas]]

## Eucharist (Klima volume)
Rejected Aquinas's claim that the bread's surviving **dimensive quantity** (its size and shape) acts as a scaffold holding up the other accidents (colour, taste). For Ockham this multiplies miracles beyond necessity — quantity is not a detachable thing, only a description of a substance's parts being arranged in space. His conclusion is more austere than Aquinas's: stop building rational bridges and admit the survival of the accidents is raw, unexplained divine power.
