# Helen Dantis

Author of "Dharma of Jesus — A Feminist Perspective" in *Jnanadeepa* 18/2 (2015) — the essay that addresses head-on the gap Schweitzer identifies in Soares-Prabhu, who acknowledged women as a marginalised group but did not sustain attention to women's liberation.
