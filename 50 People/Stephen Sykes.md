# Stephen Sykes

Anglican theologian, Bishop of Ely, Regius Professor at Cambridge.

## Signature idea
*The Integrity of Anglicanism* — a church that treats **comprehensiveness** as its identity has evaded the question of identity. Conflict about the essence of Christianity is normal and must be conducted, not dissolved into breadth.
