# Helen Dantis

The feminist voice this notebook's synodality literature does not itself sustain — engages the gap
directly rather than leaving it unaddressed.

## Signature idea
Writing in the *Jnanadeepa* 18/2 symposium on the Dharma of Jesus (also engaging Soares-Prabhu, who by
his own limitation gave sustained attention to the poor but not to women's liberation), Dantis supplies
the feminist reading absent from the male-authored synodality literature more broadly — relevant here
because most of the volume's own authors on gender (Ekka, Stanislaus) name tribal patriarchy but do not
write from within a feminist method themselves.
