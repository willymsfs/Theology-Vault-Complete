# Austin Farrer

Oxford Anglican philosopher, biblical scholar and preacher; C. S. Lewis's friend and the finest stylist of the Anglican wisdom line.

## Signature idea
**Double agency** — divine and creaturely causality operate at different levels and do not compete, so God can act through a creature's own free action without displacing it. The standard non-interventionist option in the divine-action debate.

## Also
*Finite and Infinite* (metaphysics of the finite); *The Freedom of the Will*; a theology of biblical **images** rather than propositions.
