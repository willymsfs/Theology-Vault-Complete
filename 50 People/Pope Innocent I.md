# Pope Innocent I

His Letter to Decentius of Gubbio (416) is the single most important patristic text on anointing: the oil of James 5 is blessed by the bishop but may be used by priests and by all Christians (omnibus christianis) in their own and their households' need, with the one restriction that it is not given to those under canonical penance. The strongest historical evidence that reservation of the sacrament to priests is a later development.
