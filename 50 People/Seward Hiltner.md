# Seward Hiltner

Princeton pastoral theologian; founder of the modern academic discipline in America.

## Signature idea
The **shepherding perspective** — practical theology is not a set of ministry techniques but an **operation-centred discipline**: look at every function of the Church from the perspective of shepherding, and let that generate theology.

## Position
The liberal-rational style, rooted in Tillich's correlation; Browning later systematizes it, Oden reacts against it.
