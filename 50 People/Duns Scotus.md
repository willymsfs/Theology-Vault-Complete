# Duns Scotus

Christocentric synthesis grounded in love.

## Signature idea
Since "God is love, everything has its origin in love."

## Connected
[[Scholasticism]]


## Penance (BTh course)
Leading attritionist: held that imperfect sorrow arising from fear or from the ugliness of sin, when joined to the sacrament, suffices for forgiveness, the sacrament supplying what the penitent's disposition lacks. Trent settled the practical question in this direction, which is what makes the sacrament pastorally available to ordinary penitents.

See: [[Penance MOC]]

## Eucharist (Klima volume — the floating sticker objection)
Objected that Aquinas's accidents, which normally have no existence of their own but merely share in the substance's *esse*, cannot suddenly acquire independent existence when separated without contradicting what "dependent" meant in the first place. To repair this, Scotus had to treat accidents as **almost self-standing**, blurring the substance/accident line Aristotle drew — a first crack in the classical system.
