# Georges Florovsky

Russian Orthodox priest, historian and ecumenist; the driving force of the **neo-patristic synthesis** that shaped how Orthodoxy presents itself in ecumenical dialogue.

## Signature idea
**Christian Hellenism** — theology must return to the *baptized* Hellenism of the Fathers rather than to modern religious philosophy.

## Key term
***Podvig*** — the achievement or exploit of the historical Jesus: the triumph of freedom over necessity and death, which liberates humanity for proper historical action. History is a pattern of free acts, not a process.
