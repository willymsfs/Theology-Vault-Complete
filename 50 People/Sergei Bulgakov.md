# Sergei Bulgakov

Russian Orthodox priest and theologian, Marxist economist turned religious philosopher; exiled to Paris, dean of the St Sergius Institute.

## Signature idea
**Sophiology** — Sophia as the organic whole of reality, fragmented in the universe but at one in God; Christ is where **uncreated and created Sophia** are united, overcoming human isolation.

## Key term
**Kenosis**, normative for all language about God in creation and redemption.

## Criticism
Condemned by both Moscow and the Karlovtsy synod; [[Vladimir Lossky]] was among his sharpest critics, and the neo-patristic synthesis displaced sophiology.
