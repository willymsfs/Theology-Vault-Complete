# Juan Luis Segundo, SJ

Uruguayan Jesuit; among the founding generation of Latin American liberation theology. *The Liberation of Theology* (1976); five-volume *Theology for the Artisans of a New Humanity*.

## Signature move
The **hermeneutic circle**: all interpretation starts from a new experience of reality and an **ideological suspicion** of the status quo, returns to the text, and re-reads it — exposing the ideology hidden in "neutral" exegesis.

## In Soares-Prabhu
The methodological backbone of his biblical interpretation. Directly adopted: the hermeneutic circle and ideological suspicion; the critique of Western exegesis's pretended neutrality — "no such thing as an autonomous, impartial, academic theology floating free above the realm of human options and biases." Soares-Prabhu's Indian extension: the circle must also pass through Indian religiosity (dhvani, moksha), not social analysis alone.
