# John Alexander

Works out the epistemology of "Conversation in the Spirit" — how discernment relates to authority.

## Signature idea
In synodal epistemology, authority (*potestas*) is subordinately bound to discernment (*discretio*):
the bishop does not create truth in isolation but gathers, synthesises and judicially confirms the
discernment of the whole community. Analyses *parrhesia* (Acts 4:31) as the courageous, transparent
speech required before silent listening can do its work — the two must come in that order.
