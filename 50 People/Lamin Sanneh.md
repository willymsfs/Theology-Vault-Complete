# Lamin Sanneh

Gambian-born historian of mission at Yale; born Muslim, became Christian.

## Signature idea
**Translatability** — Christianity has no sacred language, so mission necessarily hands the faith over to the vernacular, and with it the power to define it. *Translating the Message*: mission was often the unintended **vindication of indigenous culture**, since translating scripture required elevating the local language and its worldview.

## Consequence
Reframes the colonial story: African Christianity is not a Western import but a vernacular movement that outran its missionaries.
