# James Ponniah

Supplies the sharpest single formulation of the gap between synodal rhetoric and lived practice in the
Indian Church.

## Signature idea
The distinction between **niti** — the formal, institutional declaration of synodality — and
**nyaya** — the actualised, lived experience of justice on the ground. A diocese can have a fully
synodal *niti* (councils, documents, consultations) while *nyaya* remains untouched.
