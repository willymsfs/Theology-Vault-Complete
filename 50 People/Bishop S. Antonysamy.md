# Bishop S. Antonysamy

Supplies the canonical analysis of why post-conciliar participatory structures remain structurally
toothless.

## Signature idea
Traces the "consultative trap" to canon 129 §1, which restricts the *potestas regiminis* to those in
sacred orders, so that even a unanimous Parish or Diocesan Pastoral Council (cc. 536, 511) can be
legally overridden by the cleric who convened it. Also engages the "not a parliament" defence of
consensus-seeking synodality and its risk of being weaponised to bypass accountability where no binding
structure backs it.
