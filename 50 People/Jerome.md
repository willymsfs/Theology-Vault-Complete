# Jerome

Translator of the Vulgate — whose rendering of mystērion at Eph 5:32 as sacramentum became the linguistic bridge for the Western sacramental theology of marriage. His Adversus Jovinianum (393) defended the superiority of virginity in terms so severe about marriage that it prompted Augustine's more balanced De bono coniugali.
