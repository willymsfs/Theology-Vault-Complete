# Pope John Paul II

Issued Reconciliatio et Paenitentia (1984) with its careful analogical treatment of social sin, Veritatis Splendor (1993) critiquing fundamental option theory, and Misericordia Dei (2002) restating the discipline of individual confession and restricting general absolution.

## Holy Orders (BTh course)
Issued Pastores Dabo Vobis (1992), whose four dimensions of formation — human, spiritual, intellectual, pastoral — are now standard in every seminary, and Ordinatio Sacerdotalis (1994), declaring that the Church has no authority to confer priestly ordination on women and that this judgement is to be definitively held.

See: [[Holy Orders MOC]]


## Matrimony (BTh course)
His Theology of the Body catecheses (1979-84) developed the nuptial meaning of the body and the language of the body as a personalist grounding for Humanae Vitae, and Familiaris Consortio (1981) contributed the law of gradualness (FC 34) and the treatment of the divorced and remarried (FC 84) that Amoris Laetitia would later revisit.

See: [[Matrimony MOC]]
