# Charles H. Long

Historian of religions; the sharpest critic of black theology from inside black scholarship.

## Signature idea
To argue liberation in **Christian theological categories** is to play an **"imitative game"** with a discourse formed by conquest. *Significations*: the religion of the oppressed must be studied through its own signifying acts — silence, opacity, the "involuntary" religious experience of the colonized — not through the coloniser's dogmatics.

## Why it matters here
The postcolonial critique arriving thirty years early, and from within.
