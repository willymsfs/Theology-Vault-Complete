# Rosemary Radford Ruether

The most influential Catholic feminist theologian of the twentieth century.

## Signature idea
The structural link between **patriarchy, militarism and environmental degradation** — the domination of women, of the poor and of the earth is one system.

## Key terms
**Gaia and God** — the tension and complementarity between divinity immanent in interconnected nature and the transcendent divine demand · the **idolatry of the male God** · reconstruction from the **prophetic and wisdom traditions**; Jesus read within the Hebrew prophetic line, in solidarity with the poor.

## Criticism
Womanist and Third World theologians charged first-wave white feminist theology with race-blindness, projecting white middle-class experience as universal "women's experience."
