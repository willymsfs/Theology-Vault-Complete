# Pope Gregory VII

Leader of the eleventh-century reform against simony, nicolaitism, and lay investiture. The resulting Investiture Controversy with Henry IV, settled at the Concordat of Worms (1122), sharpened the enduring distinction between the sacramental power conferred by ordination and the jurisdiction and temporalities attached to office.
