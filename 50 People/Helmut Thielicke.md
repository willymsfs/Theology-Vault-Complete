# Helmut Thielicke

German Lutheran theologian and preacher; dismissed under the Nazi regime, later rector at Hamburg.

## Signature idea
Against Bultmann: theology must not be reduced to **anthropology**. The creative Word of God draws the hearer into **its** sphere rather than being absorbed into modern self-consciousness — the difference between being addressed and being confirmed.

## Also
*Theological Ethics* in three volumes; sermons that made him the most heard preacher in post-war Germany.
