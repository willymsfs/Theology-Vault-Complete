# Marcel Lefebvre

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Bishops/Marcel Lefebvre (Vatican II course)|Marcel Lefebvre — Vatican II course treatment]].


Co-founder of the Coetus Internationalis Patrum with Sigaud; warned that episcopal conferences represented a "new kind of collectivism"; later founded the Society of St Pius X, rejecting the Council outright.
