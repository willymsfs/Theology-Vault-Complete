# Nicholas Lash

Indian-born English Catholic; first lay Norris-Hulse Professor at Cambridge.

## Signature idea
Theology as **"the uneasy conscience of faith"** — it exists to disturb the Church's settled speech, not to supply it with certainties.

## Position
A sustained critique of modern **reductionist rationality** and of the god of "theism" (a large item in the universe rather than the mystery of all that is). Shares postliberal suspicion of liberalism while keeping the liberal duty of rigorous self-critique.
