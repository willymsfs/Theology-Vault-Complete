# Eberhard Jüngel

Barth joined to existential Lutheranism (Bultmann, Fuchs, Ebeling).

## Signature idea
**God's being is in becoming** — the doctrine of God derived from the cross, where the "death of God" expresses God's **sovereign freedom to identify with the crucified Jesus**.

## Key terms
The word as an eschatological **speech-event** interrupting human self-identity · anthropology grounded in justification, passive reception over self-realization.
