# Hans Küng

Peritus at Vatican II; later a leading critic of how the Council's paradigm shifts were received in practice, and (at MTh level) of the scope of papal infallibility.

## Vatican II (BTh course)
Peritus at Vatican II; declared the rejection of the Curial drafts a "change of atmosphere"; later a founding co-editor of Concilium (1964) and a leading voice of the "betrayed revolution" reading of the Council's reception.
