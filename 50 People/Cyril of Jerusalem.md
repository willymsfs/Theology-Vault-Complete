# Cyril of Jerusalem

Author of the Mystagogical Catecheses, delivered during Easter week to explain the initiation rites already undergone — one of the four great specimens of patristic mystagogy, and a primary source for the shape of fourth-century Jerusalem initiation.

## Eucharist (BTh course)
Mystagogical Catecheses 4-5 instruct the newly baptised in vividly realist terms ("do not judge the matter by taste, but by faith be fully assured") and give the earliest detailed description of the epiclesis and of the manner of receiving: "make your left hand a throne for the right, as for that which is to receive a King."
