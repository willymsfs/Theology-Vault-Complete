# Marie-Dominique Chenu

> A note of the same name exists in the **Vatican II course package**: [[BTh Level Notes/VaticanII-Notes/Theologians/Marie-Dominique Chenu (Vatican II course)|Marie-Dominique Chenu — Vatican II course treatment]].


Rector of Le Saulchoir; argued (Une école de théologie, 1937) that Aquinas must be read in his own thirteenth-century context; placed on the Index in 1942, a direct trigger for the "nouvelle théologie" label.
