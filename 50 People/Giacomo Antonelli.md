# Giacomo Antonelli

Cardinal Secretary of State to [[Pius IX]]; the architect of papal diplomacy for nearly thirty years, and a layman in orders who never became a priest.

## What he did
Called on Austria, France, Naples and Spain to restore the pope after the Roman Republic (1849); ran foreign policy while Pius concentrated on spiritual duties after 1850.

## In the American Civil War (Alvarez)
The clearest window on his method. He held that a **reconstituted United States would eventually challenge British ascendancy** — good for a papacy that believed Britain was working to strip its temporal power. He told the American consul he "desires most cordially that we should in some way cripple England." He urged conciliation over the *Trent* affair, floated **papal mediation** in 1862, and later assured Washington the pope's letter to Davis had no political significance — while privately remaining preoccupied with **the dangers of untimely emancipation**.

## The lesson
Papal diplomacy here is **calculating and interest-driven**, not merely humanitarian: the Holy See behaving as a state among states.
