# Joseph de Maistre

Savoyard diplomat and philosopher; **father of European conservatism**.

## Signature works and ideas
***Considerations on France*** (1797) — the Revolution as **divine punishment** for the shortcomings of the *ancien régime*.
***Du Pape*** (1819), a bestseller — the papacy is essential **as a restriction on the power of the state**; champion of **papal infallibility**, and so the intellectual charter of [[Ultramontanism]].

## Placed beside Burke
**Edmund Burke** grounded the same turn to the right differently: religion as the basis of international law, "the great ligament of mankind," and rejection of church-state separation.

## The contrast that matters
**De Maistre and [[Felicite de Lamennais]]** are two Catholic answers to the same Revolution — the papacy as **brake on the state**, or as **ally of the people**. Rome chose de Maistre.
