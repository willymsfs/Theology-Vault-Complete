# John B. Cobb Jr

The leading process theologian; Claremont.

## Signature idea
**Creative transformation** — a tradition grows by genuinely receiving what is other to it. Applied to Buddhism: incorporate *śūnyatā* (emptying) and non-self into Christian theology, where they meet Whitehead's relational ontology and Paul's understanding of the self.

## Also
*A Christian Natural Theology*; with Herman Daly, an early theological economics of ecological limits.

## Criticism
Process theology's internal split — Cobb retrieves much of the tradition where Loomer and Wieman discard it; critics say the system reduces God to the structure of a world-process rather than a free agent.
