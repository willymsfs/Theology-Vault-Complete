# John Zizioulas

Author of Being as Communion; grounds divine being in personhood and the Father's free monarchy, defining personhood as ekstasis, an event of communion — criticized by Volf for the asymmetry this projects into a hierarchical, episcopally-centered ecclesiology.

## Eucharist (BTh course)
His eucharistic ecclesiology grounds the local church's full ecclesial reality in the assembly gathered around its bishop: wherever the Eucharist is celebrated, there is the whole Church, not a fragment of it. This is where his Trinitarian ontology (the monarchy of the Father) and his ecclesiology meet — and, per Volf's critique, where the asymmetry of the one is transmitted to the other.
