# John W. de Gruchy

South African Reformed theologian; anti-apartheid churchman, Bonhoeffer scholar, and author of the *Modern Theologians* chapter on the visual arts.

## Signature idea
*Christianity, Art and Transformation* — aesthetics is not decoration added to a serious theology: **beauty and social transformation belong together**, and a church formed by ugliness will not resist injustice well.

## Bridging concept
**Embodiment** — the artist communicates through material reality as God encounters humanity through the Incarnation.
