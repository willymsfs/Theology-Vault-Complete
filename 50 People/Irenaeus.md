# Irenaeus of Lyons

*Against Heresies*; *Demonstration of the Apostolic Preaching*. First architect of a coherent systematic Christian theology.

## Signature idea
**Recapitulation** — Christ the second Adam undoing the fall; the unity of the two Testaments; the salvation of the flesh.

## Method
Strictly canonical and holistic: **Scripture can only be understood by Scripture**. Against Gnostics who assembled cherry-picked verses like a mosaic, he traces the grand plan from creation to consummation to show that Creator and Saviour are one God. Sets an enduring limit on speculation: we know *that* God acted through revelation without always knowing *how* or *why*.

## Criticism
Dismissed by some as blundering, confused, "barbarous" in style.
