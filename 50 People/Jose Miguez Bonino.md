# José Míguez Bonino

Argentine Methodist; the leading Protestant voice of Latin American liberation theology and the only Latin American Protestant observer at Vatican II.

## Signature idea
**History is the arena of God's action** — and human beings must *intentionally create* history through Christian praxis rather than wait on providence.

## Also
*Doing Theology in a Revolutionary Situation*; a sustained critique of the political neutrality claimed by North Atlantic theology.
