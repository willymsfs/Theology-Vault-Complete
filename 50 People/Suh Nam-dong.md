# Suh Nam-dong

Korean theologian, dismissed and imprisoned under the Park regime; with [[Ahn Byung Mu]] the founder of **minjung theology**.

## Signature idea
The **social biography of the minjung** — the people's own story of suffering is a text to be read alongside scripture, and the Spirit works in the *confluence* of the two streams: the tradition of Jesus and the tradition of the Korean people's struggle.

## Key term
**Han** — accumulated, unresolved indignation and grief; theology's task is *han-pu-ri*, its unravelling.
