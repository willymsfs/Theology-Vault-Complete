# Berengar of Tours

Argued dialectically that accidents cannot remain without their substance, so the bread must remain bread and the presence be spiritual. Made to swear two oaths — the crude 1059 formula ("crushed by the teeth of the faithful") and the precise 1079 formula (substantialiter converti), the direct ancestor of transubstantiatio.
