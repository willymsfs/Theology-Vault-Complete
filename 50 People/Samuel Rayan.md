# Samuel Rayan, SJ

Kerala-born Jesuit; longtime professor at Vidyajyoti (Delhi); pioneer of Indian liberation theology and EATWOT voice. Theology written with poetic imagination — Spirit as "breath of fire" — always from the side of the oppressed.

## Signature move
Reading the Bible "not as academic text but a religious book," where "the academic resources of the West and the spiritual resources of the East come together" in service of the concrete struggles of the people.

## In Soares-Prabhu
Celebrated as the prime example of the journey **from alienation to inculturation** — the theologian who actually made it. Soares-Prabhu agrees entirely with his approach; Rayan models the fusion of Western criticism and Eastern spirituality that the Integral Indian Reading formalizes.
