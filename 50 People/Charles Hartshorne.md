# Charles Hartshorne

Whitehead's student; systematized the process doctrine of God.

## Signature idea
**Dipolar theism** — God is absolute in existence and relative in actuality: unsurpassable by any other, yet genuinely affected and enriched by the world. Classical impassibility is rejected as a Greek intrusion.

## Criticism
A rationalism that undercuts Whitehead's ultimate "Creativity"; and the worry that God becomes the abstract structure of a world-process rather than a free agent.
