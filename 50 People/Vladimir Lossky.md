# Vladimir Lossky

Russian Orthodox theologian in Paris; rejected Russian philosophical idealism for an "authentic" patristic synthesis.

## Signature idea
**Apophaticism as the primordial theological moment** — a stripping of the intellect that leads to ecstasy and to the birth of personal being in response to God's self-gift.

## Key term
The **essence/energies distinction** of Gregory Palamas: God's essence remains unknowable while his energies are genuinely participated in, so that God is really shared without ceasing to be incomprehensible.
