# David F. Ford

Irish Anglican theologian, Regius Professor at Cambridge; editor of *The Modern Theologians*, the standard map of Christian theology since 1918.

## Signature contributions
**The five types** — a spectrum classifying theologies by how they relate Christian identity to modernity (see [[01 - What Makes Theology Modern - Ford's Map]]). **Scriptural Reasoning** — Jewish, Christian and Muslim scholars reading their scriptures together, co-founded with [[Daniel Hardy]]. **Wisdom theology** in the Anglican line.

## The twelve theses
His epilogue offers twelve maxims for twenty-first-century theology without commentary — prayer as the beginning and end of theology; scripture at its heart; theology practised in friendship; theology as broker of the arts, humanities, sciences and common sense; conversation around scriptures at the heart of interfaith relations. Full list: [[12 - Ford's Twelve Theses and Theology Today]].
