# John Paul II

Karol Wojtyła: Polish philosopher-theologian formed under Nazi occupation and Soviet communism; Pope 1978–2005.

## Signature idea
**Personalism** — the human person, known through acting, as the route to God and the measure of every social system. *Redemptor Hominis*: Christ reveals humanity to itself.

## Position in post-conciliar theology
Where Rahner and Schillebeeckx correlate and Küng reforms, Wojtyła **confronts**: the modern "cultures of death" must be met by the transforming power of the gospel and a disciplined, missionary Church.
