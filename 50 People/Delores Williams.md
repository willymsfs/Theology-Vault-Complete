# Delores S. Williams

Womanist theologian; the decisive critic of early black theology's patriarchal blindness.

## Signature idea
The **Hagar** paradigm — God's provision for black women has been **"survival and quality of life"** rather than straightforward liberation.

## Key move
The **surrogacy critique of the cross**: if black women's history is one of coerced surrogacy, a soteriology that makes Christ's substitution the saving act risks sacralizing that coercion.
