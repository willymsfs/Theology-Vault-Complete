# Pope Pius XII

His Apostolic Constitution Sacramentum Ordinis (1947) definitively settled the matter and form of Holy Orders, declaring the imposition of hands alone to be the matter and correcting Florence's teaching that the traditio instrumentorum was essential — a model instance of magisterial correction on historical evidence. Also issued Mystici Corporis (1943) and Mediator Dei (1947), which prepared the ground for LG 10 on the two priesthoods.
