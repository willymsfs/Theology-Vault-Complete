# Susan Visvanathan

Analyst of Indian Christian ritual life, noted for the distinction between "canonical" rituals — the official sacraments performed by the priest inside the church — and "domestic" or vernacular rituals such as tonsure, sea-bathing, and vows, managed by family or catechist, between which Indian Catholics move comfortably.
