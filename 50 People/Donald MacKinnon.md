# Donald MacKinnon

Scottish Anglican philosopher-theologian, Norris-Hulse Professor at Cambridge; teacher of a generation including [[Rowan Williams]].

## Signature ideas
**Kenosis and establishment** — a Church holding social power cannot preach a self-emptying God without contradiction. Theology must stay with **tragedy**: he refused the consolations that tidy up the cross, Auschwitz, or moral conflict.

## Temper
Fragmentary, interrogative, morally exacting; the conscience of the Anglican wisdom tradition and the reason it never became a comfortable liberalism.
