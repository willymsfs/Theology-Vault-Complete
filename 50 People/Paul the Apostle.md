# Paul the Apostle

Born Shaul of the tribe of Benjamin, a student of Gamaliel, and the Church's fiercest persecutor before becoming its greatest missionary. Acts 9 gives the call — the doubled name of solemn divine summons (cf. Gen 22:11; Ex 3:4; 1 Sam 3:10) and the question that founds his ecclesiology: "why do you persecute *me*?" Three journeys, five defences, one shipwreck, and a two-year house arrest in Rome from which the letters and the last word of Acts come.
