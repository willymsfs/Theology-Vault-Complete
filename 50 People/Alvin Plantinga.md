# Alvin Plantinga

Notre Dame philosopher; made theistic belief respectable again in analytic philosophy.

## Signature ideas
**Reformed epistemology** — belief in God can be **properly basic**, rationally held without inference from other beliefs, so the demand for evidence rests on an unargued foundationalism. The **free will defence** against the logical problem of evil; *Warranted Christian Belief*.
