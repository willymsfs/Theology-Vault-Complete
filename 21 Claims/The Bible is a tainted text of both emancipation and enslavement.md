# The Bible is a tainted text of both emancipation and enslavement

The postcolonial claim against liberation hermeneutics. The biblical texts were produced under Egypt, Assyria, Babylon and Rome, and carry imperial impulses — land appropriation, conquest, hegemonic intent — as well as liberating ones. They then travelled with colonial power. Treating the Bible as straightforwardly the charter of the oppressed romanticizes it.

Test case: **Exodus**. Read from the slaves' side it is liberation; read from the Canaanites' side it is displacement. Adivasi and Palestinian readers are in the second position.

Marked medium confidence because the further move — that the text is therefore not normative — does not follow from the observation, and Sugirtharajah himself stops short of it. What does follow is that **which standpoint you read from is a decision, and must be declared.**
