# Thonippara's vox populi warning holds that vote-counting synodality is indistinguishable from
faction politics without genuine spiritual conversion

Parish and diocesan elections in India are frequently marred by groupism and ethnic lobbying — the
same mechanics that produce faction politics anywhere. A synodal structure that stops at counting votes
has no principled way to distinguish its own outcomes from ordinary electoral capture; only the
interior conversion Conversation in the Spirit aims at supplies that distinction, which is why method
(silence, discernment) cannot be treated as optional procedural colour.
