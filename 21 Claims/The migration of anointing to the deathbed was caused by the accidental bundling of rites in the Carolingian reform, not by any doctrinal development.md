# The migration of anointing to the deathbed was caused by the accidental bundling of rites in the Carolingian reform, not by any doctrinal development

When the reformers clericalised the anointing and incorporated it into the deathbed ordo alongside canonical penance — which itself was deferred to the end because of its lifelong disabilities — the anointing simply travelled with the sequence it had been attached to, and the theology of the reliquiae peccati was then constructed to explain a practice that already existed.
