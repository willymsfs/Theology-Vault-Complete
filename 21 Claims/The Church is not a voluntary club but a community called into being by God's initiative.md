# The Church is not a voluntary club but a community called into being by God's initiative

The etymology of ekklesia (EK + KALEO) and its OT root in qahal Yahweh both name a people constituted by divine call, not by voluntary association — the same logic that later makes baptism, not personal choice alone, the doorway into the community (see Unit 4).
