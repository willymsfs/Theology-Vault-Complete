# Method is a pattern of operations, not a set of rules

Lonergan's formula — *a normative pattern of recurrent and related operations yielding cumulative and progressive results* — rules out two common confusions. Method is not a **technique**, applied mechanically and identically to every case; nor is it a **system**, which is the dated end-product a method throws off at one moment. A system is what a theologian *has*; a method is what a theologian *does*. Systems age; method regenerates them.
