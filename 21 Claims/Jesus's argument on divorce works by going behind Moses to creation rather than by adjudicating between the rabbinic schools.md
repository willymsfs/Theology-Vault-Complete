# Jesus's argument on divorce works by going behind Moses to creation rather than by adjudicating between the rabbinic schools

Asked to rule between Shammai and Hillel on the meaning of 'erwat dabar, Jesus instead cites Genesis 1:27 and 2:24 and attributes the Mosaic permission to sklerokardia — which makes the Mosaic concession a historical accommodation rather than a divine norm, and is the structural foundation of the doctrine of indissolubility.
