# Dialogue requires an impartial vocabulary before it can compare

Comparison conducted in one tradition's loaded terms has decided its result in advance. Burtt's procedure therefore inserts a step: after *epoché* and the survey of existing agreement, build neutral terminology that does not beg the question — "value" rather than "good/bad" — and only then compare positions and synthesise their dependable goods.
