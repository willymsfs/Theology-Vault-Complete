# Moltmann's and Zizioulas's shared appeal to the Cappadocians produces opposite ecclesiologies because each retrieves a different, genuinely contested strand within Cappadocian theology itself

Zizioulas draws on the monarchy-of-the-Father strand (closer to Nazianzen and Basil's causal ordering) while Moltmann effectively needs Nyssa's more symmetrical "three men" model to ground egalitarian unity — meaning the 20th-century dispute reenacts an ambiguity already latent among the Cappadocians themselves (Part 2).
