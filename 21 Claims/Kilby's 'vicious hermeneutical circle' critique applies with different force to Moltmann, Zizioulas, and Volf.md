# Kilby's 'vicious hermeneutical circle' critique applies with different force to Moltmann, Zizioulas, and Volf

Zizioulas's political payoff (hierarchical episcopacy) matches his own ecclesial commitments almost too neatly, as does Volf's (congregational democracy) and Boff's (socialism) — the very fact that each social trinitarian's political conclusion mirrors their prior ecclesial or political commitments is itself the strongest evidence for Kilby's projection charge, independent of which side is theologically correct.
