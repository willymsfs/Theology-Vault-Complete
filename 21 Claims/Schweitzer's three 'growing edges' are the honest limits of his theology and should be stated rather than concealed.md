# Schweitzer's three 'growing edges' are the honest limits of his theology and should be stated rather than concealed

He neglected feminist theology despite acknowledging women as a marginalised group; he tended to hold that the Bible "finally has one story to tell" from Exodus to Jesus, flattening canonical diversity and missing ecological traditions; and he rejected Chalcedon and Nicaea as culturally conditioned while declining to build alternatives, leaving "an important Christian moral source untapped." Naming these strengthens rather than weakens any use of him.
