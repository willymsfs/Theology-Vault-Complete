# Pentecost reverses Babel

At Babel one language became many and the builders were scattered (Gen 11:1-9). At Pentecost many languages hear one message and the hearers are gathered. The judgment is not cancelled but converted: the diversity remains, and the Church becomes the community "which speaks all tongues" (*Ad Gentes* 4). Read alongside the Sinai staging of Acts 2 — fire, theophany, covenant — the claim is that a new law is given, the law of the Spirit (Rom 8:2).
