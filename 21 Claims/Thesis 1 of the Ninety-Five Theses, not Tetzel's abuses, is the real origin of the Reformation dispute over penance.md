# Thesis 1 of the Ninety-Five Theses, not Tetzel's abuses, is the real origin of the Reformation dispute over penance

Luther's claim that Christ "willed the entire life of believers to be one of repentance" rests on the translation point that the Vulgate's poenitentiam agite narrows the Greek metanoeite from lifelong interior conversion into a discrete ritual act — and every subsequent Protestant objection (to enumeration, satisfaction, and the priestly tribunal) follows from that single move.
