# The rejection of De Fontibus Revelationis is the clearest single case of ressourcement defeating neo-scholasticism at the Council

Ottaviani's draft embodied the "two-source" post-Tridentine model; its rejection on 20-21 November 1962 and rebuilding into Dei Verbum by periti trained in ressourcement method shows the theological shift in concrete, datable action.
