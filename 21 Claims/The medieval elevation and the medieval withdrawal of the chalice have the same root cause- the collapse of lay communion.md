# The medieval elevation and the medieval withdrawal of the chalice have the same root cause: the collapse of lay communion

Once actual reception became rare enough to require legislation (Lateran IV c. 21, annual communion), seeing replaced eating as the ordinary mode of lay participation, making the elevation devotionally central and making the practical arguments for withholding the cup persuasive — so the two developments are symptoms of one underlying pastoral failure rather than independent doctrinal choices.
