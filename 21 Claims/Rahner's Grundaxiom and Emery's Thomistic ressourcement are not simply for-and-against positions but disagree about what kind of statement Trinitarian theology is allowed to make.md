# Rahner's Grundaxiom and Emery's Thomistic ressourcement are not simply for-and-against positions but disagree about what kind of statement Trinitarian theology is allowed to make

Rahner treats the axiom as licensing inference from the economy back into God's inner being; Emery denies any "pure and simple identity" is needed because, on classical grounds, the missions already fully bear the eternal processions — the dispute is less about the Trinity itself than about the proper logical relationship between oikonomia and theologia.
