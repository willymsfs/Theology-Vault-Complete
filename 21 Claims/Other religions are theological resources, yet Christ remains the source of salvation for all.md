# Other religions are theological resources, yet Christ remains the source of salvation for all

Recognizing other religions as resources is "perhaps, the greatest challenge facing our time." They "reflect a ray of that truth which enlightens all" ([[Nostra Aetate]] 2), and "the sacred Scriptures of all religions are the heritage of the entire humanity" — while the book still affirms Jesus as the source of salvation for all. Openness, not relativism.
