# The community, not the individual, is the locus theologicus

*Locus theologicus* = the "place/source" of theology. Chapter 1 locates it in the believing **community**, where faith is guarded and handed on — yet "every reform and renewal is initiated by the inspiration and creativity of individuals who have a prophetic call or gift." So theology is communal in ground, prophetic in renewal.

**Schema:** Polarity 4 (Community ↔ Individual Theologian) in [[Seven Creative Polarities of Theologizing]].
**Example:** the community carries the faith; an Aquinas or Rahner sparks renewal from within it.
