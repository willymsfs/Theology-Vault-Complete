# CCC 1308 quietly repudiates the most common parish catechesis of Confirmation

The Catechism explicitly warns against confusing adult faith with adult natural age and cites Aquinas that bodily age does not determine the soul's — which means the widespread pastoral practice of framing Confirmation as an adolescent's mature personal choice stands in direct tension with the Church's own official teaching.
