# The Council of Florence's collapse proves that a technically competent theological compromise (tamquam ex uno principio, dia tou Huiou treated as equivalent) cannot substitute for genuine reception by the wider Church

Bessarion and Isidore's formula was doctrinally sophisticated and arguably defensible on both sides' own terms, yet it failed within years precisely because it was negotiated under political duress and rejected by the laity and lower clergy — reception, not negotiation, is what actually settles doctrine.
