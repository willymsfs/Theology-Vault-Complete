# Vatican II's most undeniable discontinuity, per John O'Malley, lies in its literary genre rather than its propositional content

The Council issued not a single anathema, replacing the legislative-judicial "canon" style of every prior council with an epideictic, persuasive register — a formal shift O'Malley argues is itself a doctrinal teaching about the nature of authority.
