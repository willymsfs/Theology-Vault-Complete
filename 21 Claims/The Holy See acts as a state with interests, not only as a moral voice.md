# The Holy See acts as a state with interests, not only as a moral voice

Alvarez's demonstration from the American Civil War. Papal policy tracked **military and political developments**, not only humanitarian concern: Antonelli wanted a reconstituted United States because it would **challenge British ascendancy** and so weaken an antagonist of the Papal States; he told the American consul he wished America would "in some way cripple England."

When the two interests separated — humanitarian (stop the killing, hence mediation and openness to a negotiated peace) versus political (support legitimate authority against insurrection) — **the political imperative won**.

**Why it matters beyond 1865**: the same double character explains Vatican conduct in every later crisis, from Benedict XV's peace note to Ostpolitik. Reading papal diplomacy as pure moral witness misreads it; reading it as pure realpolitik misses why the moral claim gives it leverage in the first place.
