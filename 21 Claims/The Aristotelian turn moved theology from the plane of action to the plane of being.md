# The Aristotelian turn moved theology from the plane of action to the plane of being

Early Christian reflection used "Semitic categories... basically functional in character" — asking what God *does*. When Aristotle reached the West, theology had to "formulate in a precise and technical manner" what Scripture said in symbols: "the focus of reflection was gradually shifted from the plane of action to the plane of being." This created the "crying need for a 'second order' of reflection" — theology as science.
