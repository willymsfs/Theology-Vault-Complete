# Bayly's judgement that the converters were themselves converted reframes Indian healing shrines as evidence of indigenisation rather than syncretism

If south Indian Christianity is a fully indigenised religious system rather than a colonial implant, then healing devotion at shrines like Velankanni is not a failure of catechesis to be corrected but the form Christianity actually took in that soil — which changes the pastoral question from how to suppress it to how the sacrament can speak within it.
