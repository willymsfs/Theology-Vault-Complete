# Trent's wording on transubstantiation is more permissive than its later reception suggests

Session XIII defines the conversion of the whole substance and says the Church "most fittingly" (aptissime) calls this transubstantiation — language that dogmatises the change while stopping short of canonising Aristotelian physics, leaving room later exploited by theologians seeking non-Aristotelian expressions of the same dogma.
