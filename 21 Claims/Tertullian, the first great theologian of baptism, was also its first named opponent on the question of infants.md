# Tertullian, the first great theologian of baptism, was also its first named opponent on the question of infants

De Baptismo is the earliest Christian treatise devoted to a single sacrament, yet in ch. 18 it argues for delaying baptism until candidates "have become able to know Christ" — meaning the paedobaptist tradition cannot claim uncontested patristic unanimity and must argue the case on other grounds.
