# Contextual method is not anti-Western but anti-absolutised-Western

Koyama and Kitamori attack Western "pure theology" as ideology and theological imperialism, and they refuse the crusading mind. But neither calls for wholesale rejection: their watchword is **mutual correction**, Western and non-Western theologies listening to and enriching one another. Inculturation is guarded from relativism by two criteria — the biblical canon and the global ecclesial community.
