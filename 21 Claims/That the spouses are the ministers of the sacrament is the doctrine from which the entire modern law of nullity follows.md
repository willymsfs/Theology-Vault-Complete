# That the spouses are the ministers of the sacrament is the doctrine from which the entire modern law of nullity follows

If the couple confect the sacrament by their consent, then any defect in that consent means no sacrament was confected — which is why c. 1095 on incapacity and c. 1103 on force and fear can void a marriage, and why the Latin Church can recognise a marriage contracted with dispensation from canonical form.
