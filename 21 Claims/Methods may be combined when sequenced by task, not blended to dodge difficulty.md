# Methods may be combined when sequenced by task, not blended to dodge difficulty

Lonergan's eight specialties are precisely a pipeline that feeds inductive, empirical work (philology, history) into constructive and pastoral work (doctrines, systematics, communications); Yoder mixes duty-, consequence- and virtue-reasoning as a concrete debate requires. What legitimates the combination is that each method does the job it is good at. Eclecticism that switches method mid-argument to escape an objection is the counterfeit.
