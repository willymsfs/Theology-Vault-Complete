# The Quam Singulari inversion is the root cause of modern confusion about what Confirmation means

By lowering First Communion to age seven while Confirmation remained tied to episcopal visitation, Pius X's 1910 reform placed the Eucharist — which CCC 1322 calls the consummation of initiation — before the sacrament meant to complete it, creating the vacuum into which the essentially Anglican "sacrament of maturity" interpretation rushed.
