# The Gentile mission is the ingathering of Israel, not a substitute for it

Paul's defence before Agrippa turns on "the promise made by God to our fathers, to which our twelve tribes hope to attain" (26:6-7). Ten of those tribes have been dispersed among the nations since 722 BC, so a mission to the nations is where the lost tribes are — and Rom 11 adds that their ingathering provokes Israel to jealousy. The gospel does not replace the promise; it executes it.
