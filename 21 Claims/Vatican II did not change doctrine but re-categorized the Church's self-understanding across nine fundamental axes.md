# Vatican II did not change doctrine but re-categorized the Church's self-understanding across nine fundamental axes

The nine paradigm shifts move the Church's self-description from institutional/hierarchical/monarchical categories toward mystery/People-of-God/collegial categories without altering the deposit of faith itself.
