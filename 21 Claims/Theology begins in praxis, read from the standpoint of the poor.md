# Theology begins in praxis, read from the standpoint of the poor

Liberation theology starts not from abstract truth but from "praxis" — commitment to the poor — using "social analysis" and "reading of the Bible from the standpoint of the poor and oppressed." The Exodus is a "key paradigmatic text": a God who liberates from unjust systems. Salvation is "holistic": social freedom, human dignity, and spiritual salvation together.
