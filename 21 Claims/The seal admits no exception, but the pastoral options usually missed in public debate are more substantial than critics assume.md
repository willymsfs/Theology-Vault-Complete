# The seal admits no exception, but the pastoral options usually missed in public debate are more substantial than critics assume

A confessor may make the fruitfulness of absolution depend on a genuine purpose of amendment that includes self-reporting or protective action, and may urge the penitent to repeat the disclosure outside the confessional where the seal does not bind — so the choice is not simply between silence and betrayal, though the confessor may never himself disclose.
