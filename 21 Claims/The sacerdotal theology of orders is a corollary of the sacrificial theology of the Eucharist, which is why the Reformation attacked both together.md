# The sacerdotal theology of orders is a corollary of the sacrificial theology of the Eucharist, which is why the Reformation attacked both together

Ministers began to be called sacerdos precisely as the Eucharist was consistently understood as thysia — one who presides at a sacrifice is naturally called a priest — and Trent states the connection outright in Session XXIII, so the two doctrines stand or fall together and cannot be defended separately.
