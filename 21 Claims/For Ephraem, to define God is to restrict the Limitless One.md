# For Ephraem, to define God is to restrict the Limitless One

Ephraem treated theology as "poetry," proceeding "by way of paradox and symbol" with "love and wonder." He held that defining God is a "blasphemous" attempt to "tie down and restrict the Limitless One," and rejected the "uninvolved" study that "reduces God to an object" on a "one-way street." Nature and Scripture are two "realms of revelation," read by the "inner eye of faith."
