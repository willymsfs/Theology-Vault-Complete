# Aquinas's per modum substantiae is the single distinction that defuses almost every popular objection to transubstantiation

Because Christ is present after the manner of substance rather than of quantity or place, he is not contained in the host as in a box, is not divided when it is broken, does not travel when it is carried, and is wholly present in every fragment — which disposes at once of the objections from fraction, from digestion, and from multiple simultaneous Masses.
