# Scripture is inerrant only for the sake of salvation

[[Dei Verbum]] 11 scopes inerrancy: Scripture teaches "without error that truth which God wanted to put into the sacred writings **for the sake of our salvation**." So Scripture is reliable in saving truth — not a manual of science or exact history.

**Example:** Genesis teaches *that* God creates and creation is good, not the age of rocks.
