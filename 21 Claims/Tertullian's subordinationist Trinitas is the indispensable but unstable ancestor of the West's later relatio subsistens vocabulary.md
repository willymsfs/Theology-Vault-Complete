# Tertullian's subordinationist Trinitas is the indispensable but unstable ancestor of the West's later relatio subsistens vocabulary

His "Father as whole substance, Son as derivation and portion" scheme supplied the Latin technical vocabulary (persona, substantia, Trinitas) that Augustine and Aquinas would later purify of subordinationism, but the same subordinationist logic ("from the Father through the Son") became crucial background to the West's filioque, not merely an embarrassing early misstep.
