# The historical-critical method is necessary but not sufficient for an Indian reading

Soares-Prabhu holds both halves. The method is culturally conditioned, over-academic and ideologically weighted toward capitalist values — yet indispensable, since it anchors the text historically and blocks fanciful subjectivism. What it cannot supply are the questions: those come from the Indian situation (poverty, caste) and the Indian mind (inclusive, cosmocentric, context-sensitive), read together with two eyes.
