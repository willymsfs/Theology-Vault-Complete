# Grace is not dropped from above but already present in human questioning

Rahner's "supernatural existential": grace is not "an alien force dropped from above" but already at work in our questioning, exploring, and desire for the Infinite. The Incarnation is therefore the answer to a question we already are — the search for an "absolute saviour."
