# Dalit theology rejects upper-caste categories and centres the Crucified and Risen Jesus

Dalit theology is a "Dalit version of liberation theology" that rejects the upper-caste philosophical categories of traditional Indian theology. The "Crucified and Risen Jesus stood at the centre": the Crucified Jesus gives power to revolt against oppression; the Risen Jesus gives hope.
