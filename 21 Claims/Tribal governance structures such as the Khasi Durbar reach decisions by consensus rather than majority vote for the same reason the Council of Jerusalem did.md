# Tribal governance structures such as the Khasi Durbar reach decisions by consensus rather than
majority vote for the same reason the Council of Jerusalem did

The Khasi Durbar, the Tangkhul Hangva, and the Munda/Ho/Santhal/Oraon village assemblies all reach
decisions through patient dialogue until consensus, never by counting votes — structurally identical
to the method of Acts 15, and for the same underlying reason: a majority vote leaves a defeated minority
inside the community, which a body that must continue living together cannot afford. This convergence
was reached independently, centuries before either tradition had access to the other's texts.
