# That the New Testament never calls a Christian minister hiereus means the Catholic doctrine of priesthood must be argued as development rather than as plain reading

The sacerdotal vocabulary is applied in the New Testament to Jewish priests, pagan priests, Christ, and all the baptised, but never to an office-holder — so the sacerdotal theology of orders enters only in the third century with Cyprian, and any exam answer that treats the priesthood as straightforwardly biblical has conceded the Reformation's strongest exegetical point without noticing.
