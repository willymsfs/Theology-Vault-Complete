# The 1972 reform's method was ressourcement, going behind the medieval development to the scriptural text

The new form's second sentence — "may the Lord who frees you from sin save you and raise you up" — tracks James 5:15's sōsei and egerei directly, so the reform recovered the sacrament's biblical warrant rather than composing a modern alternative, exactly as the 1971 confirmation form recovered the Byzantine "seal of the gift."
