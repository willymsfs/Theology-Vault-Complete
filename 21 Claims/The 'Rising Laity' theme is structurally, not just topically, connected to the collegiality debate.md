# The 'Rising Laity' theme is structurally, not just topically, connected to the collegiality debate

Suenens's July 1963 proposal to place "People of God" before "Hierarchy" in Lumen Gentium is the same structural logic that later grounds Christus Dominus's collegiality and the Synod of Bishops — baptismal equality is the ecclesiological premise beneath both the lay apostolate and episcopal collegiality.
