# Trent's teaching that absolution is a judicial act is the premise from which the requirement of integral confession follows as a matter of internal logic

If the priest acts as a judge rather than merely announcing forgiveness, he must know the case he judges — so the obligation to confess mortal sins by species and number is not an arbitrary discipline but an entailment of the sacrament's judicial character, which is why any defence of Catholic practice should argue from canon 6 rather than from positive law.
