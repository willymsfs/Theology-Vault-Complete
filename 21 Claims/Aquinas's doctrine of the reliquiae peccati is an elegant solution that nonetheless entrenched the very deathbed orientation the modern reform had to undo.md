# Aquinas's doctrine of the reliquiae peccati is an elegant solution that nonetheless entrenched the very deathbed orientation the modern reform had to undo

Needing an effect proper to anointing and not already covered by penance or the Eucharist, Aquinas identified the residual spiritual debility left after guilt is forgiven — but if the proper effect is preparation of the soul for glory, then the sacrament's natural moment is precisely the end, so the systematisation reinforced the accident it was systematising.
