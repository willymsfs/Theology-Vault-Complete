# Chauvet, Pickstock, and Marion converge on the claim that the modern difficulty with transubstantiation is a problem in the philosophy of the sign, not in physics

All three locate the impasse not in whether substance-language survives modern chemistry but in a post-Cartesian semiotics that can imagine only naive realism or reductive symbolism — which explains why simply repeating Trent's formula fails to communicate while abandoning it collapses into memorialism.
