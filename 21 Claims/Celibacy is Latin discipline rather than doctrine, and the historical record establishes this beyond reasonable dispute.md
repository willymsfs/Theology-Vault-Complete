# Celibacy is Latin discipline rather than doctrine, and the historical record establishes this beyond reasonable dispute

Elvira legislated continence for a married clergy, Trullo fixed an Eastern settlement permitting married presbyters that Rome fully accepts, celibacy became a condition of the clerical state in the West only at Lateran II in 1139, and married clergy exist in the Latin Church today through the permanent diaconate and the Ordinariates — so the viri probati question is prudential, not dogmatic.
