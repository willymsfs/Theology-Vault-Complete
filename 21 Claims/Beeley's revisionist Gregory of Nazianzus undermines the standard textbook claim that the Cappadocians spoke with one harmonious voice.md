# Beeley's revisionist Gregory of Nazianzus undermines the standard textbook claim that the Cappadocians spoke with one harmonious voice

Gregory's documented estrangement from Basil over pastoral "economy," his rejection of Nyssa's "three men" analogy, and his distinct grounding of unity in the Father's monarchy rather than shared ousia show three genuinely different Cappadocian trinitarian theologies loosely bundled under one label by later tradition.
