# Sacramentum Ordinis inadvertently weakened one pillar of Apostolicae Curae fifty years after the fact

Since the traditio instrumentorum was declared unnecessary for validity in 1947, the Edwardine Ordinal's omission of it could no longer count against Anglican orders — which, together with Old Catholic co-consecrations since the Bonn Agreement of 1931, is why the practical centre of Anglican-Roman discussion has shifted away from the 1896 judgement to the question of women's ordination.
