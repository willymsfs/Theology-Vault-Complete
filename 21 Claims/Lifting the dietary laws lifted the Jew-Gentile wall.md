# Lifting the dietary laws lifted the Jew-Gentile wall

The sheet in Acts 10 is not a dietary reform. Clean and unclean animals had come to stand for Jew and Gentile, and the table was the wall — hence the vision's threefold repetition to a man who resists it, and hence Peter's own interpretation when he arrives at Cornelius' house: "God has shown me that I should not call any man common or unclean" (10:28). Christ's blood, not kosher separation, is the new standard of holiness (Eph 2:11-16).
