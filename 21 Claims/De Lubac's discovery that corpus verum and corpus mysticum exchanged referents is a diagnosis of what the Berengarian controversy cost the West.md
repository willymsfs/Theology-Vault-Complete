# De Lubac's discovery that corpus verum and corpus mysticum exchanged referents is a diagnosis of what the Berengarian controversy cost the West

When corpus verum migrated from the Church to the Eucharist, the sacrament became an object to be defended and adored rather than primarily the sacrament of the Church's unity, and the Church became an abstract "mystical" reality — a drift that Lumen Gentium's communion ecclesiology and the formula "the Eucharist makes the Church" were designed to reverse.
