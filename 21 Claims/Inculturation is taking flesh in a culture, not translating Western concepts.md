# Inculturation is taking flesh in a culture, not translating Western concepts

True inculturation is a "natural process" in which the Church becomes fully involved in the "struggles, languages, and 'smaller cultures'" of a people — not the mere translation of Western concepts into Indian words. "Inculturation has been always a watchword in Indian theology."
