# The condemnation of the personalists in 1944 and their substantial reception in 1965 is a clean counter-example to any linear account of doctrinal development

The Holy Office explicitly reaffirmed the hierarchy of ends against Doms and von Hildebrand, yet Gaudium et Spes deliberately declined to repeat that hierarchy and rejected amendments seeking to restore it, and the 1983 Code names bonum coniugum first — so a position formally corrected in one generation became the framework of the next.
