# His critique of Latin American liberation theology is the key to what makes his own theology Asian rather than derivative

He accepted its analysis of structures and judged it insufficient for Asia: a theology that "focuses almost exclusively on societal change will not therefore satisfy Asian aspirations," because it omits liberation "from personal bondage (moksha)." Any presentation of him that ends in social programme alone has made precisely the mistake he spent a career naming.
