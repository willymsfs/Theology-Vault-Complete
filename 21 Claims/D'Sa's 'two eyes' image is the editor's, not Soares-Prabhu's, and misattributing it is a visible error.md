# D'Sa's 'two eyes' image is the editor's, not Soares-Prabhu's, and misattributing it is a visible error

The synchronisation of an eye that sees "real but problematic relations" with one that sees "ideal but healthy and healing relationships" comes from Francis X. D'Sa's introduction to the 2003 anthology. It expresses Soares-Prabhu's method faithfully, which is precisely why it circulates as though he wrote it.
