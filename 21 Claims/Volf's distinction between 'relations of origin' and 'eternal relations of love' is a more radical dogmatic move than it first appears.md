# Volf's distinction between 'relations of origin' and 'eternal relations of love' is a more radical dogmatic move than it first appears

By allowing the Father's causal priority to persist unchanged while declaring the loving relations fully symmetrical, Volf effectively brackets the very monarchy-of-the-Father logic that Nazianzen, Basil, and Zizioulas treat as constitutive of trinitarian unity itself, raising the question whether Volf's Trinity remains recognizably Cappadocian at all.
