# Galatians 3:27-28 makes baptismal theology the sharpest available critique of caste within Indian Christianity

If baptism genuinely incorporates into a body where there is "neither slave nor free," then the persistence of caste among the baptised is not a cultural residue but a contradiction of the sacrament's own stated effect — the same logic by which Soares-Prabhu judged caste-segregated Eucharist a "sacrilege" applies with equal force to the font.
