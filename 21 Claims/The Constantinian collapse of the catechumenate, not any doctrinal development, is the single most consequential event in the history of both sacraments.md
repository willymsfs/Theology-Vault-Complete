# The Constantinian collapse of the catechumenate, not any doctrinal development, is the single most consequential event in the history of both sacraments

Once conversion became socially advantageous, the three-year apprenticeship lost its function and infant baptism became the norm; every subsequent problem — the separation of confirmation, the ritual fossilization of the infant rite, the medieval anxiety about limbo, and the modern confusion over confirmation's meaning — traces back to this single sociological shift.
