# Ordinatio Sacerdotalis closed the question of priestly ordination without addressing the diaconate, which is why the diaconal question remains genuinely open

The 1994 letter speaks of priestly ordination specifically, and the historical evidence for ancient women deacons — Phoebe as diakonos, and Byzantine ordination rites closely paralleling those for male deacons — is real though contested in interpretation, which is precisely why two papal commissions have examined it without reaching unanimity.
