# The whole history of both sacraments is a series of attempts to answer one question: how tightly does God bind himself to the signs he has given?

Bind too tightly and you get limbo, emergency intrauterine baptism, and magic; bind too loosely and the sacraments become optional illustrations of grace available anyway. Baptism of desire, the Donatist settlement, LG 16, and ITC 2007 are all calibrations of the same balance, which each era must strike again rather than inherit as a formula.
