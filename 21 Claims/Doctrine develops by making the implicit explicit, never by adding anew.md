# Doctrine develops by making the implicit explicit, never by adding anew

Developments are "only explicitations and new understandings of the deposit of faith, never entirely new additions." The implicit becomes explicit — a seed becoming a tree, same life in fuller form.

**Example:** the full Trinity doctrine took centuries to state, yet was already implicit in the NT.
