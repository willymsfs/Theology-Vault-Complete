# The consultative vote required by canons 511, 536 and 537 means a unanimous council can be legally
overridden by the cleric who convened it

Canon 129 §1 reserves the power of governance to those in sacred orders; the participatory bodies
the Council itself mandated — diocesan and parish pastoral councils, parish finance councils — are
consequently defined as consultative only. The canonist Sabine Demel's diagnosis is exact: this reduces
lay co-responsibility to a concession granted by clerical authority, not a right rooted in baptism. The
practical effect recorded in the Indian sources is disillusionment and withdrawal — people declining
to participate and settling into "let the priest decide."
