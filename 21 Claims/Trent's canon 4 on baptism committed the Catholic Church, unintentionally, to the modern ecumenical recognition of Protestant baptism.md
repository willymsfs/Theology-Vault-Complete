# Trent's canon 4 on baptism committed the Catholic Church, unintentionally, to the modern ecumenical recognition of Protestant baptism

In upholding Pope Stephen against Cyprian and condemning the view that heretical baptism is invalid, Trent locked in a principle it had no ecumenical intention of applying — which Unitatis Redintegratio 3 and 22 then cashed four centuries later as the "sacramental bond of unity."
