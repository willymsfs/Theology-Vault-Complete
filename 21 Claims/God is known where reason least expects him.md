# God is known where reason least expects him

Luther's theology of the cross is a rule about the source of knowledge of God before it is a doctrine of atonement: God is revealed in weakness, suffering and hiddenness, not by reasoning upward from creation to glory. Kitamori's theology of the pain of God states a neighbouring claim in Japanese idiom — a God incapable of suffering could not bear the sins of the world.
