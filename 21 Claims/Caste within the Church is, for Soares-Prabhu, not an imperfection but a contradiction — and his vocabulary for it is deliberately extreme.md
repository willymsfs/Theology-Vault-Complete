# Caste within the Church is, for Soares-Prabhu, not an imperfection but a contradiction — and his vocabulary for it is deliberately extreme

"Christian dalit" is "like 'square circle', a contradiction in terms"; caste-segregated seating and communion queues "parody the Eucharist," turning Jesus' liberating meal into "the self-righteous celebrations of the Pharisaic haburim." The register is chosen: he judged that milder language had allowed the practice to persist.
