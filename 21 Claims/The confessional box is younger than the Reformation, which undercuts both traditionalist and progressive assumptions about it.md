# The confessional box is younger than the Reformation, which undercuts both traditionalist and progressive assumptions about it

Charles Borromeo introduced it in Milan in the 1560s, principally to guarantee anonymity and prevent solicitation — so the piece of furniture most identified with the sacrament in the popular mind postdates Luther, and neither its retention nor its abandonment can be argued from antiquity.
