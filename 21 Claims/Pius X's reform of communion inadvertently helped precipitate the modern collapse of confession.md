# Pius X's reform of communion inadvertently helped precipitate the modern collapse of confession

When communion was infrequent, confession functioned as its normal gateway and the two practices reinforced each other; once near-universal reception at every Mass became the norm, the practical link dissolved, removing the structural occasion that had sustained regular confession — an unintended consequence exactly parallel to Quam Singulari's inversion of the order of initiation.
