# Rahner dissolves the two-tier separation of nature and grace

Neo-scholasticism stacked a supernatural storey on a self-contained natural one. Rahner's **supernatural existential** holds instead that human nature is *always already* graced and dynamically oriented to God: grace is not an add-on but God's self-communication to the human spirit. The methodological payoff — and the point of contention — is that anthropology becomes theology, and that grace can be operative outside explicit faith.
