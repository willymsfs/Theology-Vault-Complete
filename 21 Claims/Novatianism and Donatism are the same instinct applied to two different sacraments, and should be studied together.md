# Novatianism and Donatism are the same instinct applied to two different sacraments, and should be studied together

Both demand that the Church be visibly pure — Novatian by denying reconciliation to the lapsed, the Donatists by denying validity to sacraments conferred by traitor-bishops — and in both cases the Church answered that holiness is Christ's gift rather than the members' achievement, establishing the corpus permixtum principle twice over.
