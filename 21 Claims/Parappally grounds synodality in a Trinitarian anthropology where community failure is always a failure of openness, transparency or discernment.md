# Parappally grounds synodality in a Trinitarian anthropology where community failure is always a
failure of openness, transparency or discernment

The human person as *imago Trinitatis* carries three relational capacities mirroring Father, Son and
Spirit: transcendence and openness to the other, transparency and the sharing of authority, and
interior discernment. Read as diagnosis rather than doctrine, every recognisable failure of community
life reduces to one of these three failing — refusal of the other, refusal to share authority and be
vulnerable, or refusal of interior attentiveness. This gives synodality a psychological, not only an
institutional, register.
