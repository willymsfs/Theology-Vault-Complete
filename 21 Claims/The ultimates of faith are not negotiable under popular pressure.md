# The ultimates of faith are not negotiable under popular pressure

Because theology reflects on God's definitive self-communication, it involves a "givenness"; its ultimates are "not negotiable under popular pressure." Criteria are needed so theology does not betray its past or mutate into heterodoxy. [[Evangelii Nuntiandi]] 65: encourage cultural expression, with the "indispensable demand that the content of faith be neither impaired nor mutilated."

**Concepts:** [[Orthodoxy]] · [[Heterodoxy]]
**Tension with:** [[All theologies are contextual; no theology is perennial]] — freedom of expression vs integrity of content.
