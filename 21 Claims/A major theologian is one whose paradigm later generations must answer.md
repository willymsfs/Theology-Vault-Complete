# A major theologian is one whose paradigm later generations must answer

Not volume of output but paradigm-setting: addressing the deepest crisis of an era with a construction that later thinkers must either adopt or define themselves against. Aquinas's Aristotelian synthesis became the Catholic textbook; Schleiermacher's turn to experience birthed liberal theology and provoked Barth; Rahner's transcendental turn reshaped a council.
