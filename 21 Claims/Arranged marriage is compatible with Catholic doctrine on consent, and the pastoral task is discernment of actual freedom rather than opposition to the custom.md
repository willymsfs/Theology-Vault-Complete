# Arranged marriage is compatible with Catholic doctrine on consent, and the pastoral task is discernment of actual freedom rather than opposition to the custom

Consent must be a free act of the will (c. 1057) and marriage under force or grave fear is invalid (c. 1103), but arrangement is not coercion — European Catholic marriage was largely arranged for most of its history — so the question is whether refusal was genuinely possible and survivable, which is a matter for judgement in the individual case rather than for a blanket rule.
