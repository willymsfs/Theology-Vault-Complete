# Caste within the Church contradicts Holy Orders more directly than any other sacrament

The ordained exist to serve and signify the unity that caste denies, and the priest is the one who presides at the Eucharist which Soares-Prabhu judges caste makes a "sacrilege" and "parody" — so a caste-stratified clergy, or the persistent under-representation of Dalit Christians in an episcopate serving a majority-Dalit Catholic population, contradicts the sacrament in its own proper act.
