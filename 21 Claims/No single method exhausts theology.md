# No single method exhausts theology

Poetry (Ephraem), system (Aquinas), anthropology (Rahner), liturgy (Schmemann), and praxis (Liberation) are all valid roads to the one Christ. Each has a strength and, taken alone, a risk: poetry → vagueness; system → dry rationalism; anthropology → blurred nature/grace; liturgy → inward-looking; praxis → Gospel reduced to politics. Held together they give a full-orbed theology.
