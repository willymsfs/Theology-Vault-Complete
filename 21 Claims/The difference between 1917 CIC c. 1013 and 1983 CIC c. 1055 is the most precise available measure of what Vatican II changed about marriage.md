# The difference between 1917 CIC c. 1013 and 1983 CIC c. 1055 is the most precise available measure of what Vatican II changed about marriage

The former ranks procreation as the primary end and mutual help as secondary; the latter states the covenant is ordered to the good of the spouses and the procreation and education of offspring, in one sentence, with the good of the spouses first and no hierarchy asserted — the change is unmistakable and is registered in law rather than merely in rhetoric.
