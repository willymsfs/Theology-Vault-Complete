# Starting from the hearer or from the form divides modern Catholic theology

Rahner asks what *a priori* conditions in the human subject make the reception of revelation possible; Balthasar says look at the **form of Christ** and let it seize you before any question about the conditions of knowing. Same Church, same council, opposite starting points.

Everything downstream follows from that one choice: the theology of religions (anonymous Christianity vs the scandal of particularity), the place of beauty, the assessment of modernity, the doctrine of hell, and which of the two the magisterium has favoured in a given decade.

The general rule it instantiates: **the choice of starting point decides the theology** — and it is a choice, not a discovery.
