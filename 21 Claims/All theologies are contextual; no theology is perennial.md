# All theologies are contextual; no theology is perennial

Every theology is "historically, socially and culturally situated." Even Scholasticism was contextual — a response to Aristotle's arrival in Europe. The appearance of a "timeless" theology is simply a context we have stopped noticing. Hence Liberation, Feminist, and Dalit theologies are not deviations but the rule made visible.
