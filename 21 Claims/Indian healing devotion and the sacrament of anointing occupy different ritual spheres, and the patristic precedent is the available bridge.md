# Indian healing devotion and the sacrament of anointing occupy different ritual spheres, and the patristic precedent is the available bridge

Visvanathan's canonical/domestic distinction predicts exactly what is observed — the sacrament belongs to the clerical and church-interior sphere while the actual religious energy around healing lives in vows, ex-votos, and pilgrimage — and the patristic pattern of blessed oil taken home for household use is structurally closer to the domestic sphere than anything in the post-Carolingian rite.
