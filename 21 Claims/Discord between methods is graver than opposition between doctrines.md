# Discord between methods is graver than opposition between doctrines

Doctrinal opposition can be argued; methodological discord cannot, because the disputants have no shared procedure for settling anything. Debates then degenerate into guerilla warfare over concrete problems while the real disagreement — unexamined and irreconcilable presuppositions — stays hidden. This is why naming one's method is not academic etiquette but a condition of honest argument.
