# The Indian and Bengal Church's existing minority experience anticipates much of what Francis asks of the universal Church

Listening to a plural society, a structural option for the poor through the Dalit and Adivasi apostolates, and CBCI/FABC's collegial practice all pre-figure Francis's program.
