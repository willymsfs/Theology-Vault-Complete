# Culas locates the safeguard of synodal method in the silence after speech, not in the speech
itself

Acts 15:12's "the entire assembly kept silence" comes immediately after Peter, Barnabas and Paul speak
— and Culas reads the placement as doing the theological work. It is not that the Council listened
attentively while people spoke; it is that it stopped and did nothing for a period afterward, which is
what actually prevents a conversation from degenerating into rebuttal and counter-rebuttal. The
safeguard is structural (a mandated pause), not merely attitudinal (a request to listen well).
