# The 1973 Rite recovered nothing new doctrinally but restored to consciousness what scholasticism had always taught

Reconciliatio cum Ecclesia was already the res et sacramentum in the scholastic analysis, so LG 11's teaching that the penitent is reconciled with God and with the wounded Church is a retrieval rather than a development — the reform's achievement was liturgical and catechetical rather than doctrinal.
