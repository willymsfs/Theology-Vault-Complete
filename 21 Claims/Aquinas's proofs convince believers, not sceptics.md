# Aquinas's proofs convince believers, not sceptics

Aquinas's Five Proofs are **cosmological** — reasoning from effects back to an Ultimate Cause / Prime Mover. They may help those who already believe, but to non-believers they seem unconvincing. Cardinal Newman: such proofs are "abstract and philosophical" and yield only a "convergence of diverse probabilities," not a knockdown demonstration.

**Example:** seeing a watch, one infers a watchmaker — persuasive to some, not airtight to all.
**Implication:** faith is reasonable but not coerced by proof → reinforces [[Theology is faith seeking understanding, not proof seeking certainty]].
