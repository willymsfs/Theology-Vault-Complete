# Ecumenism is not diplomatic courtesy but flows theologically from the Church's own nature as communion and sacrament of unity

LG 1's description of the Church as sacrament of the unity of the human race makes disunity among Christians a direct contradiction of the Church's own nature, not merely an inconvenience.
