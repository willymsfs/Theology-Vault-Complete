# Innocent I's Letter to Decentius (416) is the single strongest historical argument that the reservation of anointing to priests is a development rather than an apostolic given

Innocent explicitly permits the blessed oil to be used by all Christians — omnibus christianis — in their own need and their households' while reserving only the blessing of the oil to the bishop, which means that for centuries the application of the sacrament was not a clerical act at all, and the modern discipline of CIC c. 1003 cannot claim unbroken antiquity.
