# Rahner's dual role as both a drafting-commission member and a co-author of the alternative revelation schema shows periti exercised more than advisory influence

Serving on the commissions for Christus Dominus and Gaudium et Spes while separately circulating (with Ratzinger) a counter-schema before the Council even organized itself demonstrates periti acting as genuine authors of conciliar direction, not merely consultants to it.
