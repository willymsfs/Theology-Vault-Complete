# The attritionist settlement at Trent is what makes the sacrament pastorally available at all

If only sorrow arising from the perfect love of God sufficed, no penitent could ever be certain of the sacrament's effect — producing exactly the anxiety of conscience the Reformers charged the system with; by teaching that attrition joined to absolution suffices, Trent made the sacrament usable by ordinary people while preserving the necessity of genuine sorrow.
