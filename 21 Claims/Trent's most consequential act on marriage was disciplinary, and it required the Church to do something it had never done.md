# Trent's most consequential act on marriage was disciplinary, and it required the Church to do something it had never done

Tametsi made the Church's own ceremony a condition of validity for the first time in fifteen centuries, solving the clandestinity problem that Lateran IV's banns had failed to solve — but its binding only where promulgated created legal chaos in mission territories until Ne Temere universalised the requirement in 1907.
