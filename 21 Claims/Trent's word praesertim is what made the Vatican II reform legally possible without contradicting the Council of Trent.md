# Trent's word praesertim is what made the Vatican II reform legally possible without contradicting the Council of Trent

By teaching that the sacrament is "especially" (praesertim) for those who lie so dangerously ill as to seem near death, rather than defining that it is only for them, Trent left a deliberate reticence that SC 73 could exploit four centuries later — structurally identical to how Trent's aptissime on transubstantiation left room for later re-expression.
