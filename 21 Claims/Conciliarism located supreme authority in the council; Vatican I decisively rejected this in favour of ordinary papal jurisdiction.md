# Conciliarism located supreme authority in the council; Vatican I decisively rejected this in favour of ordinary papal jurisdiction

Haec Sancta (1415) claimed conciliar authority even over the pope; Pastor Aeternus (1870) located full, supreme, ordinary, immediate jurisdiction in the pope alone.
