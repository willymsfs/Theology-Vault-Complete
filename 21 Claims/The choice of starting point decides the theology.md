# The choice of starting point decides the theology

An **anthropological** start looks first at faith within human living and then asks what God's message means for that world; a **revelatory** start begins from Scripture and Tradition and moves outward to human implications. The choice is not neutral packaging — it fixes which questions can be asked and which answers will look adequate. Hence a paper should state its starting point in the opening paragraph.
