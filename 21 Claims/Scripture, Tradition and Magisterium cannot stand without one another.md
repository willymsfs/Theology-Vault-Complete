# Scripture, Tradition and Magisterium cannot stand without one another

[[Dei Verbum]] 10: the three are "so linked and joined together that one cannot stand without the others." A tripod — remove any leg and it falls. Scripture alone opens to endless private readings; Tradition alone loses its anchor; Magisterium alone has nothing to guard.
