# Acts 20:17 and 20:28 establish that presbyteros and episkopos named the same men in the New Testament, which the tradition has never denied

Paul summons the presbyters of Ephesus and tells those same men that the Spirit made them episkopoi, and Titus 1:5-7 does the same within two verses — meaning the threefold ministry is a genuine post-apostolic development, and the honest Catholic position defends it as a legitimate and Spirit-guided development rather than as an apostolic datum.
