# The two oaths imposed on Berengar are the clearest available case of the magisterium overshooting under polemical pressure and then self-correcting

The 1059 formula's claim that Christ's body is "crushed by the teeth of the faithful" is a physicalist over-correction the tradition itself abandoned within twenty years for the 1079 oath's substantialiter converti, and which Aquinas explicitly repudiated by assigning the breaking and chewing to the species alone (ST III q.77 a.7).
