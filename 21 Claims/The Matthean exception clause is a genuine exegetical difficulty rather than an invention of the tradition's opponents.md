# The Matthean exception clause is a genuine exegetical difficulty rather than an invention of the tradition's opponents

Matthew twice qualifies an otherwise absolute prohibition, and the four principal readings — unlawful union, adultery, separation without remarriage, betrothal infidelity — are each defensible on the Greek, so an honest Catholic treatment defends its own reading as the most probable rather than pretending the clause is not there.
