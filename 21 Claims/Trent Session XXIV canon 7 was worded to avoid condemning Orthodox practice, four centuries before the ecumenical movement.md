# Trent Session XXIV canon 7 was worded to avoid condemning Orthodox practice, four centuries before the ecumenical movement

The canon anathematises the claim that the Church errs in teaching that the bond is not dissolved by adultery, rather than condemning the contrary practice directly — a formulation pressed by Venetian bishops representing Greek communities, and a fine instance of conciliar precision serving a purpose the era had no vocabulary for.
