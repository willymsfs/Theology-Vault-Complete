# Graded authority calls for graded assent

Not everything the Church teaches carries equal weight. Four gradations, four responses: definitive **dogma** → Assent of Faith; definitive **doctrine** → Firm Acceptance; **non-definitive authoritative** doctrine → Religious Submission of Intellect and Will; **prudential admonitions** → obedience to their spirit. Theologians must weigh statements "according to their qualitative differences."
