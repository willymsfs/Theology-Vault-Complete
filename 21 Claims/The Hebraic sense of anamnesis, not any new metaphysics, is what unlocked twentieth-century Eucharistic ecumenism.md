# The Hebraic sense of anamnesis, not any new metaphysics, is what unlocked twentieth-century Eucharistic ecumenism

Once zikkaron was understood as an active memorial making a past saving event present rather than as mental recollection, Protestants could affirm that the Supper makes Christ's one sacrifice present without conceding a repetition — which is precisely the move ARCIC's Windsor Statement and BEM both exploit to reach convergence on the point that had divided the churches most bitterly.
