# Heterodoxy may carry insights orthodoxy later needs

Fidelity to authentic faith (orthodoxy) is a perennial concern, and heterodoxy is condemned — yet heterodoxy "may contain new insights for doctrinal and theological developments." History of theology is therefore a live tension between guarding truth and testing new insight.
