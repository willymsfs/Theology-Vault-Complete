# Inculturation drawn from Brahminical Hinduism sacralizes the caste order

The Dalit critique of the Indian inculturation movement. Sanskrit *advaita*, ashram forms, *Saccidānanda* Trinity-language and classical Vedantic categories are drawn from the very tradition that legitimated the exclusion of the outcastes. Inculturation done in that register does not merely fail Dalits; it can baptize the order that keeps them out — Christian dress on the same hierarchy.

The general form of the problem is **incarnation versus prophecy**: rooting the Church in a culture, and denouncing that culture, are both obligatory and pull against each other. Oduyoye states the African version — appropriate the tradition *critically*, neither denigrating it nor exempting it.

Working test before adopting any Indian-Christian symbol: **whose Hinduism is this drawn from, and who pays for it?**
