# The reordering of absolution before satisfaction, an accidental feature of the Irish system, is what made the theology of indulgences structurally possible

Once the penitent was absolved first and performed satisfaction afterward, satisfaction necessarily came to be understood as remedying something other than guilt — the temporal punishment — and once that residue could be quantified in assigned works, it could be commuted, and once commuted, monetised.
