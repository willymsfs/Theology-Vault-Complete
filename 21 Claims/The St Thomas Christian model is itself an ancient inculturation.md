# The St Thomas Christian model is itself an ancient inculturation

The St Thomas Christians (tradition: St Thomas in South India, 52 AD) were "Hindu in culture, Christian in faith and Syrian in worship" — Indian in social/cultural life, Christian in belief, Syrian in liturgy. A living, ancient example of [[Inculturation]] long before the word existed.
