# John 6 is the strongest scriptural warrant for Eucharistic realism precisely because the evangelist escalates rather than softens the offensive language

At exactly the point where the audience objects, John abandons esthiō for trōgein ("to gnaw, chew") and sōma for sarx, and accepts mass defection as the cost — a narrative choice that makes no sense if a symbolic clarification had been available and intended.
