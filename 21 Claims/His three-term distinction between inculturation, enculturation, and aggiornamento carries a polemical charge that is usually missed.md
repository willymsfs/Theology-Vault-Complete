# His three-term distinction between inculturation, enculturation, and aggiornamento carries a polemical charge that is usually missed

Inculturation is a decolonizing conversion across cultures, proper to victims of cultural imperialism; enculturation is consolidation once established; aggiornamento is merely updating. His charge is that **what the West calls inculturation is really only aggiornamento** — reinterpreting doctrine for modernity without crossing any cultural boundary. Conflating the terms neutralises the critique.
