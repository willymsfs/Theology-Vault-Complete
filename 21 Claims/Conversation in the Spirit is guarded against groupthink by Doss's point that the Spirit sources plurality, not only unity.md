# Conversation in the Spirit is guarded against groupthink by Doss's point that the Spirit sources
plurality, not only unity

If the Spirit's role in synodal discernment were only to produce unity, consensus process would risk
becoming a mechanism for manufacturing agreement — the theological cover for groupthink. Doss's
correction: the Spirit sources plurality as much as unity, creating harmony out of diverse charisms
rather than demanding uniformity. Genuine Conversation in the Spirit should therefore sometimes produce
a maintained, blessed disagreement, not always a resolved one.
