# Viaticum, not anointing, is the sacrament of the dying, and modern pastoral practice routinely inverts this

The tradition is emphatic that the Eucharist as food for the journey has priority for one who is dying — c. 921 even obliges its reception and permits it to those who have already communicated that day — yet popular usage identifies "last rites" almost exclusively with anointing, a confusion inherited directly from the extreme-unction centuries.
