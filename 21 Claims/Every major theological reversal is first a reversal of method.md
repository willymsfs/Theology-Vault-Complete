# Every major theological reversal is first a reversal of method

Luther does not merely reject Aquinas's conclusions; he refuses his starting point, calling reason the Devil's whore. Barth does not merely correct Schleiermacher; he abolishes his source, experience. Rahner does not merely soften neo-scholasticism; he relocates its starting point in the questioning subject. In each case the doctrinal quarrel is downstream of a prior decision about where theology may begin.
