# Veritatis Splendor's critique of fundamental option concedes the theory's premise while rejecting its practical conclusion

The encyclical accepts that freedom has a fundamental orientation and that not every grave matter involves full personal engagement, but denies that this orientation can be insulated from concrete choices — holding that a gravely disordered act freely chosen with full knowledge can itself constitute the rejection of God, which is precisely the inference the theory was constructed to block.
