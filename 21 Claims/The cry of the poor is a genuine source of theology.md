# The cry of the poor is a genuine source of theology

Beyond the three primary sources, "God speaks today in a very special way through the cry of the poor, their struggles, aspirations, visions and hopes." Liberation theology calls this the "epistemological privilege of the poor" — they see the Gospel from an angle the comfortable miss.
