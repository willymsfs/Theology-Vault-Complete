# The scholastic denial that the episcopate is a distinct sacramental order followed logically from defining orders by its relation to the Eucharist

If the sacrament is ordered to the Eucharist and a priest can already consecrate, then priesthood is the summit and episcopacy can only add jurisdiction — which is why the question remained genuinely open for eight centuries until LG 21 reframed it, and why Vatican II's teaching is a real development rather than a restatement.
