# The Jerusalem Council is the prototype of every ecumenical council

Acts 15 shows the shape that later councils repeat: a genuine doctrinal crisis (must Gentiles be circumcised?), open debate, scriptural warrant (Am 9:11-12; Jer 12:15; Is 45:21), Peter's judgment as decisive and James' as pastoral application, and a decree issued in a formula no merely human assembly could write — "it has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us" (15:28). The keys of Mt 16:13-19, read against the prime-minister pattern of Is 22, are being used.
