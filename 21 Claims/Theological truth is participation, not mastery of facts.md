# Theological truth is participation, not mastery of facts

The epistemological condition for reuniting theology and spirituality. If knowing God were mastering a body of propositions about God, prayer would be optional to theology and theology optional to prayer — which is exactly the modern arrangement Stolz, Weil and Balthasar refuse.

Their alternative: theology is transformative participation in the divine life. Hence "the theologian is the one who prays" (Evagrius); "the life of the saints is theology in practice" (Balthasar); attention purified of egocentrism as itself a form of prayer (Weil). Sobrino states the liberationist version — discipleship is the *condition* of knowing who Christ is, not its consequence.

Implication for seminary formation: the spiritual director and the dogma professor cannot be teaching two subjects.
