# Peter's prison escape is written as a new Exodus

Acts 12 is dated to Passover and staged in Exodus vocabulary: "Get up quickly," gird yourself, put on your sandals (cf. Ex 12:11), and the Lord "brought me out" of Herod's hand using the verb of Ex 3:8. The counterfeit king who holds the people dies under judgment while "the word of God grew and multiplied" — Pharaoh's plot line, transposed.
