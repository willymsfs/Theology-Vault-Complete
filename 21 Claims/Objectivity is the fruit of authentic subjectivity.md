# Objectivity is the fruit of authentic subjectivity

Against the myth that knowing is "taking a good look" at facts, Lonergan locates objectivity in the *authenticity of the operating subject*: attentive experiencing, intelligent understanding, reasonable judging, responsible deciding. There is no view from nowhere to retreat to, so the guarantee of truth cannot be impersonal detachment; it is the self-transcendence of the knower, secured by [[Conversion]].
