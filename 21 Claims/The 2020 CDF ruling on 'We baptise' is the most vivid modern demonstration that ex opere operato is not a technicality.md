# The 2020 CDF ruling on 'We baptise' is the most vivid modern demonstration that ex opere operato is not a technicality

Ministers substituted "we" for "I" precisely to emphasize community, and in doing so obscured that Christ, not the assembly, is the agent; the practical consequence — invalid baptisms entailing invalid confirmations, marriages, and in some cases ordinations — shows the doctrine has direct and severe pastoral force.
