# Mysterium Fidei's objection to transignification is not anti-modern but logical: meaning changes because being changes, not instead of it

Paul VI did not condemn the personalist categories outright but denied they could substitute for ontological conversion — because if the presence rests on the community's act of signification, it depends on the community rather than on Christ's word, which is exactly what Trent excluded and what the whole ex opere operato tradition exists to prevent.
