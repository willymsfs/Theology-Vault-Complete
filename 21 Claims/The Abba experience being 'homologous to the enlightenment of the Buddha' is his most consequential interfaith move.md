# The Abba experience being 'homologous to the enlightenment of the Buddha' is his most consequential interfaith move

By presenting the foundational Christian religious experience as structurally comparable to a Buddhist and a prophetic one, he makes it possible to present the Gospel in India without requiring the hearer to "become spiritually a Semite" or "intellectually a Greek" — the same logic that generates Sadguru as a rendering of Christ and jivanmukta as a rendering of freedom.
