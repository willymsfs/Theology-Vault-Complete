# Mulackal's dual-ecclesiology diagnosis explains why synodal structures coexist with unreformed
centralisation

Vatican II's own texts hold two ecclesiologies side by side without reconciling them: a traditional
monarchical-hierarchical model and a communio-synodal model of the People of God. Because both are
textually present, progressives and conservatives each find genuine conciliar support for opposed
visions — which is why synodal renewal keeps being announced and keeps being muzzled by the same
institution: the Council authorised both moves at once.
