# Conversion, not logic, is what makes theology faithful

Logic can order a theology but cannot make it true to its object. In Lonergan the control lies in religious, moral, intellectual and psychic conversion — an otherworldly falling in love that reorients the whole horizon. Method therefore places conversion at the pivot between retrieving the tradition and handing it on: unconverted rigour produces coherent theology about the wrong God.
