# The Reformation is not a single spectrum, because presence and sacrifice divide the parties differently

Luther stands closer to Rome than to Zwingli on the real presence while standing further from Rome than Zwingli on the sacrifice of the Mass — so any exam answer that arranges the Reformers on one axis from "most Catholic" to "least" has misdescribed the actual disagreement.
