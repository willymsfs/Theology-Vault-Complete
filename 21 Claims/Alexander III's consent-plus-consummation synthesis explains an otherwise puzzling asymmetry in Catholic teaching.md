# Alexander III's consent-plus-consummation synthesis explains an otherwise puzzling asymmetry in Catholic teaching

Because consent alone makes a valid marriage while consummation makes it absolutely indissoluble, an unconsummated sacramental marriage can be dissolved by papal dispensation while a consummated one cannot — a distinction that would be arbitrary but for the twelfth-century resolution of the Gratian/Lombard dispute.
