# The universal theology of the textbooks was always somebody's particular theology

The claim underneath the whole of Part V of Ford and Muers. What presented itself as theology *simpliciter* — European, male, academic, written — was a standpoint whose particularity had been made invisible by the power of the people holding it. Feminist, black, Latin American, African, Asian and postcolonial theologies do not add a perspective to a neutral core; they expose that there was never a perspectiveless core.

Consequence for method: the honest move is not to abandon particularity but to **declare** it. Cone — there is no neutral God-talk in a racialized society. Gutiérrez — the interlocutor changes from the bourgeois unbeliever to the non-person.
