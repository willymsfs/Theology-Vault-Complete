# Historical consciousness turned the sources of theology into data

Before the modern period Scripture, councils and Fathers functioned as **premises**; after it they function as **evidence** requiring critical investigation. Every modern theological method — Schleiermacher's turn to consciousness, Barth's turn to the Word, Tillich's correlation, Lonergan's functional specialties — is an answer to the single question this opened: how can criticised history still be normative?
