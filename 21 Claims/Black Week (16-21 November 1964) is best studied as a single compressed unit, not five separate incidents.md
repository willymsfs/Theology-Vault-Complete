# Black Week (16-21 November 1964) is best studied as a single compressed unit, not five separate incidents

The Nota Praevia, the Dignitatis Humanae postponement, the nineteen unvoted ecumenism amendments, and the Mother of the Church proclamation all cluster within five days because Paul VI was managing a single underlying crisis of confidence between the conservative minority and the progressive majority, not four unrelated events.
