# Salvation in Acts is spoken in three tenses

Scripture says we *have been* saved (Rom 8:24), *are being* saved (1 Pet 3:21), and *will be* saved if we persevere (Mt 10:22; Mk 13:13). The three tenses together rule out both presumption and despair, and they are the grammatical form of the Catholic doctrine of cooperation with grace (CCC 1996–2005). Acts 4:12 — "no other name" — is the first tense; the rest of the book is the second.
