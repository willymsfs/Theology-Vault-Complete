# Aquinas's and Bonaventure's disagreement over the Father's innascibility (pure negation vs. fontalis plenitudo) is a genuine, unresolved fault line inside orthodox Trinitarian theology, not a settled question

Both remain within Nicene-Constantinopolitan orthodoxy while giving incompatible metaphysical accounts of what grounds the Father's priority — showing that scholastic consensus on the Trinity's persons and relations did not extend to consensus on the deepest metaphysical explanation of the Father's own status.
