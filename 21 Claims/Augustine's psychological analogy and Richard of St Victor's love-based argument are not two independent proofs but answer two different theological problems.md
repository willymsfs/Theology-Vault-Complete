# Augustine's psychological analogy and Richard of St Victor's love-based argument are not two independent proofs but answer two different theological problems

Augustine's analogy addresses how one mind can contain real internal distinction without division; Richard's addresses why a solitary, self-enclosed God would be theologically inadequate — the two arguments are complementary rather than competing routes to the same conclusion, which is why Bonaventure could integrate both.
