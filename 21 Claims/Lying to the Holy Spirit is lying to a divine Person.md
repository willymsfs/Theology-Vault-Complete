# Lying to the Holy Spirit is lying to a divine Person

Peter's indictment of Ananias moves in one sentence from "lie to the Holy Spirit" to "you have not lied to men but to God" (Acts 5:3-4). The identification is casual, which is what makes it evidentiary: the divinity of the Spirit is assumed in the earliest narrative layer, not argued — the position Constantinople would define in AD 381.
