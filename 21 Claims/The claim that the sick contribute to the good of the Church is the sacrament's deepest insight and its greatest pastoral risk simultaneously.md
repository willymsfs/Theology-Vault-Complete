# The claim that the sick contribute to the good of the Church is the sacrament's deepest insight and its greatest pastoral risk simultaneously

LG 11 and CCC 1521, grounded in Colossians 1:24, make the sick person an agent whose suffering united to Christ's is fruitful for the body — which answers the sufferer's deepest fear of having become useless, but handled badly becomes a spiritualisation of pain that instructs the suffering to be grateful for it.
