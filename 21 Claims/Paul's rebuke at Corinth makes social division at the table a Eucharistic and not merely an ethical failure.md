# Paul's rebuke at Corinth makes social division at the table a Eucharistic and not merely an ethical failure

"Discerning the body" (1 Cor 11:29) follows immediately on the ecclesial use of "body" at 10:17, so failing to recognise the assembled poor is presented as a failure to recognise the sacrament itself — which makes caste practised within the Church, in Soares-Prabhu's judgement, a contradiction of the sacrament rather than an imperfection alongside it.
