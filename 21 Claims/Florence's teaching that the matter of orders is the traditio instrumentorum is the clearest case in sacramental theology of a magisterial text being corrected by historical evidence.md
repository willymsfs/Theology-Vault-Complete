# Florence's teaching that the matter of orders is the traditio instrumentorum is the clearest case in sacramental theology of a magisterial text being corrected by historical evidence

The handing over of chalice and paten was a medieval Western innovation unknown to the East and to the first Christian millennium, and Pius XII's Sacramentum Ordinis (1947) declared by apostolic authority that the imposition of hands alone is the matter — a correction running toward the more ancient and more ecumenically shared practice.
