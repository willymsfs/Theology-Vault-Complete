# Whether the nine paradigm shifts have been fully received in practice, and not merely proclaimed in text, remains genuinely contested

Küng and Phan's "restorationist" critique argues implementation has lagged text, especially on collegiality and the unresolved questions of infallibility, celibacy, and women's ordination.
