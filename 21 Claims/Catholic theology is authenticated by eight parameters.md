# Catholic theology is authenticated by eight parameters

A theology is authentically Roman Catholic if it holds: (1) knowability of God, (2) faith **and** reason, (3) universality of Christ, (4) communitarian context (*sensus fidelium*), (5) catholicity in time (continuity without relativism), (6) the sacramental principle, (7) Scripture **and** Tradition, (8) the Magisterium. These are the test for legitimate pluralism vs heterodoxy.
