# The coherence of 'person'-language for the Trinity depends entirely on which historical definition of 'person' is smuggled into the discussion

Boethius's and Aquinas's definitions (individual substance of a rational nature; subsisting relation) do not entail multiple centers of consciousness and so permit orthodox 'three persons' language, while the post-Lockean definition does entail this and so cannot be applied to the Trinity without producing tritheism — meaning much of the modern psychological-vs-social debate is really an unacknowledged equivocation over what 'person' means.
