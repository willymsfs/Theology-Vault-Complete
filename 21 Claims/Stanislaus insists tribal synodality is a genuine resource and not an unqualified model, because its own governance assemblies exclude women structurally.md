# Stanislaus insists tribal synodality is a genuine resource and not an unqualified model, because
its own governance assemblies exclude women structurally

The Khasi Durbar and Tangkhul Hangva are held up throughout this literature as evidence synodality
predates the Church's word for it — and rightly. But both are structurally restricted to male clan
heads, which means tribal culture is a resource for synodality, not an unqualified model of it.
Presenting it as the latter would import a fresh exclusion under the banner of correcting an old one.
