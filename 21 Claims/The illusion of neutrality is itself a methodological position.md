# The illusion of neutrality is itself a methodological position

No one approaches a text without a horizon. Claiming neutrality does not remove one's social location, formation and cultural assumptions; it only removes them from scrutiny. Responsible method names them — which is also why the theologian's standpoint belongs in the paper, not in a private preface.
