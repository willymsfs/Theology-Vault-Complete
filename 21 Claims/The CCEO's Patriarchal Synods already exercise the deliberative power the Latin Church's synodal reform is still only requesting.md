# The CCEO's Patriarchal Synods already exercise the deliberative power the Latin Church's synodal
reform is still only requesting

The Syro-Malabar and Syro-Malankara Synods elect their own Major Archbishops, erect eparchies and enact
particular law without prior Roman approval — genuine legislative and judicial authority, not
consultation. This means the Latin synodal reform is not asking for something unprecedented in the
Catholic communion; it is asking to catch up with a canonical model that already exists inside it.
