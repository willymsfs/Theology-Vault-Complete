# He never sacralises poverty, and any reading that makes the poor holy for being poor inverts him at the foundation

The poor are blessed "not because their dehumanizing poverty is a spiritual value but precisely because it is an evil which he is about to bring to an end." He calls poverty dehumanising, evil, and born of injustice and exploitation — and treats the poor as a social group with its own insight rather than as objects of charity.
