# The four volumes are collected articles, which explains the shape of his thought

Most chapters first appeared in Vidyajyoti, Jeevadhara, Jnanadeepa, and Bible Bhashyam. He was an essayist, not a monograph writer, and his characteristic architecture — broad contextual opening, biblical foundation, Indian application, closing appeal for a paradigm shift — is the shape of the journal essay rather than of the systematic treatise.
