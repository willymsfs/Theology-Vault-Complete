# Marcellus of Ancyra's condemnation, not Arius's, is what actually delayed Nicene reception in the East for a generation

Marcellus's modalist reading of homoousios directly tainted the Nicene term itself in Eastern eyes; the Cappadocian settlement had to rehabilitate homoousios from the modalism charge, not merely defeat Arianism outright, which is why the Basil/ousia-hypostasis distinction was theologically necessary rather than a mere refinement.
