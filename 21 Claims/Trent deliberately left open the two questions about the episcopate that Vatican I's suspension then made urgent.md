# Trent deliberately left open the two questions about the episcopate that Vatican I's suspension then made urgent

Trent taught that bishops are superior to priests and can ordain without defining the episcopate as a distinct sacramental degree, and left undecided whether episcopal jurisdiction comes directly from God or through the pope — so Vatican I's definition of papal primacy without a corresponding theology of the bishop produced the imbalance that made collegiality the fiercest battle of Vatican II.
