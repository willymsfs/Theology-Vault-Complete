# Pluralism is a disguised exclusivist secular agnosticism

D'Costa's charge against the threefold typology. Pluralism presents itself as the tolerant third option beyond exclusivism and inclusivism, but it **rules out in advance** every truth-claim that fails its own liberal modern criteria — that religions are culturally conditioned responses to an unknowable Real, that Christological language is metaphor. That is an exclusivism with a secular content rather than a confessional one.

The alternative he proposes is not a fourth box but a different operation: **trinitarian engagement**, holding radical difference and fragmentary commonality in tension without resolving either.

South Asian theology arrives at the same suspicion from the other side — the paradigm has already moved past exclusivism and inclusivism, and a theory that decides in advance what all traditions must be saying is no dialogue at all.
