# The Eastern location of the form in the crowning rather than in the consent is a structural divergence that explains the different treatment of second marriages

If the priest is the minister and the Church's blessing effects the marriage, then the Church retains a latitude in blessing that it does not have if the couple's own consent is the efficient cause — which is part of why oikonomia for a second marriage within a penitential rite is available to the East and not easily to the West.
