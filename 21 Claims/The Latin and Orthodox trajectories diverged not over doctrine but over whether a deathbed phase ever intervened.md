# The Latin and Orthodox trajectories diverged not over doctrine but over whether a deathbed phase ever intervened

The euchelaion retained the healing orientation continuously and never developed an "extreme unction," while the Latin rite passed through a millennium in which healing was effectively forgotten — meaning the 1972 reform did not move toward the East doctrinally so much as rejoin a trajectory the East had never left.
