# Indian liberation theology must add caste and pluralism to class analysis

Unlike Latin America, Indian liberation theology cannot work with **class** analysis alone. It must integrate **caste** (Dalit) and **religious pluralism** simultaneously. [[Sebastian Kappen]]'s "Jesuology" places Jesus in India's "socio-religious movements of dissent, contestation and revolt"; Dalit theology adds the caste dimension.
