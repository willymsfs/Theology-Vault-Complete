# The medieval practice created a self-reinforcing pastoral trap that only structural reform could break

Calling for the priest announced that the patient was dying, and surviving anointing could impose penitential disabilities for life — so families delayed; because they delayed, the sacrament was only ever observed at deathbeds, which confirmed the popular belief that it was for the dying and made the next family delay longer.
