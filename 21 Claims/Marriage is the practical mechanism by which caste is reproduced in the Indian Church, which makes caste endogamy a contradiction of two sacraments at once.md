# Marriage is the practical mechanism by which caste is reproduced in the Indian Church, which makes caste endogamy a contradiction of two sacraments at once

Baptism incorporates into a body in which there is neither slave nor free, and matrimony among the baptised is itself a sacrament signifying Christ's union with the Church — so caste-endogamous marriage among Christians both contradicts what baptism effected and enlists the sacrament of unity as the instrument of division, generation after generation.
