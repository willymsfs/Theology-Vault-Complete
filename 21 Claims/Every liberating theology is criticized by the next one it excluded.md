# Every liberating theology is criticized by the next one it excluded

The pattern running through Part V, in four documented moves:

1. **Womanist → black theology** — you made black women invisible (Delores Williams).
2. **Womanist and Third World → white feminist theology** — your "women's experience" was white and middle class.
3. **Postcolonial → liberation hermeneutics** — you romanticized the Bible, privileged Exodus while ignoring the displaced Canaanites, and homogenized "the poor" in purely economic terms (Sugirtharajah).
4. **Dalit → Indian inculturation** — you drew on the tradition that excluded us.

The lesson is structural, not moral: a theology built from one axis of oppression tends to naturalize the others. It is also the strongest argument for **kyriarchy** over a single-axis analysis.
