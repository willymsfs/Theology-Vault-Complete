# Theology is faith seeking understanding, not proof seeking certainty

Theology does not first prove God and then believe; it believes, and belief itself drives the search for understanding. Anselm's *fides quaerens intellectum* and *credo ut intelligam* ("I believe in order to understand") make faith the starting engine, not the conclusion. Augustine says the same: "Believe that you may understand." Understanding is the *reward* of faith, not its precondition.

**Evidence:** the whole medieval definition of theology rests on this; even Aquinas's "sacred science" borrows its principles from revelation, not from unaided reason.
