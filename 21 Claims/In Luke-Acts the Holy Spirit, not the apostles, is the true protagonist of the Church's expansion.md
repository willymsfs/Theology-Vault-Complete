# In Luke-Acts the Holy Spirit, not the apostles, is the true protagonist of the Church's expansion

From Pentecost onward, the initiative in Acts consistently belongs to the Spirit — directing Philip to the Ethiopian official, Peter to Cornelius, and the Antioch church to send out Paul and Barnabas.
