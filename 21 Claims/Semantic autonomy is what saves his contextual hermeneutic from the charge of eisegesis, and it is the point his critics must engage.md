# Semantic autonomy is what saves his contextual hermeneutic from the charge of eisegesis, and it is the point his critics must engage

Drawing on Ricoeur, he holds that the text's language "embodies an originary experience of the Absolute" and so resists absorption into the interpreter's "self-enclosed world" — which is why he retains historical criticism as base and control even while subordinating it to the service of the poor. A critique that accuses him of reading India into the Bible without addressing this warrant has not engaged his actual position.
