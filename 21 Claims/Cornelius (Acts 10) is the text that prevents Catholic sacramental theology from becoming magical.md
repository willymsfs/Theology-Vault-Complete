# Cornelius (Acts 10) is the text that prevents Catholic sacramental theology from becoming magical

Because the Spirit fell before the water, Luke establishes that God is not confined to the means he has bound the Church to use; Aquinas formalizes this as "God is not bound to the sacraments" (ST III q.64 a.7), which is precisely what licenses the doctrines of baptism of desire and baptism of blood.
