# The Bible is a library of theologies, not a single treatise

The Bible is "a library, a collection of books and consequently of theologies." The OT alone holds Yahwist, Elohist, Priestly, Deuteronomic, and Wisdom theologies — reflecting Israel's shifting history (agrarian → monarchy → vassal state). The NT holds Matthean, Marcan, Lucan, Johannine theologies.

**Consequence:** pluralism is not a modern deviation but the biblical norm → [[Legitimate pluralism runs from the Bible to today's contextual theologies]].
