# Tribal commensality functions as the levelling mechanism synodality elsewhere has to build by
canon law

Where the Latin Church needs canons, councils and consultative structures to approximate equal
standing before decisions, tribal commensality — the practice of eating together after every ritual
occasion — already performs that levelling function informally and continuously, smoothing personal
discord and restoring solidarity as an ordinary cultural reflex rather than a legislated procedure.
