# Legitimate pluralism runs from the Bible to today's contextual theologies

The same principle governs the whole history: one Christ, many legitimate expressions. It runs from OT/NT plurality, through Alexandrian vs Antiochene, to modern Black, Feminist, Dalit, and Minjung theologies. "The theologies in the Bible kept a delicate balance between maintaining the single focus — Christ, and allowing a legitimate pluralism of expression."
