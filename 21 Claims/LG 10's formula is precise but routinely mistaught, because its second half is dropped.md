# LG 10's formula is precise but routinely mistaught, because its second half is dropped

The common and ministerial priesthoods differ "in essence and not only in degree," which resists reducing ministry to a function of the community — but the same paragraph insists they are "ordered one to another" and that the priest exists to form and rule the priestly people, and omitting that clause turns a statement of relation into a statement of rank.
