# Jesus is the new King and the new Temple

Stephen's speech argues that God twice made a concession to Israel's demand for permanence — a king (1 Sam 8) and a temple — and that Israel repeatedly mistook the concession for the point. "Heaven is my throne… what house will you build for me?" (Is 66:1-2). The charge of blasphemy against the Temple is therefore answered by relocating the Temple: Jesus is it. This is why Stephen's death, not his argument, is the speech's conclusion.
