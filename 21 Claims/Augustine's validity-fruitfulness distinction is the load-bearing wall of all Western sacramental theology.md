# Augustine's validity/fruitfulness distinction is the load-bearing wall of all Western sacramental theology

Every subsequent doctrine depends on it: the unrepeatability of baptism (character), ex opere operato, the recognition of sacraments conferred by unworthy or schismatic ministers, the scholastic triple distinction, and the doctrine of reviviscentia all presuppose that a sacrament can genuinely exist while failing to profit its recipient.
