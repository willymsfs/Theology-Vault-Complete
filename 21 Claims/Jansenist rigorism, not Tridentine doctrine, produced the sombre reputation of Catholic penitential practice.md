# Jansenist rigorism, not Tridentine doctrine, produced the sombre reputation of Catholic penitential practice

Arnauld's programme of long deferral of absolution and infrequent communion formed generations in scrupulosity and fear, and its influence long outlasted its formal condemnation — which means critiques of "Tridentine" severity are frequently aimed at a Jansenist distortion of Trent rather than at Trent itself.
