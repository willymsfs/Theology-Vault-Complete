# Anubhava sets Indian method apart from doctrine-first theology

Indian theology characteristically begins from direct spiritual experience rather than propositional doctrine, institutionalised in the ashram and integrating jnana, bhakti and nishkama karma. The critical qualification comes from within: Soares-Prabhu judges many Christian ashrams elitist and disconnected from the struggles of the poor — an *anubhava* that never meets the poor is not yet an Indian starting point.
