# The gap between niti and nyaya names why formal synodal declarations coexist with unchanged lived
practice

James Ponniah's distinction: *niti* is the formal, institutional declaration of synodality (documents,
councils, consultations); *nyaya* is the actualised, lived experience of justice on the ground. A
diocese can accumulate a fully synodal *niti* while *nyaya* — who is actually heard, who actually
decides — remains untouched. The distinction explains why enthusiastic synodal documentation and
unreformed practice are not in tension for the institutions producing them; they are answering different
questions.
