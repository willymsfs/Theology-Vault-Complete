# Trent's seminary decree shaped Catholic priesthood more decisively than any of its doctrinal canons, including in ways it did not intend

Requiring every diocese to form candidates from adolescence in a residential college produced the first uniformly educated clergy in Christian history, but also produced a clergy formed in enclosure and separated from the laity from youth — the structural precondition of exactly the clerical culture that Optatam Totius and later critiques of clericalism target.
