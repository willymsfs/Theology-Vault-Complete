# Theology must hold mystery and intelligibility together

Between the errors of **Fideism** (faith only, no reason) and **Rationalism** (reason only, no mystery), theology walks a middle road: reason understands faith to a limited extent, but "faith alone makes it possible to penetrate the mystery." Yet "what we know and speak about is not the whole truth; there is an underlying deeper mystery to which we can only point."

**Example:** we can explain much about the Trinity, yet never exhaust it.
