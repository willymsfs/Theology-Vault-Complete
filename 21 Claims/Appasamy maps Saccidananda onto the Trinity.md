# Appasamy maps Saccidananda onto the Trinity

[[A. J. Appasamy]], from the [[Bhakti]] tradition, used [[Saccidananda]] (Being–Consciousness–Bliss) to explain the **Trinity** — Sat→Father, Chit→Son, Ananda→Spirit — while keeping "our separate personalities in our experience of union with God" (unlike strict [[Advaita]]).

**Comparative:** [[Saccidananda ↔ Trinity]]
**Concepts:** [[Saccidananda]] · [[Bhakti]] · [[Trinity]]
