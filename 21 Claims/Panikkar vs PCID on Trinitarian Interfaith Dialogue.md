# Panikkar's cosmotheandric vision and the PCID's 'two hands of God' framework represent genuinely incompatible, not merely differently emphasized, uses of Trinitarian theology in interfaith dialogue

Panikkar relativizes the historical particularity of the Incarnation beneath an abstract triadic pattern common to all religions, while the PCID explicitly uses the Trinity's fully realized historical particularity as the very thing that makes authentic (non-relativizing) dialogue possible — D'Costa's "inversion of the vestigia tradition" critique of Panikkar is essentially the argument that Christian orthodoxy and pluralist Trinitarianism cannot both be preserved at once.
