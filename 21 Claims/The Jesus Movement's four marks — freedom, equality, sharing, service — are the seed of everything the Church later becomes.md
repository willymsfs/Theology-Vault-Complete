# The Jesus Movement's four marks — freedom, equality, sharing, service — are the seed of everything the Church later becomes

Table fellowship, the disciples as brothers and sisters rather than master and servants, the common purse, and diakonia together prefigure the Church's later self-understanding as koinonia (Unit 4) and as servant (Unit 6).
