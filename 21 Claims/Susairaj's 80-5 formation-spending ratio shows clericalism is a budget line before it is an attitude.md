# Susairaj's 80-5 formation-spending ratio shows clericalism is a budget line before it is an
attitude

Approximately 80% of Indian diocesan financial resources go to seminary formation and the upkeep of
priests and religious, who make up around 5% of the Catholic population; the remaining 95% receive
almost no theological, canonical or leadership formation. Calling for a change in "clerical attitudes"
without redirecting this ratio treats a structural allocation as if it were a matter of individual
disposition — the fund exists before the mindset does, and the mindset is downstream of what the
budget makes possible and impossible.
