# Acts ends unfinished because the reader must finish it

Luke has Paul's death available to him and declines to narrate it. The book stops instead on two years of house arrest and one adverb — *akolytos*, "unhindered" (28:31) — leaving the third clause of Acts 1:8 grammatically open. The ending is a commissioning device: the ends of the earth have not been reached, and the reader is the next chapter.
