# Ignatius of Antioch establishes that Eucharistic realism is a corollary of Incarnational realism, not an independent doctrine

His argument against the Docetists is that they abstain from the Eucharist because they deny Christ had real flesh — meaning the two denials stand or fall together, and any christology that weakens the Incarnation will weaken the Eucharist by the same logic, as Irenaeus's forward argument to the resurrection of the body confirms.
