# The poor left the base communities for the churches of the Spirit

The hardest empirical fact facing liberation theology. Since the 1990s the Latin American poor have moved in large numbers from **basic ecclesial communities** into **Pentecostal** churches — the constituency in whose name the theology was written chose a different Christianity.

Why: Pentecostalism's **holistic worldview**, uniting the physical and the spiritual, meets felt needs for healing, deliverance and power that a rationalistic theology of structures does not address, and it offers immediate agency rather than deferred structural change.

This is why the movement is described as in crisis and has shifted from a strictly socioeconomic critique to a broader **sociocultural** register. The same question presses in India: any pastoral plan that ignores the pull of healing and power is answering a question nobody is asking.
