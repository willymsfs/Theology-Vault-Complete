# The 1971 form of Confirmation is the clearest single instance of ecumenical ressourcement in the reformed Latin rites

Paul VI replaced the medieval Latin formula not with a modern composition but with a direct translation of the Byzantine "Seal of the gift of the Holy Spirit," reaching backward past the medieval West to a formula shared with the churches from which the West had separated — Vatican II's method applied to sacramental form itself.
