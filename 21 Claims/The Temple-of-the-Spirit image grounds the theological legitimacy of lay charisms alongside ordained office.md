# The Temple-of-the-Spirit image grounds the theological legitimacy of lay charisms alongside ordained office

LG 4 and 12 describe the Spirit distributing both "hierarchic and charismatic" gifts — charisms are not a concession to the laity but a Spirit-given structural feature of the Church.
