# The reception crisis of Humanae Vitae had consequences for the sacrament of penance as severe as for marriage itself

Widespread quiet disagreement over contraception removed from the confessional a matter that had previously brought Catholics to it regularly, and episcopal statements emphasising conscience were widely read as licensing that disengagement — which is one of the substantial causes of the post-1960s collapse in the frequency of confession documented in the Penance course.
