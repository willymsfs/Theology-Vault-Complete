# We learn who God is by worshipping

Schmemann: theology articulates the Church's liturgical experience — "Lex orandi lex credendi," the norm of prayer is the norm of belief. Liturgy is "the revelation, the embodiment and the expression of the Church's faith." First worship; then put into words what the worshipping community has already tasted.
