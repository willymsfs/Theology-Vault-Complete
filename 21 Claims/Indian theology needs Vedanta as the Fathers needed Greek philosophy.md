# Indian theology needs Vedanta as the Fathers needed Greek philosophy

[[Brahmabandhab Upadhyaya]]'s programme: just as the early Church Fathers used Greek philosophy to translate the Gospel to the Hellenistic world, Indian Christians must use [[Vedanta]] to make the faith "understandable and acceptable to the Indian people." He wore "saffron cloth and became a sannyasi."
