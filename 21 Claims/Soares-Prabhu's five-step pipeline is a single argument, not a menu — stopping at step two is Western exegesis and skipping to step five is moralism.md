# Soares-Prabhu's five-step pipeline is a single argument, not a menu — stopping at step two is Western exegesis and skipping to step five is moralism

Commitment, historical criticism, social analysis, the dual Indian reading, and praxis are ordered so that each licenses the next: the declared social location makes the exegesis honest, the exegesis earns the structural critique, and the dual reading prevents the praxis collapsing into either pietism or programme. Extracting any single step and calling it his method misrepresents him.
