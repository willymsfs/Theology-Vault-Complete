# Persecution scatters the gospel rather than stopping it

Stephen's death triggers the first general persecution, "and they were all scattered throughout the region of Judea and Samaria" — whereupon "those who were scattered went about preaching the word" (Acts 8:1, 4). The persecution is what executes the second clause of Acts 1:8. Luke repeats the pattern: prison produces the Philippian jailer's household; custody produces five defences before ever-higher courts; house arrest in Rome produces the book's last word, *unhindered*.
