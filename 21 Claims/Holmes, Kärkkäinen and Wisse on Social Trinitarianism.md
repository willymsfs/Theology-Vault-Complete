# Holmes's and Kärkkäinen's simplicity-based critique of social trinitarianism and Wisse's Augustinian critique of the same movement (Part 3) converge on an identical diagnosis from different centuries

Both identify the same underlying move — treating God's unity as one item among several to be balanced against relationality, rather than as the very ground of relationality — as the structural error beneath every social-trinitarian model from Moltmann to Boff.
