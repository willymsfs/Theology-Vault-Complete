# The Gospels are history interpreted through faith

The Gospels are "anecdotal rather than biographical"; place and time markers are "infrequent, vague and sometimes conflicting." They express "not merely objective history (what takes place), but the religious significance of these events" — "history interpreted through faith."

**Implication:** reading a Gospel means reading a faith-proclamation, not a police report — grounds the need for literary-form interpretation ([[Dei Verbum]] 12).
