# His phrase 'thoroughly utopian law' is a reductio of the legalistic reading, not his verdict on the Sermon on the Mount

Read as juridical code the Sermon is impossible and productive of what Matthew calls hypocrisy; read as dharma it simply describes how someone who has undergone the Abba experience actually lives. Quoting the phrase flat inverts the argument of the essay it comes from — a common and detectable error.
