# Over-emphasis on experience, history or reason distorts theology in three predictable ways

Balance among the sources is itself a methodological requirement. Let experience dominate and theology becomes **mysticism**; let history dominate and it becomes **false naturalism**; let reason dominate and it becomes **rationalism**. The two opposite failures at the extremes are methodological imperialism (importing the natural sciences wholesale) and irrationalism (abandoning reason, which breeds sectarianism).
