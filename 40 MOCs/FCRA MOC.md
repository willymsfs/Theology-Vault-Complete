# FCRA — Map of Content

Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act compliance and expenditure classification for **BCPTI** (Bengal Catholic Priests' Training Institute).

> **Live tooling:** the `fcra` skill classifies any expenditure against the Schedule. Its `references/fcra-purposes.json` holds all 105 approved purposes keyword-indexed. Source notebook: `087dd849-2913-4ee1-807c-707cd2d19a0c`.

## The question this answers
Given any item of spending — *"maintenance of the seminary building"*, *"salary of the accountant"* — which category, which Schedule item, programme or administrative, and does it count against the 20% cap.

## BCPTI at a glance
- **Registration:** S/23735 of 1978-79, WB Societies Registration Act 1961, registered 10 Jan 1979, Barrackpore
- **FCRA purposes:** Educational (theological training) + Religious (formation without conversion)
- **Geography:** West Bengal — Ecclesiastical Region of Bengal
- **⚠ Object (g)** of the charter (aid to other charities) is **barred for foreign contribution** under Sec 7
- **Deadlines:** FC-6F by **22 June 2027** · FC-4 by **31 December** annually
