## Claims
- [[Parappally grounds synodality in a Trinitarian anthropology where community failure is always a failure of openness, transparency or discernment]]
- [[Tribal commensality functions as the levelling mechanism synodality elsewhere has to build by canon law]]
- [[Bishop Lawrence Pius reads the Eucharist as the primary synodal event because it is where status, class and gender are relativised before the table]]
- [[The consultative vote required by canons 511, 536 and 537 means a unanimous council can be legally overridden by the cleric who convened it]]
- [[Demel's diagnosis that consultative structures make lay co-responsibility a clerical concession rather than a baptismal right names the trap synodal reform keeps falling into]]
- [[Thonippara's vox populi warning holds that vote-counting synodality is indistinguishable from faction politics without genuine spiritual conversion]]
- [[The CCEO's Patriarchal Synods already exercise the deliberative power the Latin Church's synodal reform is still only requesting]]
- [[The Anglican three-house system shows a deliberative lay vote is compatible with apostolic ministry, which is the exact possibility Catholic canon law forecloses]]
- [[Conversation in the Spirit is guarded against groupthink by Doss's point that the Spirit sources plurality, not only unity]]
- [[Alexander's ordering of parrhesia before silence explains why courage and stillness are both necessary and neither is sufficient alone in synodal discernment]]
- [[Culas locates the safeguard of synodal method in the silence after speech, not in the speech itself]]
- [[Susairaj's 80-5 formation-spending ratio shows clericalism is a budget line before it is an attitude]]
- [[Stanislaus insists tribal synodality is a genuine resource and not an unqualified model, because its own governance assemblies exclude women structurally]]
- [[The gap between niti and nyaya names why formal synodal declarations coexist with unchanged lived practice]]
- [[Mulackal's dual-ecclesiology diagnosis explains why synodal structures coexist with unreformed centralisation]]
- [[The St Thomas Christian Yogams and the CCEO show synodality was a functioning Indian and Eastern Catholic reality before Vatican II supplied the word]]
- [[Tribal governance structures such as the Khasi Durbar reach decisions by consensus rather than majority vote for the same reason the Council of Jerusalem did]]
