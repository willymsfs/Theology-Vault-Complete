- [[Innocent I's Letter to Decentius (416) is the single strongest historical argument that the reservation of anointing to priests is a development rather than an apostolic given|Innocent I's Letter to Decentius (416) is the single strongest historical argument that the reservation of anointing to priests is a development rather than an apostolic given]]

- [[The medieval practice created a self-reinforcing pastoral trap that only structural reform could break|The medieval practice created a self-reinforcing pastoral trap that only structural reform could break]]
- [[Trent's word praesertim is what made the Vatican II reform legally possible without contradicting the Council of Trent|Trent's word praesertim is what made the Vatican II reform legally possible without contradicting the Council of Trent]]
- [[Cajetan's exegesis shows the case for reform was available within Catholic theology before the Reformation and was not merely a Protestant objection|Cajetan's exegesis shows the case for reform was available within Catholic theology before the Reformation and was not merely a Protestant objection]]
- [[Aquinas's doctrine of the reliquiae peccati is an elegant solution that nonetheless entrenched the very deathbed orientation the modern reform had to undo|Aquinas's doctrine of the reliquiae peccati is an elegant solution that nonetheless entrenched the very deathbed orientation the modern reform had to undo]]
- [[The 1972 reform's method was ressourcement, going behind the medieval development to the scriptural text|The 1972 reform's method was ressourcement, going behind the medieval development to the scriptural text]]
