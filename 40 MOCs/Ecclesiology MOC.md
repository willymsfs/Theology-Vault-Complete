## Claims by unit
**Unit 01** — [[The Church is not a voluntary club but a community called into being by God's initiative]] · [[The Jesus Movement's four marks — freedom, equality, sharing, service — are the seed of everything the Church later becomes]]
**Unit 02** — [[The Church is Christ's kingdom preaching translated into a Spirit-led community after Easter]] · [[NT ecclesiology is a plurality of local adaptations, not a single blueprint]]
**Unit 03** — [[In Luke-Acts the Holy Spirit, not the apostles, is the true protagonist of the Church's expansion]]
**Unit 04** — [[For John, unity is not an optional virtue but the very sign by which the world recognizes Christ's disciples]] · [[For Paul, baptism incorporates and Eucharist sustains one and the same ecclesial communion]]
**Unit 05** — [[The threefold ministry crystallized only gradually, as the Church settled into history after the apostolic generation passed]] · [[Vatican I's ecclesiology is a fragment, not a completed synthesis]]
**Unit 06** — [[No single model exhausts the mystery of the Church; a mature ecclesiology holds several models simultaneously]] · [[The institutional model, taken alone, reproduces exactly the four marks criticized in Unit 5]]
**Unit 07** — [[Conciliarism located supreme authority in the council; Vatican I decisively rejected this in favour of ordinary papal jurisdiction]] · [[Vatican II was the first genuinely world Church council in its composition and outlook]]
**Unit 08** — [[Vatican II did not change doctrine but re-categorized the Church's self-understanding across nine fundamental axes]] · [[Whether the nine paradigm shifts have been fully received in practice, and not merely proclaimed in text, remains genuinely contested]]
**Unit 09** — [[LG places People of God before Hierarchy to assert baptismal equality as prior to office]] · [[The Church, for LG, is sacrament, not simply the Kingdom of God itself]]
**Unit 10** — [[LG's communion shift re-reads the universal Church as constituted in and from local churches, not the reverse]] · [[The Temple-of-the-Spirit image grounds the theological legitimacy of lay charisms alongside ordained office]]
**Unit 11** — [[Collegiality is grounded sacramentally, not merely granted administratively by the Pope]] · [[Ut Unum Sint (1995) opened, for the first time, an official invitation to rethink how the papacy is exercised, without questioning its substance]]
**Unit 12** — [[Infallibility is not identical with revelation, inspiration, or indefectibility]] · [[All four Pastor Aeternus conditions must be met together for a papal statement to be infallible]]
**Unit 13** — [[Ecumenism is not diplomatic courtesy but flows theologically from the Church's own nature as communion and sacrament of unity]] · [[In the Indian context, ecumenism aims at unity in spirit, not uniformity of structure]]
**Unit 14** — [[Francis's synodal reforms aim to complete, in institutional practice, paradigm shifts Vatican II had already proclaimed in text]] · [[The Indian and Bengal Church's existing minority experience anticipates much of what Francis asks of the universal Church]]
