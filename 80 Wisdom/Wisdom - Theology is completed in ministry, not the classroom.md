# Theology is completed in ministry, not the classroom

> [!note] Example Wisdom note — Fr. Willy's own synthesis. Replace/extend with lived experience.

The classroom gave me [[Theology is faith seeking understanding, not proof seeking certainty]]. Ministry is teaching me what *kind* of understanding faith seeks: at a deathbed, the understanding sought is not information about God but the presence of God. [[Rahner]]'s "absolute saviour" is an abstraction in a lecture and a lifeline in a hospital corridor.
